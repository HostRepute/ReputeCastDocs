To schedule an email campaign, or send it at once, you open its <UiPath path="composer.reviewSend" /> page, pick when it goes out and confirm what [Send Readiness](/campaigns/send-readiness/) found. ReputeCast checks the campaign again at the send time, holds it if something changed that you didn't confirm, and guards it with [auto-pause](/reference/glossary/#auto-pause) while it sends.

## Before you begin

- Sending or scheduling needs `campaigns:send`. Without it, the send button is disabled and names who can send it; when the workspace's approval policy has the "author lacks the send permission" rule on (it is on by default), you can [request approval](/campaigns/approvals/) instead.
- The campaign has a sender, an audience and content, and Send Readiness isn't Blocked.
- A member, API key or agent limited to some lists sends only to those lists, with no segment, and one limited to some sender domains sends only from them, whoever picked the audience or the From address. Since 1.5.0 every way of sending, scheduling, moving or resuming a campaign checks it as it is at that moment. See [roles and permissions](/team/roles-and-permissions/).

## Send or schedule

<Steps>

1. Open the campaign and select <UiPath path="composer.reviewSend" />. Send Readiness runs if its last result is out of date; sending stays locked until it finishes.

2. Fix every blocking issue. The verdict must be Ready or Ready with warnings. If some checks couldn't run, retry them or continue without them (see [when checks can't run](/campaigns/send-readiness/#when-checks-cant-run)).

3. Check the <UiPath path="review.audience" /> card. Unengaged contacts are left out by default; select <UiPath path="review.includeThem" /> on their line only if this send should reach them, which adds a warning. With a [holdout group](/reference/glossary/#holdout-group), decide whether this send [includes it](#include-the-holdout-group).

4. Check the <UiPath path="review.thresholdsTitle" /> card. It shows the thresholds this send pauses at, the workspace defaults unless the campaign has its own, and what pauses when one trips. To change them for this campaign only, open <UiPath path="review.changeForThisSend" />:
   - Rates: <UiPath path="review.thresholdBounce" /> (0.1–20%), <UiPath path="review.thresholdComplaint" /> (0.01–5%) and <UiPath path="review.thresholdMailbox" /> (0.01–5%, at most 5 times the complaint rate).
   - <UiPath path="review.thresholdErrors" />: a route whose failed calls pass this share over three minutes is handled as an outage.
   - Scope, under <UiPath path="review.whenTrips" />: <UiPath path="review.pauseRoute" /> (the default) or <UiPath path="review.pauseCampaign" />.

   Changes save as you leave a field and are recorded in the campaign's history; the thresholds are fixed once the send starts. See [monitor a send and auto-pause](/campaigns/live-monitor/) for how they are applied.

   Under it, the <UiPath path="review.linksCard" /> card says whether the send [adds UTM tags](#add-utm-tags-to-this-send) to its links.

5. Under <UiPath path="review.scheduling" />, choose when it goes out:
   - Select <UiPath path="review.modeNow" /> to start within a minute of confirming.
   - Select <UiPath path="review.modeAt" /> to pick a date and a time in the <UiPath path="review.timezone" /> you choose. The time zone starts as the workspace's. The earliest time is one minute from now.
   - Select <UiPath path="review.modeBest" /> to send each contact the email at the hour they open email most often, within 24 hours after the time you set. See [how best time works](#how-best-time-works).
   - Select the option for one time of day in each recipient's time zone, then pick the day and the time: everyone gets it at that time where they are. See [how local time works](#how-local-time-works).

6. Select the main action. It states what it will do, for example "Send now to 31,192", "Schedule for Sun 1 Nov, 09:00 ET", "Schedule best time from Sun 1 Nov, 09:00 ET" or "Schedule 09:00 local from Sun 1 Nov". On a phone it sits in a bar at the bottom of the screen.

7. Read the confirmation. It shows when the send starts and when the checks run again, the sender, reply-to address, subject, A/B test, route, cost and duration (with the time the A/B winner is picked), the auto-pause thresholds and who is excluded (suppressed, role addresses, duplicates and unengaged contacts).

8. Choose the <UiPath path="review.canaryLabel" />:
   - <UiPath path="review.canaryAuto" /> lets the sending engine decide: a new sending domain or IP, contacts never mailed from the domain, or an audience over twice the usual one start with a canary. The hint under the field says what it will do for this send.
   - <UiPath path="review.canaryOn" /> sends to your most engaged contacts and your [seed mailboxes](/reference/glossary/#seed-mailbox) first, and goes on when their results look healthy.
   - <UiPath path="review.canaryOff" /> sends to everyone at the route's pace.

   See [canary](/reference/glossary/#canary) and the [live monitor](/campaigns/live-monitor/) for how a canary is watched.

9. Tick each warning to confirm it, for example "I understand 412 unengaged contacts are included." A warning with a one-click fix offers it as a link, such as "Send via SES only instead". Warnings someone dismissed in the composer are listed as already confirmed.

10. If the send is unusual, type the recipient count under <UiPath path="review.typeToConfirm" />. The dialog says why it asks: an audience much larger than usual, a first send to a list, from a sending domain or through a sending provider, or a blocklist listing above low impact. A routine send doesn't ask. The workspace sets "much larger" in [sending rules](/campaigns/sending-rules/#set-the-audience-defaults).

11. Select <UiPath path="review.sendNowButton" /> or <UiPath path="review.actionSchedule" />.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
The <UiPath path="senders.providers.confirmOver" /> field in the workspace sending defaults is saved but not used. When a send asks for the typed count follows <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.sendingRules" />.
</Aside>

## How best time works

ReputeCast looks at each contact's human opens in the last 180 days (machine opens such as Apple Mail Privacy Protection prefetches don't count) and takes the hour of the day, in UTC, when they opened most often.

- The send starts at the time you set. A contact whose hour is the current hour gets the email at once; everyone else gets it at the next start of their hour, always less than 24 hours after the scheduled time.
- Contacts with no human opens in 180 days get the email at the scheduled time.
- An A/B test on send time overrides best time for the contacts in the test.

## How local time works

With one local time, ReputeCast sends the email at the time you picked in each recipient's own time zone, as texts already do. Contacts without a time zone get it at that time in the zone you chose.

- The send starts when that time first comes anywhere, in UTC+14, and each recipient waits for their own; the last get it in UTC−11, about 25 hours later. Review & send's estimate covers the whole span.
- Send Readiness checks the campaign again before the send starts, and the send-time recheck holds it the same way as any scheduled send.
- The [campaign calendar](/campaigns/calendar/) shows the campaign on its day with its local time.
- An A/B test on send time sets its own times for the contacts in the test.

## How cadence works

Readers choose on the [preference center](/forms/public-pages/) how often they hear from you: <UiPath path="pagelabels.prefs.all" /> (also everyone who never chose), <UiPath path="pagelabels.prefs.weekly" /> or <UiPath path="pagelabels.prefs.monthly" />. Each campaign says which of them it reaches: in the [composer](/campaigns/composer/), open <UiPath path="composer.chrome.sendingTo" /> and, under <UiPath path="composer.cadence" />, choose <UiPath path="composer.cadenceRegular" /> (the default) or <UiPath path="composer.cadenceAnnouncement" />. The drawer counts the readers a regular issue leaves out.

- A regular issue reaches <UiPath path="pagelabels.prefs.all" /> readers only.
- An issue of a weekly or monthly [recurring digest](/campaigns/recurring-digests/) also reaches the readers who chose that rhythm. Daily digests and digests sent after a number of posts are regular issues.
- An announcement reaches everyone on its lists, whatever they chose. Keep it for news that matters to all.

The readers left out appear as a line of the <UiPath path="review.audience" /> card on Review & send, and the approval snapshot, the send and the report count the same audience.

## Add UTM tags to this send

The <UiPath path="review.linksCard" /> card on Review & send decides, since 1.6.0, whether this send adds UTM tags to its links, so your web analytics show which visits the email brought. Its <UiPath path="review.utmTags" /> switch starts as the workspace's <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.senders > senders.utmLinks" />, named under it ("Workspace default: off"), and turning it on or off makes the choice this campaign's own.

<Screenshot
  id="review-send-utm"
  alt="The Links card on Review & send: the Add UTM tags switch off, with what it adds and why, and Workspace default: off with a link to Settings › Senders."
/>

- On, the card lists the tags the send adds: `utm_source` with the workspace's short name from its address, such as `acme-newsletter`, `utm_medium=email` and `utm_campaign` with the campaign's name as a slug, such as `utm_campaign=weekly-digest-142`. The slug keeps letters and digits, drops accents ("Bản tin" becomes `ban-tin`) and joins words with dashes.
- They go on every web link the email tracks clicks on, whether click tracking is on or not, before the email is compiled, so the link readers see, the click redirect and the plain-text part carry the same address. A link keeps the UTM parameters it already sets, and a link with Liquid in its address stays as written.
- The switch saves at once and needs `campaigns:edit`; if the change can't be saved, it goes back. It changes what [workspace rules](/campaigns/sending-rules/#how-rules-appear-in-send-readiness) about UTM tags see, so the checks run again; if the page offers <UiPath path="review.runChecksAgain" /> instead, select it before you send.

## Include the holdout group

When the workspace keeps a [holdout group](/audience/holdout-group/) out of campaigns, the <UiPath path="review.audience" /> card leaves it out, with its own line such as "Holdout group: 1,598 left out (5%)". Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

<Screenshot
  id="review-send-holdout"
  alt="The Audience card of Black Friday Early Access: the Weekly Digest list's 31,701 contacts, minus role addresses, 187 duplicates, Holdout group: 1,598 left out (5%), 1 reader who chose a weekly or monthly rhythm, 738 stopped by the sunset policy and 849 unengaged contacts, down to 28,325 final recipients, and the unticked Include the holdout group with its hint."
/>

<Steps>

1. Before you schedule, decide whether this send must reach everyone, such as a change to your terms.

2. If it must, tick <UiPath path="review.includeHoldout" /> under the final recipients. The choice saves at once and changes the campaign's audience, so the checks run again; if the page offers <UiPath path="review.runChecksAgain" /> instead, select it.

3. Once the checks finish, the holdout line is gone, and a note under the box says how many of the group's contacts the send reaches, such as "The 1,560 contacts in the holdout group (5%) get this send." Send or schedule as usual.

</Steps>

- The choice is part of the campaign's audience: an [approval](/campaigns/approvals/#the-holdout-group) compares it, and the [holdout group's results](/audience/holdout-group/) count the send among the campaigns that included the group.
- It needs `campaigns:edit` and can't change while the campaign is scheduled. Select <UiPath path="review.unschedule" /> first.
- The send works out who the group leaves out when it starts building its audience, so a change in [sending rules](/campaigns/sending-rules/#hold-out-a-group-to-measure-marketing) during the send doesn't split it.

## After you confirm

- **Send now:** the send starts and the [live monitor](/campaigns/live-monitor/) opens. A send that starts now relies on checks run in the last 15 minutes; if the run is older, the checks run again first and you send when they finish.
- **Scheduled:** the banner shows the time, the audience, the route and when the checks run again (15 minutes before the send). To change the time, select <UiPath path="review.unschedule" />: the campaign goes back to draft (or stays approved), and the checks, settings and approval stay as they are. To move it to another day, drag it on the [campaign calendar](/campaigns/calendar/) instead.
- **What the send keeps:** whether `List-Unsubscribe` carries a `mailto:` address (since 1.6.0, only when <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.senders > senders.listUnsubscribeMailto" /> is on) and the UTM tags are decided when the send starts, so changing them while it sends never splits one send.

### When the send-time recheck holds the send

At the send time, the checks run again and are compared with what you confirmed. The send is held, and nothing goes out, when a check now blocks, a new warning or Unknown appears that nobody confirmed, the audience changed by more than 10%, or the content, audience, sender or route changed after you scheduled it. Review & send then shows <UiPath path="review.heldRecheckTitle" /> with the reason, and you and the approvers are notified.

Select <UiPath path="review.reviewAgain" /> to confirm the send on the fresh checks; a changed audience asks you to type the new count. A held send nobody reviews goes back to Draft 3 hours after its time, with its schedule and A/B test kept. See [held sends](/campaigns/send-readiness/#held-sends).

## When an approval is needed

If a rule in the workspace's [approval policy](/campaigns/approvals/) matches, Review & send shows <UiPath path="review.requiredTitle" /> and the rules that matched, named in people's words ("Your role, Content Writer, needs an approval to send: Marcus Chen or Linh Nguyen can approve"). Select <UiPath path="review.requestApproval" /> instead of sending. Once an approver approves, the author schedules it with <UiPath path="review.actionApproved" />; that needs `campaigns:edit`, not `campaigns:send`. An approval sends nothing by itself: scheduling is a send by whoever schedules it, so their lists and sender domains apply. Changing the time of an approved campaign invalidates the approval.

Owners can use <UiPath path="review.moreActions" /> › <UiPath path="review.sendWithoutApproval" /> in an emergency when the policy allows it. They give a reason and, if their account has a password, confirm it, then select <UiPath path="review.sendNowWithout" /> or <UiPath path="review.scheduleWithout" />. Every approver is notified, and Send Readiness still runs.

## Check that it worked

- The campaign shows Scheduled (or Sending) in the [campaigns list](/campaigns/overview/).
- The `campaign.scheduled` [webhook event](/reference/webhook-events/) fires for a scheduled send.
- At the send time the live monitor shows progress. Before then, the banner on Review & send shows the next recheck.

## Troubleshooting

- **The send button is disabled and says who can send it.** Your role can't send campaigns. Ask one of the people it names, or request approval.
- **"Pick a time in the future, or send now."** The time you chose is less than a minute away or in the past. Choose a later time.
- **"The campaign is scheduled. Unschedule it to change when it goes out."** Select <UiPath path="review.unschedule" /> first, change the time, then schedule again.
- **"The campaign is scheduled. Unschedule it to change who gets it."** <UiPath path="review.includeHoldout" /> can't change on a scheduled campaign. Unschedule it, change the choice, then schedule again.
- **"Your access is limited to some sender domains, and this campaign sends from news.acme.io."** You, or the key or agent, are limited to other sender domains. Pick a From address on one of yours in the composer, or ask someone who can send from that domain.
- **"Your access is limited to some lists: send only to those lists, not to other lists or a segment."** The audience reaches contacts outside your lists. Send to your lists only, or ask someone without that limit.
- **"Couldn't save the UTM tags"** or **"Couldn't change who gets this send"**: the switch or the box went back to its saved state. Try again; the message under it says why.
- **"The checks ran 22 minutes ago, so they run again before a send that starts now."** Wait for the checks to finish, then send.
- **"Send Readiness ran again since you looked."** The results changed while the confirmation was open. Review the new results and confirm again.
- **"Type 31192 to confirm the send."** The typed number must match the recipient count. Separators are ignored.
- **"Confirm each warning before sending."** Tick every warning in the confirmation, or fix them on Review & send.
- **Sending is locked.** The checks are running again, or a fix is being saved. The button says which; it unlocks when they finish.
- **The scheduled send didn't start.** The send-time recheck held it. Open Review & send, read why, and select <UiPath path="review.reviewAgain" />, or <UiPath path="review.unschedule" />.
