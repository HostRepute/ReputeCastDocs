[Send Readiness](/reference/glossary/#send-readiness) is the pre-send email deliverability check on every campaign's <UiPath path="composer.reviewSend" /> page. It checks your sender reputation, authentication, content, links, compliance, audience and route in one run, gives a [verdict](/reference/glossary/#verdict), and offers a fix for most problems it finds. It runs again before the send starts, so a problem that appears overnight holds the send instead of reaching your list.

## Verdicts and check results

Each check returns one result, and the results decide the verdict:

| Check result                              | What it means                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <UiPath path="review.statusPass" />    | Nothing to do.                                                                  |
| <UiPath path="review.statusWarn" />    | The send can go, but you confirm the warning when you send.                     |
| <UiPath path="review.statusBlock" />   | The send can't go until it is fixed.                                            |
| <UiPath path="review.statusUnknown" /> | The check couldn't run, for example because blocklist data is unavailable.      |

| Verdict                                     | When                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| <UiPath path="review.verdictBlocked" />  | Any check blocks. The send button stays disabled until every block is fixed.               |
| <UiPath path="review.verdictWarnings" /> | Nothing blocks, but something warns.                                                       |
| <UiPath path="review.verdictReady" />    | Nothing blocks or warns.                                                                   |

Unknown results don't change the verdict, but sending stays disabled until you retry the checks or choose to continue without them (see [when HostRepute doesn't respond](#when-hostrepute-doesnt-respond)).

## The ten check groups

Groups appear in this order. A group with nothing to check is left out: Languages only appears for an email with language versions, and Conditional content only for an email with conditional blocks.

1. **<UiPath path="review.groupReputation" />.** Blocklist listings of your sending domains and of the dedicated IPs on the route: a listing on a high-impact list blocks, others warn, and missing data is Unknown. Each sending provider's own account health from its last 7 days of bounces and complaints: a warning from 80% of the provider's limit, a block at the level where the provider pauses accounts. A [warm-up](/providers/warm-up/) day cap below the recipients routed to that provider also warns.
2. **<UiPath path="review.groupAuthentication" />.** A sender is set; SPF authorizes the sending provider; DKIM is verified (a key under 1024 bits warns); DMARC exists and is valid (under 90% aligned mail in the last 7 days warns); the MAIL FROM domain keeps SPF aligned. A domain that was never checked warns. See [set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC](/deliverability/spf-dkim-dmarc/).
3. **<UiPath path="review.groupContent" />.** An empty subject blocks. Subject spam heuristics (all caps, repeated punctuation, urgency and money words), images over 60% of the email, HTML from 85 KB (Gmail clips at 102 KB) and an empty plain-text part warn. Incomplete polls, sponsor and upgrade blocks are checked here too. See [fix content warnings before sending](/campaigns/content-checks/).
4. **<UiPath path="review.groupLanguages" />.** Which version each group of contacts gets, and warnings for Outdated and Draft versions. This group never blocks. See [send each reader their language](/campaigns/languages/).
5. **<UiPath path="review.groupAccessibility" />.** Serious issues (missing alt text, links with no text, low contrast) warn, or block when the workspace policy is Block. Moderate issues warn. See [check email accessibility](/campaigns/accessibility/).
6. **<UiPath path="review.groupConditional" />.** A condition that points at a deleted segment, list or field blocks, and so does a version with nothing between the header and the footer. A version over 102 KB warns. Above 50 versions, the 50 with the largest audiences plus the largest and smallest email are checked. See [show blocks to some readers](/campaigns/conditional-content/).
7. **<UiPath path="review.groupLinks" />.** A link domain on SURBL, URIBL or Spamhaus DBL blocks. Broken links (404, 410, a 5xx answer or no answer), URL shorteners and buttons without a link warn.
8. **<UiPath path="review.groupCompliance" />.** The body (of every language version) needs an unsubscribe link and a postal address, or the send blocks. The one-click `List-Unsubscribe` header needs an HTTPS public URL, or it warns. The [bulk sender rules](/deliverability/bulk-sender-requirements/) of Gmail and Yahoo: DMARC that doesn't pass warns, and the route's 7-day complaint rate warns from 0.1% and blocks from 0.3%.
9. **<UiPath path="review.groupAudience" />.** An audience with nobody to send to blocks. Unengaged contacts (1% of the audience or 500 contacts), contacts that soft-bounced in the last 30 days, risky addresses from [address verification](/audience/verification/) and inactive contacts not verified in 90 days warn.
10. **<UiPath path="review.groupRouting" />.** A stream without a sending provider, or a provider on the route that is down, paused or degraded, blocks. It only warns when the stream's failover policy isn't "pause and ask" and another provider can take the share. A provider in sandbox mode or one whose terms don't allow bulk mail blocks. A 24-hour quota below the share and provider-side open or click tracking (double tracking) warn. See [route mail across providers](/providers/routing/).

## One-click fixes

Most warnings and blocks come with a button that fixes them in place, for example "Insert footer block", "Replace with destination URL", "Send 100% via SES", "Exclude 412 unengaged" or "Mark as decorative". The banner can apply every block's fix, or every warning's fix, at once.

- A fix needs `campaigns:edit`. A fix that changes the route also needs `campaigns:send`.
- Every fix is recorded in the audit log, and Send Readiness runs again right after it.
- A fix that changes the campaign also re-checks an open [approval](/campaigns/approvals/) against what was approved.

## When it runs

- **When you open Review & send**, if the last run is missing or out of date and you can edit the campaign. Editing the campaign doesn't rerun the checks by itself; it marks the last run as out of date. Changing only the schedule keeps the run valid.
- **When you select** <UiPath path="review.rerun" />, and right after a one-click fix.
- **When you request approval**, if the last run is out of date.
- **15 minutes before a scheduled send.** A block here holds the send.
- **When the send starts.** With Send now, a block means nothing is sent. With a schedule, the campaign is held.

A held send shows "Send-time recheck turned Blocked" on Review & send, with <UiPath path="review.cancelSend" />. ReputeCast notifies the person who requested approval (or last edited the campaign) and the approvers, sends the `campaign.paused` [webhook](/developers/webhooks/) with `paused_by: send_readiness`, and checks again every 15 minutes. When a recheck passes, the send starts, at once if its time has already passed.

## When HostRepute doesn't respond

Send Readiness doesn't call [HostRepute](/reference/glossary/#hostrepute) during a run. It reads the blocklist results that the hourly HostRepute sync and the built-in DNS blocklist checks stored. A HostRepute request gives up after 30 seconds and 3 attempts. When HostRepute data can't be refreshed and no newer built-in result exists, the blocklist checks are Unknown, never Block, and the page says "Couldn't finish: HostRepute didn't respond". Provider account health still comes from each sending provider's API and is unaffected.

You then choose:

- Select <UiPath path="review.retryBlocklist" /> to run the checks again.
- Or select <UiPath path="review.continueProvider" /> to send without the blocklist results. ReputeCast records the skipped checks in the campaign's audit trail and runs them again when the send starts.

See [connect HostRepute](/deliverability/hostrepute/) and [check and fix blocklist listings](/deliverability/blocklists/).

## Estimates

Next to the checks, Review & send shows what the send will do:

- **Audience:** the list or segment, minus the shared suppression list, role addresses, duplicates (plus tags and Gmail dots are ignored) and contacts stopped by the sunset policy, down to <UiPath path="review.finalRecipients" />. With language versions it also shows recipients by version.
- **<UiPath path="review.routeBreakdown" />:** each sending provider's share, its cost at the provider's price per 1,000 and how long it takes. Routes run in parallel. A provider that is down shows its share as held, with what moving it would cost.
- **<UiPath path="review.throttling" />:** the sending rate for Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud and other mailbox providers, which sets how long the send takes. The rates come from [workspace sending defaults](/providers/workspace-defaults/).

## When to change them

The check thresholds are fixed. What you can change is how strict the outcome is:

- Switch the workspace [accessibility policy](/campaigns/accessibility/) to Block so serious accessibility issues stop a send.
- Set a stream's failover policy to "pause and ask" if a down provider should block a send instead of moving its share.
- Adjust the sunset policy in [list health](/audience/list-health/): it sets which contacts count as unengaged.
- Turn on the approval rule for Ready with warnings if every warning should get a second pair of eyes.
