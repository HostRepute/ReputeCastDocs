[Send Readiness](/reference/glossary/#send-readiness) is the pre-send email deliverability check on every campaign's <UiPath path="composer.reviewSend" /> page. It checks your sender reputation, authentication, content, links, compliance, audience, route and your workspace's own [sending rules](/campaigns/sending-rules/) in one run, gives a [verdict](/reference/glossary/#verdict), and offers a fix for most problems it finds. It runs again at the send time, so a problem that appears after you schedule holds the send instead of reaching your list.

## Verdicts and check results

Each check returns one result, and the results decide the verdict:

| Check result                           | What it means                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <UiPath path="review.statusPass" />    | Nothing to do.                                                                                                   |
| <UiPath path="review.statusWarn" />    | The send can go, but you confirm the warning when you send.                                                      |
| <UiPath path="review.statusBlock" />   | The send can't go until it is fixed.                                                                             |
| <UiPath path="review.statusUnknown" /> | The check couldn't run, for example because DNS didn't answer. Not knowing is never counted as clean or as Block. |

| Verdict                                  | When                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <UiPath path="review.verdictBlocked" />  | Any check blocks. The send button stays disabled until every block is fixed. |
| <UiPath path="review.verdictWarnings" /> | Nothing blocks, but something warns.                                         |
| <UiPath path="review.verdictReady" />    | Nothing blocks or warns.                                                     |

The banner adds what is left to do: "Ready with warnings · 2 to review", "Blocked · 3 to fix". While a check is Unknown, the verdict can't be Ready and sending stays disabled until you retry the checks or choose to continue without them (see [when checks can't run](#when-checks-cant-run)).

## How Review & send is laid out

The page puts what needs you first:

- **The banner** shows the verdict, how many checks passed, warn and block, and when the checks ran. Once the campaign is scheduled it says when it goes out, to how many recipients, through which sending providers, and when the checks run again, with tiles for the time left, the A/B test, the auto-pause thresholds and who scheduled it.
- **Needs attention** lists the issues. When something blocks, it lists the blocking issues, then the warnings, then the checks that couldn't run. Each row is one plain sentence with its fix buttons; under each button a short line says what applying it changes, such as "+3 min · +$0.31", "Audience becomes 31,192" or "Changes 2 links · preview first". <UiPath path="review.technicalDetails" /> holds what the sentence leaves out: SPF and DMARC records as DNS returns them, DKIM keys and MAIL FROM per sending provider, the `List-Unsubscribe` headers as sent, the HTTP status of links and images, DNS answers such as `SERVFAIL`, provider error codes and, for a workspace rule, the rule itself.
- **Warnings dismissed in the composer** stay in the list with who dismissed them and why, and count as already confirmed. Anyone who can edit the campaign can undo the dismissal there. See [fix content warnings before sending](/campaigns/content-checks/).
- **Passed** collapses every passing check into one chip per group, with its count; when the send is blocked, it shows one summary line per group instead. Expand it to read every check, grouped.
- **<UiPath path="placement.entryTitle" />** shows the campaign's latest [placement test](/deliverability/placement-tests/) per mailbox provider and starts a new one. It is optional and never changes the verdict.
- **The right column** holds the estimates (audience, <UiPath path="review.routeBreakdown" />, <UiPath path="review.throttling" />), the <UiPath path="review.thresholdsTitle" /> thresholds, <UiPath path="review.scheduling" />, <UiPath path="review.testSends" /> and the main action. The main action states the schedule, for example "Schedule for Sun 1 Nov, 09:00 ET" or "Send now to 31,192".

Test sends work while the campaign is Blocked, so you can check a fix in your inbox before the checks clear.

## The check groups

A run has up to 11 groups. A group with nothing to check is left out: Languages only appears for an email with language versions, Conditional content only for an email with conditional blocks, and Workspace rules only when a rule applies. Passed checks and the full list show the groups in this order.

1. **<UiPath path="review.groupReputation" />.**
   - Blocklist listings of the sending domain, its parent domain (`acme.io` for `news.acme.io`), the return-path (MAIL FROM) domains of the sending providers that carry the send, and the tracking domain. Domains that [Deliverability](/deliverability/blocklists/) already checks use its results; the others are looked up live on Spamhaus DBL and SURBL. A listing of any of them counts: a high-impact list blocks, others warn. A lookup that fails is Unknown and says why.
   - The dedicated IPs on the route: a listing on a high-impact list blocks, others warn. An IP that Deliverability's daily checks haven't reached yet is Unknown, with a link to run the checks now.
   - Reverse DNS of those dedicated IPs: missing, or pointing to a name that doesn't resolve back to the IP, blocks; a failed lookup is Unknown.
   - Each sending provider's bounce and complaint rates over the last 7 days, against the provider's own limits: a warning from 80% of a limit, a block at the level where the provider pauses accounts. Rates aren't judged until the provider carried enough mail (see [defaults and limits](#defaults-and-limits)). The complaint rate counts only mail delivered to mailbox providers that report complaints; Gmail and iCloud never do, so with only their mail it reads "n/a".
   - A [warm-up](/providers/warm-up/) cap on the send day below the recipients routed to that provider warns: the rest waits for the next day's cap.
2. **<UiPath path="review.groupAuthentication" />.** A sender is set; SPF authorizes each sending provider; DKIM is verified (a key under 1024 bits warns); DMARC exists and is valid (under 90% aligned mail in the last 7 days warns); mail through a provider with neither verified DKIM nor an aligned return path blocks, because it won't pass DMARC; the MAIL FROM domain keeps SPF aligned (warns otherwise); an invalid BIMI record warns. A domain that was never checked warns. A DNS lookup that fails twice in a run is Unknown, never a Block that calls the record invalid. See [set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC](/deliverability/spf-dkim-dmarc/).
3. **<UiPath path="review.groupContent" />.** Checked in every language version and every [A/B variant](/campaigns/ab-testing/). An empty subject blocks, and so does a broken Liquid tag, because the email can't be rendered. Spam heuristics in the subject, preheader or body text, a tag that isn't a contact field, a first-name tag without a fallback for recipients without a first name, images over 60% of the email, HTML from 85 KB with links rewritten for tracking (Gmail clips at 102 KB), images that don't load or load over plain HTTP, missing alt text and an empty plain-text part warn. Incomplete polls, Sponsor and Upgrade blocks and removed store products are checked here too. See [fix content warnings before sending](/campaigns/content-checks/).
4. **<UiPath path="review.groupLanguages" />.** Which version each group of contacts gets, and warnings for Outdated and Draft versions. This group never blocks. See [send each reader their language](/campaigns/languages/).
5. **<UiPath path="review.groupAccessibility" />.** Serious issues (missing alt text, links with no text, low contrast) warn, or block when the workspace policy is Block. Moderate issues warn. See [check email accessibility](/campaigns/accessibility/).
6. **<UiPath path="review.groupConditional" />.** A condition that points at a deleted segment, list or field blocks, and so does a version with nothing between the header and the footer. A version over 102 KB warns. Above 50 versions, the 50 with the largest audiences plus the largest and smallest email are checked. See [show blocks to some readers](/campaigns/conditional-content/).
7. **<UiPath path="review.groupLinks" />.** Link domains are looked up live on Spamhaus DBL and SURBL, the same lookups the composer uses: a listed domain blocks, a domain that couldn't be looked up is Unknown. Every link is opened: a 404, 410, a 5xx answer or no answer warns, and the row counts honestly ("40 of 60 links respond; 20 couldn't be checked") when a site refuses automated requests (LinkedIn answers 999) or the email has more links than a run opens, which makes it Unknown. URL shorteners and buttons without a link warn. A tracking domain that doesn't resolve blocks; tracked links over plain HTTP warn.
8. **<UiPath path="review.groupCompliance" />.** The body of every version and variant needs an unsubscribe link and a postal address, or the send blocks. The one-click `List-Unsubscribe` header needs an HTTPS public URL, and a sending provider that doesn't sign those headers with DKIM warns. The bulk-sender row covers the [Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender rules](/deliverability/bulk-sender-requirements/): DMARC that doesn't pass warns; the route's 7-day complaint rate, and Gmail's spam rate from Postmaster Tools, warn from 0.1% and block from 0.3%; with Postmaster data for the sending domain, the row shows Google's own status, Compliant or Needs work (which warns).
9. **<UiPath path="review.groupAudience" />.** An audience with nobody to send to blocks. [Unengaged contacts](/reference/glossary/#unengaged-contact) are left out by default; included, they warn from 1% of the audience or 500 contacts. Contacts that soft-bounced in the last 30 days, segments that couldn't be counted, risky addresses from [address verification](/audience/verification/) and inactive contacts not verified in 90 days warn.
10. **<UiPath path="review.groupRouting" />.** A stream without a sending provider, or a provider on the route that is down, paused or not reporting events, blocks. It only warns when the stream's failover policy isn't "pause and ask" and another provider can take the share. A provider in sandbox mode or one whose terms don't allow bulk mail blocks. A 24-hour quota below the share, provider-side open or click tracking (double tracking) and a fallback provider that couldn't take this send warn. See [route mail across providers](/providers/routing/).
11. **<UiPath path="review.groupRules" />.** One row per [sending rule](/campaigns/sending-rules/) that is on for Broadcasts: a broken rule warns or blocks, as the rule says.

## One-click fixes

Most warnings and blocks come with a button that fixes them in place, for example <UiPath path="composer.canvas.insertFooter" />, "Send via SES only", "Exclude 412 unengaged", "Add utm_campaign=weekly-digest-142" or "Mark as decorative". Under each button, a line says what it changes before you apply it.

The button above the issues ("Review 3 fixes") opens a drawer that lists every one-click fix with a checkbox and its consequences: the route, sending time, cost and warm-up it changes, each link before and after, and what stays unchanged. Select <UiPath path="review.applyFixes" /> to apply the ones you ticked together.

- **Links are never guessed.** A listed or shortened link is offered "Replace with destination URL" only when the link redirects to a page whose domain is clean on Spamhaus DBL and SURBL; otherwise the fix is to edit the link. If the destination turns out to be listed, or can't be checked, the link isn't changed.
- **HTML stays intact.** A campaign written in HTML gets no fix that edits MJML, and saving an empty body over a body is refused.
- **Sending is locked** while a fix saves and while the checks run again after it.
- **Fixes can be undone.** The fixes applied together are kept as one batch. Select <UiPath path="review.undoFixes" /> on the toast, under the running banner or under the verdict to put back the content (restored as a new version), the content settings, the route and the audience, as long as the campaign hasn't changed since. Fixes that changed language versions, or were saved together with someone else's edit, are undone from Version history instead.
- Fixes need `campaigns:edit`, and a fix (or an undo) that changes the route also needs `campaigns:send`; a disabled button names who can help. Fixes, undos and dismissals are people's actions: API keys and agents can't apply them.
- Every fix and undo is recorded in the audit log (`readiness.fixes_undone` for an undo), and a fix that changes the campaign also invalidates an open [approval](/campaigns/approvals/).

## When the checks run

A run is a background job, one per campaign at a time. While it runs, the banner shows how many checks are done and each group shows its own progress, such as "9 of 14 links" or "24,310 of 31,988 contacts"; sending stays locked until it finishes. The checks run:

- **When you open Review & send**, if the campaign never had a run, changed since the last one, or is set to send now and the last run is more than 15 minutes old. This needs `campaigns:edit`. Editing the campaign doesn't rerun the checks by itself; it marks the last run as out of date. Changing only the schedule keeps the run valid.
- **When you select** <UiPath path="review.rerun" />, and after a one-click fix or an undo.
- **When you request approval**, if the last run doesn't describe the campaign as it is; the request waits for the run.
- **15 minutes before a scheduled send**, and again when the send starts. A send that starts now relies on the run you confirmed, which must be recent; an older one runs again first.

### Held sends

When you schedule a send, ReputeCast keeps what you confirmed: the run, its warnings, the checks you continued without, the recipient count, and the content, audience, sender and route as they were. The send-time recheck compares the fresh run with that, and holds the send ([send-time hold](/reference/glossary/#send-time-hold)) when:

- a check blocks;
- a warning appears that nobody confirmed, or a check is Unknown that nobody chose to send without (warnings dismissed in the composer count as confirmed);
- the audience grew or shrank by more than 10%;
- the content, the audience definition, the sender or the route changed after scheduling.

A held send shows <UiPath path="review.heldRecheckTitle" /> on Review & send, with why, the count you reviewed against the count now, who was told and when it expires. ReputeCast notifies whoever scheduled it (or requested approval) and the approvers, records `campaign.send_held` in the audit log and sends the `campaign.paused` [webhook](/developers/webhooks/) with `paused_by: send_readiness`. Nothing is sent.

- Select <UiPath path="review.reviewAgain" /> to confirm the send on the fresh checks and count. An audience that changed by more than 10% asks you to type the new count.
- While it is held, the send is checked again every 15 minutes. When nothing holds it any more, for example after a blocklist listing clears, it starts, at once if its time has passed.
- A held send nobody reviews goes back to Draft (or to Approved when its approval still covers it) 3 hours after its send time, with its schedule and A/B test kept, and everyone notified gets a "Held send expired" notification.

An approved send that a block stops shows <UiPath path="review.heldTitle" /> instead, with <UiPath path="review.cancelSend" />: fixing the block in the campaign invalidates the approval and requests it again with the difference attached; fixing it outside the campaign, such as getting a domain delisted, keeps the approval.

## When checks can't run

Some checks depend on answers from outside: DNS, the sites your links point to, and [HostRepute](/reference/glossary/#hostrepute). A check whose answer didn't come is Unknown, and the banner says <UiPath path="review.incompleteHostRepute" /> when sender blocklists are among them, otherwise <UiPath path="review.incompleteTitle" />.

- Send Readiness doesn't call HostRepute during a run. It reads the blocklist results that the hourly HostRepute sync and the built-in DNS blocklist checks stored, and says when HostRepute's hourly check ran. A HostRepute request gives up after 30 seconds and 3 attempts. When HostRepute data can't be refreshed and no newer built-in result exists, the domain and IP blocklist checks are Unknown, never Block.
- Domains Deliverability doesn't check, and link domains, are looked up live over DNS during the run. A lookup that times out, or that the lists refuse because the server uses a public DNS resolver, is Unknown. See [check and fix blocklist listings](/deliverability/blocklists/).
- Provider bounce and complaint rates come from each sending provider and are unaffected.

You then choose:

- Select <UiPath path="review.retryBlocklist" /> (or <UiPath path="review.retryChecks" /> when the unknown checks aren't blocklists) to run the checks again.
- Or continue without them: <UiPath path="review.continueProvider" /> when every unknown check is sender reputation data, otherwise <UiPath path="review.continueSkipped" />. This needs `campaigns:edit`. ReputeCast records the skipped checks in the campaign's audit trail and looks them up again at the send-time recheck, where a new Unknown, or a skipped check that now blocks, holds the send.

After you continue, the verdict names what was skipped, for example "Ready · blocklist checks skipped" or "Ready with warnings · 2 checks skipped", on the banner, the approval card and the confirmation. A Blocked send stays Blocked.

## Estimates

Next to the checks, Review & send shows what the send will do, worked out with the sending engine's own rules:

- **<UiPath path="review.audience" />:** the lists or segments, minus the shared suppression list, role addresses, duplicates (plus tags and Gmail dots are ignored), contacts stopped by the sunset policy and the unengaged contacts left out, down to <UiPath path="review.finalRecipients" />. Select <UiPath path="review.includeThem" /> on the unengaged line to send to them this time; the Audience group then warns. With language versions it also shows recipients by version.
- **<UiPath path="review.routeBreakdown" />:** each sending provider's share, its cost at the provider's price per 1,000 and how long it takes. Routes run in parallel. The estimate includes each provider's rate limit (or its default rate when it publishes none), [warm-up](/providers/warm-up/) caps that spread a share over several days, a 24-hour quota that makes messages wait, the A/B test's wait for a winner, best-time sending over 24 hours and the throttles below, and a sentence under the table says which applied. A provider that is down shows its share as held, with what moving it would cost.
- **<UiPath path="review.throttling" />:** the sending rate for Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud and other mailbox providers, from the [workspace sending defaults](/providers/workspace-defaults/).

## When to change them

The check thresholds are fixed. What you can change is how strict the outcome is:

- Add [workspace sending rules](/campaigns/sending-rules/) for your own requirements, such as UTM tags or a required line in the footer, at Warn or Block.
- In <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.sendingRules" />, change when a sender types the recipient count, or stop leaving unengaged contacts out by default.
- Switch the workspace [accessibility policy](/campaigns/accessibility/) to Block so serious accessibility issues stop a send.
- Set a stream's failover policy to "pause and ask" if a down provider should block a send instead of moving its share.
- Adjust the sunset policy in [list health](/audience/list-health/): it sets which contacts count as unengaged.
- Turn on the approval rule for Ready with warnings if every warning should get a second pair of eyes.
