A ReputeCast campaign report tells you what readers did, not what their mail apps did. The email open rate counts
[human opens](/reference/glossary/#human-opens) of the readers whose opens can be seen, so Apple Mail Privacy
Protection prefetches sit in their own bucket instead of inflating it, and clicks count only once they
[settle](/reference/glossary/#settled-click) as a person's. Every tile is compared with your last issues, and every
result is split by mailbox provider and by sending provider, so you can see where a problem started.

## Before you begin

- Reading a report needs `reports:view`.
- Exporting it and creating read-only links needs `reports:share` (Owner, Admin and Editor have it; Viewer doesn't).
- Adding bounced addresses to the suppression list from the report needs `subscribers:edit`.
- Drafting a retry for addresses blocked for your reputation needs `campaigns:edit`.
- Summaries and questions answered with AI need `ai:use` and a model connected for the
  [AI assistant](/integrations/ai-assistant/).

## Open a report

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.campaigns" /> and select <UiPath path="campaigns.report" /> on a sent campaign. Review & send
   also links to the report once a campaign has finished.

2. Check the status line under the title. "results coming in live" means the campaign is still sending. "results still
   coming in · final" with a date means the send finished less than 72 hours ago: opens, clicks, complaints and late
   bounces are still arriving, and nothing is compared with your last issues yet. "results final since" with a date
   means the numbers have settled.

3. While the results are still coming in, select <UiPath path="reports.emailWhenFinal" /> to be told when they are
   final, in the app and by email as your notification preferences say. Only a signed-in person can ask, not an API key
   or an agent.

4. Read the eight tiles at the top: Sent, Delivered, <UiPath path="reports.humanOpens" />,
   <UiPath path="reports.privacyOpens" />, <UiPath path="reports.humanClicks" />, Unsubscribes, Complaints and Bounces.
   Once the results are final, each tile shows its change against your last issues and a trend line.

</Steps>

## How opens are counted

ReputeCast sorts every open into one of three buckets:

- **Human opens**: a person opened the email. A person's settled click counts as a human open too.
- **Privacy-protected opens**: Apple Mail Privacy Protection (Apple's network or its bare user agent), or another
  privacy proxy on the instance's [bot filter](/reference/glossary/#bot-filter), fetched the email when it arrived.
  Whether anyone read it can't be seen, so these are neither people's opens nor machines'.
- **Machine opens**: security scanners (by user agent, or from a scanner network as the email arrives), Gmail's and
  Yahoo's image proxies in the first seconds after arrival, requests with no user agent or a `HEAD` request, and any
  open in the first seconds after arrival. The note under the tiles counts them as scanner opens; they count nowhere
  else.

The <UiPath path="reports.humanOpens" /> tile shows two rates. "of delivered" divides human opens by every delivered
message, including readers whose opens can't be seen, so it is a floor. "of measurable" is the
[measurable open rate](/reference/glossary/#measurable-open-rate): human opens divided by delivered minus
privacy-protected. Expand <UiPath path="reports.measurableTitle" /> under the tiles for the sum with this campaign's
numbers. The arrows on opens compare the measurable rate, so a change in how many of your readers use Apple Mail
doesn't move them.

Windows are measured from delivery when the sending provider reports it, else from the moment the provider took the
message. When a workspace keeps a sending provider's own tracking, its opens and clicks are sorted the same way from
the user agent and address the provider passes on.

## How clicks are counted

A click always redirects the reader at once, but it counts only after it settles:

1. A click that looks like a person's waits a minute.
2. ReputeCast then looks at the clicks around it. If the same email clicked several of its links within seconds, or
   followed the email's hidden link that no person can see, the click was a scanner's and is filed as a bot click.
3. Otherwise it settles as a person's and counts once: as a human click, a human open, a unique click on its link,
   and in automations, segments, scores and engagement tiers.

Clicks by a scanner user agent, from a scanner network as the email arrives, or in the first seconds after arrival are
bot clicks at once. The <UiPath path="reports.humanClicks" /> tile counts people, each once, and shows how many bot
clicks were filtered; select that line to see where they landed on the click map. An order is still credited to a
click that is settling, because buying is a person's act.

The same rules apply in every workspace of the instance. Instance admins add networks and user agents in
[Instance admin › Bot filter](/self-hosting/instance-admin/#edit-the-bot-filter).

## Complaints, counters and comparisons

- **Complaints only where reported.** Gmail and iCloud don't report complaints to senders, so their complaint rates
  read "n/a" with the reason, and the Complaints tile's "where reported" rate leaves their mail out. Gmail's spam rate
  is in Google Postmaster Tools, on Deliverability. Mailbox providers are told apart by each domain's MX record: Gmail
  includes Google Workspace and Outlook includes Microsoft 365.
- **Counters from message states.** The numbers add up the state of each message (delivered, bounced, opened,
  clicked), so a late or repeated event never counts twice. Once the results are final, and whenever the counting
  rules change, each campaign's counters are rebuilt from its stored messages and events. Campaigns imported from
  another platform keep their imported totals.
- **Comparisons with your last issues.** Each tile compares with the median of the last six issues sent to the same
  lists and segments, so one unusual issue doesn't move the baseline: in percent for Sent, in points for the rates.
  The trend line draws the tile over those issues and this one. A report shows "No earlier issue to compare with yet"
  when there is none.

## What each section shows

- **Summary by AI and Ask about this report.** See [summarize and ask with AI](#summarize-and-ask-with-ai).
- **Insights.** What stands out, worked out from the report's own numbers: messages a mailbox provider blocked for the
  sending IP's reputation (<UiPath path="reports.checkDelisting" />), a mailbox provider that bounced far more than the
  others (<UiPath path="reports.insight.openSnds" /> for Outlook, <UiPath path="reports.insight.openPostmaster" /> for
  Gmail), or a link that drew much of the human clicks (<UiPath path="reports.insight.showLink" />). Insights describe
  numbers; they never guess a cause.
- **Growth & revenue from this issue.** New subscribers from forwards, referrals and the web post, and
  <UiPath path="reports.webViews" /> of the issue's archive page and website post, counted on the server without
  cookies. With Earn, upgrades to paid and product conversions credited to this email.
- **Engagement over the first 72 hours.** Human opens, privacy-protected opens and human clicks as a share of
  delivered, as a chart or a table. With an A/B test, a mark shows when the winner went to the rest.
- **Click map.** Each link's share of human clicks, over the email that was sent. Switch to
  <UiPath path="reports.colBotClicks" /> to see where scanners clicked. With an A/B test, choose
  <UiPath path="reports.variantToShow" />: <UiPath path="reports.allRecipients" /> or one variant. A content test has
  no map of everyone: each variant's map shows its own email. With language versions you can switch the map's
  language.
- **Results per mailbox provider and per sending provider.** Delivery, human opens of measurable recipients,
  privacy-protected opens, human clicks, bounces and complaints for each, plus unsubscribes per mailbox provider and
  the median time to delivery, cost and warm-up day per sending provider. A problem with one mailbox provider
  (Outlook complaints) looks different from a problem with one sending provider (a Postal outage).
- **Results per language.** Shown when two or more language versions were sent. See
  [send each reader their language](/campaigns/languages/).
- **Bounces by type.** Hard, soft, blocked by policy or reputation, and other, each with the sending providers'
  original codes such as `550 5.1.1`. Hard bounces are suppressed automatically and soft bounces are retried at the
  next send. Addresses blocked for your sending IP or domain are valid, so they are never suppressed for it: see
  [retry blocked addresses after fixing](#retry-blocked-addresses-after-fixing). Other bounces open a review of what
  the providers said, from which you can add them to the [suppression list](/audience/suppressions/). Look up a code
  in [SMTP error codes](/smtp-errors/).
- **Unsubscribe reasons.** What people chose on the unsubscribe page, and how many chose fewer emails instead.
- **A/B test.** Each variant's sample, its result on the metric that decided, and how the winner was picked. See
  [A/B test subject, content and send time](/campaigns/ab-testing/#read-the-result).
- Sections that appear when they apply: polls, revenue (needs `earn:view`), sponsor, store orders, web post and
  product sales.

## Retry blocked addresses after fixing

When a mailbox provider blocks mail for your sending IP's or domain's reputation, the addresses are fine. Fix the
cause first, then send them the issue again:

<Steps>

1. In **Bounces by type**, select <UiPath path="reports.investigate" /> on the Blocked · policy or reputation row. The
   dialog shows what each sending provider said.

2. Select <UiPath path="reports.checkDelisting" /> to open Deliverability, and get the IP or domain delisted or the
   block lifted.

3. Back in the dialog, under <UiPath path="reports.retryTitle" />, select the retry button, which names how many
   addresses the retry goes to. ReputeCast drafts a new campaign named after the issue with "· retry", addressed only
   to the blocked addresses still on your list, and opens it in Review & send.

4. Send it from Review & send once Send Readiness agrees. Each address gets the issue once.

</Steps>

Addresses that unsubscribed or were suppressed since are left out. An issue has one retry at a time, a retry is never
retried again, and the button appears only once the send has ended. When a retry exists, the dialog links to it:
<UiPath path="reports.retryOpen" /> while it is a draft, or its report once it went out.

## Export a report

- Select <UiPath path="reports.exportCsv" /> to download the whole report as one CSV file: headline numbers with human
  opens of measurable recipients, privacy-protected opens, the scanner opens and bot clicks left out, complaints where
  reported, the mailbox provider, sending provider and language tables, hourly engagement, links (per variant for a
  content test), bounces, unsubscribe reasons and the A/B result.
- Select <UiPath path="reports.exportPdf" /> to open your browser's print dialog with a print layout; save it as PDF
  from there.

Both need `reports:share` and are recorded in the audit log.

## Share a read-only link

<Steps>

1. Scroll to <UiPath path="reports.shareTitle" /> at the bottom of the report. <UiPath path="reports.shareLink" /> at
   the top takes you there and copies the newest active link.

2. Under <UiPath path="reports.newLink" />, choose what else the link shows. The numbers, charts and tables are always
   in; tick <UiPath path="reports.includeCost" />, <UiPath path="reports.includeWarmup" /> or
   <UiPath path="reports.includeEmail" /> (the message as sent, in the click map).

3. Under <UiPath path="reports.expires" />, choose 7, 30 or 90 days.

4. Select <UiPath path="reports.createLink" />. The link (`https://<your public URL>/r/<token>`) is copied and appears
   under the active links with who created it, when it expires and what it includes. Anyone with it sees the report
   without signing in.

5. To stop a link working, select <UiPath path="reports.revoke" /> next to it. It stops at once.

</Steps>

Every link expires. Links created before ReputeCast 1.2.0 never expired; the upgrade gave each of them 30 days from
the moment it ran. Through the API and MCP (`share_campaign_report`) a link can last any number of days from 1 to 90.

The shared page shows aggregate numbers only: it never shows who received, opened or clicked, the AI summary or the
Ask box. It leaves out polls, revenue, store orders and the web section, and it is marked `noindex` and never cached.

<Aside type="caution">
A shared report still shows the campaign's name, the A/B variants' subject lines and your sending provider names, and
the email with its links when you include it. Share it only with people who may see those.
</Aside>

## Summarize and ask with AI

If the workspace has connected a model for the [AI assistant](/integrations/ai-assistant/), members with `ai:use` see
<UiPath path="ai.summarize" />. It writes a short summary of the report from its aggregate numbers, never an address.

- Every number in the summary is checked against the report. "Every number matches the report" means you can keep it;
  otherwise the numbers that aren't in the report are listed, and an edited summary with such numbers can't be kept.
- A summary you keep is visible to everyone who can read the report, marked
  <UiPath path="ai.suggest.numbersMatchReport" /> or with the numbers that changed as late results came in. It never
  appears on read-only links.
- Type a question in <UiPath path="ai.suggest.askTitle" />, such as "Why did Outlook bounce more?". The answer comes
  from the report's numbers only, every number in it is checked against the report, and it says so when the numbers
  can't answer.

[MCP agents](/developers/mcp/) read the same report through `get_campaign_report`, with the same permissions and the
same three open buckets.

## Check that it worked

- The status line reads "results final since" with a date once the numbers have settled, and the tiles show their
  change against your last issues.
- A read-only link opens in a private browser window without signing in, shows "Read-only report", and stops working
  on its expiry date.

## Troubleshooting

- **Export CSV and Share read-only link are disabled.** They need `reports:share`; the button says who can help.
- **"There's nothing to share yet."** Read-only links exist only for campaigns that have started sending.
- **"This report already has 10 active links."** Revoke one you no longer need, then create a new one.
- **"This link doesn’t open a report."** The link was revoked or it expired. Create a new one.
- **"Event ingestion is N minutes behind."** A sending provider's delivery events are arriving late, so recent numbers
  are low. See [delivery events and suppression sync](/providers/events-and-sync/).
- **Complaints read "n/a" for Gmail and iCloud.** They don't report complaints to senders. Check Gmail's spam rate in
  Google Postmaster Tools on Deliverability.
- **The tiles show no arrows.** The results are still coming in, or no earlier issue went to the same lists and
  segments.
- **The retry button is missing or disabled.** The send hasn't ended, the issue is itself a retry, a retry exists
  already, every blocked address was delivered or retried, or you don't have `campaigns:edit`. The dialog says which.
- **The open rate looks low next to another tool's.** That tool probably counts Apple Mail prefetches as opens.
  Compare the measurable open rate instead.
- **Human clicks are lower than the clicks your link shortener counts.** Bot clicks are left out. Check the bot
  clicks line under the tile.
