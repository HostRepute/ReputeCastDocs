An email poll asks one question inside a campaign and records each reader's answer on a page that opens from the email, so link scanners and prefetchers can't vote. A survey asks up to 20 questions on its own page. Both can save answers on the contact and start an [automation](/automations/overview/).

## Before you begin

- Adding a [poll](/reference/glossary/#poll) to a campaign needs `campaigns:edit`. Creating and publishing a survey needs `forms:edit`.
- Reading results needs `reports:view`; exporting answers with each contact's name and email needs `subscribers:export`.
- To save answers on contacts, create the [custom field](/audience/contacts/) first: a text field for choice answers, a number field for a 0–10 or emoji scale.
- Polls work in regular campaigns and recurring digest issues, not in transactional templates or automation emails.

## Add a poll to a campaign

<Steps>

1. In the [composer](/campaigns/composer/), open <UiPath path="composer.canvas.tabBlocks" /> and drag <UiPath path="composer.blocks.poll" /> from <UiPath path="composer.canvas.groupReputecast" /> onto the canvas, or type `/` on an empty line in Write mode and choose <UiPath path="composer.blocks.poll" />. Select the block to open its settings in <UiPath path="composer.tabSettings" />.

2. Pick the kind of poll and write the question:

   - A <UiPath path="polls.kindChoice" /> poll has two to five answers. An answer can start with an emoji. Lay them out as stacked buttons or in one row.
   - A <UiPath path="polls.kindScale" /> poll takes answers from 0 to 10, with your own words for both ends. Turn on **Use the NPS wording** for a Net Promoter Score question.
   - An <UiPath path="polls.kindEmoji" /> poll shows three or five faces. Each face is sent with its words, such as "1 of 5, very unhappy", for screen readers and the plain-text version.

3. Under <UiPath path="polls.save" />, keep the answer with the poll's results only, give each choice a tag, or write it to a contact field. A changed answer swaps the tag or overwrites the field. Test answers never touch a contact.

4. Under <UiPath path="polls.closes" />, choose after a number of days (7 days after sending by default), on a date (at the end of that day in the workspace's time zone), or by hand.

5. Under <UiPath path="polls.after" />, show a thank-you message or <UiPath path="polls.afterSurvey" />: the answer fills the survey's first question, so pick a survey whose first question has the same kind and answers.

6. Decide what happens to people who aren't contacts (readers of the web archive or a forwarded copy without its token): count their answers separately, or don't accept them. Turn on <UiPath path="polls.showResults" /> to show the shares on the answer page after someone answers.

7. Select <UiPath path="polls.previewPage" />, then send yourself a test and answer it. Test answers are marked Test and never counted; <UiPath path="polls.clearTests" /> removes them.

</Steps>

Each answer in the email is a plain link drawn as a button, 44 px tall on phones, so the poll works in every mail app. Poll links aren't counted as clicks.

## How answers are recorded

1. A reader selects an answer in the email. The answer page opens and says which answer they picked; opening it saves nothing.
2. When the page has been visible for a moment, it saves the answer. In a browser without JavaScript, the reader presses **Confirm my answer**.
3. The reader can add a comment of up to 500 characters, or pick another answer. The latest answer counts until the poll closes, and the earlier ones stay in the contact's history.

Link scanners and mail gateways open links without running the page or pressing the button, so they never become answers. ReputeCast counts those visits apart as automated visits. A forwarded email answers as its original recipient, and the answer page says so.

When the poll is closed, its page says when it closed and records nothing.

## Read the results

Open the campaign's [report](/campaigns/reports/). The Poll answers and Automated visits ignored tiles sit near the top, and each poll gets its own section.

<Screenshot
  id="poll-results"
  alt="A 0–10 poll's results: 2,412 answers from 7.7% of delivered, 1,377 automated visits ignored, a Net Promoter Score of 26 with bars per score, a breakdown by engagement tier and the answers over the first 72 hours."
/>

The section shows:

- the poll's status (open, or when and how it closed), the number of answers and their share of delivered messages,
- the count of each answer, or for a 0–10 poll the NPS (promoters, 9–10, minus detractors, 0–6) and a bar per score,
- a breakdown by engagement tier, list or language; rows with fewer than 30 answers read "Too few to compare",
- answers over the first 72 hours, refreshed every minute while that window is open,
- comments, with names shown to members who have `subscribers:view`,
- how many people changed their answer.

<UiPath path="pollReport.closeNow" /> closes the poll at once (you can reopen it; both need `campaigns:edit`). <UiPath path="pollReport.exportResults" /> downloads the totals as CSV; <UiPath path="pollReport.exportAnswers" /> includes each contact and needs `subscribers:export`. Exports are recorded in the [audit log](/team/audit-log/).

## Build a survey

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.forms > forms.tabs.signupForms" />, select <UiPath path="surveys.newForm" />, choose <UiPath path="surveys.kindSurvey" /> and select <UiPath path="surveys.createSurvey" />. A draft called "Untitled survey" opens.

2. On <UiPath path="surveys.tabQuestions" />, select <UiPath path="surveys.addQuestion" /> and pick a type: Single choice, Multiple choice, 0–10 scale, Emoji scale, Short text or Long text. Mark a question as required, and choose whether to save its answer to a contact field or as a tag per answer.

3. In the survey's settings, set how long it stays open, the time zone, its address and introduction, and the thank-you text. One response per contact is on by default: people can change their answers until the survey closes, and the newest count. People who aren't contacts are refused unless you turn on **Accept people who aren't contacts**.

4. Select <UiPath path="surveys.publish" />. Publishing waits until every question is complete.

5. Link to the survey from an email: in the composer's link editor, choose **Survey:** and the survey's name. The link carries each contact's token, so answers are linked to them. A poll can also continue into the survey.

</Steps>

<Screenshot
  id="survey-builder"

The survey page is at `/p/<workspace>/s/<address>`, or `/s/<address>` on your [custom domain](/forms/branding-and-domain/). Opening it records nothing; answers are saved when the person presses **Send**. A draft survey's page doesn't open, and a closed survey takes no answers. Select <UiPath path="surveys.closeSurvey" /> to close it early.

The results tab shows how many people started and completed the survey, a chart per choice question, the NPS for scales with the NPS wording, the text answers, and a table of responses. It exports the totals or the answers with contacts as CSV.

## Start an automation from an answer

Use the trigger <UiPath path="automations.graph.kindPollAnswered" /> to start a flow when a contact answers a poll (any answer, or only some) or submits a survey. Test answers and answers from people who aren't contacts never start it. See [triggers and steps](/automations/triggers-and-steps/).

## What Send Readiness checks

The Content group of [Send Readiness](/campaigns/send-readiness/) blocks a campaign whose poll is incomplete (no question, fewer than two answers, a missing tag or field). It warns when the poll closes before the campaign sends, when the survey it continues to isn't published or closes before the send, and when your publisher domain isn't verified, so answer links use the instance's address.

## Check that it worked

- Your test answer shows in the block's test answers table, marked Test.
- After the send, the report's Poll section counts answers and automated visits separately.
- A contact who answered has the tag or field you chose on their profile.

## Troubleshooting

- **The answer page says the poll is closed.** It closed by date, by its number of days or by hand. Reopen it from the report, or set a later closing date before sending.
- **Answers from the web archive aren't counted.** Archive and view-in-browser copies carry no contact token. Count non-contacts' answers separately, or accept that they're refused.
- **A survey can't be picked under Continue to a survey.** Its first question must be the same kind with the same answers as the poll.
- **Publishing a survey does nothing.** A question is incomplete: a choice question needs two answers, and a saved answer needs its field or tags.
- **Many automated visits.** That is mail security scanning links; they're never counted as answers.
