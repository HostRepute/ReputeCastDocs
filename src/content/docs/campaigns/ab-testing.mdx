An [A/B test](/reference/glossary/#a-b-test) sends two or three versions of a campaign to a sample of its audience and
sends the winner to everyone else, but only when the result is real: every variant needs a minimum sample and the
leader needs a significant lead, otherwise the rest get variant A. Use it for A/B testing email subject lines, the
email's content or the time it goes out.

## Before you begin

- Setting up a test needs `campaigns:edit`; reading the result needs `reports:view`.
- A/B tests are part of regular email campaigns. Recurring digests and SMS, push and WhatsApp campaigns don't have them.
- Set the campaign's audience first: the sample is a share of it. Check that each variant's share of the sample
  reaches the minimum sample in the table below; with a few hundred recipients, a test can only end with "No clear
  winner".

## How a test runs

1. When the send starts, ReputeCast takes the sample share of the audience and splits it about evenly across the
   variants. With a 20% sample and two variants, about 10% get A and 10% get B. The sample is picked by contact, not at
   random per send: with the same sample size, the same contacts are in the sample of every test.
2. Everyone else waits. The [live monitor](/campaigns/live-monitor/) counts them as waiting.
3. The test length starts when the last sample recipient is due (for a send-time test, the latest variant time). When
   it ends, ReputeCast decides on the test's metric:
   - **Clicks**: [settled human clicks](/reference/glossary/#settled-click) per delivered message.
   - **Human opens**: [human opens](/reference/glossary/#human-opens) per measurable recipient, that is delivered minus
     privacy-protected, so Apple Mail prefetches can't decide it.
4. A variant wins only when every variant reached the minimum sample and the leader's lead over the runner-up is
   significant in a two-sided two-proportion z-test at the test's confidence. The winner then goes to everyone who
   waited. Otherwise there is no clear winner and they get variant A, your default.

The sending engine and the report apply the same rule, so the report can't call a winner the engine didn't pick. The
decision is kept with the send, with the numbers it was made on, and the recipients who got the winner afterwards are
counted apart from the sample: the report compares the samples alone.

## Set up a test

<Steps>

1. Open the campaign in the [composer](/campaigns/composer/) and select <UiPath path="composer.preview" />. In the side
   panel, find <UiPath path="composer.preview.abTitle" /> and select <UiPath path="composer.preview.setUp" />.

   On the canvas, the **+** button next to <UiPath path="composer.canvas.variantGroup" /> A starts a subject test in one
   step (<UiPath path="composer.canvas.startAb" />).

2. Pick what the variants differ in:

   - For a <UiPath path="composer.preview.kindSubject" /> test, write a second (and third) subject. Everything else
     stays the same.
   - For a <UiPath path="composer.preview.kindContent" /> test, the body is copied as variant B. Switch the canvas to B
     and edit it. Route and audience stay shared.
   - For a <UiPath path="composer.preview.kindSendTime" /> test, the variants are the same email at different times,
     09:00 and 14:00 by default.

3. Select <UiPath path="composer.preview.addVariant" /> for a third variant, C. A test has two or three variants.

4. Set <UiPath path="composer.preview.sampleSize" />: 10%, 20% (the default), 30%, 40% or 50% of the audience. Each
   option shows the number of recipients.

5. Set <UiPath path="composer.preview.testLength" />: 1, 2 (the default), 4, 8 or 24 hours.

6. Set <UiPath path="composer.preview.winnerBy" />: <UiPath path="composer.preview.winnerClicksRec" />,
   <UiPath path="composer.preview.winnerOpensPlain" /> or <UiPath path="composer.preview.winnerByHand" />. Apple Mail
   opens every email on download, so opens overstate interest; clicks don't. Under it, the panel states the rule, for
   example "ReputeCast picks a winner only when it is significant at 95% with at least 500 recipients per variant;
   otherwise everyone else gets A", and when the rest go out.

7. Check each variant on its own. Under variant B (and C), the panel says whether it passes its checks or lists its
   blocking issues and warnings. The preview's <UiPath path="composer.preview.previewing" /> switch shows each subject
   or variant, in the inbox preview and the plain-text version.

8. Select <UiPath path="composer.reviewSend" />. Send Readiness checks every variant on its own (its subject, spam
   heuristics, personalization, size and image share, and the unsubscribe link and postal address in its body), with a
   fix that opens the variant in the editor. It shows the test, its sample share and recipients per variant, when the
   sample goes out, when the winner is picked and when everyone is done; the confirmation step lists it before you
   send.

</Steps>

To remove the test, select <UiPath path="composer.preview.stopTesting" />. Changing or removing a test is recorded in
the version history. If the campaign was already approved, changing the test sends it back for
[approval](/campaigns/approvals/).

The minimum sample and the confidence can be changed only through the API, as `minSample` and `confidence` in the
campaign's `abTest`.

### Send-time tests

Each variant goes out at its time of day in each recipient's own time zone, or the workspace's when the contact has
none. A time that has already passed when the send starts moves to the next day, and the test length starts after the
latest of them. The <UiPath path="composer.preview.bestTime" /> option in this panel has no effect on sending in this
build; to send each contact at their best hour, choose that in
[Schedule and send a campaign](/campaigns/schedule-and-send/) instead of running a send-time test.

### Pick by hand

<UiPath path="composer.preview.winnerByHand" /> holds the rest of the audience until someone picks the winner;
Send Readiness says "the rest waits for you to pick the winner". The app has no button to pick it: use the
[pick A/B winner API operation](/api/operations/pick-live-ab-winner/), which needs `campaigns:send` and can also end
any test early. The report then shows "Winner B · picked by a person". Prefer clicks unless you have a script ready to
choose.

## Read the result

Open the campaign's [report](/campaigns/reports/). The A/B card is the last card on the page.

<Screenshot

  alt="The A/B test card of October Product Update: Winner B at 98% confidence, decided on clicks, with 6.7% human clicks against 5.6% for A and human opens of measurable recipients under each bar, and how the winner was picked: a two-proportion z-test 2 hours after the sample went out, at least 500 recipients per variant, the remaining 38,384 got B at 12:00."
/>

For each variant the card shows its subject (or content or send time), recipients and sample share, its rate on the
metric that decided (at the decision while results come in, after 72 hours once final) and the other metric with
complaints. The badge says how the test ended:

| Badge                                         | Meaning                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Testing · decides at 12:00                    | The test length is still running.                                                                                               |
| Winner B · 98% confidence · decided on clicks | B won: every variant reached the minimum sample and B's lead was significant. The confidence shows at most 99%.                 |
| No clear winner                               | A variant fell short of the minimum sample, or the lead could be chance. The rest got A, your default, and the card says why.   |
| Winner B · picked by a person                 | Someone picked B by hand.                                                                                                       |
| B chosen · confidence not recorded            | The test was decided before ReputeCast kept a record of each decision, so how sure it was isn't known. It claims no confidence. |

**How the winner was picked** gives the test and the numbers it was decided on, for example "Two-proportion z-test on
human clicks 2 h after the sample went out (B 3.4%, A 2.6%); at least 500 recipients per variant; the remaining 38,384
got B at 12:00." Without a clear winner the card leads with how far the leader was ahead and at what confidence. With
three variants, the test compares the leader with the runner-up.

The [click map](/reference/glossary/#click-map) can show each variant's recipients on their own. For a content test it
always does: each variant's map is drawn over its own email, and the top-link insight names its variant.

The same result appears in the live monitor while the test runs ("variant B leading"), under the campaign's name in
the [campaigns list](/campaigns/overview/), in the report's CSV export and to MCP agents.

## Check that it worked

- The canvas shows a <UiPath path="composer.canvas.variantGroup" /> switch with A and B (and C).
- Send Readiness shows the test with its sample and the time the winner is picked.
- After the test length, the report's A/B card names the winner, or says there is no clear winner, and how many of
  the rest got which variant.

## Troubleshooting

- **No A/B option.** The campaign is a recurring digest, or your role lacks `campaigns:edit`.
- **"No clear winner" and everyone got A.** A variant had fewer recipients with a measurable result than the minimum sample, or the
  difference could be chance. Use a larger sample or a longer test, test on clicks, or test a bigger change.
- **"Write a subject for variant B".** A subject test needs a subject for every variant.
- **Send Readiness blocks on variant B.** Variant B has its own problem, such as a broken personalization tag or no
  unsubscribe link. Use the check's fix to open variant B in the editor and fix it there.
- **"B chosen · confidence not recorded".** The test was decided before ReputeCast 1.2.0; its confidence wasn't
  stored.
- **The rest of the audience never got the email.** The test uses <UiPath path="composer.preview.winnerByHand" />;
  pick the winner through the API.
