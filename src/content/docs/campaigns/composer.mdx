The [composer](/reference/glossary/#composer) is the drag and drop email builder MJML powers in ReputeCast: you build the email from blocks on a canvas, and every change is also MJML code you can read and edit, compiled to HTML that renders in Outlook, Gmail and Apple Mail.

## Before you begin

- Editing needs `campaigns:edit`. Without it the composer opens read-only: you can look, preview and send yourself a test, but not change anything.
- A campaign can be edited while it is a draft, pending approval, approved or scheduled. Once it is sending or sent, its content is locked; [duplicate it](/campaigns/overview/#reuse-a-sent-campaign) to reuse the content.
- Open the composer from <UiPath path="nav.campaigns" />: select a draft's name or <UiPath path="campaigns.edit" /> in its row. A new campaign opens on the [template gallery](/campaigns/templates/).

The row above the canvas holds the subject, the preheader and an inbox preview that shows how they read in Gmail (on the desktop canvas) or iPhone Mail (on the mobile canvas). The header shows the campaign name, its status and version (for example "Draft · autosaved 09:41 · v12"), and the audience, sender and route pickers.

## Build the email

<Steps>

1. Write the <UiPath path="composer.subject" /> and the <UiPath path="composer.preheader" />. Content checks flag spam-like subjects as you type; see [fix content warnings](/campaigns/content-checks/).

2. Add blocks from <UiPath path="composer.canvas.tabBlocks" />: drag a tile onto the canvas, or select it to insert it. Section blocks go after the selected section; content blocks (Text, Image, Button and the like) go into the selected column, so select a column first. Use **Search blocks** to filter the tiles.

3. Select any element on the canvas and change it in the inspector. <UiPath path="composer.tabStyle" /> holds colors, typography, padding, borders and alignment; <UiPath path="composer.tabSettings" /> holds links, button text, image alt text, text roles (paragraph or heading level), the visibility rules and the name shown in Layers. Double-click text to edit it on the canvas.

4. Switch the canvas between <UiPath path="composer.canvas.desktop" /> and <UiPath path="composer.canvas.mobile" />. Columns stack below 480 px, as MJML renders them, and the inspector shows mobile checks (buttons at least 44 px tall, text at least 14 px).

5. Personalize text with tokens such as `{{ first_name | default: "there" }}`: select <UiPath path="composer.canvas.insertToken" /> in the text toolbar. The [Liquid personalization reference](/campaigns/personalization/) lists every variable and filter.

6. Select <UiPath path="composer.preview" /> to see the email in each email client, then send yourself a test.

7. Select <UiPath path="composer.reviewSend" /> to save and open [Send Readiness](/campaigns/send-readiness/).

</Steps>

A **Product grid** block appears when a store is connected ([Shopify or WooCommerce](/integrations/shopify-woocommerce/)), and **Feed items** appears in [recurring digest](/campaigns/recurring-digests/) templates. If the email has no footer, the ReputeCast group moves to the top and the footer tile is outlined in red.

Images come from the image library: PNG, JPEG or GIF files up to 5 MB, served from your ReputeCast address so they load in every inbox. A pasted image URL must start with `https://`.

### Inspector tabs

- The tabs <UiPath path="composer.tabStyle" /> and <UiPath path="composer.tabSettings" /> are always there. With nothing selected, they tell you to select a component; under them, a summary shows the selected section's images, links and warnings.
- The <UiPath path="composer.deliverability" /> tab appears, with a count, while content checks report a Block or a warning. Each row has its one-click fixes; see [fix content warnings before sending](/campaigns/content-checks/).
- The <UiPath path="accessibilityComposer.tab" /> tab appears once the canvas has content; see [check email accessibility](/campaigns/accessibility/).
- The <UiPath path="website.composer.tabWeb" /> tab appears when your [newsletter website](/forms/website/) is on, to publish the campaign as a post.

In Settings, **Visibility** has two separate controls: hiding an element on mobile or on desktop (Outlook for Windows ignores this), and [who sees a block](/campaigns/conditional-content/).

## Edit the MJML or HTML

The view switch above the canvas has three views:

- <UiPath path="composer.canvas.viewVisual" /> is the canvas.
- **MJML** shows the MJML source, the source of truth, next to the compiled HTML. Edit the code, then select <UiPath path="composer.code.applyToCanvas" />; leaving the view applies it too. <UiPath path="composer.code.format" /> tidies the code and <UiPath path="composer.code.importMjml" /> replaces it with a file.
- **HTML** shows only the compiled HTML, read-only: what the sending providers receive, before tracking links and the open pixel are added. Use <UiPath path="composer.code.copyHtml" /> to take it elsewhere.

<Screenshot
  id="composer-code"

The panel on the right has two tabs. <UiPath path="composer.code.tabCompile" /> reports the compile time and every error or warning with its line, including columns that add up to more than 100% and images wider than their column. <UiPath path="composer.code.tabTokens" /> lists the personalization tokens; select one to copy it.

Applying MJML that doesn't parse (an unclosed tag, or no `<mj-body>`) shows an error with its line. The canvas keeps the last good version until you fix the code or undo your changes, and you can't switch views until it compiles. Subject and preheader stay in sync with `<mj-title>` and `<mj-preview>` in the MJML head.

To bring in existing HTML, use <UiPath path="composer.importTile" /> in the template gallery. HTML goes into one Raw HTML section: it is sent as it is, but you can't edit it block by block.

## Preview the email

Select <UiPath path="composer.preview" /> in the header. The preview shows the email as Gmail on the web, Outlook for Windows, Apple Mail on iOS and Yahoo would show it, each in light and dark mode, at desktop or mobile width.

<Aside type="caution">
  The previews are approximations: ReputeCast renders the compiled HTML in your browser and imitates each client's
  dark mode. Outlook uses Word's rendering engine, so send a test to see the real result in the clients your
  readers use. For inbox placement, run a [placement test](/deliverability/placement-tests/).
</Aside>

Below the cards, **Dark-mode checks** flag logos without a transparent background and text whose contrast drops when a client inverts colors, and a card shows the plain-text version. The side panel holds the inbox preview with subject and preheader lengths, the <UiPath path="composer.preview.personalization" /> values for the contact you preview as, and the [A/B variants](/campaigns/ab-testing/).

<UiPath path="composer.preview.previewAs" /> fills the email with a real contact from the audience, a contact without a first name (to check your fallbacks) or a random Active contact. Picking real contacts needs `subscribers:view`; without it you see a sample contact.

## Send a test

<Steps>

1. Select <UiPath path="composer.sendTest" />. The composer saves first, so the test always shows the saved version.

2. In <UiPath path="composer.sendATest" />, tick workspace members (your own address is ticked) or type other addresses. With several content languages, pick the language of the test or one of each.

3. Select **Send**.

</Steps>

A test goes to at most 10 addresses through the [System stream's route](/providers/routing/), with "[Test]" before the subject and a note under the email saying it is a test. Tokens are filled with the default preview contact's data, not with each recipient's, and the unsubscribe and preferences links are test links. Without `campaigns:send` you can only send tests to yourself: the button reads **Send test to me**.

The arrow next to the button also offers a [placement test](/deliverability/placement-tests/), which needs `placement:run`.

## Autosave and version history

The composer saves on its own: every 30 seconds while there are changes, and 2 seconds after you add or remove a section or element. It doesn't save while you are typing in a text block. Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> on Windows and Linux) to save a named version at any time; there is no Save button.

Every save is a new version. The **Version history** button in the status bar under the canvas lists the latest 50 with who saved them and what changed. Tick two versions and select <UiPath path="composer.dialogs.compareTwo" /> to see a line-by-line diff of the MJML, subject and preheader, or select <UiPath path="composer.dialogs.restore" /> on an older one. Restoring saves it as a new version, so nothing is deleted.

### When someone else saved first

If a teammate saves while you are editing, a conflict card appears above the canvas naming who saved which version and which parts each of you changed. You choose:

- keep yours, saved as the next version on top of theirs,
- load their version, which replaces your unsaved edits, or
- compare the two first.

Nothing is lost either way: both versions stay in the history.

### When you are offline

If a save fails because the connection dropped, the composer keeps your edits in the browser tab and retries every 10 seconds. Keep the tab open until it saves, or select <UiPath path="composer.retryNow" />. <UiPath path="composer.downloadBackup" /> saves the MJML to a file you can import later.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>/</kbd> in the composer to list its shortcuts: save a version, undo and redo, copy, paste and delete the selected component, bold and italic while editing text, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> to stop editing. <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> saves and opens Send Readiness. The full list is in the [keyboard shortcuts reference](/reference/keyboard-shortcuts/).

## Check that it worked

- The header shows "autosaved" with the time and a version number that goes up after your changes.
- The test arrives with "[Test]" in the subject, sent through your System route.
- <UiPath path="composer.reviewSend" /> opens Send Readiness without a Block from the content checks.

## Troubleshooting

- **The canvas doesn't accept a Text or Image block.** Content blocks go inside a column. Select a column on the canvas, or drag the block into one.
- **Nothing can be edited.** The campaign is sending or sent, or your role lacks `campaigns:edit`. Duplicate a sent campaign to reuse it.
- **Review & send reads Request approval.** Your role lacks `campaigns:send`; see [require approval for risky sends](/campaigns/approvals/).
- **The test fails with "no sending provider can deliver them yet".** Tests go through the System stream. [Connect a sending provider](/providers/connect/) and give the System stream a route.
- **Undo and redo buttons are missing.** While content warnings are open, the toolbar shows the warning counts instead. <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> and <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> still work.
- **"Fix the MJML first".** The MJML view has an error. Go to the line it names, or undo your changes.
