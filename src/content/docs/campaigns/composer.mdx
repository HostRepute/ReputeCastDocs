The [composer](/reference/glossary/#composer) is the drag and drop email builder MJML powers in ReputeCast: you write the email as a document, arrange it from blocks on a canvas, or edit its MJML code, and all three edit the same email. ReputeCast compiles it to HTML that renders in Outlook, Gmail and Apple Mail.

## Before you begin

- Editing needs `campaigns:edit`. Without it the composer opens read-only: you can look, preview, comment and send yourself a test, but not change anything.
- A campaign can be edited while it is a draft or scheduled. A campaign waiting for approval or approved opens read-only until you choose to edit it, because editing cancels the approval; see [edit an approved campaign](#edit-an-approved-campaign). Once it is sending or sent, its content is locked; [duplicate it](/campaigns/overview/#reuse-a-sent-campaign) to reuse the content.
- Open the composer from <UiPath path="nav.campaigns" />: select a draft's name or <UiPath path="campaigns.edit" /> in its row. A new campaign opens on the [template gallery](/campaigns/templates/).

## Build the email in Design mode

<Steps>

1. Select <UiPath path="composer.chrome.modeDesign" />. Write the subject and preheader in the <UiPath path="composer.chrome.inbox" /> strip. Content checks flag spam-like subjects as you type, such as "Warn · 3 spam heuristics"; see [fix content warnings](/campaigns/content-checks/).

2. Add blocks from <UiPath path="composer.canvas.tabBlocks" />: drag a tile onto the canvas, or select it and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Section blocks go after the selected section; content blocks (Text, Image, Button and the like) go into the selected column, so select a column first. Use <UiPath path="composer.canvas.searchBlocks" /> to filter the tiles.

3. Select any element on the canvas and change it in the inspector. <UiPath path="composer.tabStyle" /> uses plain words (see below); <UiPath path="composer.tabSettings" /> holds links, button text, image alt text, text roles (paragraph or heading level), the visibility rules and the name shown in <UiPath path="composer.canvas.tabLayers" />. Double-click text, or press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, to edit it on the canvas.

4. Switch the width between <UiPath path="composer.chrome.desktopTitle" /> and <UiPath path="composer.chrome.mobileTitle" />. Columns stack below 480 px, as MJML renders them, and the inspector shows mobile checks (buttons at least 44 px tall, text at least 14 px).

5. Personalize text with <UiPath path="composer.canvas.insertToken" /> in the text toolbar. A token shows on the canvas as a chip, such as "First name · fallback “there”"; select it to change its fallback. See [personalize emails with Liquid](/campaigns/personalization/).

6. Check the <UiPath path="composer.chrome.tabChecks" /> tab, select <UiPath path="composer.preview" /> to see the email in each email client, and send yourself a test.

7. Select <UiPath path="composer.reviewSend" /> to save and open [Send Readiness](/campaigns/send-readiness/).

</Steps>

Undo and redo cover the subject, the preheader and the A/B test settings as well as the canvas.

### The Style panel

<UiPath path="composer.tabStyle" /> names choices the way you would describe them, and shows the numbers under each one:

- A section has <UiPath path="composer.chrome.spacing" /> (S, M, L, for example "16 px above and below, 24 px at the sides"), <UiPath path="composer.chrome.background" /> (White, Grey, Tint or an image), <UiPath path="composer.chrome.alignment" />, <UiPath path="composer.chrome.columns" /> (50/50, 60/40, 40/60; on phones the left column comes first) and <UiPath path="composer.chrome.textStyle" /> (Body, Small or Large, with the font and size).
- A block in a column has its own choices, such as space inside, <UiPath path="composer.chrome.corners" /> (Square, Rounded, Pill), Small, Medium or Large, Fit the text or Full width, and a divider's thickness and length.
- A value that no choice matches shows as "Custom:" and the value.
- <UiPath path="composer.chrome.advanced" /> holds the raw attributes, such as `padding`, `background-color` and `mj-class`.

With several [language versions](/campaigns/languages/), the panel says that the style is shared by all of them.

### Images

Choose an image from the image library, upload one, or paste an `https://` image address. Uploads are PNG or JPEG files up to 20 MB, or GIFs up to 5 MB. ReputeCast stores them the way email needs them:

- at most 1,200 px wide, keeping their proportions and never enlarged,
- photos turned upright and compressed, and a photo uploaded as PNG saved as JPEG,
- with metadata removed (EXIF with its location and camera details, XMP, IPTC, comments and embedded thumbnails); color profiles stay. GIFs keep their frames and lose only their comments.

A notice says what changed, such as the size it was resized from. Then <UiPath path="composer.dialogs.altTitle" /> asks for alt text: type it, choose <UiPath path="composer.dialogs.altDecorative" />, or <UiPath path="composer.dialogs.altSkip" />. Images are served from your ReputeCast address, so they load in every inbox, and the library is shared by every campaign in the workspace.

### Work with the keyboard

- Once you click the email or pick an element in <UiPath path="composer.canvas.tabLayers" />, <kbd>Enter</kbd> edits the selected text (or selects the first element inside a section or column), and <kbd>Esc</kbd> stops editing text (or selects the parent).
- <UiPath path="composer.canvas.tabLayers" /> is a tree: <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> move between layers, <kbd>→</kbd> opens one, <kbd>←</kbd> closes it and <kbd>Enter</kbd> selects it on the email.
- With an element's Move button focused, <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> move it up or down.

## Write mode

[Write mode](/reference/glossary/#write-mode) shows the email as a document: you type it like one, and every edit goes to the same blocks Design mode shows. Layout, columns and spacing stay as set in Design.

<Screenshot
  id="composer-write"

Comments and Share for review sit at the right."
/>

<Steps>

1. Select <UiPath path="composer.chrome.modeWrite" /> in the top bar.

2. Type. Markdown shortcuts work at the start of a line: `#` and a space for a heading, `##` for a subheading, `-` or `*` for a bulleted list, `1.` for a numbered list, `---` for a divider, and `**words**` for bold.

3. On an empty line, type `/` to open <UiPath path="composer.write.insertBlock" />: a heading, lists, an image, a button, a divider, an article card, a poll, a personalization token or a conditional block. Keep typing to filter, press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to insert, or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to keep what you typed as text.

4. Paste text from Google Docs, Word, Notion or a web page. Headings, links, lists, bold and italics stay; inline styles, classes and fonts are removed, so the email's own styles apply, and images are left out (add them with `/`). The notice says what was kept and removed, with <UiPath path="composer.write.undoPaste" />.

5. Use <UiPath path="composer.write.personalize" /> in the formatting bar to insert a token with a fallback, and <UiPath path="composer.write.comment" /> to comment on the block you are in.

</Steps>

Images take their alt text right under them, or <UiPath path="composer.write.decorative" />. Blocks whose words aren't typed as text show as cards that say where they change: synced blocks with <UiPath path="composer.brandkit.editInBrandKit" />, and polls, product cards, feed items and sponsor blocks with <UiPath path="composer.write.editInDesign" />. A column's blocks start with a line such as "2 columns · column 1 of 2 in Design". Press <kbd>Alt</kbd> with <kbd>↑</kbd> or <kbd>↓</kbd> to move a block.

The right of the formatting bar counts open comments and holds <UiPath path="composer.collab.share" />; see [comments and review links](/campaigns/comments-and-review-links/).

## Edit the MJML or HTML

Select <UiPath path="composer.chrome.modeCode" />. A switch above the code moves between <UiPath path="composer.chrome.modeWrite" />, <UiPath path="composer.canvas.viewVisual" />, **MJML** and **HTML**:

- **MJML** shows the MJML source, the source of truth, next to the compiled HTML. Edit the code, then select <UiPath path="composer.code.applyToCanvas" />; leaving the view applies it too. <UiPath path="composer.code.format" /> tidies the code and <UiPath path="composer.code.importMjml" /> replaces it with a file.
- **HTML** shows only the compiled HTML, read-only: what the sending providers receive, before tracking links and the open pixel are added. Use <UiPath path="composer.code.copyHtml" /> to take it elsewhere.

<Screenshot
  id="composer-code"
  alt="Code mode: the MJML source on the left, the compiled HTML on the right, and the Compile panel reporting a compile in 14 ms with 0 errors and 0 warnings, notes about rc: comments and tracking, and the personalization tokens available."
/>

The panel on the right has two tabs. <UiPath path="composer.code.tabCompile" /> reports the compile time and every error or warning with its line, including columns that add up to more than 100% and images wider than their column. <UiPath path="composer.code.tabTokens" /> lists the personalization tokens; select one to copy it.

Applying MJML that doesn't parse (an unclosed tag, or no `<mj-body>`) shows an error with its line. The canvas keeps the last good version until you fix the code or undo your changes, and you can't switch modes until it compiles. Subject and preheader stay in sync with `<mj-title>` and `<mj-preview>` in the MJML head.

To bring in existing HTML, use <UiPath path="composer.importTile" /> in the template gallery. HTML goes into one Raw HTML section: it is sent as it is, but you can't edit it block by block.

## Check the email as you edit

The <UiPath path="composer.chrome.tabChecks" /> tab lists what the content checks found, in one place: a summary row each for <UiPath path="composer.chrome.groupDeliverability" />, <UiPath path="composer.chrome.groupAccessibility" /> and sender reputation (from the latest Review & send), then each issue with its fixes, then what passed. The tab's count and the <UiPath path="composer.reviewSend" /> button show the blocking issues and warnings that are left, A/B variants included. Checks rerun as you edit; [Send Readiness](/campaigns/send-readiness/) runs the full set before anything sends.

<Screenshot
  id="composer-warnings"
  alt="The Checks tab of Flash sale: 48 hours only with 2 blocking issues and 3 warnings: a button linking to a domain listed on SURBL and Spamhaus DBL, no unsubscribe link or postal address with Insert footer block, and a 91 KB email near Gmail's 102 KB clipping limit with a fix and Dismiss."
/>

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="composer.chrome.tabChecks" />. Select an issue's fix, such as <UiPath path="composer.canvas.insertFooter" />, or select the element on the canvas and change it.

2. To accept a warning, select <UiPath path="composer.chrome.dismiss" /> and choose why: the image is decorative, you'll stay under 102 KB, you've sent this subject style to this list before, the check is wrong here, or other. Add a note for reviewers if you like.

3. Select <UiPath path="composer.chrome.dismissWarning" />.

</Steps>

A dismissed warning stays listed with your name and reason, stops counting as a warning, and Review & send counts it as confirmed. A size warning comes back when the email grows, and a subject warning when the subject changes. <UiPath path="composer.chrome.undo" /> counts it again. Blocking issues can't be dismissed: they stop the send until they are fixed, for example a link on a blocklist, a missing unsubscribe link or postal address, an empty subject or personalization that doesn't render. Only a signed-in person can dismiss a warning; an API key or an [MCP agent](/developers/mcp/) can't.

## Preview the email

Select <UiPath path="composer.preview" /> in the top bar. The preview shows the email as Gmail on the web, Outlook for Windows, Apple Mail on iOS and Yahoo would show it, each in light and dark mode, at desktop or mobile width. With an A/B test, a switch above the cards picks the subject or content variant to preview; every variant is checked and previewed on its own.

<Screenshot
  id="composer-preview"
  alt="The preview of Weekly Digest #142: Gmail web cards in light and dark mode marked Approximated, a note that real renders need Litmus or Mailgun Inspect with a Connect button, and the side panel with the inbox preview, the personalization values and the A/B variants."
/>

The built-in previews are approximations, and they say so: ReputeCast renders the compiled HTML in your browser and imitates each client's dark mode. Outlook uses Word's rendering engine, so send a test, or connect a rendering service for screenshots from real email clients. For inbox placement, run a [placement test](/deliverability/placement-tests/).

Below the cards, <UiPath path="composer.preview.darkChecks" /> flag logos without a transparent background and text whose contrast drops when a client inverts colors, and a card shows the plain-text version. The side panel holds the inbox preview with subject and preheader lengths, the <UiPath path="composer.preview.personalization" /> values for the contact you preview as, and the [A/B variants](/campaigns/ab-testing/).

<UiPath path="composer.preview.previewAs" /> fills the email with a real contact from the audience, a contact without a first name (to check your fallbacks) or a random Active contact, using their own first and last name fields. Picking real contacts needs `subscribers:view`; without it you see a sample contact.

### See real renders with Litmus or Mailgun Inspect

ReputeCast can take screenshots in real email clients through your workspace's own Litmus or Mailgun Inspect account. Each client you render is one preview on that account's plan.

<Steps>

1. In <UiPath path="composer.preview" />, select <UiPath path="composer.renders.connect" /> in the note above the previews. Connecting needs `integrations:manage`; without it the note names who can.

2. Choose the <UiPath path="composer.renders.service" />, paste the <UiPath path="composer.renders.apiKey" /> (your Litmus API key for the Instant API, or your Mailgun private API key with Inspect on your plan) and, for Mailgun, the <UiPath path="composer.renders.region" />. Select <UiPath path="composer.renders.testAndConnect" />. ReputeCast tests the key by listing the account's email clients, then stores it encrypted; it is never shown again.

3. Select <UiPath path="composer.renders.renderReal" /> (needs `campaigns:edit`), pick the email clients and start the render. Your unsaved edits are saved first.

</Steps>

ReputeCast sends the saved version, or one A/B variant, personalized for a sample contact, and the screenshots appear under the previews as they arrive. A render of an older version says that the email has changed since. <UiPath path="composer.renders.manage" /> tests the key again or replaces it, and <UiPath path="composer.renders.disconnect" /> deletes the key from ReputeCast; renders already made stay with their campaigns.

## Send a test

<Steps>

1. Select <UiPath path="composer.sendTest" />. The composer saves first and the test waits for that save: if it fails (no connection, or a conflict), the test isn't sent, because it would send an older version.

2. In <UiPath path="composer.sendATest" />, tick workspace members (your own address is ticked) or type other addresses. With several content languages, pick the language of the test or one of each.

3. Select <UiPath path="composer.send" />.

</Steps>

A test goes to at most 10 addresses through the [System stream's route](/providers/routing/), with "[Test]" before the subject and a note under the email saying it is a test. A test sent from the preview is personalized as the <UiPath path="composer.preview.previewAs" /> contact, and the toast says so ("Personalized as Olga Bennett"); from the editor it is personalized as the audience's first contact. The unsubscribe and preferences links are test links. Without `campaigns:send` you can only send tests to yourself: the button reads <UiPath path="composer.sendTestToMe" />.

The arrow next to the button also offers a [placement test](/deliverability/placement-tests/), which needs `placement:run`.

## Saving and version history

### Autosave and the browser journal

The composer saves on its own: every 30 seconds while there are changes, the words you are typing included, and 2 seconds after you add or remove a section or element. Saves run one after another, in order. Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> on Windows and Linux) to save a version at any time; there is no Save button.

Between saves, every edit is also written to a journal in your browser within 2 seconds, one per person, campaign and language version. If the tab crashes or the laptop dies, reopening the campaign shows how many minutes of edits weren't saved: select <UiPath path="composer.banners.restoreThem" /> to save them as a new version on top of the current one, or <UiPath path="composer.banners.discard" />, which can't be undone. If someone saved since, their version stays in the history.

### When someone else saved first

If a teammate saves while you are editing and you changed different sections, the composer keeps both: your save takes their sections and says "Saved together with Linh Nguyen's changes". The subject, the preheader and the email styles merge the same way when only one of you changed them. If they changed the A/B test, you get the conflict banner instead.

When you both changed the same part, a conflict banner names who saved which version and what each of you changed. Your edits stay in the browser until you choose:

- <UiPath path="composer.banners.keepMine" /> saves yours as the next version on top of theirs.
- <UiPath path="composer.compare" /> shows both versions side by side, so you can take sections from each.
- <UiPath path="composer.banners.loadTheirs" /> keeps your edits in the version history as a version of their own, then opens theirs.

Nothing is lost either way. Switching the language version or restoring a version saves your edits first; if that save fails, a restore doesn't go ahead and a language switch asks what to do with your edits.

### When you are offline

If a save fails because the connection dropped, the composer keeps your edits in the browser and retries every 10 seconds. Keep the tab open until it saves, or select <UiPath path="composer.retryNow" />. <UiPath path="composer.downloadBackup" /> saves the MJML to a file you can import later.

### Version history

Every save is a new version. The version history button in the status bar lists them newest first, 50 at a time (<UiPath path="composer.dialogs.showOlder" /> loads more), with who saved each one and what changed. Select <UiPath path="composer.dialogs.nameButton" /> to name a version, such as "Sent to legal"; <UiPath path="composer.dialogs.namedVersionsBare" /> lists only named versions, which are never cleaned up. Tick two versions and select <UiPath path="composer.dialogs.compareTwo" /> to see a line-by-line diff of the MJML, subject and preheader, or select <UiPath path="composer.dialogs.restore" /> on an older one. Restoring saves it as a new version, so nothing is deleted.

### What the server checks

The composer compiles the MJML in your browser and sends the HTML with it. The server compiles the same MJML itself and compares what both show and do: links, images, words and the markers of [conditional blocks](/campaigns/conditional-content/). If they differ, it keeps its own compile. The same applies to A/B variants, language versions, templates and drafts written by [MCP agents](/developers/mcp/).

## Edit an approved campaign

A campaign that is approved, or waiting for approval, opens read-only with a banner saying who approved it (or asked) and for which version.

<Steps>

1. Select <UiPath path="composer.banners.editCampaign" /> in the banner.

2. Read what editing changes: the approval is cancelled (or the request withdrawn), a scheduled time stays but the send won't start without a new approval, and the approvers get what changed when you request approval again.

3. Select <UiPath path="composer.banners.editAnyway" />.

</Steps>

Once your first edit saves, the approval is cancelled and the top bar stops saying "Approved". See [require approval for risky sends](/campaigns/approvals/).

## Troubleshooting

- **The canvas doesn't accept a Text or Image block.** Content blocks go inside a column. Select a column on the canvas, or drag the block into one.
- **Nothing can be edited.** The campaign is approved or waiting for approval (select <UiPath path="composer.banners.editCampaign" /> in the banner), it is sending or sent, or your role lacks `campaigns:edit`.
- **Review & send reads Request approval.** Your role lacks `campaigns:send`; see [require approval for risky sends](/campaigns/approvals/).
- **Send test is unavailable with "Send test needs the latest save".** The last save failed. Fix the connection or the conflict, then try again.
- **The test fails with "no sending provider can deliver them yet".** Tests go through the System stream. [Connect a sending provider](/providers/connect/) and give the System stream a route.
- **The width switch does nothing.** It applies in Design mode only.
- **There is no Dismiss for an issue.** It is blocking; fix it instead.
- **"Fix the MJML first".** The MJML view has an error. Go to the line it names, or undo your changes.
