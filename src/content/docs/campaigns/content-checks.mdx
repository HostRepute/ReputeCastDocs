The composer runs an email spam checker and a set of content checks while you write, so you can fix what would hurt inbox placement or break the law before [Send Readiness](/reference/glossary/#send-readiness) runs its full review. Most findings come with a [one-click fix](/reference/glossary/#one-click-fix), and a warning you judged harmless can be dismissed with a reason.

## Before you begin

- The checks run for anyone who can open the campaign. Applying a fix or dismissing a warning needs `campaigns:edit`; dismissing is a person's judgment, so API keys and agents can't do it.
- The checks rerun about a second after each change to the canvas, subject or preheader. They don't open your links; Send Readiness does that. They do look up your link domains live on Spamhaus DBL and SURBL, the same lookups Send Readiness uses, and both reuse a recent answer (see [check and fix blocklist listings](/deliverability/blocklists/)).
- Set your postal address under <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.senders" /> (<UiPath path="senders.footerAddress" />), so the footer block can carry it.

## Fix the warnings

<Steps>

1. Open the campaign in the [composer](/campaigns/composer/) and select the <UiPath path="composer.chrome.tabChecks" /> tab in the inspector. Its label counts the blocks and warnings nobody dismissed. On top, one row each says how <UiPath path="composer.chrome.groupDeliverability" /> and <UiPath path="composer.chrome.groupAccessibility" /> stand, and two rows show the [workspace rules](/campaigns/sending-rules/) and sender reputation from the campaign's latest Send Readiness run.

2. Read the findings below them: blocks first, then warnings, then the subject's findings, with dismissed warnings last. The issues from Review & send, the A/B variants' findings and the <UiPath path="composer.chrome.groupAccessibility" /> group follow, and a folded list of the checks that passed ends the tab. Select a finding's title to select the element it is about on the canvas.

3. Apply a fix button, or change the email yourself. The list updates as you edit.

4. For a warning you decided to keep, select <UiPath path="composer.chrome.dismiss" />, pick why and, optionally, leave a note for reviewers (see [dismiss a warning](#dismiss-a-warning)).

5. Select <UiPath path="composer.reviewSend" />. While blocks remain, the button turns red and names the count.

</Steps>

Alt text findings move to the <UiPath path="composer.chrome.groupAccessibility" /> group of the same <UiPath path="composer.chrome.tabChecks" /> tab, which runs the fuller [accessibility checks](/campaigns/accessibility/) as soon as the canvas has content.

### Subject heuristics

The subject trips a heuristic when it has:

- **All caps**: at least 6 letters, 70% or more of them uppercase.
- **Repeated punctuation**: two or more `!` or `?` in a row, or four or more dots.
- **Urgency phrasing**: "act now", "urgent", "hurry", "last chance", "limited time", "today only", "expires", "ending soon", "ends today", "don't miss", "now or never", "before it's gone", or a deadline such as "48 hours only" or "only 3 days left". "Members only" and "2 hours of Q&A" don't count.
- **Money words**: "free", "guarantee", "cash", "no cost", "risk-free" or `$$`. "Free" inside a compound such as "gluten-free" or "free-range" doesn't count.
- **Click-bait**: "you won't believe", "shocking", "jaw-dropping", "mind-blowing", "what happens next", "doctors hate" and similar phrases.

Urgency, money and click-bait phrases are matched in English subjects and in emails without a language; [language versions](/campaigns/languages/) in other languages are checked for all caps and repeated punctuation only. The suggestion lowercases a shouting subject, removes repeated punctuation and urgency words, and turns "48 hours" into "48-hour", so `FLASH SALE!!! 48 HOURS ONLY` becomes `48-hour flash sale`.

The composer checks only the subject for spam phrases. Send Readiness also checks the preheader and the body text, where it warns when three or more of these phrases, or repeated punctuation three or more times, pile up. What else decides whether mail reaches the inbox is in [email deliverability for self-hosted senders](/deliverability/overview/).

### How the footer check finds the link and the address

- The **unsubscribe link** counts when a link points to `{{ unsubscribe_url }}`, or a link's text contains "unsubscribe".
- The **postal address** counts when your workspace's footer address appears in the email text, or the text has something that reads as a street address (a number followed by a street word such as Street, Ave, Road or Rue) or a postcode.

The <UiPath path="composer.blocks.footer" /> block covers both and adds the preferences and view-in-browser links. Mailbox providers and anti-spam laws require both; see [consent and anti-spam law basics](/deliverability/compliance/) and [one-click unsubscribe](/deliverability/one-click-unsubscribe/).

## What each fix does

| Fix                                            | What it changes                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <UiPath path="composer.canvas.insertFooter" /> | Adds the footer block at the end of the email, with your footer address and the unsubscribe, preferences and view-in-browser links. |
| **Use suggestion**                             | Replaces the subject with the suggested one.                                                                                          |
| **Minify inline CSS**                          | Turns on CSS minifying for this campaign's saves and sends. The canvas and the MJML stay as they are.                                |
| **Remove** a section                           | Deletes the section named in the finding. Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> to undo.                                                    |
| **Edit link**                                  | Selects the element and puts the cursor in its Link URL field in <UiPath path="composer.tabSettings" />.                              |
| **Save** (alt text)                            | Sets the image's alt text to what you typed.                                                                                          |

The composer never rewrites a link for you. Send Readiness, which opens every link, offers "Replace with destination URL" for a listed or shortened link only when the link redirects to a page whose domain is clean on Spamhaus DBL and SURBL.

## Dismiss a warning

Some warnings are deliberate: a decorative image, an email you'll keep under the clipping size, a subject style your list already knows.

<Steps>

1. On the warning, select <UiPath path="composer.chrome.dismiss" />.

2. Pick why: <UiPath path="composer.chrome.reasonDecorative" />, <UiPath path="composer.chrome.reasonSize" />, <UiPath path="composer.chrome.reasonSubject" />, <UiPath path="composer.chrome.reasonWrong" /> or <UiPath path="composer.chrome.reasonOther" />. Add a note for reviewers if it helps.

3. Select <UiPath path="composer.chrome.dismissWarning" />.

</Steps>

The warning stays listed in the Checks with your name and reason, and no longer counts. A size warning comes back if the email grows, and a subject warning if the subject changes. Select <UiPath path="composer.chrome.undo" /> on it to count it again. On [Review & send](/campaigns/send-readiness/), the same warning shows who dismissed it and why, and counts as already confirmed, at the send's confirmation and at the send-time recheck.

Blocks can't be dismissed: they stop the send until they are fixed. The composer says why for each one: a blocklisted link would get the whole send filtered, Gmail, Yahoo and CAN-SPAM require the footer, an email without a subject can't be sent, and broken personalization would show to every reader.

## What Send Readiness checks on top

[Send Readiness](/campaigns/send-readiness/) repeats these checks in its Content, Links and Compliance groups, in every language version and A/B variant, and adds more:

- It opens every link and image (up to its per-run limit) and warns about links that return 404, 410 or a server error, or don't respond, and about images that don't load or load over plain HTTP. A site that refuses automated requests makes the check Unknown instead of passing it.
- It measures the size with links rewritten for tracking, which can push an email over Gmail's limit.
- It warns whenever the plain-text version is empty, checks the preheader and body for spam phrases, and warns when recipients without a first name would get an empty greeting.
- A broken Liquid tag blocks there too: an email whose personalization doesn't render can't start sending.
- A Warn there needs a confirmation before you can send, unless someone dismissed it in the composer; a Block stops the send until it is fixed.

## Check that it worked

- The <UiPath path="composer.chrome.tabChecks" /> tab shows no count, and its <UiPath path="composer.chrome.groupDeliverability" /> row passes.
- The toolbar shows undo and redo again instead of the warning counts.
- <UiPath path="composer.reviewSend" /> no longer names blocks.

## Troubleshooting

- **No fix buttons.** Your role lacks `campaigns:edit`, or the campaign is sending or sent.
- **No Dismiss on a finding.** It is a block, and blocks can't be dismissed. Fix it instead.
- **The footer warning stays after you typed an address.** Use the address saved as your footer address, or write it with a street number and street word or a postcode.
- **"Couldn't check 2 link domains on Spamhaus DBL and SURBL".** DNS didn't answer in time, or the lists refused the server's resolver; Send Readiness tries again. If it keeps happening, the server needs its own recursive resolver (see [check and fix blocklist listings](/deliverability/blocklists/)).
- **The size warning stays after minifying.** Minifying removes only whitespace and HTML comments. Remove a section, shorten long text, or split the email.
- **Send Readiness reports a broken link the composer didn't.** Only Send Readiness opens links. Fix the address and run the checks again.
