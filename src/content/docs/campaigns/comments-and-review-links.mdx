Your team can discuss a campaign where it is written: comments sit on the blocks they are about, @mentions bring people in, and the [composer](/campaigns/composer/) shows who else has the campaign open. For people outside the workspace, an email review link shows one saved version of the email and takes their comments without a sign-in.

## Before you begin

- Commenting and replying need `campaigns:view`. Resolving a thread needs `campaigns:edit`.
- Sharing, listing and revoking [review links](/reference/glossary/#review-link) need `campaigns:edit`.
- Comments and review links are people's actions: they record who wrote, resolved or shared them. An API key or an [MCP agent](/developers/mcp/) can read comments but can't comment, resolve, or create or revoke a review link.
- Comments and review links are for campaigns. Transactional templates don't have them.

## Comment on a block

<Steps>

1. Open the campaign in the composer and select the block, such as a button or a section. To comment on the whole email, select nothing.

2. Open <UiPath path="composer.collab.tabComments" /> in the inspector. The new comment box says what it is on, for example "On the button “Read the docs”". In [Write mode](/campaigns/composer/#write-mode), use <UiPath path="composer.write.comment" /> in the formatting bar instead.

3. Write the comment. Type `@` and a few letters to mention a member of the workspace: <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> choose, <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Tab</kbd> picks, <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the list.

4. Select <UiPath path="composer.collab.comment" />.

</Steps>

When you can edit the campaign, the thread is attached to the block: the block carries a marker that is left out of the compiled email, the composer saves it, and the canvas shows the author's avatar on the block. When you can't edit, the comment names the block but isn't attached to it. A thread whose block was removed says it is no longer in the email.

The tab's label counts open threads, for example "Comments · 2". Threads refresh every 20 seconds while the composer is open.

### Mentions

Each person you @mention gets a notification with a link to the thread, and an email too unless they turned it off under <UiPath path="notify.event.comment_mention" /> in their [notification preferences](/team/notifications/). Mentioning yourself notifies no one. Only members of the workspace can be mentioned.

### Reply and resolve

- Select <UiPath path="composer.collab.reply" /> under a thread to answer it. Replies can mention people too.
- Select <UiPath path="composer.collab.resolve" /> when the thread is settled. Resolved threads fold into a count at the end of the tab, with who resolved each one; <UiPath path="composer.collab.reopen" /> opens one again.

Comments, resolves and reopens are recorded in the [audit log](/team/audit-log/).

## See who else is editing

The top bar shows the avatars of the other people who have the campaign open, each marked editing or viewing. The composer says it is there every 20 seconds; someone not heard from for a minute drops off. API keys and agents never appear.

When two people edit at once, the composer keeps both sets of changes if they touched different sections: the later save takes the other person's sections and says "Saved together with" their name. When both changed the same part, a conflict banner lets you choose; see [when someone else saved first](/campaigns/composer/#when-someone-else-saved-first).

## Share a review link

<Steps>

1. In the composer, select <UiPath path="composer.chrome.modeWrite" />, then <UiPath path="composer.collab.share" /> at the right of the formatting bar.

2. Your unsaved edits are saved first, so reviewers see them. ReputeCast shows the campaign's newest working link, or creates one for the saved version.

3. Select <UiPath path="composer.collab.copyLink" /> and send the link to your reviewers.

</Steps>

The panel says who created the link and which version it shows. If you saved a newer version since, it says so and offers to share that version with a new link. The version a link shows is named "Shared for review" in the [version history](/campaigns/composer/#version-history) when it has no name, so it is never cleaned up.

### What reviewers see

A reviewer opens the link in any browser, without an account:

- The email of that version as the reader sees it, personalized for a sample reader (never a contact's data), with the sender, subject and preheader. Nothing about your audience, settings or other campaigns.
- When the link expires, under the campaign's name.
- The comments written through the same link, and your team's replies to them, with the names of the members who replied.

To comment, a reviewer gives a name and writes. They can reply to threads written through the same link. Your team sees their comments in <UiPath path="composer.collab.tabComments" />, marked <UiPath path="composer.collab.reviewer" />, and can reply and resolve them as usual. Review pages are kept out of search engines and caches.

### Revoke a link

Open <UiPath path="composer.collab.share" /> and select <UiPath path="composer.collab.revoke" />. Anyone who opens the link then sees that it no longer works. Select <UiPath path="composer.collab.newLink" /> to share again. A link also stops working on its own when it expires. Creating and revoking links are recorded in the audit log.

<Aside type="caution">
  Anyone with a working link can read that version of the email and comment on it. Share it only with the people who
  should review, and revoke it when the review is done.
</Aside>

## Check that it worked

- The person you mentioned gets a notification that opens the composer on the thread.
- The commented block shows your avatar on the canvas.
- In a private browser window, the review link opens the email without a sign-in, and a test comment appears in <UiPath path="composer.collab.tabComments" /> marked <UiPath path="composer.collab.reviewer" />.

## Troubleshooting

- **There is no Share for review button.** It is in Write mode's formatting bar, and only for people with `campaigns:edit`.
- **There is no Resolve button.** Resolving needs `campaigns:edit`; the tab says who can.
- **A reviewer sees "This review link no longer works".** It expired or was revoked. Create a new link.
- **A reviewer sees "Too many requests from your network".** The review page limits requests per network. Wait a few minutes, then try again.
- **Reviewers see an older version.** A link always shows the version it was created for. Share the latest version with a new link.
- **A mentioned person got no email.** They turned off email for mentions in their notification preferences; the in-app notification still arrives.
- **A comment isn't on any block.** It was written by someone who can't edit the campaign, or its block was removed.
