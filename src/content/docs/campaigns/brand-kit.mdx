The email brand kit holds your logo, colours, fonts and button style, so every new email, template and block in the [composer](/campaigns/composer/) starts on brand. It also holds the [synced blocks](/reference/glossary/#synced-block): a Header and a Footer, and any section you save, that you edit once and every draft follows.

## Before you begin

- Changing the [brand kit](/reference/glossary/#brand-kit) needs `templates:edit`. Everyone who can view campaigns can see it.
- Editing, saving and deleting synced blocks needs `campaigns:edit`, because it changes emails. The same holds for two side effects of the kit: a new logo reaches the emails holding the synced Header only when the person saving also has `campaigns:edit`, and an import adds the logos it finds to the image library only for them. See [permissions and roles](/reference/permissions/).
- Set your postal address under <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.senders" /> (<UiPath path="senders.footerAddress" />): the synced Footer uses it.

## Set up the brand kit

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.brandKit" />. Until you save, the page shows ReputeCast's defaults and says it is not set up yet. The composer's template gallery also links here with <UiPath path="composer.brandkit.setUp" />.

2. Optionally, under <UiPath path="brandkit.importTitle" />, type your <UiPath path="brandkit.website" /> (such as `acme.io`) and select <UiPath path="brandkit.import" />. ReputeCast reads the logo, colours and fonts it finds and fills them in below for you to check. Nothing changes until you save; <UiPath path="brandkit.discardImport" /> drops what was read.

3. Under <UiPath path="brandkit.logo" />, upload a logo <UiPath path="brandkit.forLight" /> and, optionally, one <UiPath path="brandkit.forDark" />, and write its <UiPath path="brandkit.altText" />. Apple Mail and Outlook for Mac switch to the dark version in dark mode; Gmail doesn't support it and keeps the light one.

4. Under <UiPath path="brandkit.colours" />, set the <UiPath path="brandkit.button" /> (filled, with white text), <UiPath path="brandkit.text" />, <UiPath path="brandkit.background" /> (behind the 600 px email column) and <UiPath path="brandkit.link" /> colours. Each one shows its contrast where it is used, graded AAA, AA or below AA; AA is the minimum for small text.

5. Under <UiPath path="brandkit.fonts" />, pick the <UiPath path="brandkit.headingFont" /> and <UiPath path="brandkit.bodyFont" />. Each is a web font (from the import) with an email-safe <UiPath path="brandkit.fallback" />, or the fallback alone. Apple Mail and iOS Mail load web fonts; Gmail and Outlook for Windows show the fallback, so the samples use it.

6. Under <UiPath path="brandkit.buttonStyle" />, choose the <UiPath path="brandkit.shape" /> (Square, Rounded or Pill) and the text size. The Button block, article cards and the Poll block use it, 44 px tall.

7. Check the preview of how new emails start, then select <UiPath path="brandkit.save" />.

</Steps>

The first save creates the synced Header (the logo and links) and the synced Footer (postal address, unsubscribe and preferences links).

### What an import reads

The import fetches the address you type through the same address guard as provider connections: loopback and private addresses are refused unless an operator turns on [`providers.allow_private_networks`](/reference/configuration/), and cloud metadata addresses are always refused. It reads the page and its stylesheets on the same host, then tells you what it found (a logo in light and dark versions, colours and fonts) and what it decided:

- A colour too pale for links (under 4.5:1 on white) isn't used for links; the next colour on your site is.
- An SVG logo isn't used, because Gmail and Outlook don't show SVG. Upload a PNG version.
- Fonts from Google Fonts or Bunny Fonts keep their stylesheet as the web font, with a matching serif or sans-serif fallback.
- Without `campaigns:edit`, logos aren't added to the image library and a note says so. Ask someone who edits campaigns to import them.

## What changes when you save

- New emails, new blocks and every starter template start from the kit: the composer's starting styles, the logo in headers, the button style. The template gallery shows the starters in your brand.
- Drafts and scheduled emails already written keep their own colours and fonts. Only the synced Header and Footer change in them.
- A new logo updates the synced Header and the drafts and scheduled emails holding it, when you also hold `campaigns:edit`. Otherwise they keep their Header until someone who can edit them changes it.
- Sent emails never change.

## Edit the synced Header or Footer

<Steps>

1. In <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.brandKit" />, under <UiPath path="brandkit.synced" />, find the <UiPath path="brandkit.header" /> or <UiPath path="brandkit.footer" />. Each row says how many emails use it and what a save would do: how many drafts and scheduled emails update, and how many sent emails stay as sent.

2. Select <UiPath path="brandkit.edit" />. Change the section's MJML and check the preview.

3. Select <UiPath path="brandkit.saveBlock" />.

</Steps>

Drafts and scheduled emails holding the block get the change as a new version, labeled "synced block updated" in their [version history](/campaigns/composer/#version-history). Emails waiting for or holding an approval, sending, paused or sent keep theirs, so an approved email stays as it was approved. The Footer must keep its unsubscribe link, and a synced block is one section (an `mj-section` or `mj-wrapper`).

In the composer, the Header + logo and Footer + unsubscribe tiles under <UiPath path="composer.canvas.groupReputecast" /> are marked <UiPath path="composer.brandkit.synced" />. Selecting a synced block in an email says where it changes, with <UiPath path="composer.brandkit.editInBrandKit" />.

## Save a section as a synced block

Any section can be a synced block: a sign-off, a promo, a sponsor slot.

<Steps>

1. In the composer, select the section.

2. In <UiPath path="composer.tabSettings" />, select <UiPath path="composer.brandkit.saveSynced" />.

3. Give it a name people will recognize in the blocks panel, then select <UiPath path="composer.brandkit.saveSyncedConfirm" />.

</Steps>

The section goes to the brand kit, and this email holds the synced block in its place. Insert it in other emails from <UiPath path="composer.canvas.tabBlocks" /> › <UiPath path="composer.canvas.groupReputecast" />, and edit it in the brand kit, where you can also rename it. A section that is a synced block already, or holds one, can't be saved as a new one.

## Unlink or delete a synced block

- **Unlink one email.** Select the synced block in the composer and choose <UiPath path="composer.brandkit.unlink" />. The section becomes part of that email only and you can edit it there. Undo puts the link back.
- **Delete a named synced block.** In the brand kit, select its delete button and confirm with <UiPath path="brandkit.deleteConfirm" />. Drafts and scheduled emails holding it keep its content as an ordinary section, in a new version; emails in an approval, sending, paused or sent keep it as they are. The Header and Footer can't be deleted; edit them instead.

An email that still holds a deleted synced block says so; unlink it to edit it there.

## Check that it worked

- The brand kit page no longer says it is not set up, and the synced blocks list a Header and a Footer.
- A new campaign's template gallery shows the starters in your colours, fonts and logo.
- After editing the Footer, a draft that holds it shows the change and a new version in its history.

## Troubleshooting

- **The page is read-only.** Changing the brand kit needs `templates:edit`; the page says who can change it. Editing synced blocks needs `campaigns:edit`.
- **"… couldn't be read" when importing.** The site didn't answer within the time limit, or its address is private. Check the address, or set the kit by hand.
- **The imported logo isn't in the kit.** It is an SVG, or you lack `campaigns:edit` so it wasn't added to the image library. Upload a PNG, or ask someone who edits campaigns to import it.
- **Drafts didn't get the new logo.** The person who saved the kit lacks `campaigns:edit`. Someone who has it can save the Header again.
- **"The footer needs the unsubscribe link".** Every email holding the Footer must have one; put the `{{ unsubscribe_url }}` link back.
- **Gmail shows a different font.** Gmail shows the fallback, not the web font. Pick a fallback close to your web font.
