An email approval workflow in ReputeCast makes a second person check a send before it goes out, but only when it is risky: a large audience, a new or warming-up sending provider, an author without send permission. Each workspace has one [approval](/reference/glossary/#approval) policy. When one of its rules matches, the author requests approval instead of sending, and an approver approves exactly what will be sent.

## Before you begin

- Changing the policy needs `workspace:manage`. Everyone else sees it read-only.
- Approvers are the people whose role can approve campaigns (`campaigns:approve`): the Owner and Admin roles can, Editor and Viewer can't. In an agency's client workspace, the Client approver role can too. The policy page lists today's approvers by name.
- Requesting approval needs `campaigns:edit`. An author never approves their own campaign.
- Where an action is refused, ReputeCast names the people who can do it or what their role lacks, not a permission key.

## Choose when approval is needed

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.approvals" />. Approval is required when any rule that is on matches.

2. Turn on the rules you want:

   | Rule                                                                                  | In a new workspace |
   | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
   | A Broadcast audience of at least N recipients, counted after exclusions               | On, 10,000         |
   | The route includes a Warming-up provider, or one connected in the last 14 days        | On                 |
   | Send Readiness is "Ready with warnings" (blocked sends never start anyway)            | Off                |
   | The author lacks the send permission (`campaigns:send`, or `sms:send` for texts)      | On                 |
   | The audience includes a list marked "Requires approval"                               | Off                |
   | An SMS or WhatsApp campaign is estimated to cost at least $N                          | Off ($50 when on)  |
   | Publishing an automation also requires approval                                       | Off                |
   | Publishing a transactional template also requires approval                            | Off                |
   | Publishing a post or page needs approval                                              | Off                |

   The rules also apply to each issue of a [recurring digest](/campaigns/recurring-digests/). In an agency's client workspace there is one more rule: every send needs one approval from a Client approver.

3. Under <UiPath path="approvals.policy.requiredLegend" />, choose <UiPath path="approvals.policy.oneApproval" /> or <UiPath path="approvals.policy.twoApprovals" />. Two approvals come from two different approvers, neither of them the author; the option is available once two members can approve campaigns.

4. Under <UiPath path="approvals.policy.bypassTitle" />, choose whether Owners can send without approval by giving a reason (see [emergency bypass](#emergency-bypass)).

5. Select <UiPath path="approvals.policy.savePolicy" />. The change is recorded in the audit log.

</Steps>

## Request approval

<Steps>

1. On the campaign's <UiPath path="composer.reviewSend" /> page, the approval card shows <UiPath path="review.requiredTitle" />, the Send Readiness verdict and the rules that matched, in people's words: "Your role, Content Writer, needs an approval to send: Marcus Chen or Linh Nguyen can approve". When someone chose to send without checks that couldn't run, the verdict says which were skipped, for example "Ready with warnings · 1 check skipped".

2. Fix every block first: a Blocked campaign can't be sent for approval. Choose when it should go out. If the checks are out of date, they run again first: request approval again when they finish.

3. Add a note for the approvers if it helps, then select <UiPath path="review.requestApproval" />. ReputeCast refuses the request if there aren't enough approvers besides you.

4. The card changes to <UiPath path="review.waitingTitle" />. Approvers get a notification in the app and by email.

5. If nobody has answered, select <UiPath path="review.remind" />. Reminders are never sent automatically, and you can remind at most once an hour. To withdraw, select <UiPath path="review.cancelRequest" />.

</Steps>

## Review a request

<Steps>

1. Open the request from the notification, from <UiPath path="review.reviewRequest" /> on the campaign's Review & send page, or from the approvals inbox at `/w/<workspace>/approvals` (it lives under <UiPath path="nav.campaigns" />). Approvers see every request; everyone else sees their own. On a phone, the composer's quick review also offers <UiPath path="composer.chrome.approve" /> when a request waits for your decision (see [approve from a phone](#approve-from-a-phone)).

2. Read what you are approving: the email, the subject, the sender, the links, the audience and its count, the route, the schedule, the thresholds and the Send Readiness result at request time, including any checks the author chose to skip.

3. Decide under "Your decision":
   - Select <UiPath path="approvals.review.approve" /> (a comment is optional).
   - Select <UiPath path="approvals.review.requestChanges" /> and say what should change.
   - Select <UiPath path="approvals.review.reject" /> and say why.

4. After an approval, the author schedules the campaign with <UiPath path="review.actionApproved" />. With changes requested or a rejection, the campaign goes back to draft and the author is notified.

</Steps>

## Approve from a phone

On a phone, the [composer](/campaigns/composer/) opens a quick review of the campaign. When a request waits for your decision, it lists <UiPath path="composer.chrome.approve" />: the sheet says who asked for which version and how many approvals are still needed. Select <UiPath path="composer.chrome.approve" /> to approve, or <UiPath path="composer.chrome.reviewRequest" /> to open the request, where you can ask for changes or reject it.

## What an approval covers

An approval covers a snapshot of the send, not the campaign in general. It is invalidated, with the exact difference shown, when any of these change after the request: subject, preheader, links, content, sender, audience definition, route, schedule or language versions. It is also invalidated when the audience count moves by more than 10% from the count at request time.

Editing an approved campaign asks first. The composer shows who approved which version and that editing cancels the approval; the first edit opens a dialog, and <UiPath path="composer.banners.editAnyway" /> cancels the approval, after which the header no longer says <UiPath path="approvals.statusApproved" />. Editing a campaign waiting for approval withdraws the request the same way. An agent's draft never cancels a person's approval: its save is refused instead.

When an approval is invalidated, the campaign goes back to draft and the author and approvers are notified. A scheduled campaign keeps its time slot but doesn't start without a new approval. Restoring the approved version of the content, audience or schedule keeps the approval.

At the send time, ReputeCast checks again: Send Readiness runs 15 minutes before the send and at its start, and a missing, rejected or invalidated approval holds the send the same way a block does. See [when the checks run](/campaigns/send-readiness/#when-the-checks-run).

## Emergency bypass

When the policy allows it, an Owner can use <UiPath path="review.moreActions" /> › <UiPath path="review.sendWithoutApproval" /> on Review & send for a send that a rule matched; for anyone else the menu item says who can. They write a reason (at least 10 characters) and, if their account has a password, confirm it. The bypass is recorded as `campaign.approval_bypassed` with the reason, IP address and the campaign snapshot, and every approver is notified. Warnings still have to be confirmed, and a Blocked Send Readiness result still stops the send. The policy page shows how often the bypass was used in the last 90 days.

## Check that it worked

- A campaign that matches a rule shows Approval required on Review & send, with the matched rules listed.
- The request appears under Pending in the inbox, and approvers have a notification.
- Integrations can follow along with the `approval.requested` and `approval.decided` [webhook events](/reference/webhook-events/).

## Troubleshooting

- **"Nobody else can approve campaigns. An Owner can let another role approve them."** You need at least one member besides the author whose role can approve campaigns (`campaigns:approve`; two for 2 approvals). Ask an Owner to change a role in <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.roles" />.
- **"Approvers were reminded less than an hour ago."** Reminders are limited to one an hour; the message says when you can remind again.
- **The approval was invalidated though nobody touched the email.** The audience count moved by more than 10%, for example after a large import. The inbox shows "audience moved" with the difference; request approval again.
- **The 2 approvals option is greyed out.** Fewer than two members can approve campaigns.
- **The policy page is read-only and says "Your role can't change it."** Changing it needs `workspace:manage`.
- **The approval went away after an edit.** Editing the content of an approved campaign cancels the approval; restore the approved version from Review & send, or request approval again.
