Email templates let you start every campaign from a tested layout instead of a blank canvas, and saved sections let you drop the same header, sign-off or promo block into any email. Both live in the [composer](/campaigns/composer/) and are written in MJML, so everything you load stays editable block by block.

## Before you begin

- Starting a campaign from a template needs `campaigns:edit`.
- Saving a template or a section needs `templates:edit`. Owner, Admin and Editor have it; Viewer only has `templates:view` and can't save. See [permissions and roles](/reference/permissions/).
- Set your postal address under <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.senders" /> (<UiPath path="senders.footerAddress" />) first: the built-in templates fill their footer with it.

## Start a campaign from a template

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.campaigns" />, select <UiPath path="campaigns.newCampaign" />, choose <UiPath path="digests.regular" /> and select **Continue**. The composer opens an empty draft on the template gallery.

2. Under <UiPath path="composer.galleryTitle" />, select a tile:

   - a built-in template (see the table below),
   - a template your team saved, under <UiPath path="composer.yours" />,
   - **Copy of** your last sent campaign, to start from its layout and content, or
   - <UiPath path="composer.importTile" />, to paste or upload an `.mjml` or `.html` file.

3. The template loads at once. Edit it like any email; the subject, preheader and audience are yours to set.

</Steps>

Each tile also shows its accessibility result, such as "All clear" or the number of issues found, so you can start from a layout without [accessibility problems](/campaigns/accessibility/). Agency staff working in a client workspace also see the agency's shared library; see [run client workspaces as an agency](/team/agencies/).

### Built-in templates

| Template       | What it holds                                                                                          |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Blank          | A body and a footer block.                                                                             |
| Weekly digest  | Header with logo, hero image and heading, an intro, two article cards with "Read the article" buttons, footer. |
| Product update | Header with logo, hero, heading, text and a "See what's new" button, footer.                           |
| Plain text     | A personal note in four paragraphs, no images, short footer.                                           |
| Announcement   | A full-width banner image, heading, two paragraphs and a "Read the announcement" button, footer.        |
| Product grid   | Two rows of three product cards with text between them, footer.                                        |

A seventh template, Blog digest, shows the feed's posts as cards and is offered only when you design a [recurring digest](/campaigns/recurring-digests/). The images in the built-in templates are placeholders: replace them with your own.

If no footer address is set, the footer reads "Your company · Street and number · City, postcode", and the [content checks](/campaigns/content-checks/) flag it until you change it.

## Replace an email's content with a template

<Steps>

1. In the composer's left panel, open <UiPath path="composer.canvas.tabTemplates" />.

2. Select a template.

3. Confirm with <UiPath path="composer.canvas.replaceContent" />, or select <UiPath path="composer.canvas.keepEditing" /> to cancel.

</Steps>

The canvas content is replaced; the subject, preheader and audience stay. The version before the change stays in the [version history](/campaigns/composer/#autosave-and-version-history), so you can restore it.

## Save a template or a section

<Steps>

1. To save one section, select it on the canvas first.

2. In the left panel, open <UiPath path="composer.canvas.tabTemplates" /> and select <UiPath path="composer.canvas.saveAs" /> at the bottom of the list.

3. Under **What to save**, choose <UiPath path="composer.dialogs.wholeEmail" /> (a template) or <UiPath path="composer.dialogs.selectedSection" /> (a saved section).

4. Give it a name of up to 120 characters and, optionally, a description of up to 300 characters.

5. Select **Save**.

</Steps>

Saving the whole email first saves the campaign as a new version, then stores its MJML and compiled HTML as a template. A section stores only that section's MJML. A confirmation under the canvas says the template is now in Templates.

Names are unique per kind: two templates, or two saved sections, can't have the same name (letter case doesn't matter).

## Insert a saved section

<Steps>

1. Select the section the new one should follow. With nothing selected, it goes at the end of the email.

2. In <UiPath path="composer.canvas.tabBlocks" />, under ReputeCast blocks, select the <UiPath path="composer.canvas.savedSections" /> tile. The panel switches to <UiPath path="composer.canvas.tabTemplates" /> and scrolls to the list.

3. Select a section. It is inserted at once.

</Steps>

Saved sections show in the panel once the canvas has content; on an empty draft, start from a template first.

## Rename or delete a template

The app has no screen to rename, edit or delete a saved template or section in this build. With `templates:edit` you can do it through the API: [update a template](/api/operations/update-template/) or [delete a template](/api/operations/delete-template/). Deleted templates are removed from the gallery and recorded in the [audit log](/team/audit-log/).

<Aside type="tip">
  To link a teammate straight to a draft built from a template, use
  `/w/<workspace>/campaigns/new?template=starter:weekly-digest` (or a saved template's ID instead of
  `starter:weekly-digest`).
</Aside>

## Check that it worked

- The template appears under <UiPath path="composer.yours" /> the next time someone opens the gallery, and in <UiPath path="composer.canvas.tabTemplates" />.
- The <UiPath path="composer.canvas.savedSections" /> tile counts your new section.

## Troubleshooting

- **There is no Save as template or section button.** Your role lacks `templates:edit`, or the campaign can't be edited (it is sending or sent).
- **The selected section option is greyed out.** Nothing is selected. Select a section, or any block inside it, on the canvas first.
- **"A template called … already exists".** Pick another name, or delete the old one through the API.
- **"The MJML doesn't parse".** The email has an MJML error. Fix it in the MJML view, then save again.
- **An imported HTML email can't be edited block by block.** HTML goes into one Raw HTML section. Rebuild it with MJML blocks, then save it as a template.
