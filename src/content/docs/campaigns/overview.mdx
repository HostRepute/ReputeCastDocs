Email campaign management in ReputeCast happens on one screen, <UiPath path="nav.campaigns" />: you start every kind of [campaign](/reference/glossary/#campaign) there, follow each one from draft to report, and reuse what worked.

## Before you begin

- To see campaigns you need `campaigns:view`. To create, edit, tag, archive or delete one you need `campaigns:edit`; without it the list has no checkboxes and no ⋯ menu. Sending or scheduling needs `campaigns:send` as well; see [roles and permissions](/team/roles-and-permissions/).
- SMS, push and WhatsApp campaigns appear as choices only after that channel is set up: [SMS](/channels/sms/), [web push](/channels/web-push/), [WhatsApp](/channels/whatsapp/).

## Start a campaign

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.campaigns" /> and select <UiPath path="campaigns.newCampaign" />.

2. Under **What are you sending?**, pick the kind of campaign:

   - The <UiPath path="digests.regular" /> choice is one email, sent once; [A/B tests](/campaigns/ab-testing/) live here. It opens the composer on the template gallery.
   - The <UiPath path="digests.recurringDigest" /> choice builds issues from an RSS, Atom or JSON feed, or a connected WordPress site, on a schedule. It opens the digest's settings.
   - The <UiPath path="sms.newText" /> choice is one text, sent once, routed by country. It opens the SMS composer.
   - The <UiPath path="channelCampaigns.newPush" /> choice is a notification to readers' browsers on your push sites. It opens the push composer.
   - The <UiPath path="channelCampaigns.newWhatsApp" /> choice sends an approved WhatsApp template to people with WhatsApp consent. It opens the WhatsApp campaign.

3. Select **Continue**. A recurring digest also needs a feed address or a connected WordPress site before Continue works; see [send RSS digests automatically](/campaigns/recurring-digests/).

4. Write the email in [the composer](/campaigns/composer/), then open [Send Readiness](/campaigns/send-readiness/) to check and send it.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
  Press <kbd>N</kbd> anywhere in the app (outside a text field) to start a regular email draft at once. It skips the
  **What are you sending?** step, so use the button for the other kinds. All shortcuts are in the [keyboard shortcuts
  reference](/reference/keyboard-shortcuts/).
</Aside>

A new regular campaign is saved as a draft named "Untitled campaign" the moment the composer opens.

## Find a campaign

<Screenshot
  id="campaigns-list"

/>

The page has four ways to narrow the list:

- **Status tabs.** Each tab counts the campaigns that match the search, channel and filters you chose. All holds every campaign that isn't archived or deleted; Drafts holds drafts, campaigns pending approval and approved ones; Scheduled holds scheduled ones, including a held send; Sending holds sending and auto-paused campaigns; Sent holds sent, cancelled and failed ones. Recurring lists your [recurring digests](/campaigns/recurring-digests/) instead of campaigns. A Paid only tab appears once a campaign goes to paid members only, <UiPath path="campaigns.filter.archived" /> once a campaign is archived, and <UiPath path="campaigns.filter.deleted" /> while a campaign deleted in the last 30 days can be restored.
- **Channel chips.** Once two or more channels are set up, chips for All, Email, SMS, Push and WhatsApp sit above the table. Choosing SMS changes the columns to opt-outs and cost instead of opens.
- **Filters.** Next to the chips: <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.whenLabel" /> (<UiPath path="campaigns.manage.when7" />, <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.when30" />, <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.whenQuarter" />, or <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.whenCustom" /> with a first and last day), <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.senderLabel" />, <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.listLabel" /> (one of the lists a campaign goes to) and, once a campaign has a tag, <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.tagLabel" />. The date is when the campaign started sending or is scheduled to; for a draft, when it was last edited. <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.clearFilters" /> removes them all.
- **Search.** <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.searchPlaceholder" /> matches part of a campaign's name or subject, in any letter case. Characters such as `%` and `_` match only themselves. It doesn't search content.

The list shows 25 campaigns per page. Sending and auto-paused campaigns come first, then scheduled ones (soonest first), campaigns waiting for or holding approval, drafts, and finally sent, cancelled and failed campaigns. Inside each group the newest is first. Columns can't be sorted. <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.calendar" />, next to the search box, shows scheduled and sent campaigns by day: see [plan sends on the campaign calendar](/campaigns/calendar/).

<UiPath path="coupons.title" />, between the search box and <UiPath path="campaigns.newCampaign" />, opens the pools that emails hand out unique codes from: see [give each reader a unique discount code](/campaigns/discount-codes/). Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

While the [publication website](/forms/website/) is on, a <UiPath path="website.elsewhere.colWeb" /> column after the status shows whether each email is also a post on it: <UiPath path="website.elsewhere.webPublished" /> with the post's address, <UiPath path="website.elsewhere.webScheduled" /> to publish with the send or at a set time, the date it was unpublished, or <UiPath path="website.elsewhere.webNotOn" /> for an unsent email that won't be published. SMS, push and WhatsApp rows show a dash. The column needs `website:view`.

The five totals above the table always cover the last 30 days of every channel, whatever tab, filter or search you choose: messages sent, delivered (with bounce and complaint rates), [human opens](/reference/glossary/#human-opens) as a share of delivered email ([privacy-protected opens](/reference/glossary/#privacy-protected-open) such as Apple Mail Privacy Protection prefetches, and machine opens such as security scanners' fetches, are counted apart, as in [campaign reports](/campaigns/reports/)), clicks with the click-to-open rate, and the sending cost per sending provider. Imported history doesn't count.

The readiness button reads **Readiness · N** when the last check found warnings, or **N blocking issues** when it found a Block, and opens [Send Readiness](/campaigns/send-readiness/). It only appears after Send Readiness has run at least once. <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.unschedule" /> on a scheduled email's row makes it a draft again (an approved one stays approved) without opening it.

Every row also has a ⋯ menu with what fits the campaign: <UiPath path="campaigns.edit" /> or <UiPath path="campaigns.report" />, <UiPath path="campaigns.duplicate" /> and <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.followUp" /> for email, <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.addTag" />, and last <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.archive" /> or <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.delete" />. SMS, push and WhatsApp rows get the same menu without the email items.

On a phone the table scrolls sideways inside its card and the sections move to the tab bar at the bottom of the screen.

## Delete or archive a campaign

What a campaign allows depends on whether it sent anything:

- A draft, a campaign pending approval or approved, and a cancelled or failed campaign that sent nothing can be **deleted**. It moves to the <UiPath path="campaigns.filter.deleted" /> tab, where it can be restored for 30 days.
- A sent campaign, and a cancelled or failed one that sent something, is **archived** instead, never deleted. It leaves the list for the <UiPath path="campaigns.filter.archived" /> tab, and its report, web post and retries stay where they are.
- A scheduled, sending or auto-paused campaign can be neither: unschedule or cancel it first.

<Steps>

1. Open the ⋯ menu on the campaign's row and select <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.delete" /> or <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.archive" />.

2. Confirm. Deleting a campaign that waits for or holds an approval closes its request, and the campaign is a plain draft again if you restore it.

3. The confirmation offers <UiPath path="ui.undo" /> for a few seconds. Later, select <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.restore" /> on its row in the <UiPath path="campaigns.filter.deleted" /> tab, which says until when it can come back, or <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.unarchive" /> in the ⋯ menu of an archived row.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
  A deleted campaign is removed for good 30 days after it was deleted, and can't be restored after that.
</Aside>

Every channel works the same way. Each delete, restore, archive and unarchive is recorded in the [audit log](/team/audit-log/) (`campaign.delete`, `campaign.restore`, `campaign.archive`, `campaign.unarchive`). Scripts use the [delete](/api/operations/delete-campaign/), [restore](/api/operations/restore-campaign/), [archive](/api/operations/archive-campaign/) and [unarchive](/api/operations/unarchive-campaign/) operations.

## Act on several campaigns at once

<Steps>

1. Tick the checkbox before each campaign's name, or the one in the table's header to select every campaign on the page. The selection covers one page: another page, tab or filter clears it.

2. In the bar above the table, choose <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.bulkAddTag" />, <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.bulkRemoveTag" />, <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.archive" /> or <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.bulkDelete" />. On the <UiPath path="campaigns.filter.archived" /> tab, <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.unarchive" /> replaces the last two. <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.bulkClear" /> drops the selection.

3. Confirm. Each campaign is handled on its own: one that can't be (a draft can't be archived, a sent campaign can't be deleted) is skipped, and a dialog lists the skipped campaigns with the reason.

</Steps>

<Screenshot
  id="campaigns-bulk"
  alt="The Campaigns list with every campaign on the page selected: the bar above the table counts them and offers Add tag, Remove tag, Archive, Delete and Clear, and each selected row is highlighted."
/>

Deleted campaigns can't be selected: restore them one at a time.

A tag marks campaigns that belong together, such as `product` or `q4 launch`, and shows after the campaign's name. Add one with <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.addTag" /> in a row's ⋯ menu or with the bar; the field suggests the tags already in use, and capital letters are saved in lower case. Then pick it under <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.tagLabel" /> to list those campaigns. The [bulk campaigns API operation](/api/operations/bulk-campaigns/) does the same from a script.

## Reuse a sent campaign

<Steps>

1. Open the ⋯ menu on the sent campaign's row and select <UiPath path="campaigns.duplicate" />, or open its report and select <UiPath path="reports.duplicate" />.

2. A new draft named "Copy of …" opens in the composer.

</Steps>

The copy keeps the subject, preheader, content, audience (lists, segments and exclusions) and sender, plus the A/B test setup without its winner. A member limited to some lists keeps the audience only if they could pick it, and one limited to some sender domains keeps the sender only if it is on one of them (since 1.5.0); otherwise the copy starts without an audience, or with a sender from one of their own domains. It doesn't keep the schedule, route or threshold overrides, language versions or approvals. Its version history starts with "copied from" and the original's name.

SMS, push and WhatsApp campaigns have a **Duplicate** button in their list row, which makes a copy in the same way. To copy a campaign from a script, use the [duplicate campaign API operation](/api/operations/duplicate-campaign/). To send a sent email again to the people who didn't open it, or to those who clicked, use a [follow-up](/campaigns/reports/#follow-up-on-non-openers-or-clickers) instead.

## Check that it worked

- Your new campaign appears under the Drafts tab with the status Draft.
- A duplicated campaign appears as "Copy of …" in Drafts, with the source's subject in the composer.
- A deleted campaign appears under <UiPath path="campaigns.filter.deleted" /> with the date until which it can be restored; an archived one under <UiPath path="campaigns.filter.archived" />.

## Troubleshooting

- **New campaign is greyed out.** Your role lacks `campaigns:edit`; the button says who can help. Ask a workspace Owner or Admin.
- **No SMS, Push or WhatsApp choice.** That channel isn't set up in this workspace yet, or your role can't see sending providers (`providers:view`).
- **A campaign you expect is missing.** Check the filters and the tab: a held send is under Scheduled, an auto-paused send under Sending, a cancelled one under Sent, an archived one under <UiPath path="campaigns.filter.archived" /> and a deleted one under <UiPath path="campaigns.filter.deleted" />. Search matches names and subjects, not content.
- **No checkboxes and no ⋯ menu.** Your role lacks `campaigns:edit`.
- **The ⋯ menu has neither Delete nor Archive.** The campaign is scheduled, sending or auto-paused. Unschedule it, or cancel the send on the live monitor.
- **"A campaign that sent anything is archived, not deleted"**. Archive it instead.
- **"This campaign was deleted more than 30 days ago and can't be restored."** It is gone for good. Duplicate a similar campaign, or start a new one.
- **"A tag is up to 40 lower-case letters, digits, spaces and - _ . /"** The tag has another character, such as `#` or `&`.
