Your brand voice says how your newsletter sounds: its tone, how it addresses readers, its sentence length, the words
it prefers and avoids, and a few examples. ReputeCast keeps it with the [brand kit](/campaigns/brand-kit/), and the
AI features that write for you use the saved brand voice as style guidance. Saving a voice never changes an email that
already exists. Available since 1.7.0.

## Before you begin

- Saving a voice needs `templates:edit` (Owner, Admin and Editor). Everyone with `campaigns:view` can read it.
- Learning a voice from sent campaigns also needs `ai:use`, a [connected model](/integrations/ai-assistant/) and the
  <UiPath path="ai.featureBrandVoice" /> feature switched on. Writing the voice by hand needs no model.

## Write the voice

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.brandKit" /> and scroll to <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.title" />,
   under the synced blocks. A badge shows the saved revision, or <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.empty" />.

2. Fill in <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.editorTitle" />. Only <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.tone" /> is required:

   - <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.tone" /> (up to 1,000 characters), such as "Calm, specific and helpful".
   - <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.address" /> and <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.sentenceLength" /> (500 each).
   - <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.preferredWords" /> and <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.avoidedWords" /> (1,000 each).
   - <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.examples" /> (2,000): generic sentences, without names, contact details,
     credentials or private links.

3. Choose <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.save" />. ReputeCast saves it as the next revision.

</Steps>

If someone saves another revision while you edit, the card says so and keeps your edits;
<UiPath path="brandkit.discardChanges" /> loads the latest revision.

## Learn a voice from sent campaigns

<Steps>

1. Under <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.learnTitle" />, search for and tick one to ten sent email campaigns that sound
   the way you want. The card shows the model and an estimate of the cost.

2. Choose <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.learn" />, check what <UiPath path="ai.draft.sharingTitle" /> lists, and
   choose <UiPath path="ai.draft.sharingConfirm" />.

3. The card shows <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.suggested" />: each field's saved value next to the suggestion, which
   you can edit. Choose <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.saveSuggested" /> to save it as the next revision, or reject it.
   A suggestion you leave expires after 24 hours.

</Steps>

Only the selected campaigns' text goes to the model, as written: Liquid tokens stay unexpanded, and nothing about the
people who received them is sent. ReputeCast refuses the request, before sending anything, when the text looks like it
holds personal data or credentials; choose other campaigns or edit them first. The sample is the text, buttons,
navigation and tables of each campaign, outside its footer; a campaign without any of these in its saved content
can't be learned from. Learning never changes the saved voice by itself.

## Choose the voice for a request

<UiPath path="ai.featureSubjectIdeas" />, <UiPath path="ai.featureRewrite" />, [drafts from a
brief](/integrations/ai-drafts/) and the [AI agent](/integrations/ai-agent/) send the voice with their request. Each
has a <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.voiceSelection" /> menu: at the top of the composer's
<UiPath path="ai.suggest.assist" /> menu, in the draft dialogs and on the agent's brief. Pick
<UiPath path="brandkit.voice.latest" /> (the default), <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.none" /> or an earlier revision.

The model reads the voice as style only: what you asked for, the facts, links and tokens come first, and it doesn't
follow instructions written into the voice. A proposal names the revision it used, as "Saved voice revision 2", and
changing the voice later doesn't rewrite drafts made with an earlier one.

## Saved revisions

<UiPath path="brandkit.voice.history" /> lists every revision, newest first, with who saved it and when; earlier
revisions are immutable. Saving a voice, learning one and rejecting a suggestion are recorded in the
[audit log](/team/audit-log/) as `brand_voice.save`, `brand_voice.suggest` and `brand_voice.reject`, without the text
sent to the model.

The API reads and saves the voice with an API key too: `GET` and `PUT /api/v1/workspaces/<workspace>/brand-kit/voice`
and `GET …/brand-kit/voice/history`. A save names the revision it starts from (`baseRevision`) and fails with `409`
when someone saved another one since. See the [API reference](/api/).

## Check that it worked

- The <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.title" /> badge shows the new revision.
- A proposal from <UiPath path="ai.draft.title" /> says which voice revision it used.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Describe the tone before saving the voice"**: <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.tone" /> is empty.
- **"The selected text may contain personal data or credentials"**: pick other campaigns, or remove the address,
  number or private link from them.
- **"Choose campaigns with unrendered text to learn from"**: a campaign you picked has no text blocks in its saved
  content outside the footer. Pick another one.
- **<UiPath path="brandkit.voice.off" />**: someone with `integrations:manage` turns on
  <UiPath path="ai.featureBrandVoice" /> in the AI assistant settings. You can still write the voice by hand.
- **The fields are read only**: your role can't edit templates; <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.readOnly" />.
