The email campaign calendar shows what goes out when: every scheduled and sent campaign of every channel, and the
[recurring digest](/reference/glossary/#recurring-digest) issues still to come, by month or by week. You can move a
scheduled campaign to another day by dragging it, and ReputeCast checks it again before it goes out.

## Before you begin

- Seeing the calendar needs `campaigns:view`.
- Moving a scheduled campaign needs `campaigns:send`. A text also needs `sms:send`, a push notification `push:send`
  and a WhatsApp campaign `whatsapp:send`. Without `campaigns:send`, items can't be dragged and have no
  <UiPath path="campaigns.calendar.reschedule" /> button.
- Moving a send schedules it again on your behalf. Since 1.5.0, a member limited to some lists or sender domains moves
  only a campaign whose audience is on their lists, with no segment, and whose From address is on one of their sender
  domains. Texts and WhatsApp campaigns follow the same rule for lists, and a push campaign reaches only their lists.

## Read the calendar

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.campaigns" /> and select <UiPath path="campaigns.manage.calendar" /> next to the search box.
   The month opens on today. On a phone, the month is a list of the days that have something.

2. Choose <UiPath path="campaigns.calendar.month" /> or <UiPath path="campaigns.calendar.week" />. The arrows step to
   the previous or next month or week, and <UiPath path="campaigns.calendar.today" /> comes back.

3. Read each item: its name, then its time and what it is. The <UiPath path="campaigns.calendar.legend" /> gives the
   colours: <UiPath path="campaigns.calendar.scheduled" /> with the recipients counted so far,
   <UiPath path="campaigns.calendar.sent" /> (or sending, or paused) with how many it reached, and
   <UiPath path="campaigns.calendar.digestIssue" /> for the next issues of each recurring digest. A campaign that goes
   out at one time of day in each recipient's time zone shows that time with "local". A month's day shows three
   items; the link under them opens that week with the rest.

4. Select an item to open it: a scheduled campaign opens Review & send, a sent one its report, and a digest issue its
   digest's page.

</Steps>

Days and times are in the workspace's time zone, as everywhere in the app.

## Move a scheduled campaign

<Steps>

1. Drag a scheduled item to another day that is still ahead. Days that have passed don't take a send. Without a
   mouse, open the week view (or the list on a phone) and select <UiPath path="campaigns.calendar.reschedule" /> next
   to the item.

2. In the dialog, check the <UiPath path="campaigns.calendar.dayAndTime" />. The time starts as the campaign's, and
   the line under the fields names the time zone. For a campaign that goes out at one time of day in each recipient's
   time zone, the time is that local time.

3. Select the button that names the new day and time. To change more than the time, select
   <UiPath path="campaigns.calendar.openReview" /> instead.

</Steps>

The new time must be at least a minute from now. Each move is recorded in the [audit log](/team/audit-log/) as
`campaign.rescheduled`, with the old and the new time. What else happens depends on the channel:

- **Email.** [Send Readiness](/campaigns/send-readiness/) runs again before the send, as for any scheduled send, and
  holds it if something changed. See [the send-time recheck](/campaigns/schedule-and-send/#when-the-send-time-recheck-holds-the-send).
- **SMS.** Each queued text gets its new moment in its recipient's time zone, inside [quiet hours](/channels/sms/#quiet-hours).
  A local time outside the quiet-hours window is refused. Once texts have gone out, pause or cancel the send instead.
- **Push.** A push campaign goes out at one time for everyone; quiet hours still apply in each subscriber's time zone.
- **WhatsApp.** Messages are planned when the send starts, each in its recipient's time zone and quiet hours. A local
  time outside the quiet-hours window is refused.

Sent campaigns and digest issues can't be moved. A digest issue's time follows the digest's schedule: change it on the
[digest's page](/campaigns/recurring-digests/).

### When an approval covers the time

An [approval](/campaigns/approvals/) covers the time it was given for. Moving an approved campaign opens a dialog that
says so instead of the day and time. Select <UiPath path="campaigns.calendar.unscheduleAsk" />: the campaign is
unscheduled and its Review & send page opens, where you pick the new time and request approval again.

## Check that it worked

- The item sits on its new day with its new time, and a message confirms the move.
- Review & send shows the new time, and the audit log has a `campaign.rescheduled` entry.

## Troubleshooting

- **An item can't be dragged.** It is sent, sending or a digest issue, or your role lacks `campaigns:send`.
- **"Pick a time at least a minute from now."** The day is today and the time has passed, or is less than a minute
  away. Choose a later time.
- **"Only times inside 09:00–20:00 are offered: quiet hours hold texts outside it."** A text at a local time must be
  inside the quiet-hours window, shown in the message. Choose a time inside it.
- **"Texts already went out: pause or cancel the send instead."** The send has started; open its report.
- **"Only a scheduled campaign that hasn't started can be moved."** A push or WhatsApp send has started.
