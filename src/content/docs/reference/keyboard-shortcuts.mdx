This page lists the ReputeCast keyboard shortcuts in the app and in the [composer](/reference/glossary/#composer), and what the [command palette](/reference/glossary/#command-palette) finds. Press <kbd>?</kbd> anywhere in the app to see the shortcuts on screen.

- On Windows and Linux, press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> where the table shows <kbd>⌘</kbd>. The shortcut list the app shows for <kbd>?</kbd> uses the key for your system.
- Two keys joined by "then" are pressed one after the other, within 1.5 seconds: <kbd>G</kbd> then <kbd>C</kbd> opens <UiPath path="nav.campaigns" />.
- Single-key shortcuts don't fire while you type in a field or editor, while a dialog is open, or with <kbd>Alt</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> or <kbd>⌘</kbd> held. <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>, <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> and <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> also work while you type.
- <kbd>J</kbd>, <kbd>K</kbd> and <kbd>E</kbd> work only in <UiPath path="nav.audience > replies.tab" />, for people who can read replies; the list for <kbd>?</kbd> shows them in a section of their own. See [read and handle replies](/audience/replies/). Available from the next release after 1.6.0.
- Every shortcut has a button that does the same thing; none is required.
- In the composer, <kbd>Enter</kbd> and <kbd>Esc</kbd> work on the canvas once you click the email or pick an element in <UiPath path="composer.canvas.tabLayers" />. Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>/</kbd> there to see the composer's list.

## Search with the command palette

<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows and Linux) opens the command palette from every page.

<Screenshot
  id="command-palette"
  alt="The command palette over Campaigns with digest typed: five campaigns with their status, date and recipients, a list with its subscribed contacts and a page, grouped by kind, and the keys to move, open and close at the bottom."
/>

- Before you type, it shows <UiPath path="shell.palette.recent" />, the last 8 results you opened in this workspace and browser, and <UiPath path="shell.palette.goTo" />: places your role can open, such as <UiPath path="campaigns.newCampaign" />, the sidebar's sections and the settings tabs. Typing filters the places too.
- From two characters on, one search finds campaigns, contacts, automations, lists, segments, signup forms and website pages by name, up to 5 of each kind. Contacts are found by address or name, and a full email address only matches itself. Archived campaigns and deleted lists aren't searched.
- <UiPath path="shell.palette.settings" /> finds single settings by the words their page shows, such as <UiPath path="workspace.retentionTitle" /> or <UiPath path="automations.complete.capTitle" />, and opens the settings tab that holds them.
- Since 1.6.0, typing "dark", "light", "theme" or "appearance" offers the themes you aren't using, under <UiPath path="appearance.title" />: <UiPath path="appearance.useDark" />, <UiPath path="appearance.useLight" /> and <UiPath path="appearance.useSystem" />. Picking one switches the theme at once and saves it on your account. See [choose the light or dark theme](/team/appearance/).
- Results show only what your role can open. A kind your role can't open isn't searched, a member limited to some lists finds only those lists and their contacts, and <UiPath path="shell.palette.recent" /> leaves out what your role can no longer open.
- A campaign opens where its status leads: the editor for a draft, the live monitor while it sends, the report once it's sent.
