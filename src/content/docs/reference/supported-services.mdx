This page lists the supported email providers and every other outside service ReputeCast connects to, one table per kind of service. Every connection uses your own account and keys with that service; a name links to its setup guide where one exists.

Automation tools (Zapier, Make, n8n) and WordPress connect through [integrations](/integrations/overview/), and [HostRepute](/deliverability/hostrepute/) adds reputation data. Neither is a provider, so they are not listed here, except for the Gmail Postmaster Tools data that HostRepute brings in (see [blocklists and reputation data](#blocklists-and-reputation-data)).

## Email sending providers

Email is sent through one or more [sending providers](/reference/glossary/#sending-provider). **Bulk marketing** says what the provider's terms allow for newsletters: _Allowed_, _Broadcast streams only_ (bulk mail must go through the provider's broadcast stream) or _Your own policy_ (your server or relay, your rules). **Event webhooks** is how the provider signs the delivery events it sends back. **Domain setup** says whether ReputeCast adds your sending domain through the provider's API or you add the DNS records yourself. **Suppression sync** says whether suppressions travel both ways, are only pulled from the provider, or stay in ReputeCast. [Choose a sending provider](/providers/choose/) compares them, and [provider adapters](/adapters/overview/) explains how to add a service that is not listed.

## SMS providers

SMS campaigns and automation steps send through one of these accounts. ReputeCast handles keyword replies such as STOP and HELP; it has no two-way SMS inbox. See [send SMS campaigns](/channels/sms/).

<ProviderMatrix family="sms" label="SMS providers" />

## Rendering services

Real renders show the saved campaign in real email clients, next to the composer's built-in previews, which are approximations. A workspace connects one service with its own key; people who manage integrations connect it. See [design emails in the composer](/campaigns/composer/).

<ProviderMatrix family="rendering" label="Rendering services" />

## Email verification services

Address checks for bulk verification, the import preview and signup forms. See [verify email addresses](/audience/verification/).

## AI models

The AI assistant uses the workspace's own model and key. See [use the AI assistant with your own model](/integrations/ai-assistant/).

<ProviderMatrix family="ai" label="AI models" />

## Payments

Paid subscriptions, gifts and digital products are sold through the publisher's own Stripe account; ReputeCast takes no fee and never stores card details. Paddle and Lemon Squeezy appear in the app as not available; you can't connect them. See [connect Stripe](/earn/connect-stripe/).

<ProviderMatrix family="payments" label="Payment providers" />

## Stores

ReputeCast imports orders and products from these store platforms and adds only customers who agreed to marketing to your lists. See [connect Shopify or WooCommerce](/integrations/shopify-woocommerce/).

<ProviderMatrix family="stores" label="Store platforms" />

## Web push

Browser notifications are built in and need no outside account: they use the Web Push standard with VAPID keys generated for each workspace, and each browser delivers them through its own push service. See [send web push notifications](/channels/web-push/).

<ProviderMatrix family="push" label="Web push" />

## WhatsApp

Messages go through Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API with your own business account. See [send WhatsApp messages](/channels/whatsapp/).

<ProviderMatrix family="whatsapp" label="WhatsApp" />

## Blocklists and reputation data

Send Readiness and the composer look up the domains of your links on these lists, and the 72-hour complaint watch after a send can read Gmail's spam rate. See [Send Readiness](/campaigns/send-readiness/) and [blocklists](/deliverability/blocklists/).

<ProviderMatrix family="lookups" label="Blocklists and reputation data" />

## Seed mailboxes

Placement tests send to mailboxes you own at these mailbox providers and read where each copy landed. See [test inbox placement with seed mailboxes](/deliverability/placement-tests/).

<ProviderMatrix family="seed" label="Seed mailboxes" />
