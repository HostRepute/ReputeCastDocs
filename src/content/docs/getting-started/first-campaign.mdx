In this tutorial you send a newsletter from start to finish: you import your contacts from a CSV file, write the email,
let [Send Readiness](/reference/glossary/#send-readiness) check it, schedule it, watch it go out and read its report. It
takes about 20 minutes, plus the time your email takes to write.

## Before you begin

- ReputeCast is running and you finished the first setup. If not, follow the
  [quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart/).
- A sending provider carries the Broadcasts stream in your workspace. Read
  [Connect a sending provider](/providers/connect/).
- Your sending domain has SPF, DKIM and DMARC records, and a sender (the From address) exists in
  <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.senders" />. Read [Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC](/deliverability/spf-dkim-dmarc/)
  and [Add sending domains and senders](/providers/senders-and-domains/).
- A CSV or XLSX file of people who agreed to get your newsletter. The first row holds the column names, and one column
  holds the email address.
- Your role includes `subscribers:import`, `campaigns:edit` and `campaigns:send`. Owners and Admins have them; an Editor
  has all three too.

## Send the newsletter

<Steps>

1. **Import your contacts.** Select <UiPath path="shell.importContacts" /> at the bottom of the sidebar and choose
   <UiPath path="imports.fileSource" /> as the source. Drop your file, pick the list the contacts go into, then select
   <UiPath path="imports.continueFields" />.

   <Screenshot
     id="import-source"
     alt="The first import step lists the sources: Brevo, Mailchimp, MailerLite, listmonk, Keila, Notifuse and CSV or XLSX."
   />

   Work through the next steps:

   - **Map fields**: check that the email column maps to Email and the other columns to the right fields.
   - **Consent**: say how consent carries over. If the list is old or people joined without confirming, choose
     <UiPath path="imports.repermission" />: everyone gets one confirmation email first. Unsubscribed, bounced and
     complained contacts become suppressions and are never subscribed again.
   - **Preview**: a dry run shows how many contacts are new, merged, suppressed, invalid or risky. Nothing is written
     until you start the import.

   Read [Import contacts from CSV or XLSX](/audience/import/) for every option.

2. **Create the campaign.** Open <UiPath path="nav.campaigns" /> and select <UiPath path="campaigns.newCampaign" />.
   Choose <UiPath path="digests.regular" />, one email sent once, and continue. The composer opens on the template
   gallery; pick a template or start blank.

   <Screenshot
     id="campaigns-list"
     alt="The Campaigns list with the New campaign button at the top right, delivery numbers for the last 30 days and each campaign's status."
   />

3. **Write the email.** At the top of the composer, name the campaign, then use
   <UiPath path="composer.chooseAudience" /> to pick the list you imported and <UiPath path="composer.chooseSender" /> to
   pick your sender. Fill in the <UiPath path="composer.subject" /> and the <UiPath path="composer.preheader" />, then
   edit the blocks on the canvas. Drafts save automatically.

   <Screenshot
     id="composer"
     alt="The composer with blocks on the left, the email on the canvas in the middle, the block's style settings on the right and the Send test and Review and send buttons at the top."
   >
     1. Blocks and layers: drag blocks onto the email.
     2. The canvas: select a block to edit it.
     3. The inspector: style and settings of the selected block.
     4. Assist, Preview, <UiPath path="composer.sendTest" /> and <UiPath path="composer.reviewSend" />.
   </Screenshot>

   Select <UiPath path="composer.sendTest" /> to get a copy in your own inbox and read it there. Personalize the email
   with tokens such as `{{ first_name | default: "there" }}`; read
   [Personalize emails with Liquid](/campaigns/personalization/).

4. **Check it with Send Readiness.** Select <UiPath path="composer.reviewSend" />. Send Readiness checks reputation,
   authentication, content, languages, accessibility, conditional content, links, compliance, audience quality and
   routing, then gives a verdict: Ready, Ready with warnings or Blocked.

   <Screenshot
     id="review-send"
     alt="Review and send with the verdict at the top, the check groups on the left and the audience, route and send settings on the right."
   >
     1. The verdict and what it means for this send.
     2. The check groups, each with its result and evidence.
     3. The audience: final recipients after suppressions, the route, cost and duration.
     4. The actions for this campaign.
   </Screenshot>

   Open each Warn or Block result to see why it fired. Many results offer a fix you can apply from the check, such as
   adding the footer with your postal address; the checks then run again. You can't send while a check blocks. Read
   [Send Readiness: check before you send](/campaigns/send-readiness/).

5. **Schedule the send.** Under scheduling, choose <UiPath path="review.sendNowButton" />,
   <UiPath path="review.schedule" /> (a date and time in any time zone) or <UiPath path="review.modeBest" />. Select
   <UiPath path="review.actionSchedule" /> (or <UiPath path="review.sendNowButton" />) and confirm the warnings you
   accept. For 1,000 recipients or more, ReputeCast asks you to <UiPath path="review.typeToConfirm" />, because a large
   send can't be recalled once it leaves the queue.

   Send Readiness runs again 15 minutes before a scheduled send and when the send starts; a Block at that point holds
   the send.

6. **Watch it go out.** When the send starts, open the <UiPath path="campaigns.liveMonitor" /> from the campaign.

   <Screenshot
     id="live-monitor"
     alt="The live monitor of a send at 64%: delivered, bounced, complained, opened and clicked counts, a table per mailbox provider and the auto-pause thresholds."
   />

   The monitor shows progress per sending provider and results per mailbox provider. If bounces or complaints cross a
   threshold, ReputeCast pauses the affected route and tells you what tripped; you decide whether to resume or cancel.
   Read [Monitor a send and auto-pause](/campaigns/live-monitor/).

7. **Read the report.** When the send is done, open the campaign's <UiPath path="campaigns.report" />.

   <Screenshot
     id="report"
     alt="A campaign report: headline numbers, engagement over the first 72 hours, a click map on the email and results per mailbox provider and per sending provider."
   >
     1. Engagement over the first 72 hours, human opens apart from machine opens.
     2. The click map: the share of clicks on each link of the email.
     3. Results per mailbox provider.
     4. Results per sending provider, side by side.
   </Screenshot>

   Results keep coming in for 72 hours after the send; [Read campaign reports](/campaigns/reports/) explains each part.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
  Contacts who never open drag down your sender reputation. Once you have sent a few campaigns, read [Keep your list
  healthy](/audience/list-health/) to set a sunset policy.
</Aside>

## What you built

You imported a list with its consent intact, wrote and tested an email, passed Send Readiness, sent the campaign with
guarded sending watching every mailbox provider and sending provider, and read a report that separates human opens from
machine opens.
