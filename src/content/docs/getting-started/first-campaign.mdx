In this tutorial you send a newsletter from start to finish: you import your contacts from a CSV file, write the email,
let [Send Readiness](/reference/glossary/#send-readiness) check it, schedule it, watch it go out and read its report. It
takes about 20 minutes, plus the time your email takes to write.

## Before you begin

- ReputeCast is running and you finished the first setup. If not, follow the
  [quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart/).
- A sending provider carries the Broadcasts stream in your workspace. Read
  [Connect a sending provider](/providers/connect/).
- Your sending domain has SPF, DKIM and DMARC records, and a sender (the From address) exists in
  <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.senders" />. Read [Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC](/deliverability/spf-dkim-dmarc/)
  and [Add sending domains and senders](/providers/senders-and-domains/).
- A CSV or XLSX file of people who agreed to get your newsletter. The first row holds the column names, and one column
  holds the email address.
- Your role includes `subscribers:import`, `campaigns:edit` and `campaigns:send`. Owners and Admins have them; an Editor
  has all three too.

## Send the newsletter

<Steps>

1. **Import your contacts.** Select <UiPath path="shell.importContacts" /> at the bottom of the sidebar and choose
   <UiPath path="imports.fileSource" /> as the source. Drop your file, pick the list the contacts go into, then select
   <UiPath path="imports.continueFields" />.

   <Screenshot
     id="import-source"
     alt="The first import step lists the sources: Brevo, Mailchimp, MailerLite, listmonk, Keila, Notifuse and CSV or XLSX."
   />

   Work through the next steps:

   - **Map fields**: check that the email column maps to Email and the other columns to the right fields.
   - **Consent**: say how consent carries over. If the list is old or people joined without confirming, choose
     <UiPath path="imports.repermission" />: everyone gets one confirmation email first. Unsubscribed, bounced and
     complained contacts become suppressions and are never subscribed again.
   - **Preview**: a dry run shows how many contacts are new, merged, suppressed, invalid or risky. Nothing is written
     until you start the import.

   Read [Import contacts from CSV or XLSX](/audience/import/) for every option.

2. **Create the campaign.** Open <UiPath path="nav.campaigns" /> and select <UiPath path="campaigns.newCampaign" />.
   Choose <UiPath path="digests.regular" />, one email sent once, and continue. The composer opens on the template
   gallery; pick a template or start blank.

   <Screenshot
     id="campaigns-list"
     alt="The Campaigns list with the New campaign button at the top right, delivery numbers for the last 30 days and each campaign's status."
   />

3. **Write the email.** At the top of the composer, name the campaign, then use
   <UiPath path="composer.chooseAudience" /> to pick the list you imported and <UiPath path="composer.chooseSender" /> to
   pick your sender. Fill in the <UiPath path="composer.subject" /> and the <UiPath path="composer.preheader" />, then
   edit the blocks on the canvas. Drafts save automatically.

   <Screenshot
     id="composer"
     alt="The composer in Design mode: blocks on the left, the email on the canvas in the middle, the selected block's Style panel on the right, and the Write, Design and Code switch and Review and send at the top."
   >
     1. The left panel: blocks to drag onto the email, your saved and synced blocks, layers and templates.
     2. The canvas: select a block to edit it.
     3. The inspector: style and settings of the selected block, and the <UiPath path="composer.chrome.tabChecks" />
        tab.
     4. The mode switch: <UiPath path="composer.chrome.modeWrite" />, <UiPath path="composer.chrome.modeDesign" /> and
        <UiPath path="composer.chrome.modeCode" />.
     5. <UiPath path="composer.reviewSend" />, with <UiPath path="composer.preview" /> and
        <UiPath path="composer.sendTest" /> to its left.
   </Screenshot>

   Select <UiPath path="composer.sendTest" /> to get a copy in your own inbox and read it there. Personalize the email
   with tokens such as `{{ first_name | default: "there" }}`; read
   [Personalize emails with Liquid](/campaigns/personalization/).

4. **Check it with Send Readiness.** Select <UiPath path="composer.reviewSend" />. Send Readiness checks reputation,
   authentication, content, languages, accessibility, conditional content, links, compliance, audience quality, routing
   and your workspace's own sending rules, showing each group's progress as it runs, then gives a verdict: Ready, Ready
   with warnings or Blocked.

   <Screenshot
     id="review-send"
     alt="Review & send for Weekly Digest #142, scheduled for Sunday 1 Nov at 09:00 New York time to 31,192 recipients via SES and Postal, with tiles for the time left, the A/B test, auto-pause and who scheduled it. Needs attention · 2 lists a workspace rule about UTM tags and a Postal IP listed on UCEPROTECT, each with its fixes and what they change; on the right, the audience leaves out 412 unengaged contacts, with Include them, and the route breakdown follows."
   >
     1. The status banner: the verdict, or once scheduled, when it goes out and when the checks run again.
     2. Needs attention: each warning or block in one sentence, with its fix and <UiPath path="review.technicalDetails" />.
     3. The audience: the final recipients once suppressions, role addresses, duplicates and unengaged contacts are taken
        out.
     4. The banner's actions for a scheduled send.
   </Screenshot>

   The issues come first; passed checks fold into one line per group below them. Many issues offer a fix you can apply
   from the row, such as adding the footer with your postal address, and a line under the button says what it changes;
   the checks then run again, and you can undo the fixes. You can't send while a check blocks. Contacts who haven't
   engaged for a long time are left out of the audience by default. Read
   [Send Readiness: check before you send](/campaigns/send-readiness/).

5. **Schedule the send.** Under <UiPath path="review.scheduling" />, choose <UiPath path="review.modeNow" />,
   <UiPath path="review.modeAt" /> (a date and time in any time zone) or <UiPath path="review.modeBest" />. The main
   action then states what it will do, such as "Schedule for Sun 1 Nov, 09:00 ET". Select it, tick the warnings you
   accept and leave the <UiPath path="review.canaryLabel" /> on <UiPath path="review.canaryAuto" />. Because this is your
   first send to the list, ReputeCast asks you to <UiPath path="review.typeToConfirm" />: it asks only for unusual sends,
   such as a first send to a list, from a sending domain or through a sending provider. Then select
   <UiPath path="review.actionSchedule" /> (or <UiPath path="review.sendNowButton" />).

   Send Readiness runs again 15 minutes before a scheduled send and when the send starts. A new Block, a warning you
   didn't confirm, an audience that changed by more than 10% or an edit after scheduling holds the send until you review
   it again. Read [Schedule and send a campaign](/campaigns/schedule-and-send/).

6. **Watch it go out.** When the send starts, open the <UiPath path="campaigns.liveMonitor" /> from the campaign.

   <Screenshot
     id="live-monitor"
     alt="The live monitor of a send in progress: delivered, bounced, complained, opened and clicked counts, a table per mailbox provider and the auto-pause thresholds."
   />

   The monitor shows progress per sending provider and results per mailbox provider. When something about the send is
   new, such as a new sending domain, it may start with a canary: your most engaged contacts first, the rest once their
   results look healthy. If bounces or complaints cross a threshold, ReputeCast pauses the affected route and tells you
   what tripped; you decide whether to resume or cancel.
   Read [Monitor a send and auto-pause](/campaigns/live-monitor/).

7. **Read the report.** When the send is done, open the campaign's <UiPath path="campaigns.report" />.

   <Screenshot
     id="report"
     alt="A campaign report: headline tiles with trend lines, insights, the engagement chart, a click map on the email, results per mailbox provider and per sending provider, and Share this report at the end."
   >
     1. The headline tiles, each with a trend line and the change against your last issues.
     2. <UiPath path="reports.insightsTitle" />: what stands out in the numbers, each with an action.
     3. <UiPath path="reports.clickMap" />: human clicks on each link of the email, with a switch to
        <UiPath path="reports.colBotClicks" />.
     4. <UiPath path="reports.mailboxTitle" />: complaints read n/a for Gmail and iCloud, which don't report them.
     5. <UiPath path="reports.shareTitle" />: read-only links that expire.
   </Screenshot>

   Results keep coming in for 72 hours after the send; [Read campaign reports](/campaigns/reports/) explains each part.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
  Contacts who never open drag down your sender reputation. Once you have sent a few campaigns, read [Keep your list
  healthy](/audience/list-health/) to set a sunset policy.
</Aside>

## What you built

You imported a list with its consent intact, wrote and tested an email, passed Send Readiness, sent the campaign with
guarded sending watching every mailbox provider and sending provider, and read a report that separates human opens from
privacy-protected and machine opens.
