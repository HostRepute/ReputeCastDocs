To import newsletter posts from Substack, Ghost or WordPress, upload the platform's export to your
[website](/forms/website/): ReputeCast reads it, shows you every post to review, then imports them in the background
with their original dates and images, and emails nobody. Their old addresses keep working on your custom domain.
Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

## Before you begin

- The [website](/forms/website/) is on. While it is off, an upload is refused: imported posts would have nowhere to
  go.
- You need the `website:publish` permission. Owners, Admins and Editors have it. Only people can import posts, not
  API keys.
- If your workspace [requires approval to publish posts](/forms/website/#require-approval-to-publish) and you would
  need one, import the posts as drafts, or ask someone who can publish without approval.
- To map paid posts to your own tiers, [create the tiers](/earn/paid-subscriptions/) first.
- Export the posts from the old platform. The upload step names where each export is too:

  | Platform  | Where the export is                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Upload                       |
  | --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
  | Substack  | **Settings** › **Exports** › **Create new export** builds a zip with your posts. Source: [Substack, How do I export my posts?](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037466012-How-do-I-export-my-posts)                         | The `.zip` as it is          |
  | Ghost     | **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Import/Export**, the **Export** tab, **All data**: keep **Content & settings** and select **Export** for one zip. Source: [Ghost, Exports](https://ghost.org/help/exports/)                                   | The `.zip`, or its `.json`   |
  | WordPress | **Tools** › **Export**, **All content**, then **Download Export File** saves an XML (WXR) file. Source: [WordPress, Tools Export Screen](https://wordpress.org/documentation/article/tools-export-screen/)                                  | The `.xml` file              |

  Substack's source checked October 2026, Ghost's and WordPress's November 2026.

This import brings posts only: subscribers stay where they are. Bring them with the
[contact importer](/migrate/overview/) or a [CSV file](/audience/import/), and paid members with
[import paid members](/migrate/paid-members/).

## Upload the export

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.forms > forms.tabs.website > website.viewPosts" /> and select
   <UiPath path="website.import.menuImport > website.import.menuFrom" />.
2. Drop the file on the page, or select <UiPath path="website.import.choose" />. It can be up to 100 MB. ReputeCast
   tells the platform from the file's content, never from its name.
3. ReputeCast reads the export in the background, usually in under a minute. You can leave the page: the jobs tray
   says when the review is ready, and the import waits for you.

</Steps>

Nothing goes live and nothing is emailed until you start the import.

The review shows what the export holds, then a card for each choice. Every change is saved as you make it.

- <UiPath path="website.import.datesTitle" />: <UiPath path="website.import.keepDates" /> (the default) puts each
  post live on your site at its original date, so the archive keeps its order. A date still in the future publishes
  the post when the import reaches it. <UiPath path="website.import.asDrafts" /> leaves every post in
  <UiPath path="website.viewPosts" /> until someone publishes it.
- <UiPath path="website.import.imagesTitle" />: <UiPath path="website.import.copyImages" /> (the default) stores
  the images and covers with your media, so they keep working after the old site goes away.
  <UiPath path="website.import.linkImages" /> leaves the posts loading their images from the old site; only images
  without an address of their own (those inside a Ghost `.zip`, and images embedded in the HTML) are copied then.
- <UiPath path="website.import.tiersTitle" />: each old tier maps to one of your paid tiers, or to
  <UiPath path="website.import.anyTier" />. ReputeCast suggests a tier by name.
- <UiPath path="website.import.authorsTitle" />: each author maps to a member of your team, whose byline and profile
  the posts get, or to <UiPath path="website.import.keepName" />, which writes the name as it was. Without authors
  in the export, posts get the byline of whoever imports them.

Below the cards, every post in the export is listed with its date, visibility, tags, problems and
<UiPath path="website.import.colDecision" />: <UiPath path="website.import.import" />,
<UiPath path="website.import.skip" /> or, for a post imported before, <UiPath path="website.import.update" />.
Published posts start as <UiPath path="website.import.import" /> and drafts as <UiPath path="website.import.skip" />.

### Posts imported before

ReputeCast remembers each post's ID on its platform. A post that an earlier import brought in is marked
<UiPath path="website.import.problem.already_imported" /> and starts as <UiPath path="website.import.skip" />.
<UiPath path="website.import.update" /> imports it again over the earlier post: its title, excerpt, cover, byline,
visibility, tiers, SEO fields and content change, and a published post shows the new version at once. Its address
and tags stay as they are.

### Problems the review finds

A post with a problem still imports as shown, and the report lists each problem:

- <UiPath path="website.import.problem.embed_link" />: a YouTube, Vimeo, X, Instagram or Spotify embed, or a
  Substack or Ghost card, becomes a paragraph that links to it.
- <UiPath path="website.import.problem.image_alt" />: add one in the composer after the import.
- <UiPath path="website.import.problem.image_failed" />: the image keeps loading from the old site, or is left out
  when the old site can't serve it.
- <UiPath path="website.import.problem.shortcode" />: WordPress code such as `[gallery]` that the site can't run.
- <UiPath path="website.import.problem.slug_taken" />: another post has the address, so the post gets a free one,
  such as one ending in `-2`.
- <UiPath path="website.import.problem.extra_tags" />: the first 5 are kept.
- <UiPath path="website.import.problem.convert_failed" />: the post fails; the others import.

## Run the import

<Steps>

1. At the bottom of the review, start the import. The button counts the posts it imports.
2. The import runs in the background, a post at a time, and shows how far it got. You can leave the page; the jobs
   tray follows it. <UiPath path="website.import.cancelImport" /> stops it after the post it's on, and the posts
   imported so far stay.
3. When it ends, whoever started it gets a notification, and the page shows the totals: published, drafts, updated,
   skipped and failed posts, images copied, problems and old addresses. Select
   <UiPath path="website.import.downloadReport" /> for each post's title, old address, outcome and problems, or
   <UiPath path="website.import.seePosts" />.

</Steps>

What the run does with each post:

- The post's HTML becomes the content of a web post: text, headings, lists, quotes, code, tables and captioned
  figures in text blocks, then images, dividers and buttons. A paywall marker (Substack's paywall jump, Ghost's
  members-only marker) becomes the [free preview](/forms/website/#write-a-web-only-post) marker. Liquid in the text
  stays text.
- A post's images are copied four at a time, and only from public addresses.
- Tags the site hasn't got are created, and the post's old addresses are recorded for
  [redirects](#old-addresses-keep-working).
- Published posts go live without an email, without `post.published` webhooks and without an audit entry each.
  One `website.posts_imported` entry in the [audit log](/team/audit-log/) records the import; uploading, changing the
  review, starting and canceling it are audited too.

If the run keeps failing, it stops, and the posts before it stay on the site.
<UiPath path="website.import.tryAgain" /> carries on after the last post it finished. Once the run ends, the
uploaded file is deleted; the report is kept for 30 days.

## Old addresses keep working

On your site's verified [custom domain](/forms/branding-and-domain/), the addresses the old site had answer 301
(moved permanently) to the imported posts:

- Substack's `/p/<slug>`, Ghost's `/<slug>/`, and WordPress permalinks and `/?p=<id>` links lead to the post.
- `/posts/<slug>/` leads to `/posts/<slug>`.
- The old feeds (`/feed/`, `/rss`, `/rss/`, `/feed.xml`, `/rss.xml`, `/feed/rss2`, `/feed/atom`) lead to `/feed`.
- A landing page with the same address wins over an old address. Any other path nothing answers gets the site's own
  404 page.

To keep the old links, make the old site's domain your custom domain in
<UiPath path="nav.forms > forms.tabs.branding" />. Addresses on the platform's own domain, such as
`<name>.substack.com`, stay with the platform.

<Aside type="tip">
  Import the posts before you move the domain. The redirects start as soon as the domain checks out.
</Aside>

## Check that it worked

1. In <UiPath path="website.viewPosts" />, the imported posts show with their original dates.
2. Open an old address on your custom domain, such as `https://news.acme.io/p/why-we-moved-our-queue-to-postgres`.
   It redirects to the post under `/posts/`.
3. Open a paid post as a visitor: the paywall card follows the free part, which ends where the old post had its
   paywall marker.

## Troubleshooting

- **"This isn't a Substack, Ghost or WordPress export"**: upload the file as the platform exported it: Substack's
  `.zip`, Ghost's `.json` or `.zip`, or WordPress's `.xml`.
- **The file is larger than 100 MB**: export the posts without media (Ghost's export can leave media files out;
  WordPress can export one date range at a time). Images are copied from the old site during the import.
- **"This export has more than 10,000 posts."**: export fewer at a time and import each file.
- **"ReputeCast couldn't read this … export"**: the file is damaged or cut short. Download a fresh export.
- **"Publishing posts needs approval in this workspace."**: choose <UiPath path="website.import.asDrafts" />, or ask
  someone who can publish without approval.
- **An old address shows the 404 page**: the post was skipped or failed, the request didn't come to your custom
  domain, or the address isn't one the export listed for the post.
