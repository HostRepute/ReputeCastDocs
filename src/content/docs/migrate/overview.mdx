When you migrate newsletter platform data to ReputeCast, three importers do the work: one for contacts and their [consent](/reference/glossary/#consent), one for paid members, and one for transactional templates. All three only read from the old platform. Nothing is written to it, and an import can never turn an unsubscribe, a bounce or a complaint back into a subscription.

## How a contact import carries consent

The wizard has five steps: <UiPath path="imports.stepSource" />, <UiPath path="imports.stepFields" />, <UiPath path="imports.stepConsent" />, <UiPath path="imports.stepPreview" /> and <UiPath path="imports.stepImport" />. A draft is saved as you go, so you can leave and come back. Along the way, this happens:

1. **Connect the source.** You paste an API key (and, for listmonk, Keila and Notifuse, the server address). ReputeCast checks it, stores it encrypted, and never shows it again. Server addresses on a private or internal network are refused unless the instance sets `providers.allow_private_networks`.
2. **Choose what to import.** Each source list goes into an existing ReputeCast [list](/reference/glossary/#list) or a new one. Options: import tags, import what the source knows about engagement, and pull existing suppressions from your connected sending providers first. All three are on by default.
3. **ReputeCast reads every row** of the chosen lists in the background, then guesses a ReputeCast field for each source field. Unmapped fields are skipped; they stay in the source.
4. **Map consent.** Every source status becomes a ReputeCast status, as in the table below. Unsubscribed, bounced and complained contacts are locked to the [suppression list](/reference/glossary/#suppression-list): no choice in the wizard can import them as subscribed.
5. **Preview.** A dry run checks every row against your contacts and the suppression list and sorts out duplicates, invalid addresses and risky groups (role, disposable, likely typo, trap-like). Nothing is written yet.
6. **Import.** Rows go in by batches with a live log. When the rows are in, ReputeCast pushes every suppression the import created, unsubscribes included, to each connected sending provider whose suppression list accepts new entries (the log names them), and sends re-permission emails if you chose them. From then on, the regular [suppression sync](/providers/events-and-sync/) pushes only hard bounces and complaints that apply to all mail.

| Status in the source                     | Becomes in ReputeCast                                                                  | Effect                                                    |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Subscribed                               | Subscribed · confirmed (default), Subscribed · pending with a re-permission email, or Skip | Confirmed contacts get Broadcasts and Automations at once |
| Pending (never confirmed double opt-in)  | Subscribed · pending (default) or Skip                                                 | No marketing mail until they confirm                      |
| Transactional only (Mailchimp)           | Skip (default) or Subscribed · pending                                                 | Not imported by default                                   |
| Unsubscribed                             | Suppressed · unsubscribed (locked)                                                     | Broadcasts and Automations blocked; System mail allowed   |
| Hard bounced (cleaned, bounced, unreachable) | Suppressed · hard bounce (locked)                                                  | All streams blocked                                       |
| Complained (spam report)                 | Suppressed · complaint (locked)                                                        | All streams blocked                                       |

A suppression belongs to the address, not to one list: a contact who unsubscribed from one source list gets no Broadcasts or Automations on any ReputeCast list. Each source guide lists the exact source statuses behind these rows.

### Consent evidence and re-permission

By default the source's opt-in is kept as consent evidence: the opt-in date and IP address where the source has them (Mailchimp's `OPTIN_TIME` and `OPTIN_IP`, MailerLite's opt-in date and IP, Notifuse's subscription date). With <UiPath path="imports.repermission" />, every subscribed contact is imported as pending instead and gets one confirmation email through the System [stream](/reference/glossary/#stream) when the import finishes. Without a provider on the System stream, no email goes out and the contacts stay pending, so connect one before you import.

Pending contacts get no marketing mail until they confirm. ReputeCast does not suppress the ones who never answer by itself; to remove them after a while, build an [automation](/automations/overview/) with a delay and a step that unsubscribes them.

Each imported contact also gets an entry in its consent history, such as "Imported from Mailchimp by Linh Nguyen, 12 Oct 2026, status subscribed, evidence OPTIN_TIME 2025-03-02".

## What never moves

- **A suppressed address stays suppressed.** If an address is already on the ReputeCast suppression list, the row is skipped with the reason <UiPath path="imports.reasonAlready" />, whatever the source says.
- **Sending history.** Past campaigns, their reports, and per-message opens, clicks and bounces stay in the old platform. Export them there if you need a record.
- **Automations, segments, forms and campaign designs.** Rebuild them with [automations](/automations/overview/), [segments](/audience/segments/), [signup forms](/forms/signup-forms/) and the [composer](/campaigns/composer/). Notifuse is the one source whose email templates come along with its contacts.
- **Sending setup.** Domains, senders and provider connections are set up in ReputeCast itself.
- **Card details.** Paid members keep paying through Stripe; ReputeCast stores no card data and changes nothing in Stripe.

## A migration checklist

1. [Add your sending domains and senders](/providers/senders-and-domains/), then [set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC](/deliverability/spf-dkim-dmarc/) for them.
2. [Connect a sending provider](/providers/connect/). If the old platform is also your sending provider (Brevo, Mailgun, SendGrid and others), you can keep it connected while you move.
3. Import contacts with suppressions pulled from your providers first, so addresses a provider already blocks stay suppressed in ReputeCast.
4. [Warm up](/providers/warm-up/) any new provider or dedicated IP address.
5. Move traffic step by step with a [weighted split](/providers/routing/), for example 90% on the provider you know and 10% on the new one.
6. Send the first campaign to the Active [engagement tier](/audience/list-health/) and watch [Send Readiness](/campaigns/send-readiness/) and the [live monitor](/campaigns/live-monitor/).
7. Stop the old platform from sending (and, for paid members, from charging) once everything runs in ReputeCast.

## When to change them

- **Re-permission instead of trusting the source** when the list is old, used single opt-in, or you can't tell how people joined.
- **Slow down to 3 requests a second** when the source keeps answering 429 Too Many Requests: the paused import offers <UiPath path="imports.slowDown" />.
- **Overwrite instead of fill** when the source is more current than ReputeCast for contacts you already have. The preview's default, <UiPath path="imports.conflictFill" />, never replaces a value you have.
- **Allow private networks** (`providers.allow_private_networks`, see the [configuration reference](/reference/configuration/)) when listmonk, Keila or Notifuse runs on your own network.
