Earn's Overview reports newsletter MRR, churn, ARR, paid members and free-to-paid conversion for the last 30 days, 90
days or 12 months. Every number has its definition in a tooltip; this page collects them. All amounts are in your
Stripe currency, and test-mode payments are left out.

## How each number is calculated

1. **MRR** is the monthly value of every Active and Past due subscription, after discounts and before tax. A yearly
   price counts as a twelfth. Trials, free periods, complimentary and gift access, gift sales, sponsors, conversions
   and test mode are left out.
2. **ARR** is MRR × 12.
3. **Paid members** are the Active and Past due subscriptions, the paying ones. Trialing, complimentary and gift
   access are counted apart ([Manage paid members](/earn/paid-members/)).
4. **Churn rate** is the paid members whose access ended in the period ÷ the paid members at its start. A scheduled
   cancellation counts when access ends, not when the reader asks.
5. **Free-to-paid conversion** is the free subscribers who became paid members in the period ÷ (free subscribers at
   the start + those who joined during it). New contacts who paid straight away are counted apart.
6. **MRR movement** splits the change in MRR:
   - **New**: first payments.
   - **Expansion**: a higher tier or price.
   - **Contraction**: a lower price, a discount starting, or a switch from monthly to yearly.
   - **Churned**: access ended.
   - **Reactivated**: paid again within 90 days of access ending.

Gifts, [sponsorships](/earn/sponsors/) and [digital products](/earn/digital-products/) appear as other income, never
in MRR.

## How upgrades are credited

An upgrade is credited to the last campaign or automation email the reader clicked within the attribution window
(7 days by default) before checkout. Without such a click, it is credited to how the reader reached the upgrade page.
Each campaign report then shows the revenue it brought in ([Read campaign reports](/campaigns/reports/)).

Your own product or shop can report conversions too: upgrades and orders sent through the conversion API with the
`conversions:create` permission. ReputeCast adds an opaque `rc_click` token to links that point to the domains you
list, so your product can pass it back with the conversion and the conversion is credited to the email that brought
the reader. Conversions are shown under **Product conversions** and are not in MRR. See
[Record events and conversions](/developers/events-and-conversions/).

## When to change them

- Lengthen the attribution window (1–30 days) in
  <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabSettings > earn.settings.tabAttribution" /> when readers usually take longer than a
  week between reading and paying; shorten it if you send daily and want credit to go to the latest issue.
- The movement categories, churn and MRR definitions are fixed, so numbers stay comparable over time.
