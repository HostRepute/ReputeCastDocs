With sponsor self-booking, [sponsors](/reference/glossary/#sponsor) find your public media kit, request an open issue
with their creative, and pay through Stripe Checkout once you approve the request. You review every request; nothing
is booked or charged before you do. Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

## Before you begin

- [Connect Stripe](/earn/connect-stripe/). Sponsors pay through it, so self-serve booking can't be turned on without a
  payment connection. Bookings you make yourself keep working without Stripe ([Sell sponsorships](/earn/sponsors/)).
- You need `sponsors:manage` to turn self-serve booking on and to approve or decline requests. Only people can do
  this, never API keys or MCP agents, because an approval asks a sponsor for money. Seeing requests needs
  `earn:view` ([Permissions and roles](/reference/permissions/)).
- Have a slot with a price above 0 on the list that carries the sponsorship.

## Turn on self-serve booking

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabSponsors > earn.sponsorRequests.tab" />. The
   <UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.ssTitle" /> card is under the requests.
2. Turn on the switch. It saves at once and publishes the media kit at your public pages' `/sponsor`, on your own
   domain once it is verified. Without a payment connection the switch goes back to off and the card says
   <UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.ssNoStripeTitle" />.

   <Screenshot
     id="earn-sponsor-selfserve"
     alt="The Self-serve booking card, switched on: the media kit page news.acme.io/sponsor with Copy and Open, the three numbers it shows (32,000 subscribers, a 40.5% average open rate and a 5.9% click rate for the Weekly Digest), the About and Audience texts, the contact email sponsors@acme.io, and the self-serve slot Main sponsor at $750 an issue, book at least 7 days ahead, pay within 3 days of approval."
   >
     1. The switch.
     2. The media kit's address.
     3. The numbers the media kit shows.
     4. The slots sponsors can request.
   </Screenshot>

3. Under <UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.numbers" />, tick what the media kit shows for the slot's list over the
   last 90 days: subscribers rounded to two significant digits, the average human open rate and the click rate.
4. Write <UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.about" /> (up to 600 characters) and
   <UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.audience" /> (up to 300), and set the
   <UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.contact" /> sponsors write to with questions. Left empty, the media kit uses
   Earn's support address. Select <UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.save" />.
5. Select <UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.editSlot" /> on a slot, or open it in
   <UiPath path="earn.sponsors.tabSlots" />, and turn on <UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.slotSelfServe" />. Write
   its <UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.slotDescription" /> (up to 300 characters, shown on the media kit) and set
   <UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.slotLead" /> (0–60 days ahead, 7 by default) and
   <UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.slotPay" /> (1–14 days of approval, 3 by default, never after the creative is
   due). Save the slot.

## What sponsors see

<Screenshot

1. The list's numbers.
2. About and Audience.
3. Each self-serve slot with its price, description and terms.
4. The issues sponsors can request.

</Screenshot>

The media kit is open to search engines. Its available issues are the self-serve slots' issues in the next 12 weeks
that no booking holds, at least the slot's days ahead and late enough for the creative to be due after today. Each
issue, and <UiPath path="sponsors.kit.request" />, opens the request form:

- the slot and the issue
- the company, an optional website, the contact's name and email
- the sponsor block as it goes out: an optional logo (PNG or JPEG up to 2 MB, at least 120 px wide) and image (PNG or
  JPEG up to 2 MB), a headline (up to 120 characters), the text (up to 280), the button text (up to 40) and the web
  address the button opens
- notes for you (up to 600 characters)

The form checks that it was sent from the page itself, catches bots with a hidden field, asks for Cloudflare Turnstile
when the workspace set it up ([spam protection](/forms/signup-forms/#spam-protection)), and takes 5 requests an hour
from one IP address. <UiPath path="sponsors.req.send" /> files the request:

- The sponsor gets "We got your request" with <UiPath path="sponsors.req.follow" />, a link to the request's private
  status page at `/sponsor/b/<token>`.
- The people who manage sponsors get a notification, by email too unless they turned off
  <UiPath path="notify.event.sponsor_request" /> in [Notifications](/team/notifications/).
- The request joins the sponsor in your library with the same email, or adds a new sponsor.

<Aside type="note">
  A request doesn't hold its issue. Two sponsors can ask for the same issue, and another booking can take it before you
  decide.
</Aside>

While self-serve booking is off, `/sponsor` answers "Acme Newsletter isn't taking sponsor bookings here" with the
contact email when one is set. `/sponsor` belongs to the media kit: no [landing or sales page](/forms/landing-pages/)
can use that address.

2. Select <UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.approve" />. You can approve it for another slot, issue or price. The

   <Screenshot
     id="earn-sponsor-approve"

3. Select <UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.approveSubmit" />. The issue is held for the sponsor until the pay-by

To turn a request down, select <UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.decline" />, write a
<UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.reason" /> (required, up to 500 characters) and select
<UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.declineSubmit" />. The sponsor is emailed the reason as you wrote it, and their
status page shows it. Approvals and declines are recorded in the audit log.

## How the sponsor pays

1. The approval email carries a new link to the request's status page; the link from the first email stops working.
   On the status page, "Pay $750" opens a one-time Stripe Checkout for the booking's price, with the sponsor's email
   filled in. Stripe Tax adds tax when your prices have it added at checkout.
2. The payment books the issue: the booking is <UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.stBooked" />, paid by Stripe, with
   its creative ready. The sponsor is emailed that they're booked, and Stripe emails the receipt. A bank debit books
   the issue once it clears.
3. Unpaid by the pay-by date, the approval expires: the issue opens again, the sponsor is emailed, and you're
   notified. Expiry is checked every 5 minutes.

A payment that arrives after the pay-by date books the issue if it is still open. When it isn't, you're notified to
book the sponsor another issue or refund the payment in Stripe; a second payment of the same booking asks you to
refund it. ReputeCast never refunds payments itself. Each payment shows in the payments event log in
<UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabSettings > earn.settings.tabPayments" />, never in the audit log.

When a sponsor pays another way, such as a bank transfer, open the booking and select
<UiPath path="earn.booking.recordPayment" />: an approved request then becomes booked.
<UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.cancelBooking" /> on an approved request frees its issue.

The sponsor's emails go by System mail, in the language of the page they sent the request from, with your branding:
the request received, approved with the pay link, declined with your reason, expired, and booked.

## Check that it worked

- Open the media kit's address in a private window. It shows your numbers, the self-serve slots and their open
  issues.
- With a Stripe test key connected, send yourself a request, approve it and pay with a Stripe test card. The pages
  say they're in test mode, and the request moves to booked.

## Troubleshooting

- **The switch goes back to off** → no payment account is connected. [Connect Stripe](/earn/connect-stripe/) first.
- **A slot is missing from the media kit** → turn on <UiPath path="earn.sponsorRequests.slotSelfServe" /> in its
  editor; a self-serve slot needs a price above 0. A slot that isn't selling isn't listed.
- **The media kit lists no issues** → every issue of the next 12 weeks is held or booked, or too close for the slot's
  days ahead. The page says so and invites sponsors to write to the contact email.
- **"Too many requests from here"** → one IP address sent 5 requests within the hour. It can try again after an hour.
- **Sponsors get no emails** → sponsor emails need a sending provider on the System stream
  ([route mail across providers](/providers/routing/)).
