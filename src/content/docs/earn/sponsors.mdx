Run newsletter sponsorship sales from <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabSponsors" />: define the slots each issue
offers, keep a library of [sponsors](/reference/glossary/#sponsor), book them into issues, collect their creative and
send them a report after the issue goes out. Sponsorships work without Stripe: payments are recorded by hand, and
ReputeCast sends no invoices and moves no money.

## Before you begin

- You need `sponsors:manage` to keep the library and book sponsors. Seeing bookings needs `earn:view`
  ([Permissions and roles](/reference/permissions/)).
- Know which campaign series carries the sponsorship, for example a weekly digest
  ([Send RSS digests automatically](/campaigns/recurring-digests/)).

## Set up slots and sponsors

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabSponsors > earn.sponsors.tabSlots" /> and select
   <UiPath path="earn.sponsors.newSlot" />. A slot is a placement in each issue of a list: where it goes (for
   example after the first story), its price, how many bookings an issue takes and the days it runs. Set when the
   creative is due before the issue (4 days by default).
2. Open <UiPath path="earn.sponsors.tabSponsors" /> and select <UiPath path="earn.sponsors.newSponsor" />. Add the
   company and its payment terms (14 days after the issue by default).

</Steps>

## Book a sponsor into an issue

<Screenshot
  id="earn-sponsors"
  alt="Sponsors, Bookings tab: sponsor income $2,250 in 30 days, the next 5 issues with 2 booked, and a table of issues #140 to #146 with slot, booking status, sponsor, price, creative and payment."
/>

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="earn.sponsors.tabBookings" />. Each upcoming issue of the slot is a row.
2. Select **Book** on an open issue, choose the sponsor and confirm the price. Use **Hold** to reserve the issue for
   a sponsor until a date while they decide.
3. Collect the creative before its due date: a headline, text (up to 280 characters), a button and a link. The
   booking shows **Waiting** with the due date until it arrives.
4. Place the <UiPath path="composer.blocks.sponsor" /> block in the issue's email. It always carries a label:
   "Sponsored" or "Sponsored by" and the sponsor's name.
5. After you are paid, select **Record a payment** on the booking. Payment shows **Unpaid** with its due date until
   then.

</Steps>

Bookings move through **Open**, **Hold**, **Booked**, **Sent** and **Canceled**.

## Share the sponsor report

Once the issue is sent, the booking's **Report** shows the sponsor's results. The numbers are final 72 hours after
the send. Share a read-only link with the sponsor; it expires after 90 days by default (30 days, 90 days, a date or
never). Sponsor income is shown apart from subscription revenue ([Revenue metrics explained](/earn/revenue/)).

## Check that it worked

- The booked issue shows **Booked** and, once the creative arrives, the creative's state instead of **Waiting**.
- In the composer's preview, the sponsor block shows the sponsor's creative with the "Sponsored" label.

## Troubleshooting

- **A creative is overdue** → Earn's Overview lists it under the items that need you. Remind the sponsor or move the
  booking to a later issue.
- **The report numbers still change** → results are final 72 hours after the send.
- **Payment shows Unpaid after the due date** → record the payment by hand once it arrives; ReputeCast does not
  collect sponsor payments.

<Aside type="note">
  Sponsor income shows in Earn's Overview as other income. It is never part of MRR.
</Aside>
