Earn is where you run a paid newsletter self-hosted on your own ReputeCast instance: readers pay for a
[tier](/reference/glossary/#tier), buy a gift or a digital product, and sponsors book a slot in your issues. Readers
pay you through your own Stripe account. ReputeCast takes no fee and never holds the money; only Stripe's own fees
apply.

## How it works

1. **Connect Stripe.** You give ReputeCast a [restricted key](/reference/glossary/#restricted-key) and it registers a
   webhook in your Stripe account. See [Connect Stripe](/earn/connect-stripe/).
2. **Set what readers pay.** Tiers, prices, trials and offers live in
   <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabPlans" />. The hosted upgrade, cancel and gift pages use them. See
   [Sell paid subscriptions](/earn/paid-subscriptions/).
3. **Readers pay on Stripe Checkout.** Card details are entered on Stripe's pages, and readers manage billing in
   Stripe's customer portal. Stripe tells ReputeCast about each payment, renewal and cancellation through the webhook.
4. **Paid status follows Stripe.** Each [paid member](/reference/glossary/#paid-member) gets a status (Trialing,
   Active, Past due, Canceled, Complimentary or Gift) that decides what they can read and which campaigns reach them.
   See [Manage paid members](/earn/paid-members/).
5. **Other income sits beside subscriptions.** [Gifts](/earn/offers-and-gifts/), [sponsorships](/earn/sponsors/) and
   [digital products](/earn/digital-products/) are counted as other income, never in MRR.
6. **The Overview adds it up.** MRR, ARR, paid members, churn and free-to-paid conversion, with each definition in a
   tooltip. See [Revenue metrics explained](/earn/revenue/).

<Aside type="note">
  Stripe is the only payment provider. Paddle and Lemon Squeezy are not supported; the connect wizard shows them as
  "Not available yet". Sponsorships work without Stripe when you record their payments by hand; only sponsor
  self-booking needs it.
</Aside>

## Teams, pauses and sponsor self-booking

Three more ways to earn from paid members and sponsors. Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

- **Teams:** a tier can have group prices per seat, so one buyer pays for a company's seats and invites the others
  ([Sell team subscriptions](/earn/team-subscriptions/)).
- **Pauses:** a member about to cancel can pause payments for 1, 3 or 6 months instead, and your team can pause and
  resume a member ([Manage paid members](/earn/paid-members/#pause-and-resume-payments)).
- **Sponsor self-booking:** sponsors request an issue on your public media kit and pay through Stripe Checkout once
  you approve ([Let sponsors book and pay themselves](/earn/sponsor-self-booking/)).

## When to change them

- Start with a Stripe **test key**. Checkout then runs with Stripe's test cards and nothing is charged, so you can try
  every page before you add the live key.
- Lengthen the attribution window in <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabSettings > earn.settings.tabAttribution" /> if
  readers often take more than a week between clicking an email and upgrading.
- Change what happens when a payment fails in <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabSettings > earn.settings.tabAccess" />:
  keep access while Stripe retries (the default) or end it at once.
- To hide Earn from the sidebar for people who never use it, turn the section off in the workspace settings
  ([Workspaces](/team/workspaces/)).

Who can see and change Earn is set by six permissions: `earn:view`, `earn:edit`, `earn:manage`, `payments:manage`,
`sponsors:manage` and `conversions:create` ([Permissions and roles](/reference/permissions/)).
