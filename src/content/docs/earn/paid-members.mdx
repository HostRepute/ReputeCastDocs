Manage paid subscribers from one list: see each [paid member](/reference/glossary/#paid-member)'s tier, status and
lifetime value, give complimentary access, pause or cancel a subscription and export the list. Paid status comes from
Stripe, so most changes happen on their own as readers pay, renew or cancel.

## Before you begin

- Seeing paid members needs `earn:view`. Granting complimentary access, importing, pausing, removing a team seat and
  canceling need `earn:manage` (sensitive). Exporting needs `subscribers:export` (sensitive; every export is logged).
  See [Permissions and roles](/reference/permissions/).
- [Connect Stripe](/earn/connect-stripe/) and [create at least one tier](/earn/paid-subscriptions/).

## Find a paid member

Open <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabMembers" />. The line above the table counts paying members, MRR and how many
are past due while Stripe retries.

<Screenshot
  id="earn-members"
  alt="Paid members: 1,185 paying, $9,010 MRR and 14 past due, with Import paid members, Export CSV and Give complimentary access. Filter chips With access, Paying, Trialing, Past due, Cancels soon, Paused, Complimentary, Gift and Ended above a table of members with tier and price, status, renewal, lifetime value and source."
/>

- **Search** by name, email, billing email or Stripe customer ID.
- **Filter** by tier, billing period, source, offer and start date.
- **Status chips:** With access, Paying, Trialing, Past due, Cancels soon, Paused
  ([Pause and resume payments](#pause-and-resume-payments)), Complimentary, Gift and Ended. Paying leaves out paused
  members; With access includes the people holding a seat in a team.
- **Source** shows where the member came from: the campaign they upgraded from, an import, a grant or a gift.
- **Show test data** adds members from test checkouts. They are labelled Test and left out of every number and every
  campaign audience.
- **Rows a page:** 8 by default, or 25, 50 or 100, picked under the table and kept in the page's address.
- <UiPath path="earn.members.erasedName" /> stands for a member whose contact was erased. The subscription stays, so
  revenue reports keep their amounts and dates, without a name, an address or a link to the contact.

Select a member to open their panel with their subscription, payments and activity.

## Give complimentary access

<Steps>

1. Select **Give complimentary access**.
2. Choose the contact and the tier.
3. Pick a reason: Team, Contributor, Press, Partner, Giveaway or Other. The reason shows in the member's Source
   column ("Granted by … · contributor").
4. Save. The contact gets the tier's access without a payment and without anything changing in Stripe.

</Steps>

Complimentary members are counted apart from paying members and never in MRR
([Revenue metrics explained](/earn/revenue/)).

## Cancel a subscription or resend a member email

Open the member's panel:

- **Cancel subscription** asks when: **At the end of the period** (the member keeps the tier until then and nothing
  more is charged) or **Now** (make any refund in Stripe). Readers can also cancel themselves through your cancel
  page, where a [retention offer](/earn/offers-and-gifts/) can appear. Canceling a team's buyer ends every seat of the
  team when the buyer's access ends.
- **Resend a member email** sends it again through Transactional, with today's details, for example when a reader
  says the welcome email never arrived.

## Pause and resume payments

Pausing stops a member's payments and paid access until a date, without canceling. Members can pause themselves from
the cancel page when you allow it in <UiPath path="earn.settings.pausingTitle" />
([Sell paid subscriptions](/earn/paid-subscriptions/#let-members-pause-instead-of-canceling)), and your team can pause
them here. Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

<Steps>

1. Open the member's panel and select <UiPath path="earn.members.pausePayments" />. Only an active subscription paid
   through Stripe can pause: not during a trial, while a payment is failing, or once it is set to end.
2. Under <UiPath path="earn.members.pauseFor" />, choose 1, 3 or 6 months, or <UiPath path="earn.members.pauseUntil" />
   up to 6 months from today. Each choice shows the day payments resume.
3. Keep the box that emails the member <UiPath path="earn.members.emailPaused" />, and add an optional note for the
   timeline.
4. Select <UiPath path="earn.members.pausePayments" />. The pause is recorded in the [audit log](/team/audit-log/).

</Steps>

While a subscription is paused:

- Stripe charges nothing: the subscription's payment collection is paused (Stripe's `pause_collection`) and the
  invoices of the paused period are voided. Stripe keeps the subscription active and resumes it on its own on the
  date.
- The member has no paid access; their free lists stay.
- The subscription counts nothing in MRR. The pause is recorded as an MRR movement of its own, never as churn, and so
  is the resume ([Revenue metrics explained](/earn/revenue/)).
- The member shows "Paused until 10 Feb 2027" and is listed under <UiPath path="earn.members.paused" />, soonest to
  resume first. A team buyer's pause reaches every seat of the team.
- <UiPath path="earn.members.emailResuming" /> goes out 7 days before payments resume.

day, and Stripe charges the price again from the next payment date. Members can resume themselves with the
<UiPath path="earn.resume.button" /> link in their <UiPath path="earn.members.emailPaused" /> email, which works for 30
days and asks with a button before it resumes.

Pauses and resumes made in Stripe's dashboard are followed too. A pause made there sends the same email, and the
timeline says "In Stripe". Automations can start on <UiPath path="earn.automation.kindPaused" /> and
<UiPath path="earn.automation.kindResumed" /> ([Triggers and steps](/automations/triggers-and-steps/)), and webhooks
send `subscription.paused` and `subscription.resumed` ([Webhook events](/reference/webhook-events/)).

## Teams in the paid members list

A team's buyer shows "Team · 8 of 10" next to their status, and the people holding seats show "Seat in Acme Inc".

The buyer's panel lists the team's seats and open invites. <UiPath path="earn.members.seatRemove" /> ends that
person's access at once, or cancels the invite, and can email them <UiPath path="earn.members.emailGroupEnded" />. A
person's own panel has <UiPath path="earn.members.removeSeatButton" /> and a link to the team's buyer; they have no
Stripe subscription of their own. The buyer invites people and changes the seats from the team's own page. See
[Sell team subscriptions](/earn/team-subscriptions/).

## Start automations when paid status changes

Since 1.6.0, four triggers start an automation from paid status: <UiPath path="earn.automation.kindStarted" />,
<UiPath path="earn.automation.kindTierChanged" />, <UiPath path="earn.automation.kindEnded" /> and
<UiPath path="earn.automation.kindTrialEnding" />. Each fires once per subscription, for subscriptions paid through
Stripe in live mode: complimentary access, gifts, team seats and test mode never start one. Set them up in
[Triggers and steps](/automations/triggers-and-steps/).

## Export paid members

Select <UiPath path="earn.members.exportCsv" /> to download the paid members the status chip and filters show, as a
CSV. Every export is recorded in the [audit log](/team/audit-log/). Since 1.5.0, a cell that starts with `=`, `+`,
`-`, `@`, a tab or a carriage return gets a single quote in front, so a spreadsheet never runs it as a formula.

## Import paid members from another platform

Select <UiPath path="earn.members.importMembers" /> to bring over paying readers from Substack, Ghost, beehiiv or Kit.
Exports are matched to customers in your Stripe account read-only; nothing changes in Stripe. Step by step:
[Import paid members](/migrate/paid-members/).

## Check that it worked

- A complimentary member shows the **Complimentary** status and appears under the Complimentary chip.
- A canceled member shows **Cancels** with the date access ends, then moves to **Ended**.
- A paused member shows "Paused until" and the date, appears under the <UiPath path="earn.members.paused" /> chip, and
  their subscription in Stripe shows collection paused until that date.

## Troubleshooting

- **A member shows Past due** → Stripe could not charge the card and is retrying. They keep access while Stripe
  retries unless you changed that in <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabSettings > earn.settings.tabAccess" />.
- **Export CSV is locked** → your role can't export contacts (`subscribers:export`). The page names who can.
- **A reader paid but isn't listed** → check the Stripe webhook in
  <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabSettings > earn.settings.tabPayments" />. The hourly catch-up adds missed payments.
- **Pause payments is missing** → the subscription is in a trial, past due, set to end, or not paid through Stripe.
  Complimentary access, gifts and team seats can't pause; pause the team's buyer instead.

<Aside type="caution">
  Canceling in ReputeCast cancels the subscription in your Stripe account. Refunds are made in Stripe.
</Aside>
