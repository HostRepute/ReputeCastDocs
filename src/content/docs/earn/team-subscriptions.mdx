Sell newsletter team subscriptions by giving a [tier](/reference/glossary/#tier) group prices per seat: one buyer
pays for the seats on Stripe Checkout, then invites the rest of the team, and each person reads the tier in their own
inbox. Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

## Before you begin

- [Connect Stripe](/earn/connect-stripe/) and [create the tier](/earn/paid-subscriptions/) the team buys.
- Group prices need `earn:edit` (sensitive). Removing someone's seat in Earn needs `earn:manage` (sensitive). See
  [Permissions and roles](/reference/permissions/).
- Publish the team emails' templates, or the buyer and the people they invite get no email (see
  [The team emails](#the-team-emails)).

## Add group prices to a tier

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabPlans" /> and select <UiPath path="earn.plans.edit" /> on the tier.
2. In <UiPath path="earn.tier.groupTitle" />, switch on <UiPath path="earn.tier.groupMonthly" />,
   <UiPath path="earn.tier.groupYearly" /> or both, and set the amount per seat.
3. Under <UiPath path="earn.tier.groupSeats" />, set the fewest and the most seats a team can buy, from 2 to 1,000.
   The buyer uses one of them.

   <Screenshot
     id="earn-tier-group-prices"
     alt="The Group prices card of Weekly Digest Insider: $6 per seat a month with no teams yet, $60 per seat a year with 1 team, 3 to 50 seats, the line readers see on news.acme.io/upgrade/insider/team, and a note that Stripe prorates seats added or removed."
   />

4. Save the tier. Each group price is a price on the tier's product in your Stripe account. Changing an amount
   creates a new price for new teams; teams that pay the old one keep it. A changed seat range applies at once.

</Steps>

The tier's card on the upgrade page now says "Buying for a team? From $60 a year per seat", with the yearly price per
seat when there is one, and links to the team page at `/upgrade/<tier>/team`.

<Aside type="note">
  Group prices are for teams only. [Offers](/earn/offers-and-gifts/) discount a tier's individual prices and never its
  prices per seat, the website's paywall sells the individual prices, and Stripe's customer portal doesn't offer group
  prices to members who switch plans.
</Aside>

## How a team buys

<Screenshot
  id="public-upgrade-team"
  alt="The team page for Weekly Digest Insider: Monthly and Yearly with 2 months free, a seats stepper at 3 for 3 to 50 seats, the team name and the buyer's email, $60 per seat a year × 3 seats = $180 a year before tax, and Continue to payment."
>

1. Billing period, when the tier has both group prices.
2. Seats, within the tier's range.
3. Price per seat × seats and the renewal date.
4. Opens Stripe Checkout.

</Screenshot>

1. On the team page, the buyer picks the period and the seats, and enters the team's name (up to 80 characters; the
   people they invite see it) and their own email.
2. <UiPath path="earn.team.word.continue" /> opens Stripe Checkout for the price per seat × the seats. Stripe asks for
   a billing address and can take a company tax ID. Team prices have no trial.
3. Once paid, one Stripe subscription with the seats as its quantity carries the team. The buyer holds the first seat
   and gets <UiPath path="earn.members.emailGroupWelcome" /> with the link to manage the team.

The team page is open to search engines, like the upgrade page.

## Invite people and change the seats

The buyer manages the team from a private page, `/team/<token>`, linked in their welcome email. The link works for 7
days. An expired link emails a fresh one to the buyer, at most one every 10 minutes, and the page says so. On the
page, the buyer can:

- **Invite people:** paste addresses separated by commas or new lines, up to 50 at a time and no more than the free
  seats, then select <UiPath path="earn.manage.inviteButton" />. Each address gets the
  <UiPath path="earn.members.emailGroupInvite" /> email.
- **Send an invite again:** <UiPath path="earn.manage.resend" /> sends a new link; the old one stops working.
- **Remove someone:** <UiPath path="earn.manage.remove" /> asks first. An invite stops working; a person loses the
  tier at once, keeps the free lists and gets <UiPath path="earn.members.emailGroupEnded" />. The team still pays
  for the same seats.
- **Change the seats:** <UiPath path="earn.manage.seatsButton" />, within the tier's seat range and never below the
  people and invites in the team ("Remove 2 people first"). Stripe prorates the change: more seats are charged for
  the rest of the period, fewer leave a credit on the next invoice.
- **See invoices:** <UiPath path="earn.manage.billingButton" /> opens Stripe's billing portal for the card, the
  billing address, the tax ID and every invoice.

While the team has no paid access, for example while its payments are paused, invites wait and the seats can't
change.

### Accept an invite

An invite works for 14 days. Its page, `/team/join/<token>`, says "Acme Inc gave you access to Weekly Digest
Insider", lists the
tier's benefits and the lists the person joins, and has one <UiPath path="earn.join.accept" /> button, so link
scanners that open every link can't accept. Accepting is the address's consent for the tier's lists, recorded with
the time, IP address and browser as for any signup ([Lists, consent and double opt-in](/audience/lists-and-consent/)).
The person then has a seat: paid access with nothing to pay.

The invite link keeps working after it is accepted, so the person can come back to it. When every seat is taken, the
page says the team has no free seat and asks the person to contact the buyer.

## Every seat follows the buyer

A seat has no Stripe subscription of its own. Its access copies the buyer's subscription:

- When the buyer's payments [pause](/earn/paid-members/#pause-and-resume-payments), the whole team loses paid access
  until they resume.
- When a payment fails and you end access at once in
  <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabSettings > earn.settings.tabAccess" />, every seat loses it too.
- When the buyer's subscription ends, every seat's access ends and each person gets
  <UiPath path="earn.members.emailGroupEnded" />.

Seats changed in Stripe's dashboard are followed. When Stripe lowers them below the people in the team, nobody is
removed: the buyer is emailed that the team has more people than seats, and the team page asks them to remove people
or add seats back.

1. The team's subscription: tier, seats, price per seat and renewal.
2. Each person and open invite, with a Remove… button.
3. Actions on the buyer's subscription, which reach every seat.

</Screenshot>

In <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabMembers" />, the buyer's row shows "Team · 8 of 10": the people holding a seat,
the buyer included, out of the seats paid for. Their price reads "$60 per seat a year × 10 seats = $600/year". Each
person with a seat shows "Seat in Acme Inc", with the price "None · Acme Inc pays".

- Open the buyer to see the team's seats. The count above the list includes open invites ("Seats · 9 of 10").
- <UiPath path="earn.members.seatRemove" /> next to a person, or <UiPath path="earn.members.removeSeatButton" /> on
  the person's own panel, ends that seat's access at once and can email them
  <UiPath path="earn.members.emailGroupEnded" />. The removal is recorded in the [audit log](/team/audit-log/). The
  buyer invites and changes seats from the team page, not here.
- <UiPath path="earn.members.pausePayments" /> and <UiPath path="earn.members.cancelSub" /> on the buyer act on the
  whole team ([Manage paid members](/earn/paid-members/)).

Changes Stripe sends, such as "Seats changed from 8 to 10", go to the payments event log in
<UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabSettings > earn.settings.tabPayments" />.

## The team emails

Publish their templates in <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabSettings > earn.settings.tabEmails" />:

| Email                                            | When                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <UiPath path="earn.members.emailGroupWelcome" /> | A team's subscription starts; to the buyer, with the team link  |
| <UiPath path="earn.members.emailGroupInvite" />  | The buyer invites someone                                       |
| <UiPath path="earn.members.emailGroupEnded" />   | A seat's access ends: removed, or the team's subscription ended |

The fresh team link and the notice that the team has more people than seats go by System mail, in the publisher's
branding.

## How teams count in revenue

A team counts in MRR as its price per seat × its seats: 10 seats at $60 a year add $50 to MRR. It is one paying
member; the people holding seats count nothing on their own and appear under
<UiPath path="earn.members.withAccess" />. Changing the seats moves MRR without counting as expansion or contraction
([Revenue metrics explained](/earn/revenue/)).

- On <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabPlans" />, select <UiPath path="earn.plans.previewTest" />, then "Buying for a
  team?", and buy seats with a Stripe test card. The test buyer appears in
  <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabMembers" /> with "Team · 1 of 3" (for 3 seats) once you turn on
  <UiPath path="earn.members.showTest" />.
- Invite your own address from the team page and accept the invite: you appear there too, with "Seat in" and the

## Troubleshooting

- **The upgrade page has no "Buying for a team?" link** → the tier has no group price yet, or the tier is archived.
- **The buyer can't invite anyone** → every seat is taken, or the team has no paid access, for example while its
  payments are paused. Add seats on the team page, remove someone, or resume the payments.
- **"Remove 2 people first" when lowering the seats** → people and open invites still use those seats. Remove
  people or cancel invites, then change the seats.
- **The team link says it has expired** → links work for 7 days. The page has already emailed a fresh one to the
  buyer's address.
- **Nobody got the invite** → publish the <UiPath path="earn.members.emailGroupInvite" /> template in
  <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabSettings > earn.settings.tabEmails" />.
