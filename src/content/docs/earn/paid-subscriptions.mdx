Set up paid newsletter subscriptions by creating [tiers](/reference/glossary/#tier) readers pay for, publishing the
hosted upgrade page and marking which content is for paid members only. Readers pay on Stripe Checkout, and their
paid status decides what they can read.

## Before you begin

- [Connect Stripe](/earn/connect-stripe/). Start with a test key to try every page without charging anyone.
- You need `earn:edit` (sensitive) to change tiers, prices and the upgrade page.
- Decide which lists paid members join. A tier adds its members to the lists you pick
  ([Lists, consent and double opt-in](/audience/lists-and-consent/)).

## Create a tier

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabPlans" /> and select <UiPath path="earn.plans.newTier" />.

   <Screenshot
     id="earn-plans"
     alt="Plans & offers: the upgrade page's numbers, then the Free card and the paid tiers with their prices, paying members and MRR, and the New tier button; the Offers table starts below."
   />

2. Name the tier and list its benefits. Readers see both on the upgrade page.
3. Set a monthly price, a yearly price or both, in the currency you chose when you connected Stripe.
4. Choose a trial: None, 7, 14 or 30 days. A trial is offered only to people who haven't had one.
5. Under **Lists and access**, pick the lists paid members join (their consent is recorded as "Paid subscription")
   and the access rank. A tier includes the paid content of every tier of a lower rank.
6. Choose the visibility: **Public** (on the upgrade page, after the Free card), **Hidden** (only through its own
   link) or **Archived** (no new paid members; current ones keep paying). You can highlight one tier on the upgrade
   page with a label such as "Most popular".
7. Save. Each tier is a product in your Stripe account. You can draft tiers before Stripe is connected; publishing
   them needs Stripe.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  Changing a price creates a new price in Stripe. Members who pay the old one keep it, and the tier lists it under
  **Legacy prices**.
</Aside>

To sell a tier to companies, add <UiPath path="earn.tier.groupTitle" /> in the tier editor: a monthly or yearly price
per seat and the seats a team can buy. One buyer pays for the seats and invites the others, and the upgrade page links
to the tier's team page. See [Sell team subscriptions](/earn/team-subscriptions/).
Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

## Edit the upgrade page

<Steps>

1. In <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabPlans" />, select <UiPath path="earn.plans.pageTitle" />.

   <Screenshot
     id="earn-upgrade-page"
     alt="The upgrade page editor: headline and intro, the tiers and their order, default period Yearly, Weekly Digest Insider highlighted as Most popular, the free card and FAQ, with a live preview at news.acme.io/upgrade where Insider's card ends with Buying for a team? From $60 a year per seat."
   />

2. Write the headline and intro, order the tiers and choose the default period (monthly or yearly).
3. Pick a highlighted tier and its label, edit the free card and the FAQ.
4. Use **Preview as** to see the page as an anonymous visitor or another reader, on desktop or mobile.
5. Select **Preview with test checkout** to run a checkout with Stripe's test cards.
6. Select **Save and publish**.

</Steps>

The upgrade page lives on your public pages (and your own domain, if you set one up in
[Brand your pages and use your own domain](/forms/branding-and-domain/)). The cancel and gift pages are edited in
<UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabSettings > earn.settings.tabPages" />.

The tier editor and the upgrade page editor ask before you leave with unsaved changes. On the upgrade page, the same
question can save and publish them.

## Let members pause instead of canceling

A paid member about to cancel can pause their payments instead, for the lengths you allow.
Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabSettings > earn.settings.tabPayments" /> and find the
   <UiPath path="earn.settings.pausingTitle" /> card. Changing it needs `payments:manage` (sensitive).

   <Screenshot
     id="earn-pausing"
     alt="The Pausing card, On: members can pause for 1 month and 3 months, 6 months unticked; only an active member who isn't in a trial or past due and hasn't paused in the last 12 months sees Pause instead; while paused, Stripe charges nothing and paid access stops."
   />

2. Under <UiPath path="earn.settings.pausingLegend" />, tick 1, 3 or 6 months. With none ticked, members can't pause,
   which is the default.
3. Select <UiPath path="earn.settings.pausingSave" />. The change is recorded in the [audit log](/team/audit-log/).

</Steps>

The cancel page then shows <UiPath path="offers.cancel.pause.title" /> before any
[retention offer](/earn/offers-and-gifts/), with each allowed length and the date payments come back. It shows only to
an active member paying through Stripe who isn't in a trial, behind on payments or already set to cancel, and who
hasn't paused in the last 12 months. Choosing a length pauses the subscription in Stripe at once, and the member gets
the <UiPath path="earn.members.emailPaused" /> email with a <UiPath path="earn.resume.button" /> link. Your team can
also pause a member from their panel, for any length up to 6 months; what a pause does is in
[Manage paid members](/earn/paid-members/#pause-and-resume-payments).

## Billing and cancel links

A paid member's billing link opens their Stripe customer portal, and their cancel link opens the cancel page. Both
expire, since 1.5.0, because anyone holding one could change the card or cancel:

- Links in member emails, and the `manage_subscription_url` of a campaign, work for 30 days.
- A link the member asks for works for an hour.
- The preference center, the unsubscribe page and the upgrade page can be reached from a forwarded email, so their
  billing and cancel buttons don't open Stripe. They email the member fresh links, valid for an hour, to the address on
  file, by System mail and in the page's language. The page answers the same whatever the address, and sends at most
  one email per member every 10 minutes.
- An expired link opens "This link has expired" with the member's email and
  <UiPath path="offers.cancel.emailMe" />, which sends a fresh one the same way.

## Make content paid-only

- **A block in an email or post:** in the [composer](/campaigns/composer/), set the block's visibility to
  **Paid access**. Free readers get the rest of the email.
- **A whole campaign:** choose who on the list gets it: Everyone, **Paid members only** or **Free subscribers only**.
- **An invitation to pay:** add the <UiPath path="composer.blocks.upgrade" /> block. It links to the upgrade page.
- **Personal details:** use the Liquid tokens `paid.tier`, `paid.status`, `paid.price`, `paid.renews_at` and
  `paid.ends_at` ([Personalize emails with Liquid](/campaigns/personalization/)).
- **Website posts:** set a post's visibility to Paid ([Publish a newsletter website](/forms/website/)).

## Turn on member emails

Nine member emails go out automatically once you publish their transactional templates in
<UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabSettings > earn.settings.tabEmails" />:

| Email                  | When                                     |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Welcome to a paid tier | When a trial or paid subscription starts |
| Trial ends soon        | 3 days before a trial ends               |
| Payment failed         | Stripe couldn't charge the card          |
| Renewal reminder       | 7 days before a yearly renewal           |
| Membership canceled    | When a cancellation is confirmed         |
| Gift receipt           | When a gift is bought                    |
| Gift payment failed    | When a gift's bank payment fails         |
| Your gift              | On the gift's delivery date              |
| Gift ending            | 7 days before a gift ends                |

An unpublished template means that email is not sent; Earn's Overview lists it under the items that need you. A
workspace that connected Stripe before an email existed gets its draft when you next open
<UiPath path="earn.settings.tabEmails" />.

## Check that it worked

- On the upgrade page, run a test checkout. The test reader appears in
  <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabMembers" /> with the **Test** label once you turn on **Show test data**.
- Send yourself a test of a campaign with a **Paid access** block, using **Preview as** with a paid and a free
  contact.

## Troubleshooting

- **The tier is missing from the upgrade page** → its visibility is Hidden or Archived, or it is unticked in
  **Tiers and their order**.
- **The currency can't be changed** → it is fixed once a tier has prices.
- **No welcome email arrives** → publish its template in
  <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabSettings > earn.settings.tabEmails" />.
