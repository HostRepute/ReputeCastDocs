Offers discount a paid [tier](/reference/glossary/#tier), and a newsletter gift subscription lets a reader pay once
for someone else's access. Both run through your own Stripe account: offers become Stripe coupons and promotion
codes, and a gift is a one-time Stripe payment.

## Before you begin

- [Connect Stripe](/earn/connect-stripe/) and [create a tier](/earn/paid-subscriptions/).
- Offers and gift options need `earn:edit` (sensitive). Changing a bought gift needs `earn:manage` (sensitive).

## Create an offer

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabPlans" /> and add an offer.
2. Choose the kind:
   - **Signup**: for new paid members, by code or link.
   - **Retention**: shown only on the cancel page, to a member who is about to cancel.
   - **Referral reward**: single-use codes that [your referral program](/grow/referrals/) gives out. Each one works
     only through its referrer's own claim link.
3. Set the discount (a percentage or an amount) and the tiers it applies to. Offers discount a tier's individual
   prices; [team prices per seat](/earn/team-subscriptions/) take no offer.
4. Choose the duration: **Once** (the first payment, so the first year on a yearly price), **For a number of months**
   (1–36) or **Forever**.
5. Optionally set a **Redemption limit** and an end date. The offer ends at its date or its limit, whichever comes
   first.
6. Save, then share the code or the offer link to your upgrade page.

</Steps>

The offer editor asks before you leave with unsaved changes.

<Aside type="note">
  A paid member sees a retention offer at most once every 12 months. Referral reward codes are valid for 60 days by
  default (1–365). When members may pause, the cancel page offers <UiPath path="offers.cancel.pause.title" /> before
  the retention offer
  ([Let members pause instead of canceling](/earn/paid-subscriptions/#let-members-pause-instead-of-canceling)).
</Aside>

## Sell gift subscriptions

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabGifts" /> and select <UiPath path="earn.gifts.newGiftOption" />.

   <Screenshot
     id="earn-gifts"
     alt="The Gifts tab: a notice that gift-ending isn't published yet, gift totals, the two gift options readers can buy with their prices and a New option button, the gift page settings, and the list of gifts bought with its search, Export CSV and status chips."
   />

2. Pick the tier and the length (1–36 months), and set the price. The buyer pays once; nothing renews.
3. Save. The option appears on your gift page. Like the upgrade page, the gift page is open to search engines, with
   its intro as the description search results show.

</Steps>

When a reader buys a gift, they choose who gets it, an optional message (up to 300 characters) and when it is
delivered: now or on a date up to a year ahead. On that date the recipient gets the **Your gift** email with a
redeem link. Redeeming starts the gift: the recipient becomes a paid member with the **Gift** status until the length
runs out.

A gift paid by bank debit counts as bought once the payment clears. If the payment fails, the gift is never sent, and
the buyer gets the **Gift payment failed** email with a link to buy the gift again.

The gift emails (Gift receipt, Gift payment failed, Your gift, Gift ending) are member emails: publish their templates
in <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabSettings > earn.settings.tabEmails" />.

<UiPath path="lib.download.exportCsv" /> above the list of gifts downloads the gifts its chip and search show. It
needs `subscribers:export`, and every export is recorded in the [audit log](/team/audit-log/).

## Check that it worked

- Open the offer link in a private window: the upgrade page shows the discounted price.
- Buy a gift with a Stripe test card: it appears in <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabGifts" /> and the test
  recipient gets the gift email on the delivery date.

## Troubleshooting

- **The upgrade page says "This offer has ended"** → the offer reached its redemption limit or end date, and readers
  see the normal prices. Raise the limit or move the date to keep it running.
- **The recipient never got the gift** → check the delivery date, then that the **Your gift** template is published.
- **A redeem link no longer works** → gift links work for 12 months after delivery by default. Change the period in
  <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabGifts" />.
