Sell digital downloads, such as guides, templates or course files, from <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabProducts" />.
A product is a file and a price, sold through Stripe Checkout on your own Stripe account. After payment, ReputeCast
emails the buyer a personal download link through your Transactional stream.

## Before you begin

- [Connect Stripe](/earn/connect-stripe/).
- Editing products needs `products:edit`; putting them on sale, taking them off and changing what they cost needs
  `products:publish` (sensitive). Handling orders needs `orders:manage`
  ([Permissions and roles](/reference/permissions/)).
- Set up a Transactional route so delivery emails can go out
  ([Transactional email in ReputeCast](/transactional/overview/)).

## Create a product

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabProducts" /> and select <UiPath path="products.newProduct" />.
2. Upload the files: up to 500 MB per file and 2 GB per product. Large uploads are sent in parts and resume if the
   connection drops.
3. Write the name and the description buyers see.
4. Set the price: **One-time** (a fixed price, at least $1) or **Pay what you want** (a minimum of at least $1 and
   an optional suggested price).
5. Under delivery, set how long a download link works (7 days by default, 1–30) and how many downloads it allows
   (5 by default, 1–20).
6. Publish the product. Products are **Draft**, **Published** or **Archived**; only published products can be
   bought.

</Steps>

Sell the product from your emails or on a [landing or sales page](/forms/landing-pages/) with a product block.

## Handle orders

<Screenshot
  id="products"
  alt="An order panel for The Deliverability Field Guide: paid $29.00 through Stripe, the buyer's consent at checkout, the product-delivery email delivered via Postmark, a download link with 4 of 5 downloads left, and the buttons Send a new link, Revoke access and Refund in Stripe."
/>

Each order has a status: **Paid**, **Refunded**, **Partially refunded**, **Disputed** or **Test**. Open an order to
see the buyer, the Stripe payment, the delivery email and the download link's state.

- **Send a new link** when the buyer's link expired or ran out of downloads. Buyers can also request new links
  themselves, up to 3 per order in any 24 hours.
- **Revoke access** ends the buyer's access to the download.
- **Refund in Stripe** opens the payment in your Stripe dashboard. Refunds and disputes are made in Stripe, arrive
  through the webhook and update the order here.

<Aside type="note">
  If the buyer ticks the list offer at checkout ("Also send me …"), the consent is recorded with the time, page and
  IP address, as for any signup ([Lists, consent and double opt-in](/audience/lists-and-consent/)).
</Aside>

## Check that it worked

- Buy the product with a Stripe test card. The order appears with the **Test** status and is left out of every
  number.
- The test buyer gets the delivery email, and the order panel shows the download link with its downloads left.

## Troubleshooting

- **The buyer got no email** → open the delivery email in the order; it links to the message in Transactional's
  message log ([Message log and alerts](/transactional/message-log/)).
- **"Link expired" for the buyer** → select **Send a new link**, or raise the link validity on the product.
- **The order still shows Paid after a refund** → check the Stripe webhook in
  <UiPath path="nav.earn > earn.tabSettings > earn.settings.tabPayments" />; the hourly catch-up applies missed
  refunds.
