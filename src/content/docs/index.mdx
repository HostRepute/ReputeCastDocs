## New in 1.2.0

ReputeCast 1.2.0 hardens the five core features: Send Readiness, guarded sending with a canary phase and traffic rules,
warm-up enforced as mail goes out, honest analytics, and a composer with autosave, comments, review links and a brand
kit. Read the [release notes](/reference/release-notes/).
