## New in 1.5.0

ReputeCast 1.5.0 lets a hosting company or an IT team that runs an instance for someone else pin its public URL and say
who upgrades it. It also fixes what a security review of the whole project found. Read the
[release notes](/reference/release-notes/).

and every feature of ReputeCast works without it.
