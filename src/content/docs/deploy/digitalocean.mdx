This guide runs ReputeCast on DigitalOcean App Platform from the repository's Deploy to DigitalOcean template: the
ReputeCast Docker image as a service, a PostgreSQL database next to it and a Spaces bucket for uploaded files.
Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

App Platform has no persistent disks, so the template keeps uploaded files in a Spaces bucket
(`storage.backend: s3`, see [object storage](/self-hosting/object-storage/)) and the secret key in a variable you
enter, instead of the `/var/lib/reputecast` volume the other platforms use.

## Before you begin

- A DigitalOcean account. See also the [system requirements](/self-hosting/requirements/): the template runs one
  `apps-s-1vcpu-1gb` instance (1 vCPU, 1 GB).
- OpenSSL or Docker on your computer, to generate the secret key.
- Optionally, a domain for ReputeCast, such as `news.example.com`, at a DNS host where you can add a `CNAME` record.

## Deploy ReputeCast

<Steps>

1. In the DigitalOcean control panel, create a Spaces bucket, such as `reputecast-files`. The template expects the
   `nyc3` region; for another region, change two variables in step 6.

2. In **Spaces Object Storage** › **Access Keys**, click **Create Access Key**, choose **Limited access**, give it
   **Read/Write/Delete** on the bucket and create it. Copy the access key and its secret, which is shown only once
   ([manage access to Spaces](https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/spaces/how-to/manage-access/), checked November
   2026).

3. Generate the secret key and save it in your password manager: `openssl rand -base64 32` or
   `docker run --rm ghcr.io/hostrepute/reputecast:v1.6.0 secrets generate-key`. Both print 44 characters ending in `=`.

4. Open the template:
   `https://cloud.digitalocean.com/apps/new?repo=https://github.com/hostrepute/reputecast/tree/main`. App Platform
   reads `.do/deploy.template.yaml` from the repository: one service, `reputecast`, that runs
   `ghcr.io/hostrepute/reputecast:latest` on port 8080, and a dev database, `db`, on PostgreSQL 18.

5. Enter the variables the template leaves empty:

   ```sh title="Environment variables"
   REPUTECAST_ADMIN_EMAIL=you@company.com
   REPUTECAST_SECRETS__KEY=paste-the-key-from-step-3
   REPUTECAST_STORAGE__S3__BUCKET=reputecast-files
   REPUTECAST_STORAGE__S3__ACCESS_KEY_ID=paste-the-access-key
   REPUTECAST_STORAGE__S3__SECRET_ACCESS_KEY=paste-its-secret
   ```

   The template marks the secret key and the bucket's keys as secrets, and sets the rest:

   - `REPUTECAST_PUBLIC_URL=${APP_URL}`, the app's address, which follows its primary domain.
   - `DATABASE_URL=${db.DATABASE_URL}`, the dev database. ReputeCast reads `DATABASE_URL` when
     `REPUTECAST_DATABASE__URL` isn't set.
   - `REPUTECAST_HTTP__TRUSTED_PROXIES=private`: App Platform's proxy reaches the app over a private network
     ([trusted proxies](/deploy/overview/#trusted-proxies)).
   - `REPUTECAST_STORAGE__BACKEND=s3`, with `REPUTECAST_STORAGE__S3__REGION=nyc3` and
     `REPUTECAST_STORAGE__S3__ENDPOINT=https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com`.

6. For a bucket outside `nyc3`, change `REPUTECAST_STORAGE__S3__REGION` and the region in
   `REPUTECAST_STORAGE__S3__ENDPOINT` to the bucket's, such as `fra1` and `https://fra1.digitaloceanspaces.com`.

7. Create the app. App Platform pulls the image and starts ReputeCast, which applies the migrations and creates the
   first Owner. The health check calls `/readyz` every 10 seconds and gives the first start 30 failed checks, about
   five minutes, before it gives up.

8. Open the app's **Runtime Logs** tab, choose the `reputecast` component and look for `ReputeCast is installed`. The
   banner holds the first Owner's temporary password:

   ```txt title="Runtime Logs"
     ReputeCast is installed.

     Sign in at  https://reputecast-abcde.ondigitalocean.app
     Email       you@company.com
     Password    SHLn-zHfR-cf6j-NEm5   (temporary: you'll replace it at first sign-in)
   ```

9. Open the app's URL and sign in. The [first sign-in](/self-hosting/first-run/) asks you to replace the password,
   add a passkey or an authenticator app, save recovery codes, confirm the instance basics and create a workspace.

10. Before you rely on the database, convert it: on the app's **Settings** tab, select the `db` component, then in
    **Database Type & Scale** click **Convert to a Managed Database**. App Platform migrates the data to a managed
    database ([manage databases](https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/app-platform/how-to/manage-databases/),
    checked November 2026).

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
  Keep `REPUTECAST_SECRETS__KEY` outside DigitalOcean with your database backups: without it, the provider credentials
  stored in the database can't be decrypted, and the links in emails already sent stop working.
</Aside>

### Use your own domain

1. On the app's **Networking** tab, in **Domains**, click **Add domain**, enter `news.example.com`, choose **You manage
   your domain** and add the `CNAME` record App Platform shows, which ends in `ondigitalocean.app`, at your DNS host
   ([manage domains](https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/app-platform/how-to/manage-domains/), checked November
   2026).
2. Once the domain is the app's primary domain, `${APP_URL}` follows it at the next deploy.
3. In ReputeCast, open <UiPath path="shell.userMenuInstanceAdmin > admin.instance" />, change the
   <UiPath path="admin.publicUrl" /> to `https://news.example.com` and save. The address saved at the first sign-in
   wins over the variable, and saving moves provider webhooks to the new address.

## Check that it works

- The app's **Activity** tab shows the deployment as live: the `/readyz` health check passed.
- `https://news.example.com/readyz` answers `ready`, or 503 with the reason, such as `database unavailable`.
- <UiPath path="shell.userMenuInstanceAdmin > admin.system" /> shows <UiPath path="admin.system.filesStorageS3" /> in
  <UiPath path="admin.system.filesTitle" />, with your bucket and endpoint.

## Upgrade

1. Read the release notes and [back up](/self-hosting/backups/) the database and the Spaces bucket.
2. Deploy the app again: App Platform pulls the image of the tag the service names, `latest` in the template. To pin
   a release, set the service's image tag to it in the app's settings.
3. ReputeCast applies new migrations when it starts ([upgrade ReputeCast](/self-hosting/upgrades/)).

## Troubleshooting

- **The deploy fails and the logs show `secrets.key: key must be base64-encoded`** or `key must be 32 bytes`:
  `REPUTECAST_SECRETS__KEY` isn't a key from step 3. Paste it again, without spaces.
- **The logs show `storage.s3.bucket is required with storage.backend s3`**: `REPUTECAST_STORAGE__S3__BUCKET` is
  empty.
- **Uploads fail with an access error**: the access key lacks Read/Write/Delete on the bucket, or the region in
  `REPUTECAST_STORAGE__S3__ENDPOINT` isn't the bucket's ([object storage troubleshooting](/self-hosting/object-storage/#troubleshooting)).
- **Links in emails still use the `ondigitalocean.app` address**: the public URL saved in Instance admin still is that
  address. Change it there (step 3 of [use your own domain](#use-your-own-domain)).
- **The deploy never goes live**: `/readyz` didn't answer `ready` within the health check's 30 tries. Read the runtime
  logs: the migrations may still be running, or the database isn't reachable.
