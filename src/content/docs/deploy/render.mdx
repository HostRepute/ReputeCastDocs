This guide runs ReputeCast on Render as two resources: a Render Postgres database and a web service that pulls the
ReputeCast Docker image, served over HTTPS on your own domain. Create both at once from the repository's Blueprint, or
one by one in the Render Dashboard.

## Before you begin

- A Render account. Use paid compute plans: a Free web service spins down after 15 minutes without traffic and can't
  have a disk, and a Free Render Postgres database expires 30 days after it's created.
- OpenSSL or Docker on your computer, to generate the secret key. See also the
  [system requirements](/self-hosting/requirements/).
- A domain for ReputeCast, such as `news.example.com`, at a DNS host where you can add a `CNAME` record.

## Deploy with render.yaml

The repository's `render.yaml` is a Render Blueprint that creates both resources. Available from the next release
after 1.6.0.

<Steps>

1. Open `https://render.com/deploy?repo=https://github.com/hostrepute/reputecast`, the Deploy to Render link in the
   repository's README, and sign in to Render.

2. Render shows what the Blueprint creates:

   - the web service `reputecast`, from the image `ghcr.io/hostrepute/reputecast:latest` on the `0.5c-512mb` plan
     (0.5 CPU, 512 MB), with `/readyz` as its health check and a 10 GB disk, `reputecast-data`, at
     `/var/lib/reputecast`;
   - the database `reputecast-db`, PostgreSQL 18 on the `0.1c-256mb` plan, with a database and user both called
     `reputecast`, reachable only over Render's private network.

3. Enter the two values Render asks for: `REPUTECAST_PUBLIC_URL`, your domain's URL without a trailing slash (or the
   service's `onrender.com` address, such as `https://reputecast.onrender.com`, until you add a domain), and
   `REPUTECAST_ADMIN_EMAIL`, the first Owner's email. Render asks for them only while it creates the Blueprint
   ([Blueprint reference](https://render.com/docs/blueprint-spec), checked November 2026).

4. Confirm, and Render creates both resources. The service gets the rest of its settings from the file:

   - `DATABASE_URL`, the database's connection string. ReputeCast reads it when `REPUTECAST_DATABASE__URL` isn't set.
   - `REPUTECAST_HTTP__TRUSTED_PROXIES=private`, which trusts Render's proxy on its private network
     ([trusted proxies](/deploy/overview/#trusted-proxies)).
   - `PORT=8080`, the port Render routes to and the image listens on.

5. Once the deploy is live, read the first Owner's temporary password in the service's **Logs**, add your domain and
   sign in, as steps 9 to 11 of [deploy by hand](#deploy-reputecast-by-hand) describe.

</Steps>

ReputeCast creates its secret key on the disk at the first start, as `/var/lib/reputecast/secret.key`. Render
snapshots the disk every 24 hours and keeps each snapshot for seven days
([persistent disks](https://render.com/docs/disks), checked November 2026), but the image has no shell, so the file
can't be copied out over Render's shell or SSH. To keep a copy of the key outside Render, deploy by hand with
`REPUTECAST_SECRETS__KEY` instead.

## Deploy ReputeCast by hand

<Steps>

1. Generate the secret key and save it in your password manager. Both commands print a key in the same format (44
   characters ending in `=`): `openssl rand -base64 32` or
   `docker run --rm ghcr.io/hostrepute/reputecast:v1.6.0 secrets generate-key`.

2. In the Render Dashboard, click **+ New** → **Postgres**. Enter a **Name** such as `reputecast-db`, choose a
   **Region**, keep **PostgreSQL Version** at 18, choose a compute plan other than **Free** and click
   **Create Database**. Leave **Database** and **User** empty: Render generates them.

3. When the database's status is **Available**, click **Connect** at the top of its page and copy the
   **Internal Database URL** on the **Internal** tab. It works only for services in the same region. Keep it as it is
   (ReputeCast uses TLS when the database offers it), or add `?sslmode=require` to refuse unencrypted connections.

4. Click **+ New** → **Web Service**. Under **Source Code**, click **Existing Image**, enter the **Image URL**
   `ghcr.io/hostrepute/reputecast:v1.6.0` and click **Connect**. The image is public: it needs no credential.

5. Enter a **Name** such as `reputecast` (it becomes part of the service's `onrender.com` address), choose the
   database's **Region** and a **Compute Plan** other than **Free**.

6. Under **Environment Variables**, add each line below as a **Key** and a **Value**, with your own values:

   ```sh title="Environment Variables"
   PORT=8080
   REPUTECAST_DATABASE__URL=paste-the-internal-database-url
   REPUTECAST_PUBLIC_URL=https://news.example.com
   REPUTECAST_ADMIN_EMAIL=you@company.com
   REPUTECAST_SECRETS__KEY=paste-the-key-from-step-1
   ```

   - `PORT=8080` tells Render where ReputeCast listens; Render's default is 10000. The image listens on 8080 whatever
     `PORT` says, because it sets `REPUTECAST_HTTP__ADDR=:8080`, which wins over `PORT`. ReputeCast also listens on
     9464 for Prometheus metrics, which stay private: Render forwards only the `PORT` port
     ([port binding](https://render.com/docs/web-services#port-binding), checked November 2026).
   - `REPUTECAST_PUBLIC_URL` is your domain's URL, without a trailing slash. To use the `onrender.com` address
     instead, copy it from the top of the service's page once the service exists and change the variable.
   - `REPUTECAST_ADMIN_EMAIL` is the first Owner's email, used only while the database has no users. With
     `REPUTECAST_SECRETS__KEY` set, ReputeCast never reads or writes a `secret.key` file.
   - Render doesn't document the address range its load balancer connects from, so leave
     `REPUTECAST_HTTP__TRUSTED_PROXIES` unset with 1.6.0 ([trusted proxies](/deploy/overview/#trusted-proxies)). The
     Blueprint sets the `private` keyword instead, which trusts Render's private network. Available from the next
     release after 1.6.0. Other settings: [configuration](/self-hosting/configuration/).

7. Expand **Advanced** at the bottom of the form and set **Health Check Path** to `/readyz`. Optionally, click
   **Add disk**, set **Mount path** to `/var/lib/reputecast` and **Size** to 1 GB. The disk keeps the files you upload
   for digital products, lead magnets and audience exports, which are otherwise lost at every deploy and restart. With a disk, Render
   stops the running instance before it starts a new one (no zero-downtime deploys) and the service can't scale out.

8. Click **Deploy Web Service**. Render pulls the image and starts ReputeCast, which applies the database migrations
   and creates the first Owner. Render sends traffic to the new instance once `/readyz` answers `200`.

9. Open the service's **Logs** page and search for `temporary`. The matching line holds the first Owner's temporary
   password, part of this banner:

   ```txt title="Logs"
     ReputeCast is installed.

     Sign in at  https://news.example.com
     Email       you@company.com
     Password    SHLn-zHfR-cf6j-NEm5   (temporary: you'll replace it at first sign-in)
   ```

10. On the service's **Settings** page, under **Custom Domains**, click **+ Add Custom Domain**, enter
    `news.example.com` and click **Save**. At your DNS host, create the `CNAME` record Render shows (it points to the
    service's `onrender.com` host name) and remove any `AAAA` record for the name. Click **Verify**: Render issues the
    TLS certificate and redirects HTTP to HTTPS.

11. Open the public URL and sign in. The [first sign-in](/self-hosting/first-run/) asks you to replace the password,
    add a passkey or an authenticator app, save recovery codes, confirm the instance basics and create a workspace.

</Steps>

If you add the domain after the first sign-in, change the public URL in
<UiPath path="shell.userMenuInstanceAdmin > admin.instance" /> too: the address saved there wins over
`REPUTECAST_PUBLIC_URL`, and saving it moves the provider webhooks.

<Aside type="caution">
  Keep a copy of `REPUTECAST_SECRETS__KEY` outside Render: without it, the provider credentials stored in the database
  can't be decrypted.
</Aside>

## Check that it works

- The service's **Deploys** page marks the deploy **Live**: the `/readyz` health check passed.
- `https://news.example.com/readyz` answers `ready`, or 503 with the reason, such as `database unavailable` when
  ReputeCast can't reach PostgreSQL. `/healthz` answers `ok`.
- Render keeps calling `/readyz` every few seconds. After 15 seconds of failures it stops sending traffic to the
  instance; after 60 seconds it restarts it ([monitoring](/self-hosting/monitoring/)).

## Upgrade

1. Read the release notes and [back up](/self-hosting/backups/). Paid Render Postgres databases also have
   point-in-time recovery.
2. On the service's **Settings** page, in the **Deploy** section, change the image URL's tag, for example to
   `ghcr.io/hostrepute/reputecast:v1.6.0`, and save. A service created from the Blueprint runs `latest`: click
   **Manual Deploy** → **Deploy latest reference** to pull it again.
3. If no deploy starts, click **Manual Deploy** → **Deploy latest reference**: Render pulls the image of the tag now
   set. ReputeCast applies new migrations when it starts. With a disk, expect a few seconds of downtime. Upgrading to
   1.3.0 converts `delivery_events` to monthly partitions and holds the migration lock while it runs
   ([upgrade to 1.3.0](/self-hosting/upgrades/#upgrade-to-130)).

See [upgrade ReputeCast](/self-hosting/upgrades/).

## Troubleshooting

- **The deploy fails because Render detects no open port, or the site answers `404 page not found`**: `PORT` isn't
  `8080`, so Render found no port to use or forwards to another one, such as the metrics port 9464. Correct it on the
  service's **Environment** page and choose **Save and deploy**.
- **The deploy never goes live**: `/readyz` doesn't answer `ready`, and after 15 minutes Render cancels the deploy
  ([health checks](https://render.com/docs/health-checks), checked November 2026). Read the logs: the migrations may
  still be running (a large upgrade such as 1.3.0's), or the workers didn't start. `database unavailable` usually means
  the database is in another region, and its internal URL works only within its own region. A region can't be changed:
  create the web service again in the database's region.
- **The logs show no `temporary` line**: the log explorer shows the **Last hour** by default; choose a longer range.
  Hobby workspaces keep logs for 7 days. ReputeCast prints the banner only on its first start, while the database has
  no users.
- **An SMTP sending provider can't connect**: Render blocks outbound port 25 on every service, and ports 465 and 587
  on Free web services. Use port 587 or 465 on a paid plan, or a provider with an HTTPS API. Render forwards one HTTP
  port per web service, so the [SMTP relay](/transactional/smtp-relay/) can't accept connections on Render.
