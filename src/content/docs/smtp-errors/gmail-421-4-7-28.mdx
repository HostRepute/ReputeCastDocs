Gmail 421 4.7.28 is a temporary refusal: Gmail has seen an unusual rate of email, often unsolicited, from your IP
address, your domain or a link domain in your messages, and slows that mail down until the rate looks normal again. This
page explains the reply and how to bring your sending back to a pace Gmail accepts.

## What Gmail means

Gmail sends the code with one of several texts. The most common one names the sending IP address:

> Gmail has detected an unusual rate of email originating from your IP address _ip-address_. To protect our users from
> spam, email sent from your IP address has been temporarily rate limited.

The other variants name what Gmail is limiting:

- your IP netblock ("unusual rate of unsolicited email originating from your IP Netblock")
- your DKIM domain or your SPF domain ("unusual rate of unsolicited email originating from your DKIM domain")
- a link domain ("unusual rate of unsolicited email containing one of your URL domains")
- your IP address, blocked for now instead of rate limited ("an unusual amount of unsolicited email originating from
  your IP address")
- repeated Message-IDs ("this sender exceeded the quota for sending messages that have the same Message-ID")

Each variant points to Google's [email sender guidelines](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/81126). Source:
[Gmail SMTP errors and codes](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/support/troubleshooting/gmail-smtp-errors-and-codes),
Google Workspace Help. Checked October 2026.

## Why it happens

Gmail rate limits when your volume, or the share of your mail its users don't want, grows faster than your
[sender reputation](/reference/glossary/#sender-reputation) supports. Common causes:

- A jump in volume from an IP address, domain or sending provider that hasn't been [warmed up](/providers/warm-up/).
- Mail to people who didn't ask for it or stopped reading it, which raises the spam rate. Google asks senders to keep the
  spam rate reported in Postmaster Tools below 0.3% ([email sender guidelines](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/81126),
  checked October 2026).
- A link domain in your content that Gmail sees in a lot of unwanted mail (the URL-domain variant).

## How ReputeCast classifies it

- While the sending provider retries, each refused attempt arrives as a [deferral](/reference/glossary/#deferral): a
  <UiPath path="providers.events.optionDeferred" /> event in the event log. Deferrals don't appear in the campaign
  report's bounces table.
- Deferrals count toward the deferral rate of the Gmail mailbox provider during a send. Above 5.0% (fixed; it counts
  after 500 messages to Gmail and at least 5 deferrals, with about 90% confidence), ReputeCast
  [auto-pauses](/reference/glossary/#auto-pause) the Gmail share of the campaign, or the whole campaign when the pause
  scope is set to the campaign.
- If the sending provider stops retrying, it reports a bounce. Gmail's text contains the word "spam", so the
  [bounce class](/reference/glossary/#bounce-class) is `spam_block` and the report row is
  <UiPath path="reports.bounceType.blocked" />. A bounce with a 4xx reply is recorded as a soft bounce and doesn't
  suppress the address, unless the sending provider itself calls it permanent (Amazon SES and Resend follow their own
  bounce type; see [when ReputeCast suppresses an address](/smtp-errors/#when-reputecast-suppresses-an-address)).
- The Message-ID variant, or the bare code without Gmail's text, is `temporary`
  (<UiPath path="reports.bounceType.deferralExpired" />).

[SMTP error codes](/smtp-errors/#how-reputecast-picks-a-bounce-class) explains the classifier.

## Before you begin

- `providers:view` to read the event log.
- `providers:manage` to change the throttles in <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.providers" />.
- `campaigns:send` to resume an auto-paused send.

## Recover from the rate limit

<Steps>

1. Find the variant. Open <UiPath path="nav.providers > providers.tabEvents" />, set
   <UiPath path="providers.events.typeFilter" /> to <UiPath path="providers.events.optionDeferred" /> and search for a
   Gmail recipient. <UiPath path="providers.events.colCode" /> shows Gmail's text, which says whether Gmail limits an IP
   address, a domain or a link.
2. Slow down to Gmail. Gmail has no throttle by default. In
   <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.providers > senders.providers.throttlesTitle" />, set a Gmail rate in messages
   per second. If a send is already auto-paused, the [live monitor](/campaigns/live-monitor/) recommends a lower rate
   for the Gmail share; resume with that change.
3. Send to engaged readers first. Contacts who stopped opening raise the share of unwanted mail. Use the
   [list health tiers](/audience/list-health/) and let Send Readiness exclude unengaged contacts when it warns about
   them.
4. If the IP address, domain or sending provider is new, follow a [warm-up plan](/providers/warm-up/) instead of sending
   the full list at once.
5. For the URL-domain variant, review the links in the campaign. [Content checks](/campaigns/content-checks/) flag
   shortened and blocklisted links; link to your own domain instead of a public link shortener.
6. For the DKIM-domain and SPF-domain variants, watch your domain's spam rate in Google Postmaster Tools. ReputeCast
   shows Postmaster data on <UiPath path="nav.deliverability > placement.health" /> once it receives it; see
   [monitor domain and provider health](/deliverability/health/).

</Steps>

## Check that it worked

- On the next send, the live monitor's Gmail deferral rate stays low and the send isn't auto-paused.
- The event log shows delivered events for Gmail recipients instead of new 421 4.7.28 deferrals.

## Troubleshooting

- **Deferrals continue for days.** Lower the Gmail throttle further and send only to your most engaged contacts until
  the deferrals stop, then raise the volume step by step.
- **The event log shows the code without Gmail's text.** Some sending providers report only the code. The class is then
  `temporary`; the fix is the same.
- **Complaints are high as well.** Read [bounces, complaints and provider limits](/deliverability/bounces-and-complaints/)
  and check that every message has [one-click unsubscribe](/deliverability/one-click-unsubscribe/).
