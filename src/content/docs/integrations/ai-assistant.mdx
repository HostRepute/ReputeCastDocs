ReputeCast's AI assistant is a chat panel inside the app. It answers questions about ReputeCast, explains the reports
and drafts you attach, and hands work to the screen that owns it: a proposal you review in the composer, an agent task
you start yourself, or a link. Its own tools never send, schedule, publish, approve or change settings. It runs on your
workspace's [AI models](/integrations/ai-models/), or on your own [personal key](/reference/glossary/#personal-key)
when managers allow it. Available from 1.8.0.

<Aside type="note">
  Upstream tested the assistant with a development demo model and fake MCP servers, not with each provider's models:
  how good its answers are depends on the model your workspace picks for it.
</Aside>

## Before you begin

- You need `ai:use` (Owner, Admin and Editor hold it). API keys and [MCP agents](/developers/mcp/) can't use the
  assistant.
- Someone with `integrations:manage` must [turn the assistant on](/integrations/ai-models/#turn-on-the-assistant) (it
  is off in every workspace until then) and pick a model for <UiPath path="ai.models.featureAssistant" /> that passed
  its [tool check](/integrations/ai-models/#the-tool-check).
- To use the tools of outside services, such as an issue tracker or a wiki, a manager connects
  [MCP servers](/integrations/mcp-servers/) first.

## Open the assistant

- <UiPath path="assistant.launcher" /> in the top bar, next to the bell.
- <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd> on a Mac, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd> elsewhere. It opens and closes the panel, also
  while you type. See [keyboard shortcuts](/reference/keyboard-shortcuts/).
- <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>): type your question and pick **Ask the assistant: …**. The
  question moves into the assistant's composer, unsent.
- <UiPath path="assistant.launcher.ask" /> on a [report's](/campaigns/reports/) insights, on a
  [Send Readiness](/campaigns/send-readiness/) row and on a composer warning. The panel opens with that report or draft
  suggested as context; nothing is shared until you attach it.

## Ask a question

A thread is one conversation, with its own model, limits and attached context.
<UiPath path="assistant.threadsTitle" /> in the panel's header lists yours, the most recently active first;
<UiPath path="assistant.newThread" /> starts another. The first message names a thread, and
<UiPath path="assistant.rename" /> and <UiPath path="assistant.delete" /> are in each thread's actions menu.
<UiPath path="assistant.search" /> looks through the titles of your 500 most recently active threads.

<Steps>

1. Open the assistant on the screen your question is about. An empty thread offers three suggestions for that screen,
   for example on a report <UiPath path="assistant.suggestReport1" />, <UiPath path="assistant.suggestReport2" /> and
   <UiPath path="assistant.suggestReport3" />. A suggestion goes into the message box; nothing is sent, and nothing
   costs anything, until you send it.

2. Write your question and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> adds a line, and nothing is sent
   while an input method is still composing a word. A message can be up to 20,000 characters.

3. The answer streams in: <UiPath path="assistant.queued" />, then <UiPath path="assistant.answering" />, then done.
   The answer runs on the server, so closing the panel, reloading the page or losing the connection doesn't stop it:
   reopen the thread and the answer is where it got to. A thread answers one message at a time.

</Steps>

Answers are Markdown: lists, tables and code with <UiPath path="assistant.copy" />. Links show their domain and open in
a new tab without a referrer. The assistant loads no images: an image in an answer is a link that names its domain.

## Attach a report or a draft

The assistant reads only what you attach. On a campaign report, or on a draft campaign's edit or review screen, the
panel suggests it with <UiPath path="assistant.attach" />.

<Steps>

1. Choose <UiPath path="assistant.attach" /> next to the suggested report or draft.

2. <UiPath path="ai.draft.sharingTitle" /> names the model's provider and what goes to it. Choose
   <UiPath path="ai.draft.sharingConfirm" />.

</Steps>

- A **report** can be attached once its campaign finished sending at least 72 hours ago. It shares its fixed totals as
  they were when you attached it. Counts are rounded down to tens and small
  numbers are withheld. No recipients, no links and no diagnosis of delivery.
- A **draft campaign** shares its subject, preheader and visible text. Links, images and Liquid tokens reach the model
  as placeholders, so their destinations stay on your instance and `{{ subscriber.first_name }}` stays as written.
- The title of each attached resource goes with every request of the thread.

A thread holds up to 10 attached resources; remove one with its chip's **×**. If you can no longer read a resource, its
chip says <UiPath path="assistant.unavailable" /> and the assistant doesn't read it: every read checks your access
again. Only reports and email campaigns can be attached. Your messages go to the model as you write them, except that
one with an email address, a phone number, or what looks like a password, key or token isn't sent: "Your message wasn't
sent: …" (`ai_assistant_private_data`). Remove it and send again.

What a thread read stays tied to where it came from: each tool result to its campaign, report or MCP server, and each
answer to everything its request carried. When you can no longer open one of them (your role changed, the campaign was
deleted, the server is no longer allowed for you), the tool's result isn't sent to the model again, an answer that used
it shows "This answer used something you can no longer open, so it's hidden.", and a summary of earlier messages made
from it is left out too.

## Pick the model and MCP servers

**Model: …** in the panel's header opens <UiPath path="assistant.modelMenu" />. Models are grouped as
<UiPath path="assistant.groupWorkspace" /> and <UiPath path="assistant.groupPersonal" /> (your own key, when managers
allow personal keys). Each shows what it can do (<UiPath path="assistant.capTools" />,
<UiPath path="assistant.capImages" />, <UiPath path="assistant.capStreaming" />), its price per million tokens and whose
key it uses. A model the assistant can't use says why: its account is disconnected, its key was rejected, it failed or
hasn't run the tool check, or personal keys are off.

<Screenshot
  id="assistant-models"
  alt="The Choose a model menu: under Workspace, Claude Sonnet 5.5, checked, from Anthropic on the workspace key with Tools, Images and Streaming at $2 in and $10 out per million tokens; and qwen3-32b on an OpenAI-compatible server, unavailable because its tool check hasn't run, with the note that a manager runs it in Settings, Integrations, AI models."

A new thread uses your own pick for the assistant under <UiPath path="ai.models.yourPicksTitle" />, or else the
workspace's. Changing a thread's model applies to its next answers; earlier answers keep the model that wrote them.

When managers have connected [MCP servers](/integrations/mcp-servers/) you may use, the header also has a server picker,
<UiPath path="assistant.mcp.noServers" /> until you pick one. Its menu marks each server
<UiPath path="assistant.mcp.connected" />, <UiPath path="assistant.mcp.shared" />,
<UiPath path="assistant.mcp.noSignIn" /> or <UiPath path="assistant.mcp.needsSignIn" />; **Sign in to …** takes you to
the server's sign-in and back. The thread may call the approved tools of the servers you pick: tools that only read run
without asking, and tools that write [ask you every time](#confirm-tools-that-write). A request offers at most 40 tools,
ReputeCast's own first.

## Costs and limits

Every answer is one or more requests to the model, paid by the model's account. The header shows the thread's cost
against its limit, such as "$0.04 of $2.00", which turns to a warning at 80%; hover it for the tool calls used.

What costs a request:

- Each answer. An answer that calls tools takes several requests, at most 8 for one message.
- **Subject ideas** and **Rewrite** for an attached draft, which make their own request with their feature's model.
- When a thread gets long (more than 60% of the model's context window), earlier messages are summarized in a request
  of its own, and the thread shows <UiPath path="assistant.summaryMarker" />.
- **Retry**, which sends a new request.

The fixed suggestions, the HostRepute card and attaching cost nothing.

<LimitsTable area="assistant" />

- At a thread's limit, the thread shows <UiPath path="assistant.threadCapTitle" />: start a new thread. Managers change
  the limits for threads started afterwards.
- At the workspace's monthly cap, workspace models pause: <UiPath path="assistant.monthlyTitle" />. With a personal key,
  you can go on with it.
- Your personal key has a cap of its own ($10 a month by default) and never counts against the workspace's.
- More answers than allowed at once wait as <UiPath path="assistant.queued" /> until another answer ends.

Every request is reserved at its largest possible cost before it is sent, and settled with the token counts the
provider reports. When a provider doesn't report them, the request counts at its maximum ("cost unknown for 1 request,
counted at its maximum") until a manager [reconciles it](/integrations/ai-models/#reserved-requests-and-unknown-usage).

## Stop an answer

While the assistant answers, <UiPath path="assistant.send" /> becomes <UiPath path="assistant.stop" />. After Stop, no
further model request or tool call starts for that message, and a call waiting for your confirmation never runs. What
was written so far stays. A request that was already answering may still be charged by the provider; the thread says
<UiPath path="assistant.stoppedNote" />

## Confirm tools that write

A tool that writes outside ReputeCast, an MCP server's tool that a manager marked
<UiPath path="mcpservers.kindWrites" />, never runs on its own. The answer stops at **Waiting for you · Server · tool**
with <UiPath path="assistant.mcp.goTo" />, and shows a [confirmation](/reference/glossary/#confirmation) card:

- the server and the tool, and <UiPath path="assistant.mcp.badge" />;
- **Asked by** the model, with the tool's name for the model;
- the exact arguments that will be sent, as JSON, with <UiPath path="assistant.copy" />;
- <UiPath path="assistant.mcp.deny" /> and <UiPath path="assistant.mcp.allow" />.

<Screenshot
  id="assistant-confirmation"
  alt="A confirmation card in the assistant: Acme Tracker · create_issue, the badge Writes outside ReputeCast, Check exactly what will be sent to Acme Tracker, Asked by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with the tool's name for the model, the exact JSON arguments with Copy, the note that nothing is sent until you allow it, and the Deny and Allow once buttons."
/>

<UiPath path="assistant.mcp.allow" /> sends that call, with those arguments, once. <UiPath path="assistant.mcp.deny" />
tells the model the call was refused, and nothing is sent. Either way the answer goes on. While a call waits, the
thread takes no new message: decide, or stop the answer.

You don't have to keep the panel open:

- You get a notification, <UiPath path="notify.event.ai_assistant_confirmation" />, that names the server and the tool,
  never the arguments. It is in the app; email for it is off unless you turn it on in your
  [notification preferences](/team/notifications/). Opening it opens the panel on that thread, or the thread's full page
  on screens without the panel.
- <UiPath path="assistant.launcher" /> shows a dot while that notification is unread, also after a reload.
- The notification goes away once you decide, stop the answer or delete the thread, or when the call expires.

A call nobody decides on expires after 24 hours with nothing sent, and the answer ends with
<UiPath path="assistant.confirmExpiredTitle" />. If a manager blocked or changed the tool after the model asked, the card
says so and Allow once can't run it; Deny lets the assistant go on. There is no "always allow": a thread can hold what
you attached and what other tools brought in from outside at the same time, and text from outside can steer a model.
Checking each call's exact arguments is the safeguard.

## What the assistant can and can't do

Its tools, as the thread's tool rows name them:

<TableScroll label="The assistant's tools">

| Row | What it does | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| <UiPath path="assistant.tool.searchHelp" /> | Searches ReputeCast's built-in help articles | — |
| <UiPath path="assistant.tool.describeScreen" /> | Says what a screen is for | — |
| <UiPath path="assistant.tool.suggestLink" /> | A card that opens a screen you may use. Nothing changes from there. | — |
| <UiPath path="assistant.tool.readAttached" /> | Reads an attached campaign or report | Something attached |
| <UiPath path="assistant.tool.readReport" /> | Reads an attached report's fixed totals | `reports:view`, a report attached |
| <UiPath path="assistant.tool.proposeEdit" /> | A proposal for an attached draft. <UiPath path="assistant.reviewProposal" /> opens it in the composer, and nothing changes until you accept it. | `campaigns:edit`, Draft from a brief on |
| <UiPath path="assistant.tool.draftAgentTask" /> | A brief for the [AI agent](/integrations/ai-agent/). <UiPath path="assistant.startAgent" /> opens it, and you review it and confirm sharing before a run starts. | The agent on |
| Subject ideas, Rewrite | The composer's [suggestions](/integrations/ai-suggestions/) for an attached draft | `campaigns:edit`, the feature on |
| Server · tool | An approved [MCP tool](/integrations/mcp-servers/) | The server picked for the thread |

</TableScroll>

The model sees only the tools you may use now, and every call is checked again as it runs. The assistant can't:

- send, schedule, publish or approve anything, or change settings: none of its own tools does. Anything else goes
  through MCP tools a manager approved, and a tool that writes runs only when you allow that call;
- read contacts, recipient lists or exports: there is no tool for them;
- read a campaign or report you didn't attach;
- load images.

### Reputation questions go to HostRepute

A message that asks to diagnose sender reputation (a blocklisting, a domain's or IP's reputation, spam traps,
Postmaster Tools or SNDS, why mail lands in spam) gets the <UiPath path="assistant.hostReputeTitle" /> card at once,
without a model request and at no cost. It offers <UiPath path="assistant.openHostRepute" /> and
<UiPath path="assistant.openDeliverability" />; nothing is sent to HostRepute. Questions about setting things up, such
as [DKIM, SPF or DMARC](/deliverability/spf-dkim-dmarc/), are the assistant's to answer.

## Privacy and retention

- **Only you can read your threads.** Nobody else in the workspace can open them, managers included.
- People who can view integrations see each member's assistant usage and cost in the AI activity log, never content.
  The [audit log](/team/audit-log/) records that you started, renamed or deleted a thread, what you attached (its kind
  and ID) and the calls you allowed or denied, never titles, messages or arguments.
- Thread content is encrypted at rest with the instance's secret key. Someone who holds both the database and that key
  can read it.
- Content is deleted 30 days after the thread's last activity. The thread then shows
  <UiPath path="assistant.expiredTitle" />; its usage stays, without content. <UiPath path="assistant.delete" /> removes
  a thread's messages, attached resources and events at once.
- When you leave a workspace or are removed from it, your threads there are deleted. Deactivation keeps them.
- What you send reaches the model's provider, and an MCP tool's arguments reach that server. Their own terms decide how
  long they keep it. Deleting a thread doesn't undo what a tool you allowed did elsewhere.

## Check that it worked

- <UiPath path="assistant.launcher" /> in the top bar opens the panel, and the header names a model.
- A question gets an answer, and the cost in the header moves from $0.00.

## Troubleshooting

Each card in the panel says what happened and what to do. When the fix is a manager's, its button names who can do it.
Every <UiPath path="assistant.retry" /> sends a new request with its own cost.

<TableScroll label="The assistant's cards">

| Card | What happened | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| <UiPath path="assistant.noModelTitle" /> | The workspace has no model account. | A manager [adds one](/integrations/ai-models/#add-a-model-account): <UiPath path="assistant.noModelAction" />. |
| <UiPath path="assistant.noUsableTitle" /> | No model can be used now, most often because none passed its tool check. | <UiPath path="assistant.switchModel" />, or a manager runs the [tool check](/integrations/ai-models/#the-tool-check). |
| <UiPath path="assistant.noAccessTitle" /> | Your role doesn't have `ai:use`. | Ask someone who manages the team. |
| <UiPath path="assistant.offTitle" /> | A manager switched the assistant off. Threads are kept, but the panel doesn't open them meanwhile. | A manager [switches it on](/integrations/ai-models/#turn-on-the-assistant): <UiPath path="assistant.offAction" />. |
| <UiPath path="assistant.expiredTitle" /> | Its content was deleted 30 days after its last activity. | <UiPath path="assistant.newThreadAction" />. |
| <UiPath path="assistant.lostTitle" /> | Your role lost `ai:use` during the answer. What was written stays. | Ask someone who manages the team. |
| <UiPath path="assistant.threadCapTitle" /> | The next request wouldn't fit the thread's tool calls, tokens or cost. | <UiPath path="assistant.newThreadAction" />. Nothing is lost. |
| <UiPath path="assistant.monthlyTitle" /> | The workspace used its monthly cap. | Use your personal key if you have one, or a manager raises the cap. |
| <UiPath path="assistant.personalCapTitle" /> | Your personal key used its monthly cap. | <UiPath path="assistant.switchModel" /> to a workspace model, or wait for next month. |
| **… rejected the key** | The provider refused the key. Its models stop; others still work. | For your own key, <UiPath path="assistant.replaceKey" />; for a workspace key, a manager replaces it. Or <UiPath path="assistant.switchModel" />. |
| **… is limiting requests** | The provider's rate limit for this key. | Wait, then <UiPath path="assistant.retry" />. |
| **… is unavailable** | The provider failed, didn't answer in time, or answered too much. The answer so far is kept. | <UiPath path="assistant.retry" /> or <UiPath path="assistant.switchModel" />. |
| <UiPath path="assistant.refusalTitle" /> | The model refused the request. | <UiPath path="assistant.editMessage" />, or ask about something you attach. |
| <UiPath path="assistant.modelGoneTitle" /> | The thread's model was removed. | <UiPath path="assistant.switchModel" />, then send again. |
| <UiPath path="assistant.disconnectedTitle" /> | The account of the thread's model has no connection. Nothing was sent. | <UiPath path="assistant.openModels" />, or <UiPath path="assistant.switchModel" />. |
| **… hasn't passed its tool check** | Nothing was sent, for example because the model's key was replaced. | <UiPath path="assistant.runCheck" /> (your own model, or as a manager), then <UiPath path="assistant.retry" />. |
| <UiPath path="assistant.personalOffTitle" /> | The thread uses your own key, and managers turned personal keys off. Nothing was sent. | <UiPath path="assistant.switchModel" /> to a workspace model. |
| <UiPath path="assistant.notConfiguredTitle" /> | There's no key for your own model, or no model account is connected. Nothing was sent. | <UiPath path="assistant.addKey" />, or <UiPath path="assistant.switchModel" />. |
| <UiPath path="assistant.confirmExpiredTitle" /> | A call waited 24 hours for your decision. Nothing was sent. | <UiPath path="assistant.retry" /> sends your message again. |
| <UiPath path="assistant.interruptedTitle" /> | The worker answering it stopped (a restart or a deploy), or no worker took it within 30 minutes. | <UiPath path="assistant.retry" />. If it keeps happening, tell whoever runs the instance ([scaling](/self-hosting/scaling/#the-ai-assistant)). |
| <UiPath path="assistant.failedTitle" /> | Any other reason, named in brackets. | <UiPath path="assistant.retry" />. |
| <UiPath path="assistant.contactsTitle" /> | The model tried to read contacts, recipients or exports. | <UiPath path="assistant.contactsAction" /> asks about segment sizes instead. |
| <UiPath path="assistant.reconnectTitle" /> | The connection to the server dropped. The answer continues on the server. | Wait, or <UiPath path="assistant.reconnectNow" />. |

</TableScroll>

Cards for MCP servers, such as <UiPath path="assistant.mcp.changedTitle" /> or **Sign in to … again**, are on
[MCP servers](/integrations/mcp-servers/#troubleshooting). Notes in the conversation without a card:
<UiPath path="assistant.stoppedNote" /> <UiPath path="assistant.partialNote" /> <UiPath path="assistant.lengthNote" />
