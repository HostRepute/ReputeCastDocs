With ReputeCast you run Shopify email marketing self-hosted, and WooCommerce too: connect your store and customers who
agreed to marketing join a list, orders are credited to the email that brought them, abandoned checkouts can start an
automation, and product blocks fill emails from your catalog. ReputeCast never stores addresses, phone numbers, payment
details or order notes.

## Before you begin

- You need `integrations:manage` (Owner or Admin) to connect, configure, sync and disconnect a store. Everyone with
  `integrations:view` can see the store page. Read [Connect other tools](/integrations/overview/).
- Shopify: you need to create a custom app in Shopify's Dev Dashboard.
- WooCommerce: you need to create a REST API key with Read/Write access, and the ReputeCast WordPress plugin for
  consent and abandoned checkouts (see [Connect WordPress](/integrations/wordpress/)).

## Connect a store

Open <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.integrations" />, find the <UiPath path="integrations.groupCommerce" /> group
and choose <UiPath path="integrations.connect" /> on the Shopify or WooCommerce card.

<Tabs syncKey="store">
<TabItem label="Shopify">

<Steps>

1. **Store address.** Enter your shop's address, such as `acme-merch.myshopify.com` or a custom domain that points to
   Shopify.

2. **Create the app.** Choose **Open the Dev Dashboard** and create a custom app. Paste the **App URL** and the
   **Allowed redirection URL** the dialog shows. Under "Protected customer data", request name and email; when Shopify
   asks why, "Email marketing to customers who agreed to it" is accurate. ReputeCast asks for the `read_customers`,
   `read_orders` and `read_products` scopes, `write_customers` if you want unsubscribes passed back to Shopify, and
   `read_inventory`, which reports stock changes as they happen for back-in-stock flows.

3. **Paste its keys.** Copy the app's **Client ID** and **Client secret** (it starts with `shpss_`). The secret is
   write-only.

4. **Install.** Choose the install button: Shopify opens its own approval page. Approve within 30 minutes.

5. **Choose what syncs.** Products; customers who accepted marketing, and the list they join; orders from the last 3, 6
   or 12 months (12 by default; you can't change it later); abandoned checkouts; and whether to tell Shopify when
   someone unsubscribes in ReputeCast. Choose **Start sync**.

</Steps>

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="WooCommerce">

<Steps>

1. **Store address.** Enter the address of the WordPress site WooCommerce runs on.

2. **REST API key.** In WordPress, open **WooCommerce › Settings › Advanced › REST API**, add a key with **Read/Write**
   access and paste the consumer key (`ck_…`) and consumer secret (`cs_…`). A read-only key is refused, because
   ReputeCast registers webhooks with it.

3. **Webhooks.** ReputeCast registers webhooks for orders and products, each with its own secret.

4. **ReputeCast plugin.** Install and connect the [ReputeCast WordPress plugin](/integrations/wordpress/) on the same
   site, then choose **Check again** and **Finish**. **Connect without the plugin** is possible, but then only
   products and orders sync: no customer joins a list, abandoned checkouts aren't reported and the store shows
   "Needs attention".

</Steps>

With WooCommerce active, the plugin adds an unchecked checkbox under the checkout's email with exactly "Email me about
new products and offers from the store. Unsubscribe any time." The order keeps the answer, the text and the time, and
that is the consent ReputeCast records. The first import has no separate customers step: consent comes with each order.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Customers and consent

- Only customers who accepted marketing in the store join a list: by default a new list named after the store, or a
  list you pick. The consent record says where it came from ("Shopify: accepted marketing at checkout", or the
  WooCommerce checkbox text) with the date and opt-in level. Read
  [Lists, consent and double opt-in](/audience/lists-and-consent/).
- Suppressed addresses are never added. A contact who left the list rejoins only if their store consent is newer than
  their leaving.
- Customers who didn't accept marketing are kept only as a link to an existing contact, with no email or name; anyone
  else counts in the store's totals without their email.
- An unsubscribe in the store removes the customer from the list. With `write_customers` granted, an unsubscribe in
  ReputeCast is passed to Shopify.

ReputeCast stores email, name and country; consent with its date and opt-in level; orders, items and totals; and the
landing page and UTM tags. It never stores addresses, phone numbers, payment details or order notes.

When a contact is [erased](/audience/contacts/#erase-contacts), ReputeCast clears what the store told it about them:
the customer's email, name and country, the landing page and free-form UTM tags of their orders, their abandoned-cart
links and their store activity. Their orders and customer record stay without them, so revenue and customer counts
don't change.

## Attribution

An order is credited to the email whose link the buyer clicked last within the attribution window (5 days by default,
1–30 under **Attribution** on the store page). The click token on the order's landing page decides first; otherwise the
order's email is matched to a contact who clicked within the window. Each order is credited to one campaign or
automation step. Links to the store get UTM parameters and the click token unless you turn off **Tag links**.

For WooCommerce, the plugin keeps the click token from an email link (`?rc=…`) in a first-party cookie for 30 days and
saves it on the order. Read [Revenue metrics explained](/earn/revenue/) for how store revenue appears in reports.

## Automations and email blocks

Store triggers start only after the first import:

- **Order placed**, with filters for product, collection, a minimum total and "the contact's first order in the store
  only".
- **First order**.
- **Abandoned checkout**, after 1–24 hours (1 by default), only for contacts who accepted marketing in the store, and
  skipping contacts who got an abandoned-cart email in the last 7 days. WooCommerce needs the plugin for this trigger.
- The goal **Placed an order** ends a flow when the contact buys.

Starter emails ("You left something behind" and "Still deciding?") come with the abandoned-checkout starter. Read
[Triggers and steps](/automations/triggers-and-steps/).

In the composer, **Product card** (fill it from the store), **Product grid** (chosen products or the newest from a
collection; 2, 3, 4 or 6) and, in emails started by a store trigger, **Checkout items** show your products. Prices and
availability are read again when the email is sent.

## Discount codes

ReputeCast can create one-use discount codes in the store for the pools in
[Campaigns › Discount codes](/campaigns/discount-codes/), and each email then carries a code of its own. Available from
the next release after 1.6.0.

- **Shopify** asks for its own permission. On the store page, the <UiPath path="commerce.syncTitle" /> card's
  <UiPath path="coupons.storeRow" /> row says <UiPath path="coupons.storeNotAllowedBadge" /> until someone with
  `integrations:manage` chooses <UiPath path="coupons.allowDiscounts" />: Shopify asks you to approve
  `write_discounts` and `read_discounts` on its own page, then sends you back. Orders, products and customers keep
  syncing meanwhile, and approving again never takes back a scope granted before. Once
  <UiPath path="coupons.storeAllowedBadge" />, the row says who allowed it and when and which pools create their codes
  there, and the page's header lists <UiPath path="coupons.storeCap" /> among the store's capabilities.
- **WooCommerce** creates coupons with the store's Read/Write REST API key; there is nothing to allow.

Codes ReputeCast creates are real discounts in the store: anyone who has one can use it once, even from a forwarded
email. When an order uses a pool's code, the order marks it redeemed (test orders redeem nothing), and the pool shows
sent and redeemed codes with the revenue of those orders.

## Website tracking and store flows

Add the store's own site to [website tracking](/integrations/website-tracking/) and link it to the store, so product
views and add-to-carts on the site name the store's products. On Shopify, the site's snippet can be a custom pixel; on
WooCommerce, the [WordPress plugin](/integrations/wordpress/) 1.5.0 sends them. Available from the next release after
1.6.0.

- [Store flows](/automations/store-flows/) start on <UiPath path="commerce.flows.startsViewed" />,
  <UiPath path="commerce.flows.startsCarted" />, <UiPath path="commerce.flows.kindBackInStock" /> and
  <UiPath path="commerce.flows.kindPriceDrop" />. With `read_inventory`,
  Shopify reports stock changes through the `inventory_levels/update` webhook; without it, the products changed since
  the last check are read every 15 minutes while a back-in-stock or price-drop flow watches the store.
- People can ask to hear when a product is back from the site (`reputecast('notifyWhenAvailable', {id})`) or through
  the API (`POST /commerce/stock-requests`, which needs `subscribers:create`), for an existing contact and a product of
  a connected store.
- Product grids in emails can show <UiPath path="commerce.flows.bestSellers" />, and each reader's
  <UiPath path="commerce.flows.recentlyViewed" />, <UiPath path="commerce.flows.boughtTogether" /> and, in store flows,
  <UiPath path="commerce.flows.viewed" />. Products bought together are rebuilt nightly from the store's last 180 days of
  orders.

## Sync and the store page

Webhooks keep the store up to date. When a webhook is missing, ReputeCast polls every 15 minutes and shows a banner, and
it reconciles everything nightly at 03:00 in the workspace time zone. **Sync now** runs a sync at once. The store page
also lists the webhooks (**Register all again**) and the store's activity.

## Disconnect

**Disconnect** removes ReputeCast's webhooks, pauses live automations that use the store's triggers (with the reason
"… is disconnected. Reconnect it to resume.") and keeps products and orders. To delete the store's data too, choose
**Delete store data** and type the store's name. Contacts the store added stay, with their consent.

**Reconnect** fetches the orders placed since, registers the webhooks again and resumes the flows it paused. If you
uninstall the app in Shopify or Shopify refuses the token, the store disconnects on its own, and members with
`integrations:manage` are notified.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Webhook … is missing"**: choose **Register all again** on the store page. Until then, ReputeCast polls every 15
  minutes.
- **WooCommerce needs attention**: the ReputeCast plugin isn't installed or connected on the store's site.
- **"This key is read-only"**: create a WooCommerce key with Read/Write access.
- **No customers joined the list**: only customers who accepted marketing join. For WooCommerce, check that the plugin's
  checkout checkbox is showing.
- **"… hasn't allowed discount codes yet"**: open the store's page and choose
  <UiPath path="coupons.allowDiscounts" /> (Shopify).
- **"The store's key can't create coupons"**: create a WooCommerce key with Read/Write access.
