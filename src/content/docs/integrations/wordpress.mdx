The "ReputeCast" WordPress newsletter plugin puts your ReputeCast signup forms on a WordPress site as a block or a
shortcode and sends every newly published post to ReputeCast as a campaign draft. With WPML or Polylang, a post's
translations become the draft's language versions; with WooCommerce, the checkout gets a marketing consent checkbox
(see [Connect Shopify or WooCommerce](/integrations/shopify-woocommerce/)).

## Before you begin

- WordPress 6.4 or later and PHP 8.1 or later.
- In ReputeCast you need `integrations:manage` (Owner or Admin). Each site gets its own key. Read
  [Connect other tools](/integrations/overview/).
- At least one [signup form](/forms/signup-forms/) in ReputeCast, if you want forms on the site.

## Connect a site

<Steps>

1. In ReputeCast, open <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.integrations > integrations.wordpress" /> and choose
   <UiPath path="integrations.connectSite" />.

2. Enter the site address (for example `blog.example.com`) and pick what the site may do: **List your signup forms**
   and **Create drafts from posts**. Copy the key: it's shown once.

3. Install the plugin. Choose <UiPath path="integrations.getPlugin" /> to download it, then in WordPress open
   **Plugins › Add New › Upload Plugin**, upload the zip and choose **Activate**. Once the plugin is in the WordPress
   plugin directory, you can also search for "ReputeCast" under **Plugins › Add New**.

4. In WordPress, open **Settings › ReputeCast**. Paste the ReputeCast address (the one you sign in at, for example
   `https://news.acme.io`) and the key, then choose **Connect**. The plugin checks the key, reports in and fetches your
   forms. ReputeCast lists the site right away.

</Steps>

The plugin stores the key encrypted with a key derived from the site's salts in `wp-config.php`, so a copy of the
database alone doesn't reveal it. **Settings › ReputeCast** shows it only masked (`rc_live_5d1e…7b20`).

## Add a signup form

- **Block**: insert the **ReputeCast signup form** block and pick a form. The block warns when the site isn't one of
  the form's allowed domains, when the form is a draft or paused, and when the form is gone. From the site's row in
  ReputeCast, **Allow** followed by the site's host adds the site to the form's allowed domains.
- **Shortcode**: paste a shortcode such as `[reputecast_form id="frm_8c2e1a"]`. **Settings › ReputeCast** lists each
  form's shortcode.

The plugin outputs the same container and public script as ReputeCast's embed code, and a popup form loads its script
only. A page view never calls ReputeCast from WordPress, and forms keep working after you disconnect. The block's
language setting, or else the page language from WPML or Polylang, decides which language the form shows.

## Turn posts into campaign drafts

On the site's row in ReputeCast, under **Post drafts**, choose **Set up a rule**: which categories, the audience,
template and sender, and who is notified. From then on, every post published on the site is sent to ReputeCast in a
background job (publishing never waits for ReputeCast), and the rule decides what becomes a draft. Updates, pages and
password-protected posts aren't sent.

With WPML or Polylang, the post in the site's default language leads and its published translations become language
versions of the draft. A translation published later joins the same draft. Read
[Send each reader their language](/campaigns/languages/).

## Site health

  id="integrations-wordpress"

Each site reports in when it connects, every 12 hours and with every post: the plugin, WordPress and PHP versions, the
feed URL, its categories and where forms are used. ReputeCast never polls a site on a schedule.

- A site that hasn't reported for 24 hours shows "Not heard from" and the integration needs attention.
- <UiPath path="integrations.checkNow" /> (`integrations:manage`) calls the plugin's status endpoint
  (`/wp-json/reputecast/v1/status`) with a request signed with the site's key, and waits up to 10 seconds.
- <UiPath path="integrations.statusUpdate" /> appears when a newer plugin version is out (ReputeCast checks the
  WordPress plugin directory when the instance's update check is on).

## Disconnect

- **In WordPress** (**Settings › ReputeCast** › **Disconnect**): the plugin forgets the key and stops its jobs; forms
  keep loading.
- **In ReputeCast** (the site's **Disconnect site**): revokes the site's key. Forms keep working through their public
  script; post drafts stop.

Do both to remove a site completely. Deactivating the plugin stops its jobs and keeps the connection; deleting it
removes its options, jobs and post data.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Not heard from"**: WP-Cron isn't running or the site can't reach ReputeCast. Open the site, check that
  **Settings › ReputeCast** shows it connected, and choose <UiPath path="integrations.checkNow" />.
- **Check now fails for a site on your own network**: ReputeCast refuses private addresses until an operator sets
  [`webhooks.allow_private_networks`](/reference/configuration/).
- **The block says the site isn't allowed**: add the site to the form's allowed domains (**Allow** on the site's row) or in the
  form's settings.
- **No draft after publishing a post**: the post doesn't match the rule's categories, it was an update, or the site's
  key can't create drafts. Check **Settings › ReputeCast**, which lists the last five posts sent and ReputeCast's answer.
