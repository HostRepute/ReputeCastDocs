The AI email generator in ReputeCast drafts one complete email, or an email flow that starts when someone joins a list,
from a brief you write. It uses your workspace's own model and [brand voice](/campaigns/brand-voice/), and shows the
result as a proposal: nothing is saved until you accept it, and accepting saves a draft, never a send. Available since
1.7.0. For work that spans several drafts, such as an email, a reminder and a web post, use the
[AI agent](/integrations/ai-agent/) instead.

## Before you begin

- The workspace needs a [connected model](/integrations/ai-models/) with both prices set, and the
  <UiPath path="ai.featureDraft" /> feature switched on (it is on by default). From 1.8.0,
  drafts use the model picked for the feature under
  [AI models](/integrations/ai-models/#pick-the-model-for-each-feature), and the
  [AI assistant](/integrations/ai-assistant/) can propose an edit to a draft you attach in a thread, which you review
  in the composer the same way.
- You need `ai:use` (Owner, Admin and Editor) and `campaigns:edit`, or `automations:edit` for a flow.
  `templates:view` lets you start from a template.
- A reverse proxy in front of ReputeCast must let a response take up to 10 minutes, or it cancels long drafts. See
  [long AI requests](/self-hosting/reverse-proxy/#long-ai-requests).

## Draft a campaign

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.campaigns > campaigns.newCampaign" />, pick <UiPath path="ai.draft.title" /> and choose
   <UiPath path="digests.continue" />.

   <Screenshot
     id="campaign-new-ai"
     alt="The New campaign dialog: Regular, then Draft from a brief (describe one email and review the proposed content before saving anything) and With the agent (turn a task into reviewed drafts), then Recurring digest, Text message, Push notification and WhatsApp message."
   >
     1. One email from a brief, on this page.
     2. Several drafts at once, with the [AI agent](/integrations/ai-agent/).
   </Screenshot>

2. Under <UiPath path="ai.draft.brief" />, write the goal, the facts and the call to action, in up to 4,000
   characters. Leave out contact details and credentials.

3. Give the <UiPath path="ai.draft.name" />, the <UiPath path="ai.draft.language" />, a
   <UiPath path="ai.draft.template" /> or <UiPath path="ai.draft.blank" />, and the voice under
   <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.voiceSelection" />. Put up to 10 complete HTTP(S) addresses under
   <UiPath path="ai.draft.links" />, one per line: the model gets placeholders, and the addresses go back into the
   email where it placed them.

4. Choose <UiPath path="ai.draft.generate" />, check what <UiPath path="ai.draft.sharingTitle" /> lists, and choose
   <UiPath path="ai.draft.sharingConfirm" />. The dialog waits for the model: up to three minutes per request, and up to
   three requests when the draft needs repairs. <UiPath path="ai.draft.stop" /> stops waiting, but the provider may
   still charge for the request.

   <Screenshot
     id="ai-draft-brief"
     alt="The Draft from a brief dialog: a proposal awaiting review, Scheduled rollouts, with Review proposal; below it the brief about scheduled flag changes, the draft name, English, Start with the brand kit, the latest saved voice and one link to preserve, and Generate proposal."
   >
     1. A proposal you can reopen without a new request.
     2. The brief.
     3. Name, language, template and voice.
     4. The links the email keeps.
   </Screenshot>

5. Review the proposal: the subject, the preheader, the exact MJML under <UiPath path="ai.draft.source" />, a preview
   and the [subject checks](/campaigns/content-checks/). The preview blocks external images, fonts and links, and
   personalization tokens stay as written.

   <Screenshot
     id="ai-draft-proposal"
     alt="A proposal marked Proposed, not saved: the model, its tokens and an estimate of less than a cent, saved voice revision 2 and the expiry; the subject Schedule flag changes for any time zone and its preheader; and a preview of the email with a Read the docs button, above Review later, Reject proposal and Accept and save draft."
   >
     1. The model, what it cost, which voice it used and when the proposal expires.
     2. Subject, preheader and the exact MJML to check, above a preview of the email.
     3. Your decision.
   </Screenshot>

6. Choose <UiPath path="ai.draft.accept" /> to create the draft and open it in the [composer](/campaigns/composer/),
   <UiPath path="ai.draft.reject" /> to drop it, or <UiPath path="ai.draft.later" /> to decide within 24 hours.

</Steps>

The server compiles and checks the email before you see it, and checks it again when you accept. The model never
writes the compliance footer or the brand header: ReputeCast puts the workspace's own back. A draft the model gets
wrong is repaired at most twice; after that nothing is saved and the dialog says so.

### Replace a draft's content

In the composer, <UiPath path="ai.suggest.assist" /> › <UiPath path="ai.draft.title" /> drafts new content for
the campaign you are editing. It is offered on a draft, in its default language and on version A of an
[A/B test](/campaigns/ab-testing/), once the composer has saved your edits. The proposal shows
<UiPath path="ai.draft.before" /> and <UiPath path="ai.draft.after" /> side by side, with
<UiPath path="ai.draft.changes" />. Accepting replaces the content only if nobody saved the campaign since; otherwise
it says "Changed since this proposal" and you generate a new one. The replaced content stays in the
[version history](/campaigns/composer/#version-history).

## Draft an automation

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.automations > automations.newAutomation" /> and choose
   <UiPath path="ai.automationDraft.title" />.

   <Screenshot
     id="automation-new-ai"
     alt="The automation template dialog: With the agent at the top, then Describe the automation (draft a list-join email sequence and review the steps and every email before saving the bundle) next to the Welcome series, Re-engagement, Onboarding and Abandoned cart templates and a blank canvas."
   >
     1. Several drafts at once, with the [AI agent](/integrations/ai-agent/).
     2. One flow from a brief, on this page.
   </Screenshot>

2. Describe the emails, delays and engagement branches under <UiPath path="ai.automationDraft.brief" />. Leave out
   contact details, credentials and URLs; put links under <UiPath path="ai.draft.links" />.

3. Give the <UiPath path="ai.automationDraft.name" />, the language, the <UiPath path="ai.automationDraft.list" />
   that starts the flow, the voice and, if you like, a template for the emails.

4. Choose <UiPath path="ai.draft.generate" /> and confirm the <UiPath path="ai.draft.sharingTitle" />.

5. Review <UiPath path="ai.automationDraft.graph" /> and every email under <UiPath path="ai.automationDraft.emails" />,
   then choose <UiPath path="ai.draft.accept" />. The flow and its emails are saved together, as drafts.

</Steps>

What this kind of draft can do is fixed:

- The flow starts on future confirmed joins of the list you picked, once per person, without imports, and lets
  people out when they leave the list. Saving it enrolls nobody: contacts enter only after you
  [publish it](/automations/publish-and-versions/), from then on.
- Its steps are emails, delays, branches on opened, clicked or replied, A/B splits and exits. Other triggers, contact
  conditions, webhooks and subscription actions need the [flow editor](/automations/triggers-and-steps/).
- Links, images and Liquid tokens you give are kept.

In the flow editor, an unpublished draft whose trigger fits these rules has a <UiPath path="ai.automationDraft.title" />
button too. Accepting then replaces the draft's steps and adds new copies of its emails; the emails it had stay as they
were.

## Reopen a proposal

A proposal is kept on the server for 24 hours. <UiPath path="ai.draft.pending" /> lists yours at the top of the dialog
and on <UiPath path="ai.agent.runs" />: <UiPath path="ai.draft.review" /> opens it again without another model request.
Only you see your proposals, and opening one checks again that you may still read what it was drafted from.

## What leaves your instance

Only what you give the dialog, when you choose to share it: your brief, the editorial text of the selected template or
draft, and the saved brand voice. Links, images and Liquid tokens travel as placeholders, and the compliance footer and
link destinations stay on your instance. No contacts, recipient lists or expanded tokens are read, and no link is
fetched. ReputeCast refuses the request, before sending anything, when the brief or the content looks like it holds
personal data or credentials. Each confirmation is recorded in the [audit log](/team/audit-log/) as
`ai.sharing.reviewed`, without the text.

<Aside type="caution">
  The filters catch recognizable personal records and credentials, not every personal detail in ordinary prose. Your
  model provider stores and may use what it receives under your account's terms; ReputeCast can't make that zero.
</Aside>

## Check that it worked

- After <UiPath path="ai.draft.accept" />, the campaign opens in the composer, or the flow in the flow editor, as a
  draft.
- The AI activity log in <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.integrations > ai.title" /> has the request under
  <UiPath path="ai.featureDraft" />, with its tokens and outcome.

## Troubleshooting

- **"This context may contain personal data or credentials"**: remove the address, phone number, key or private link
  from the brief or the selected content, and generate again.
- **"This source cannot be safely projected for AI drafting"**: the template or draft has blocks the generator can't
  carry, such as feed items or polls. Start from a plain template.
- **"The generated draft failed content validation"**: the model's MJML still broke the rules after two repairs.
  Revise the brief, or write the email in the composer.
- **"Save or discard your composer edits before generating or accepting a replacement"**: wait for the composer to
  save, or discard your edits.
- **The dialog stops after about a minute**: a reverse proxy ends long responses; raise its read timeout.
- **<UiPath path="ai.draft.generate" /> is greyed out**: the model isn't connected, the feature is off, or you lack
  `ai:use` or the edit permission. The note in the dialog says which.
