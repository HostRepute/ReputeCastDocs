ReputeCast's AI email marketing agent turns a brief into a plan you confirm, then builds the drafts in it: campaigns,
web posts, builder segments and unpublished email flows. It works with the model your workspace connected, inside
per-run and monthly limits, and nothing it builds becomes a draft of yours until a person keeps it. The agent never
sends, schedules, publishes, approves, changes settings or reads contacts. Available since 1.7.0.

<Aside type="note">
  The agent was tested with a recorded model, not with each provider's models: how good its plans and drafts are
  depends on the model you connect.
</Aside>

## Before you begin

- The workspace needs a [connected model](/integrations/ai-assistant/) with both prices set (0 is a valid price for a
  model you run yourself). With unknown prices, ReputeCast can't bound a run's cost and refuses to start one.
- Someone with `integrations:manage` (Owner and Admin) turns the agent on and tests the model's tool calling, as below.
- Starting and continuing a run needs `ai:use` (Owner, Admin and Editor) plus the permissions of what it drafts:
  `campaigns:view` and `campaigns:edit` for campaigns, `automations:view` and `automations:edit` for flows,
  `website:view` and `website:edit` for posts. A segment also needs `subscribers:view` and `segments:edit`, and a
  member limited to some lists can't select audience conditions. API keys and [MCP agents](/developers/mcp/) can't
  hold `ai:use`, so they can't start runs.

## Turn the agent on

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.integrations > ai.title" /> and go to
   <UiPath path="ai.agent.settings" />, under the assistant's features.

2. Choose <UiPath path="ai.agent.testTools" />. ReputeCast sends one fixed request with no workspace content and
   checks that the model calls a test tool correctly. The badge then says
   <UiPath path="ai.agent.capabilityVerified" /> or <UiPath path="ai.agent.capabilityFailed" />. The test counts
   toward the monthly cap. A failed check stops the agent's model work until a test passes or the connection changes;
   saved drafts stay available.

3. Turn on <UiPath path="ai.agent.enabled" /> and check the <UiPath path="ai.agent.defaults" />: 40
   <UiPath path="ai.agent.calls" />, 300,000 <UiPath path="ai.agent.tokens" /> and $2.00 for
   <UiPath path="ai.agent.costLimit" />. They apply to runs started after you save. Choose
   <UiPath path="common.save" />.

</Steps>

Turning the agent off pauses its model work. People can still open their runs and keep or discard the drafts in them.

## Start a run

Start from <UiPath path="nav.campaigns > campaigns.newCampaign > ai.agent.entry" />, from
<UiPath path="nav.automations > automations.newAutomation > ai.agent.entry" /> (the draft type starts at
<UiPath path="ai.agent.kindAutomation" />), from <UiPath path="ai.agent.ask" /> in the
[command palette](/reference/keyboard-shortcuts/#search-with-the-command-palette), or with
<UiPath path="ai.agent.newBrief" /> on <UiPath path="ai.agent.runs" /> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>, then
<UiPath path="ai.agent.runs" />).

<Steps>

1. Describe the work under <UiPath path="ai.agent.brief" />, in up to 4,000 characters: the goal, the facts and the
   call to action. Leave out contact details and credentials, and put links in their own field.

2. Choose the <UiPath path="ai.agent.goal" /> (<UiPath path="ai.agent.kindCampaign" />,
   <UiPath path="ai.agent.kindAutomation" /> or <UiPath path="ai.agent.kindPost" />), the
   <UiPath path="ai.agent.language" />, the [brand voice](/campaigns/brand-voice/) under
   <UiPath path="brandkit.voice.voiceSelection" /> and a <UiPath path="ai.draft.template" />, or
   <UiPath path="ai.draft.blank" />.

3. Add context if the task needs it. Each part is optional:

   - <UiPath path="ai.agent.poolSelect" />: new emails keep the pool's code and redemption link as
     [discount code tokens](/campaigns/discount-codes/). Only the pool's type and how many codes are free reach the
     model; codes are never read, created or reserved.
   - <UiPath path="ai.agent.report" />: one email campaign that finished sending at least 72 hours ago. Only fixed
     totals reach the model, rounded down to tens. A send under 100 and a metric or its complement under 20 are
     withheld, not zero; names, content, links, rates and filters never go. The numbers are a snapshot that never
     refreshes and is removed 30 days after it was first selected.
   - <UiPath path="ai.agent.localAudience" />: [segment builder](/audience/segments/) conditions that stay on your
     instance. The model sees symbols, not the values, and no segment is saved until you keep its bundle.
   - For an automation, the <UiPath path="ai.agent.trigger" />: <UiPath path="ai.agent.triggerJoined" /> (a list
     with confirmed consent), <UiPath path="ai.agent.triggerEntered" /> or <UiPath path="ai.agent.triggerLeft" />.
     With selected audience conditions, <UiPath path="ai.agent.fromAudience" /> makes them the new segment the flow
     starts from. Contacts enter once; a list trigger leaves out imports and lets people out when they leave the list.

4. Under <UiPath path="ai.draft.links" />, list up to 10 complete HTTP(S) addresses, one per line. The model gets
   placeholders; the addresses stay on your instance and come back in the drafts.

5. Choose <UiPath path="ai.agent.planStart" />. The <UiPath path="ai.draft.sharingTitle" /> dialog says what goes to
   the model and asks you to confirm that you reviewed it and may share it; choose
   <UiPath path="ai.draft.sharingConfirm" />. Cancelling sends nothing.

</Steps>

## Confirm the plan

The run page has three tabs, <UiPath path="ai.agent.planTab" />, <UiPath path="ai.agent.steps" /> and
<UiPath path="ai.agent.review" />, next to the run's budget and what the model sees.

- **The plan** lists up to 10 drafts. Each says what it is for, the checks it must pass and the permissions it needs,
  and which other draft it depends on. Drafts that depend on each other form a bundle, such as a new segment and the
  flow that starts from it.
- **Questions.** When the brief leaves something open, such as which readers or which offer, the run says
  <UiPath path="ai.agent.stateNeedsAnswers" /> and asks up to three questions. Answer them under
  <UiPath path="ai.agent.questions" /> and choose <UiPath path="ai.agent.answerSend" />.
- **<UiPath path="ai.agent.audience" />**: with audience conditions, about how many contacts match, of how many, and
  when that was counted. Only you see it, never the model, and consent, suppressions and membership are checked again
  when someone sends or publishes.
- **The build estimate** gives a low and a high cost, from the configured prices. It is not a quote or a spending
  limit; when the high one passes what is left of the run's ceiling, the run says it will pause at the limit.

Choose <UiPath path="ai.agent.build" /> and confirm. You confirm this version of the plan: the agent may stage only
these drafts, and anything that changes an existing draft is a separate proposal you accept or reject. The run goes
to <UiPath path="ai.agent.stateQueued" /> or <UiPath path="ai.agent.stateBuilding" />. A workspace runs two model
requests at a time, and a person one, so another run waits and shows its place in the queue.

The <UiPath path="ai.agent.steps" /> tab lists each step and its tool calls: what the agent did and the outcome, such
as <UiPath path="ai.agent.outcomeRecorded" /> or <UiPath path="ai.agent.outcomeOutsideScope" />, never the model's
hidden reasoning. A draft that fails validation is repaired at most twice; after that the run pauses, keeps the drafts
that passed and asks you to finish the rest by hand.

## Keep or discard the drafts

When every planned draft is staged, the run is <UiPath path="ai.agent.stateReview" /> and you get a notification. The
<UiPath path="ai.agent.review" /> tab counts the decisions left.

<Screenshot
  id="agent-review"
  alt="The Review tab of a run Ready to review: 3 decisions remaining, the note that Keep is not approval to send, and under New drafts the bundles Feature flags are generally available and Reminder: try feature flags, each Not reviewed, with Review content, Keep bundle and Discard bundle."
>
  1. Decisions left.
  2. The bundle's draft, with its content to review.
  3. Keep or discard the whole bundle.
</Screenshot>

- **<UiPath path="ai.agent.newDrafts" />**: <UiPath path="ai.agent.keep" /> saves every draft of a bundle as an
  ordinary draft at once, or none of them. Nothing is sent, published or enrolled: a kept flow stays unpublished, and
  a kept segment doesn't put its current members into any flow. <UiPath path="ai.agent.discard" /> removes only those
  staged drafts. A bundle that misses a planned draft can't be kept.
- **<UiPath path="ai.agent.existingDrafts" />**: <UiPath path="ai.agent.accept" /> applies the change only if the
  draft is still at the revision the proposal was made from. If someone edited it since, the run says "Changed since
  this proposal. Nothing was overwritten.": reject it, or choose <UiPath path="ai.agent.refresh" /> and confirm a new
  plan. <UiPath path="ai.agent.reject" /> leaves the draft as it
  is. Accepting can make an existing [approval](/campaigns/approvals/) stale, as any edit does.
- Each decision needs the edit permission of what it saves, for example `campaigns:edit` for a campaign.
- A kept draft opens with <UiPath path="ai.agent.composer" />, <UiPath path="ai.agent.flowEditor" />,
  <UiPath path="ai.agent.segmentEditor" /> or <UiPath path="ai.agent.postEditor" />. From there it is yours: sending,
  publishing and approval requests happen on their usual screens, with [Send Readiness](/campaigns/send-readiness/)
  and publish checks as usual. <UiPath path="ai.agent.propose" /> asks the agent for a change to a kept draft, which
  comes back as a proposal.

Once every decision is made, the run is <UiPath path="ai.agent.stateCompleted" />. That means everything was reviewed,
not that anything was sent.

## Refine a run in conversation

Under <UiPath path="ai.agent.conversation" />, add an instruction and choose what it applies to with
<UiPath path="ai.agent.scope" />:

- An unkept draft, such as "Make the reminder shorter": the agent revises that draft within the confirmed plan, and
  you review the new revision.
- <UiPath path="ai.agent.wholePlan" />: the agent prepares a new plan version. Confirming it replaces the unkept
  drafts and proposals it names, which the confirmation lists; kept drafts never change.

Every instruction, answer and source the run shares goes through the same <UiPath path="ai.draft.sharingTitle" />
confirmation.

## Budgets, pauses and stopping

The <UiPath path="ai.agent.budget" /> card counts <UiPath path="ai.agent.calls" />,
<UiPath path="ai.agent.tokens" /> and <UiPath path="ai.agent.cost" /> for the whole run: planning, building, repairs,
answers and revisions. Before each request, ReputeCast reserves its largest possible cost against both the run's
ceilings and the workspace's monthly cap, then keeps what the provider reports. The provider's invoice can differ
from these estimates.

When a ceiling is reached, or something else needs a person, the run is <UiPath path="ai.agent.statePaused" /> with
the reason and the drafts it finished:

<TableScroll label="Why a run pauses">

| Reason | What to do |
|---|---|
| The run's ceiling can't cover the next request | Someone with `integrations:manage` chooses <UiPath path="ai.agent.raise" /> and raises the ceilings. Usage isn't reset, and the monthly cap still applies. Others see <UiPath path="ai.agent.manager" /> |
| The monthly cap can't cover the next request | Raise the cap in the AI assistant settings, or wait for the 1st |
| A request's billing is uncertain | Someone with `integrations:manage` reconciles it under <UiPath path="ai.holdsTitle" />; see [reserved requests and unknown usage](/integrations/ai-assistant/#reserved-requests-and-unknown-usage) |
| The provider didn't complete the request, the connection changed or the price is unknown | Check the model connection and its prices |
| Your access changed | Restore the membership, permissions or list scope the run needs |
| The agent is off | Someone with `integrations:manage` turns it on again |
| The model declined the task, or its answer was incomplete | Revise the brief |
| The model stopped before finishing the plan or every draft | Continue the missing work, or revise the plan |
| The task exceeds the model's context | Keep the valid drafts and start a smaller task |
| The worker lost its slot, for example on a restart | Saved work remains; check the billing, then continue |
| The output failed validation after two repairs | Keep the valid drafts and finish the rest in the editor |

</TableScroll>

Then choose <UiPath path="ai.agent.continue" />. Continuing never resets the counters or buys another budget.

<UiPath path="ai.agent.cancel" /> stops a run: no later step starts, but a request already sent to the provider may
still be billed. The drafts it finished stay on <UiPath path="ai.agent.review" /> to keep or discard.

## Who sees a run, and for how long

- A run is visible to the person who started it and to members with `integrations:manage`, within their own list and
  sender-domain scope. Only the person who started it can continue, answer or stop it. Losing `ai:use` stops the
  model work, not the access to drafts you may still keep.
- A run expires 30 days after it started, whatever its state. Its brief, plan, steps and unkept drafts are then
  removed; kept drafts are ordinary drafts and stay. The conversation with the model is removed 7 days after the run
  ends, if that comes first. Your model provider's own storage and your backups follow their own rules.
- The person who started the run gets <UiPath path="notify.event.ai_agent_ready" /> when a plan is ready, a question
  waits, the drafts are ready or the run pauses. The notification links to the run and holds no content. See
  [notifications](/team/notifications/).
- The [audit log](/team/audit-log/) records each step of a run as `ai.agent.*` actions without its content, and a
  draft kept from a run as the usual `campaign.create` or `segment.create`, with `via: ai_agent` and the run's ID. The
  person who decided is the actor. Each model request is in the AI activity log under
  <UiPath path="ai.agent.title" />.

## What the agent can't do

- Send, schedule, publish, approve or request approval, send tests, change subscriptions or suppressions, change
  routing, providers or settings.
- Read contacts, recipient lists, events of single people, replies, orders, exports, rendered previews or discount
  codes, run SQL, or fetch a web page.
- Change a draft without your decision, or change an active segment, a published post or a live flow. Those can only
  be copied.
- Start flows other than on future list joins or segment entries and exits, or use steps other than emails, delays,
  engagement branches, splits and exits.

Reputation and delivery questions belong in [Deliverability](/deliverability/overview/); the agent says so instead
of guessing.

## Check that it worked

- <UiPath path="ai.agent.runs" /> lists the run with its state, model, usage and expiry.
- After <UiPath path="ai.agent.keep" />, the draft is on <UiPath path="nav.campaigns" />,
  <UiPath path="nav.automations" />, the segments list or the website's posts, as a draft.

## Troubleshooting

- **<UiPath path="ai.agent.planStart" /> is greyed out**: the agent is off, the model has no prices, its tool-calling
  check failed, or you lack `ai:use` or the draft type's permissions. The note under the form says which.
- **"This report cannot be selected"**: pick an email campaign that finished sending at least 72 hours ago whose
  snapshot hasn't expired, or start without a report.
- **"Changed since this proposal"**: someone edited the draft after the agent proposed its change. Reject it, or
  refresh the source and confirm a new plan.
- **The run stays queued**: two runs are already working in the workspace, or one of yours is. It starts when they
  finish.
