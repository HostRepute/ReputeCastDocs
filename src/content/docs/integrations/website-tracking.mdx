ReputeCast's website tracking script, `rc.js`, records page views, product views and add-to-carts on your own shop or
blog, so store flows, [segments](/reference/glossary/#segment) and product recommendations can follow up on what known
contacts looked at. It waits for the visitor's [consent](/reference/glossary/#consent) unless you say otherwise, never
runs in a browser that sends Global Privacy Control, and is never on ReputeCast's own pages: your website, landing
pages and signup forms stay cookie-less. Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

## Before you begin

- Adding, editing and deleting sites and rotating identify secrets need `integrations:manage` (Owner and Admin).
  Everyone with `integrations:view` can open the page and see the sites, their last events and what's kept.
- Each site is one origin: `https`, a domain and a port only when it isn't 443, such as `https://shop.acme.io`. While
  ReputeCast runs in development, `http://localhost` works too. Your instance's own address and the hosts a workspace
  holds (a custom domain, the publication website, link domains) are refused.
- To have product events name products, connect the store first: see
  [connect Shopify or WooCommerce](/integrations/shopify-woocommerce/).
- If the site waits for consent, its consent banner must call `reputecast('consent', true)` when a visitor says yes.

<Tabs syncKey="snippet">
<TabItem label="Script">

Add this to every page of the site, before the closing `</head>`:

```html title="Every page of https://shop.acme.io"
<script>window.reputecast=window.reputecast||function(){(reputecast.q=reputecast.q||[]).push(arguments)};</script>
<script async src="https://news.acme.io/track/v1/rc.js" data-workspace="acme-newsletter" data-site="01a1d6b7-9ff2-7727-bedc-b02b1d98e851"></script>
```

The first line lets the page call `reputecast()` before the script has loaded. `rc.js` is under 5 KB gzipped and
loads from your ReputeCast address. Besides the workspace and the site, the tag reads two attributes:

- `data-wait-consent="false"`: the site doesn't wait for consent. ReputeCast adds it to such a site's snippet.
- `data-auto-page="false"`: no automatic page views. By default the script sends one when the page loads and when a
  single-page app changes the address (`history.pushState`, back and forward).

On a store without the Shopify pixel or the WordPress plugin, call `reputecast('product', {id})` on product pages and
`reputecast('cart', {id, quantity})` when something goes in the cart, with the store's product ID.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Shopify custom pixel">

In Shopify, open **Settings › Customer events**, add a custom pixel named "ReputeCast" and paste the code. For a site
that waits for consent, set its **Customer privacy** permission to **Required**, so Shopify runs it only after the
visitor's consent. Then choose **Save** and **Connect**.

The pixel sends Shopify's own `page_viewed`, `product_viewed` and `product_added_to_cart` events, keeps the visitor ID
in Shopify's browser cookie, and picks up the click token of a visit from an email. For a site that waits, it sends
only once the visitor allows analytics.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="WordPress">

The [ReputeCast WordPress plugin](/integrations/wordpress/) 1.5.0 or later adds the script for you. In WordPress, open
**Settings › ReputeCast**, paste the site ID under **Site tracking**, tick **Wait for visitors' consent** if the site
waits in ReputeCast, paste the identify secret so signed-in customers are recognized, and choose
**Save site tracking**. With WooCommerce, the plugin also reports product views and add-to-carts.

</TabItem>

## Consent comes first

- **A site that waits** stores nothing before `reputecast('consent', true)`. Until then, events wait in the page's
  memory only (at most 100) and go when consent comes. ReputeCast also drops a waiting site's events that don't say
  consent was given, so even an edited tag stores nothing before consent.
- `reputecast('consent', false)` drops what's waiting and deletes the visitor ID. The browser remembers either answer.
- **A site that doesn't wait** sends from the first page. The switch's note says it: turned off, the site takes
  responsibility for its own legal basis.
- **Global Privacy Control is always honored.** A browser that sends it is never tracked, whatever the site's
  setting: the script does nothing, and the server drops calls with `Sec-GPC: 1`.
- **The visitor ID** is random: 22 characters in a first-party cookie, `rc_vid`, on your site's domain (400 days,
  `SameSite=Lax`, `Secure` on HTTPS), mirrored in the browser's local storage. ReputeCast stores only a hash of it.

## How a visitor becomes a contact

ReputeCast never takes an email address a page states. A visitor is linked to a contact in one of three ways:

1. **A click from an email.** The click redirect adds the click token `rc` to links to a tracked site's host, as it does
   for store links. The script on the landing page sends it once.
2. **A signup on the same page.** When a ReputeCast [signup form](/forms/signup-forms/) on a tracked page creates a new
   contact, the form hands the script a signed token that works for 10 minutes. Typing someone else's address never
   links a browser to them.
3. **A signed identify call from the site's server.** Your server signs the visitor's address with the site's identify
   secret, and the page calls `reputecast('identify', {email, signature})`.

Only an existing contact who hasn't been erased is linked. Their last 30 days on the site then join their profile.
Everyone else stays a visitor. A browser linked to one contact and then identified as another (a shared computer)
belongs to the second from then on; earlier events stay with the first.

### Sign identify calls

The signature is `hex(HMAC-SHA256(secret, lowercase(email)))`, made on your server, never in the browser:

<Tabs syncKey="lang">
<TabItem label="Node.js">

```js title="identify.js"
import { createHmac } from 'node:crypto'

const email = customer.email.trim().toLowerCase()
const signature = createHmac('sha256', process.env.REPUTECAST_IDENTIFY_SECRET).update(email).digest('hex')
// In the page: reputecast('identify', { email, signature })
```

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="PHP">

```php title="identify.php"
$email = strtolower(trim($customer->email));
$signature = hash_hmac('sha256', $email, getenv('REPUTECAST_IDENTIFY_SECRET'));
// In the page: reputecast('identify', { email, signature })
```

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## The script's commands

Every call goes through `reputecast()`. Events are sent in batches: after a second, at 20 events, and when the page is
hidden or closed.

<TableScroll label="rc.js commands">

| Call                                          | What it does                                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `reputecast('consent', true)`                 | The visitor allowed tracking: waiting events go, and the answer is remembered.                                                           |
| `reputecast('consent', false)`                | The visitor refused: waiting events and the visitor ID are deleted, and the answer is remembered.                                        |
| `reputecast('page')`                          | A page view. Sent on its own unless the tag has `data-auto-page="false"`.                                                                 |
| `reputecast('product', {id})`                 | A product view. `id` is the store's product ID, a string or a number; Shopify's global IDs (`gid://shopify/Product/…`) keep their number. |
| `reputecast('cart', {id, quantity})`          | An add-to-cart. `quantity` is 1 to 1,000, 1 when left out.                                                                                |
| `reputecast('notifyWhenAvailable', {id})`     | "Email me when it's back" for a known contact: back-in-stock flows take them when the product returns.                                   |
| `reputecast('track', name, props)`            | A custom event. `name` is lowercase letters, digits and `_`, up to 40; `props` an object of up to 2 KB.                                   |
| `reputecast('identify', {email, signature})`  | A signed identify call (see [above](#sign-identify-calls)).                                                                               |

</TableScroll>

ReputeCast's signup forms call `reputecast('link', token)` themselves; you don't need to.

## What the collect endpoint accepts

The script posts to `POST /p/{workspace}/track/{site}/e` on your ReputeCast address. ReputeCast:

- accepts calls only from the site's own origin. Calls from any other origin are refused and counted, and the site's
  events card says where from: "Events from https://www.shop.acme.io were refused", with
  <UiPath path="tracking.addAsSite" />. Calls without an origin (Shopify's sandboxed pixel) are kept only for events
  on the site's own pages.
- drops calls from bots, link previews, prefetches and browsers without a user agent.
- answers `429` with `Retry-After: 60` when one address calls one site too often, and takes a limited number of events
  a call (see [defaults and limits](#defaults-and-limits)).
- keeps an event's time within the last 10 minutes, by its own clock.

## What's kept

- **Stored:** page paths with only their `utm_*` parameters, the referring domain, product views and add-to-carts, and
  custom events (up to 2 KB each).
- **Never stored:** IP addresses, user agents, full query strings and form contents.
- **Events** are kept 90 days, or 30 days when <UiPath path="workspace.detailLabel" /> is set to 30 days in
  [data retention](/team/workspaces/#set-data-retention). Visitors never linked to a contact are deleted 30 days after
  they were last seen, and a contact's product interest 180 days after their last view or add-to-cart.

### Where it shows

- **The contact's timeline** gets one line per day and site, "Viewed 3 products on shop.acme.io" or "Visited
  shop.acme.io", with up to three product names and the add-to-carts. Read
  [a contact's profile](/audience/contacts/#read-a-contacts-profile).
- **Segments** gain <UiPath path="segments.field.site_viewed_product" /> (in the last days, any product or one of the
  linked store's collections) and <UiPath path="segments.field.site_last_seen" />. Read
  [build segments](/audience/segments/).
- **Store flows** start when someone viewed a product or added it to the cart and didn't buy, and on back in stock and
  price drops. Read [store flows](/automations/store-flows/).
- **Product grids** in emails can show what each reader viewed recently. Read
  [design emails in the composer](/campaigns/composer/).
- **Forms** can show only to visitors in a segment. Read [form targeting](/forms/form-targeting/).
- **The holdout group** counts site visitors in each group. Read [the holdout group](/audience/holdout-group/).

**Erasing a contact** deletes all of it: the browsers linked to them and their events, their product interest, the
store flows they entered and their back-in-stock requests. Their data export has a Site activity section with the
last 90 days.

## Manage a site

- <UiPath path="tracking.edit" />: change the name, the store and the consent switch. The origin stays: add another
  site for another origin.
- <UiPath path="tracking.rotate" /> (in the row's menu): the current secret stops working at once, so identify calls
  signed with it link nobody until your server uses the new one, shown once. Clicks from emails, signups on the page
  and every event keep working.
- <UiPath path="tracking.delete" />: the script stops being accepted at once and its events are refused. Events already
  collected stay until they expire, at most 90 days. Store flows get no new people from the site; people already in
  them carry on. Remove the snippet from the site's pages too.

Each change is recorded in the audit log: `tracker_site.updated`, `tracker_site.secret_rotated` and
`tracker_site.deleted`.

## Check that it worked

- The site's row shows <UiPath path="tracking.colEvents" /> and a <UiPath path="tracking.colLast" /> time, and the page
  header says "Connected · last event 2 min ago".
- **Last events** lists the selected site's last 20 events, newest first, each from a known contact or a visitor.
- On a contact who clicked an email to the site, the timeline shows a Website tracking line.

## Troubleshooting

- **"This site waits for visitors' consent" and nothing arrives**: your consent banner doesn't call
  `reputecast('consent', true)` when a visitor says yes, or you tested in a browser that sends Global Privacy Control.
- **"Events from https://www.shop.acme.io were refused"**: the snippet is on another origin than the site's (often the
  `www.` host). Choose <UiPath path="tracking.addAsSite" /> if it's yours.
- **"No store linked"**: product events still count for the site but don't name products, so store flows and
  recommendations can't use them. Choose <UiPath path="tracking.linkStore" />.
- **"… shows ReputeCast's own pages, which are never tracked"**: the origin is your instance or a host a workspace
  holds. Add your own site or store instead.
- **"… is already a tracked site in this workspace"**: each origin can be added once.
- **Identify calls link nobody**: the secret was rotated, the address wasn't lowercased before signing, or no contact
  has the address. Identify never creates a contact.
