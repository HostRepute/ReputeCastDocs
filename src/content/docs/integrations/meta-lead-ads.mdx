Connect Meta Lead Ads and the people who fill in your Facebook lead ads and Instagram lead forms become contacts on
your lists within seconds, with the form's own consent checkbox as their [consent](/reference/glossary/#consent).
ReputeCast uses your own Meta app, so leads go from Meta straight to your instance, and it keeps only the answers you
map. Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

## Before you begin

- Connecting, choosing Pages, mapping forms and disconnecting need `integrations:manage`, a
  [sensitive permission](/reference/glossary/#sensitive-permission) that only people hold (Owner and Admin). Everyone
  with `integrations:view` can open the page; contact names show only to members with `subscribers:view`.
- You need a Meta business that manages the Facebook Pages whose lead forms you run, and access to Meta for
  Developers and Meta Business Settings.
- A workspace has one Meta connection. It can collect from every Page its token reaches.
- Meta posts new leads to your instance, so ReputeCast's public URL should be reachable from the internet over HTTPS
  (read [reverse proxy and TLS](/self-hosting/reverse-proxy/)). Without it, ReputeCast checks for new leads every
  15 minutes instead.

## Connect Meta

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.integrations" />, find Meta Lead Ads in the
   <UiPath path="integrations.groupLeads" /> group and choose <UiPath path="integrations.connect" />. The dialog,
   <UiPath path="leads.connect.title" />, has three steps.

2. **<UiPath path="leads.connect.stepApp" />.** In Meta, as the dialog lists:

   - In Meta for Developers, create an app of the **Business** type and add the **Webhooks** product.
   - In Meta Business Settings › **System users**, add a system user and give it the app and your Pages.
   - Generate the system user's token for the app, with no expiry and these permissions: `leads_retrieval`,
     `pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement`, `pages_manage_metadata` and `ads_management`.
   - In Business Settings › **Integrations › Leads access**, give the app access to your Pages' leads.

   The dialog notes that App Review isn't needed for Pages your business manages, and says whether Meta can reach your
   server. Meta's [guide to retrieving leads](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/guides/lead-ads/retrieving)
   explains what each permission covers, and that `pages_manage_metadata` is for webhooks (checked November 2026).
   Choose <UiPath path="leads.connect.next" />.

3. **<UiPath path="leads.connect.stepCredentials" />.** Enter a name for the connection (only shown in ReputeCast, such
   as your business's name), the <UiPath path="leads.connect.appId" /> and <UiPath path="leads.connect.appSecret" />
   from the app's **App settings › Basic**, and the <UiPath path="leads.connect.token" />. Choose
   <UiPath path="leads.connect.connect" />.

   ReputeCast checks with Meta first: the app ID and secret together, then the token, by listing the Pages it reaches.
   A refusal shows on its field (see [troubleshooting](#troubleshooting)) and nothing is saved. The secret and the token
   are kept encrypted and never shown again.

4. **<UiPath path="leads.connect.stepPages" />.** The dialog lists the Pages the token reaches, each with its number of
   lead forms. Pick the Pages whose leads ReputeCast collects and choose **Subscribe to 2 Pages** (the count follows
   your choice). The step says whether Meta confirmed the webhook.

</Steps>

Connecting subscribes your app to Pages' `leadgen` webhook at the connection's own callback URL,
`/hooks/meta-leads/{token}` on your ReputeCast address, and installs the app on each chosen Page. You don't paste
anything into Meta's webhook settings: Meta checks the address at once with its verification request. If Meta refuses
the subscription, the connection still works and ReputeCast checks for new leads every 15 minutes.

## Map a form

A form collects nothing until it's mapped. On the connection's page, under <UiPath path="leads.formsTitle" />, choose
<UiPath path="leads.map" /> on a form.

<Steps>

1. **<UiPath path="leads.mapping.questions" />.** For each question, choose what it's saved as:
   <UiPath path="leads.mapping.targetEmail" /> (exactly one question, required),
   <UiPath path="leads.mapping.targetFirstName" />, <UiPath path="leads.mapping.targetLastName" />,
   <UiPath path="leads.mapping.targetFullName" /> (split at the first space), <UiPath path="leads.mapping.targetCompany" />,
   <UiPath path="leads.mapping.targetPhone" />, one of your [custom fields](/audience/lists-and-fields/), or
   <UiPath path="leads.mapping.dontKeep" />. ReputeCast suggests a target from each question's type. A phone number is
   saved only on a contact who has none.

2. **<UiPath path="leads.mapping.lists" />.** Pick at least one list the leads join. You can pick only lists you have
   access to.

3. **<UiPath path="leads.mapping.tags" />.** Up to 10 tags, added to every contact from this form.

4. **<UiPath path="leads.mapping.consent" />.** Choose one:

   - <UiPath path="leads.mapping.double" />: ReputeCast emails a confirmation link, and the lead joins once they click
     it. The form's disclaimer, if it has one, is kept as the consent text.
   - <UiPath path="leads.mapping.single" />: the lead joins at once, with the form's consent checkbox as their consent.
     The dialog quotes the checkbox; its text, as Meta returns it, is the consent text. Leads who don't tick it aren't
     added. A form without a consent checkbox can't use single opt-in: add one under **Custom disclaimer** in Meta.

5. Choose <UiPath path="leads.mapping.save" />. The audit log records `lead_form.mapped`.

</Steps>

- **The header** shows the status: <UiPath path="leads.statusReceiving" />,
  <UiPath path="leads.statusPolling" />, <UiPath path="leads.statusAttention" /> or
  <UiPath path="leads.statusDisconnected" />. <UiPath path="leads.refreshForms" /> lists the chosen Pages' forms again
  (new forms, new questions); the menu has <UiPath path="leads.replaceCredentials" /> and
  <UiPath path="leads.disconnect" />.
- **<UiPath path="leads.howTitle" />** shows whether leads arrive by webhook or by polling, the Meta app's ID, when the
  token was last checked and who connected Meta.
- **<UiPath path="leads.formsTitle" />** lists each form with its Page, leads in 30 days, lists, consent mode and status
  (<UiPath path="leads.formActive" />, <UiPath path="leads.formPaused" /> or <UiPath path="leads.formNotMapped" />).
  <UiPath path="leads.choosePages" /> adds or removes Pages: Pages left out are unsubscribed and keep their mappings.
  Forms are listed with their questions and consent checkboxes, and refreshed every 15 minutes, on
  <UiPath path="leads.refreshForms" /> and when a lead names a new form.
- **A form's menu** has <UiPath path="leads.pause" /> (its leads wait at Meta), <UiPath path="leads.resume" /> and
  <UiPath path="leads.importPast" />, which brings in the form's leads of the last 90 days, each once. A form shows how
  many leads arrived before it was mapped or while it was paused; map or resume it, then import past leads to add them.
- **<UiPath path="leads.activityTitle" />** lists the last 50 leads with their time, form, outcome and contact. The
  answers go to the contact's fields; ReputeCast doesn't keep them here.

Connecting, choosing Pages, replacing credentials, pausing, resuming and importing are recorded in the
[audit log](/team/audit-log/) (`lead_connection.created`, `lead_connection.updated`, `lead_form.paused`,
`lead_form.resumed`, `lead_form.imported`). Single leads aren't: their consent records are the evidence.

## How leads arrive

1. **Meta posts the lead.** ReputeCast answers Meta's verification request only with the connection's own verify
   token, and checks each delivery's `X-Hub-Signature-256` against your app secret. A delivery whose signature doesn't
   match is answered `401` and never applied (its first 2 KB are kept for diagnosis). A good one is stored as it came,
   which is only IDs, and answered `200` at once; when the database can't take it, ReputeCast answers `429` so Meta
   tries again. Meta describes the verification request, the signature and its retries in
   [Webhooks: getting started](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/webhooks/getting-started/) (checked
   November 2026).
2. **A background job applies it.** It reads the lead from Meta with the Page's token. Every call made with the system
   user's or a Page's token carries `appsecret_proof`, so a token alone is useless to whoever reads it
   ([securing Graph API requests](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/guides/secure-requests/), checked
   November 2026). Leads for a form that isn't mapped or is paused are counted, not stored.
3. **The person is signed up** through the form's mapping, lists and tags, once per lead and form: another delivery,
   a poll or an import of the same lead changes nothing. The consent record names the form (source `lead_ad:<form>`),
   the lead's time and the note "Facebook/Instagram lead form Field Guide download".

Each lead ends with one outcome in <UiPath path="leads.activityTitle" />:

| Outcome                                | What happened                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <UiPath path="leads.outcomeCreated" /> | A new contact joined the form's lists.                                              |
| <UiPath path="leads.outcomeUpdated" /> | The address was already a contact, who joined the form's lists.                     |
| <UiPath path="leads.outcomePending" /> | Double opt-in: the confirmation email went out.                                     |
| <UiPath path="leads.outcomeBlocked" /> | The address is on the suppression list, so nobody was added.                        |
| <UiPath path="leads.outcomeInvalid" /> | The lead had no valid email, or none of the form's lists exists any more.           |
| <UiPath path="leads.outcomeDeclined" />| Single opt-in, and the lead didn't tick the consent checkbox.                       |
| <UiPath path="leads.outcomeTest" />    | A lead from Meta's testing tool. Test leads create nobody.                          |

### When ReputeCast polls

Every 15 minutes ReputeCast checks each connection: the token, the Pages' forms, and whether the webhook works. It
reads new leads of active forms itself, every 15 minutes, when:

- nothing came from Meta for 24 hours while forms are active;
- Meta refused the app's webhook subscription;
- your instance's public URL isn't reachable from the internet.

The page then shows <UiPath path="leads.statusPolling" /> and a banner with the reason. When Meta asks ReputeCast to
slow down, calls pause for 15 minutes and the banner says when it tries again.

### Needs attention

When Meta refuses the token (rejected, expired or missing a permission), the connection needs attention. New deliveries are held, the page shows a banner with
<UiPath path="leads.replaceCredentials" />, and members with `integrations:manage` get one notification, "Meta refused
the lead ads token" (`lead_ads_attention`). The banner says leads wait at Meta for up to 90 days. Replacing the
credentials (blank secrets stay as they are) applies the deliveries that waited, and the check every 15 minutes also
clears the state once the token works again.

## Use the leads

- **Automations**: the trigger <UiPath path="leads.automation.kind" /> (any lead form, or one) starts a flow when a
  lead joins, or, with double opt-in, once the lead confirms. It's in the flow editor's
  <UiPath path="leads.automation.group" /> group once there is a lead form, also as a second or third trigger. Read
  [triggers and steps](/automations/triggers-and-steps/).
- **Growth**: joins from a form count under the source "Lead ads · Field Guide download". Read
  [growth sources](/audience/growth-sources/).
- **Erasure**: erasing a contact leaves only which lead form they sent and when, without them, so importing past leads
  never adds them back. Meta's deliveries hold only IDs and are deleted after 30 days. Read
  [erase contacts](/audience/contacts/#erase-contacts).

## Disconnect

Choose <UiPath path="leads.disconnect" /> in the page's menu. ReputeCast stops collecting from the chosen Pages,
removes their subscriptions, deletes the credentials and Page tokens, and the callback URL stops working. Contacts and
their consent records stay, and the page keeps its recent leads. To stop for a while, pause the forms instead. The
audit log records `lead_connection.disconnected`.

## Check that it worked

- The header shows <UiPath path="leads.statusReceiving" />, and <UiPath path="leads.howTitle" /> shows "Receiving · last
  lead …".
- Send a test lead with Meta's testing tool: it shows in <UiPath path="leads.activityTitle" /> as
  <UiPath path="leads.outcomeTest" /> and creates nobody.
- The Meta Lead Ads card in the catalog says "Acme Marketing · 2 Pages · 41 leads in 30 days".

## Troubleshooting

- **"Meta didn't accept this app ID and secret together"**: copy both again from **App settings › Basic**.
- **"Meta refused the token: it expired"**: generate the system user's token again with no expiry.
- **"The token lacks a permission ReputeCast needs"**: generate it again with all five permissions.
- **"The token can't see any Page"**: give the system user your Pages in Business Settings.
- **"This workspace is connected to Meta already"**: one connection per workspace; open it and choose
  <UiPath path="leads.choosePages" />.
- **"Map … first"**: a form must be mapped before it can be resumed or its past leads imported.
- **Leads arrive late**: the page shows <UiPath path="leads.statusPolling" /> and a banner with the reason. For an
  unreachable instance, choose <UiPath path="leads.checkPublicUrl" />; when Meta couldn't reach the webhook, check that
  the app has the Webhooks product, then replace the credentials to subscribe again.
- **Single opt-in isn't offered**: the form has no consent checkbox. Add one under **Custom disclaimer** in Meta, then
  choose <UiPath path="leads.refreshForms" />.
