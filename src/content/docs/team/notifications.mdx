ReputeCast tells you about approvals, auto-paused sends, sending-provider incidents, team changes and HostRepute
alerts in the app and by email. This page shows where to read them and how to change your email notifications
settings for each event type.

## Before you begin

- Notifications are personal: each one goes to the people its event concerns, for example approvers, or the people
  whose role can manage sending providers. Your preferences apply in every workspace you belong to.
- Emails go out on the workspace's System stream. Until a sending provider handles it, notifications stay in the app
  (see [connect a sending provider](/providers/connect/)).

## Read notifications

The bell at the top right shows the unread count for this workspace. Its list shows the latest six, unread first;
<UiPath path="shell.notificationsMarkAll" /> clears the count, and <UiPath path="shell.notificationsAll" /> opens the
full page. Opening a notification marks it read and takes you to the screen where you can act.

Notifications are kept for 90 days, read or not.

## Change your preferences

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="shell.userMenuNotifications" /> from the account menu, or <UiPath path="account.editPreferences" />
   on the <UiPath path="account.notificationPreferences" /> card of [Account & security](/team/account-security/).

2. In <UiPath path="notifications.myPreferences" />, turn the in-app and email switches on or off for each event. Each row
   says who receives it in plain words, such as the people who can send campaigns, never a permission key. Most events
   email by default; a few, such as silent webhooks, accepted invitations and HostRepute alerts, stay in the app unless
   you turn email on.

3. Set <UiPath path="notifications.quietHours" /> to hold emails overnight, for example 22:00 to 07:00. Quiet hours
   use the timezone in your profile.

4. Pick an <UiPath path="notifications.emailDigest" />: immediately, an hourly digest, or a daily digest at 09:00.

5. Choose <UiPath path="notifications.savePreferences" />.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  Urgent events always email at once, ignoring quiet hours and digests: auto-paused sends and SMS campaigns, sending
  providers or SMS providers down, and an unreachable SMTP relay. You can still turn their email off.
</Aside>

The <UiPath path="notify.event.send_paused" /> row also covers a send blocked when it starts, complaints that pass a
limit during the 72 hours after a send, and a template that failed for some recipients of a send. Two rows concern only
you:

- <UiPath path="notify.event.comment_mention" />: someone @mentioned you in a campaign's comments.
- <UiPath path="notify.event.results_final" />: you chose <UiPath path="reports.emailWhenFinal" /> on a
  [campaign report](/campaigns/reports/) whose results were still coming in. ReputeCast tells you once, 72 hours after
  the send, if your role can still read the report.

Some notices have no preference row and always appear in the app: finished sends, imports and exports, a two-factor
reset, a deleted workspace, an ownership transfer, an MCP agent's proposal and access requests.

## Check that it worked

- A notification email has the subject `[<workspace>] <title>`, or `[<workspace>] <N> notifications` for a digest, and
  links back to your preferences.
- The <UiPath path="account.notificationPreferences" /> card on Account & security summarizes your in-app, email and
  quiet-hours choices.

## Troubleshooting

- **No emails arrive.** The preferences card says when no sending provider handles the System stream. Emails skipped
  then are not sent later.
- **A teammate gets alerts you don't.** Most events go only to the people whose role can do something about them;
  check the line under the event and your [role](/team/roles-and-permissions/).
- **Emails come late.** Check your quiet hours and digest setting.
