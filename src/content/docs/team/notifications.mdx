ReputeCast tells you about approvals, auto-paused sends, automations that need attention, sending-provider incidents,
failing webhook endpoints, team changes and HostRepute alerts in the app and by email. This page shows where to read
them, how to change your email notifications settings for each event type, and how to get a weekly or monthly
sending summary.

## Before you begin

- Notifications are personal: each one goes to the people its event concerns, for example approvers, or the people
  whose role can manage sending providers. Your preferences apply in every workspace you belong to.
- Emails go out on the workspace's System stream. Until a sending provider handles it, notifications stay in the app
  (see [connect a sending provider](/providers/connect/)).

## Read notifications

The bell at the top right shows the unread count for this workspace. Its list shows the latest six, unread first;
<UiPath path="shell.notificationsMarkAll" /> clears the count, and <UiPath path="shell.notificationsAll" /> opens the
full page. Opening a notification marks it read at once, in the bell and on the page, and takes you to the screen where
you can act; if ReputeCast can't save that, it shows as unread again.

## Change your preferences

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="shell.userMenuNotifications" /> from the account menu, or <UiPath path="account.editPreferences" />
   on the <UiPath path="account.notificationPreferences" /> card of [Account & security](/team/account-security/).

2. In <UiPath path="notifications.myPreferences" />, turn the in-app and email switches on or off for each event. Each row
   says who receives it in plain words, such as the people who can send campaigns, never a permission key. Most events
   email by default; a few, such as silent webhooks, accepted invitations and HostRepute alerts, stay in the app unless
   you turn email on.

3. Set <UiPath path="notifications.quietHours" /> to hold emails overnight, for example 22:00 to 07:00. Quiet hours
   use the timezone in your profile.

4. Pick an <UiPath path="notifications.emailDigest" />: immediately, an hourly digest, or a daily digest at 09:00.

5. Choose <UiPath path="notifications.savePreferences" />.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  Urgent events always email at once, ignoring quiet hours and digests: auto-paused sends and SMS campaigns, sending
  providers or SMS providers down, and an unreachable SMTP relay. You can still turn their email off.
</Aside>

The <UiPath path="notify.event.send_paused" /> row also covers a send blocked when it starts, complaints that pass a
limit during the 72 hours after a send, and a template that failed for some recipients of a send. Two rows concern only
you:

- <UiPath path="notify.event.comment_mention" />: someone @mentioned you in a campaign's comments.
- <UiPath path="notify.event.results_final" />: you chose <UiPath path="reports.emailWhenFinal" /> on a
  [campaign report](/campaigns/reports/) whose results were still coming in. ReputeCast tells you once, 72 hours after
  the send, if your role can still read the report.

Two rows are about something that keeps failing:

- <UiPath path="notify.event.automation_attention" />: a step of a flow keeps failing, or the flow's emails are held
  because no sending provider on the Automations route is healthy. It goes to the people who can edit automations,
  once per alert (see [build automations](/automations/overview/#troubleshooting)).
- <UiPath path="notify.event.webhook_failing" />: a webhook endpoint has failed for an hour, or a delivery to it was
  given up on. It goes to the people who manage webhooks, once each time the endpoint starts failing (see
  [receive webhooks](/developers/webhooks/#answer-quickly-reputecast-retries-the-rest)).

Some notices have no preference row and always appear in the app: finished sends, imports and exports, a two-factor
reset, a deleted workspace, an ownership transfer, an MCP agent's proposal and access requests.

### Replies, discount codes, comments and lead ads

Eight more rows cover receiving mail, discount codes, the website, sponsors, lead ads and signup forms. Each goes only
to the people the line under it names. Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

| ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | --------- |

New replies have their own badge, Audience; comments and post imports show as Website, sponsor requests as Earn,
lead ads as Integrations and ended form tests as Other.

## Get a sending summary by email

Members whose role can view reports can get the workspace's numbers by email, once a week or once a month, since
1.6.0. It is a different email from the notification digest above, which batches notifications.

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="shell.notificationsTitle" /> and go to <UiPath path="notifications.summaryTitle" />, the last
   card on the page.

   <Screenshot
     id="notifications-summary"
     alt="The Sending summary by email card: Off, Weekly on Mondays at 09:00, selected, and Monthly on the 1st at 09:00, with the line Next one: Mon 16 Nov · 09:00 · sent to linh@acme.io."
   />

2. Pick <UiPath path="notifications.summaryWeekly" /> (Mondays at 09:00), <UiPath path="notifications.summaryMonthly" />
   (the 1st at 09:00) or <UiPath path="notifications.summaryOff" />. The choice is saved at once, and the line under
   the card says when the next one goes out and to which address.

</Steps>

What the summary holds:

- **Campaigns** started in the period, with their totals of sent, delivered, human opens, clicks, unsubscribes,
  bounces and complaints, how many are still collecting results, and the five most opened. Complaints count only the
  mailbox providers that report them.
- **Audience**: how many joined and left, the net change and the subscribers now, if your role can view contacts.
- **Sender health**: the score of the Overview, if your role can view deliverability.

A weekly summary covers Monday to Monday and a monthly one the previous month, both in the time zone of your profile
(else the workspace's), and it is written in your app language. The first one goes out on the next Monday or 1st and
covers the period you turned it on in; nothing is sent about earlier periods. A period with no campaign started and
no net change in the audience sends nothing. The summary goes out on the System stream, like notification emails, and
is skipped while no sending provider handles it. If your role stops showing reports, the setting stays but nothing is
sent; if you leave the workspace, it is removed.

Turning the summary on, changing it or turning it off is recorded in the audit log as `report_summary.set` or
`report_summary.remove`. The API equivalent is `GET` and `PUT /api/v1/workspaces/<workspace>/report-summary`, for
signed-in people only: an API key has no inbox to send it to.

## Check that it worked

- A notification email has the subject `[<workspace>] <title>`, or `[<workspace>] <N> notifications` for a digest, and
  links back to your preferences.
- The <UiPath path="account.notificationPreferences" /> card on Account & security summarizes your in-app, email and
  quiet-hours choices.

## Troubleshooting

- **No emails arrive.** The preferences card says when no sending provider handles the System stream. Emails skipped
  then are not sent later.
- **A teammate gets alerts you don't.** Most events go only to the people whose role can do something about them;
  check the line under the event and your [role](/team/roles-and-permissions/).
- **Emails come late.** Check your quiet hours and digest setting.
- **The sending summary card is missing.** Your role can't view reports in this workspace.
- **No sending summary arrived.** Nothing happened in the period (no campaign started and no net change in the
  audience), or no sending provider handles the System stream.
