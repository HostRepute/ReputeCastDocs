Run email marketing agency client accounts from one ReputeCast instance: each client gets its own
[workspace](/reference/glossary/#workspace), your staff work across them from the Agency area, and the usage page
shows what to rebill each client. [Agency mode](/reference/glossary/#agency-mode) is free like every other feature.

## Before you begin

- Turning on agency mode needs an [instance admin](/reference/glossary/#instance-admin). There is one agency per
  instance, and it is off by default.
- Staff permissions are instance-level (`agency:view`, `agency:usage`, `agency:library`, `agency:manage`). They never
  go into a workspace role, an API key or an MCP agent.
- Each client keeps its own sending providers, domains, contacts, suppressions, consent, keys and audit log.

<Steps>

1. **Turn on agency mode.** Open <UiPath path="shell.userMenuInstanceAdmin > admin.instance > agency.adminTitle" /> and
   switch on <UiPath path="agency.adminMode" />. Set the <UiPath path="agency.adminName" />,
   <UiPath path="agency.adminOwnWorkspace" />, the <UiPath path="agency.adminDefaultTemplate" /> and which workspaces are
   clients, then choose <UiPath path="agency.adminTurnOn" />. The agency's own workspace can't also be a client.

2. **Add staff.** Instance admins are agency managers automatically. In the Agency area, open
   <UiPath path="agency.navStaff" /> and choose <UiPath path="agencyStaff.addStaff" />, either
   <UiPath path="agencyStaff.fromUsers" /> or <UiPath path="agencyStaff.byEmail" />. An **agency manager** sees every
   client and gets Admin in each, including new ones. An **account manager** sees only the clients they are assigned
   to; use <UiPath path="agencyStaff.assign" /> to give them a role in a client. You can only assign roles whose
   permissions you hold there, and never Owner. In the client's Team page, assigned staff show "via" the agency.

3. **Create a client.** Choose <UiPath path="agency.newClient" /> (needs `agency:manage`). Enter the name, the slug used
   in its URLs and the time zone, pick a <UiPath path="agency.fieldTemplate" />, an Owner among the agency managers and up to 20
   <UiPath path="agency.fieldManagers" /> (each becomes Admin). Under <UiPath path="agency.firstPerson" />, you can invite
   the client as <UiPath path="agency.roleClientApprover" /> or <UiPath path="agency.roleClientViewer" />. Then choose
   <UiPath path="agency.createClient" />. The result lists what the template added and the next steps: connect a
   sending provider, verify a domain and invite the client.

4. **Require client sign-off.** In the client's <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.approvals" />, turn on
   <UiPath path="agency.signoffTitle" /> (and, if you want, <UiPath path="agency.signoffAutomations" />). Campaigns on
   every channel then wait for an approval from someone at the client: approvals from your staff are recorded but don't
   count. See [require approval for risky sends](/campaigns/approvals/).

5. **Watch every client.** The Agency <UiPath path="agency.navOverview" /> shows approvals waiting, incidents in the last
   7 days, sends in the next 7 days and the client list. Filter it with <UiPath path="agency.filterAll" />,
   <UiPath path="agency.filterAttention" /> or <UiPath path="agency.filterMine" />.

   />

</Steps>

## Client roles

| Role            | Can see                                                                    | Can do                       |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Client approver | Campaigns, templates, automations, reports, deliverability and contacts     | Approve sends; never export  |
| Client viewer   | Campaigns, templates, automations, reports and deliverability; no contacts  | Nothing else                 |

Both roles exist only in client workspaces and are read-only, like the other
[built-in roles](/team/roles-and-permissions/).

## Templates and the shared library

- **Workspace templates** (in <UiPath path="agency.navLibrary" />) set up a client: empty lists, paused signup forms,
  automations as drafts, saved sections, campaign templates, the approval policy with client sign-off, custom roles,
  branding without logo and colors, and default From names and footer address. Providers, domains, contacts,
  suppressions, consent, keys, integrations and members are never copied. Applying a template only adds what's
  missing; it never overwrites or deletes. Create one with <UiPath path="agencyLibrary.newTemplate" />, or from a
  client's general settings with <UiPath path="agency.saveAsTemplate" />.
- **The shared library** holds saved sections, campaign templates, automation templates and transactional templates
  added from any client. Using an item copies it into the client; copies are not linked, and the composer shows when
  the library has a newer version.

## Usage and rebill rates

<Screenshot
  id="agency-usage"
  alt="Agency usage for one month: tiles for emails, SMS segments, push notifications, provider cost and the amount to rebill, then one row per client with its rebill rate."
/>

<UiPath path="agency.navUsage" /> shows, per client and month, emails by stream, SMS segments by country, push
notifications and provider cost in USD. Choose <UiPath path="agencyUsage.setRate" /> or
<UiPath path="agencyUsage.editRate" /> to combine a percentage on top of cost, a price per 1,000 emails, a price per
SMS segment and a monthly fee, or a flat monthly amount instead. A new rate applies from the first day of the current
month. ReputeCast calculates the amount to rebill; it doesn't send invoices or take payments.
<UiPath path="agencyUsage.exportCsv" /> downloads one row per client and channel. Rates and costs are visible only to
staff with `agency:usage`.

<Aside type="note">
  Provider cost is part of the amount to rebill only when the rate includes the percentage part.
</Aside>

## Check that it worked

- Staff see the Agency area in the workspace switcher, and clients appear on the Overview.
- The client's Team page lists your staff "via" the agency, and the client's audit log shows `agency.client_created`.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Agency mode is off on this instance."** An instance admin turns it on in Instance & updates.
- **A send says nobody can give client sign-off.** Invite the client's approver with the Client approver role first.
- **A contact search finds nothing.** <UiPath path="agency.searchPlaceholder" /> matches contacts only by their exact
  email address, and only in clients where you hold `subscribers:view`. Every contact search is recorded in the audit
  log.
- **Turning agency mode off.** <UiPath path="agency.adminTurnOff" /> removes the Agency area and the agency roles; every
  workspace, membership, template and library item stays and comes back when you turn it on again.
