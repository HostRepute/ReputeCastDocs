Run multiple newsletter workspaces on one ReputeCast instance, one per newsletter, brand or client, and manage each
one from its general settings. A [workspace](/reference/glossary/#workspace) keeps its own contacts, lists,
campaigns, sending providers, team and audit log. Every feature is available in every workspace, and the number of
workspaces is unlimited.

## Before you begin

- Any signed-in person can create a workspace and becomes its Owner.
- Changing a workspace's settings needs `workspace:manage` (Owner and Admin).
- Transferring ownership and deleting the workspace are for the Owner only (`workspace:transfer`,
  `workspace:delete`). See [roles and permissions](/team/roles-and-permissions/).

<Steps>

1. **Create a workspace.** Open the workspace switcher under the logo and choose
   <UiPath path="shell.switcherCreate" />. Enter a workspace name (up to 80 characters) and a timezone, then create
   it. You become the Owner, with the four built-in roles and the default approval policy. The workspace ID in its
   URLs (`/w/<id>/…`) comes from the name and never changes when you rename the workspace. Instance admins can also
   create workspaces for someone else in <UiPath path="shell.userMenuInstanceAdmin > admin.workspaces" />.

   To look around first, choose <UiPath path="overview.exploreDemo" /> on the Overview of a workspace that has no
   sending provider connected yet. ReputeCast creates a separate workspace you own, Acme Newsletter (demo), with three
   lists, 240 sample contacts, demo connections of Amazon SES, Postmark and Postal with their routing, and a draft
   campaign, then opens it. Demo connections never send. Choosing it again opens the same demo workspace.

2. **Switch workspaces.** The switcher lists every workspace where your membership is active, oldest first, with
   your role and its number of contacts. On a phone, it sits in the <UiPath path="nav.more" /> sheet. Instance admins
   also get <UiPath path="shell.switcherAllWorkspaces" />.

3. **Edit the general settings.** Open <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.general" />.

   <Screenshot
     id="workspace-settings"
     alt="Settings › General for Acme Newsletter: the Workspace card with name, timezone, default sender and workspace ID, the Sidebar sections card with switches for Transactional, Grow and Earn, and the Languages card."
   />

   - <UiPath path="workspace.cardWorkspace" />: the name, the timezone used by schedules and reports, and the default
     sender new campaigns start with. Add senders first in [sending domains and senders](/providers/senders-and-domains/).
     The workspace ID is read-only; API URLs and the audit log use it.
   - <UiPath path="shell.sectionsTitle" />: hide Transactional, Grow or Earn for everyone in the workspace. The switch
     applies at once. Hidden sections keep working, and members can still open them by link.
   - <UiPath path="languages.settings.title" /> and <UiPath path="accessibility.policyTitle" />: the content languages
     of emails and public pages (see [send each reader their language](/campaigns/languages/)) and the
     [accessibility policy](/campaigns/accessibility/).
   - <UiPath path="workspace.cardApprovals" /> and <UiPath path="workspace.cardMembers" />: summaries with links to the
     [approval policy](/campaigns/approvals/) and to the Team and Roles pages.

   Without `workspace:manage`, the fields are read-only and the page names who can change them.

4. **Transfer ownership.** In <UiPath path="workspace.cardDanger" />, choose
   <UiPath path="workspace.transferOwnership" />, pick the new Owner and confirm with your password. Only active members
   with two-factor authentication (an authenticator app, a passkey or an identity provider that enforces it) can become
   Owner. If you sign in only through single sign-on, the transfer is accepted within 10 minutes of signing in. You
   become an Admin, the new Owner is notified, and the audit log records `workspace.transferred`.

5. **Delete the workspace.** In <UiPath path="workspace.cardDanger" />, choose
   <UiPath path="workspace.deleteWorkspace" />. Use <UiPath path="workspace.exportEverything" /> to download a ZIP with
   contacts and consent, lists, suppressions, campaigns as MJML and reports as CSV (plus the audit log as JSON if you
   hold `audit:view`). Provider credentials, API keys and secrets are never exported. The export needs
   `subscribers:export` and is refused to a member limited to some lists or sender domains. Then type the workspace
   name exactly to confirm.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
  Deleting a workspace cancels its scheduled and approved campaigns at once, and its API keys and MCP agents stop
  working. An instance admin can restore it for 7 days in <UiPath path="shell.userMenuInstanceAdmin > admin.workspaces" />
  with <UiPath path="admin.restoreWorkspace" />; after that it is purged. Member accounts and the audit log entries are
  kept.
</Aside>

## Check that it worked

- The new workspace appears in the switcher with your role.
- After a transfer, the Team page shows the new Owner and you as Admin.
- After a delete, every member gets a notification with the last day it can be restored.

## Troubleshooting

- **A member is missing from the transfer list or marked as having no two-factor.** The new Owner needs two-factor
  authentication first: ask them to add a passkey or an authenticator app in
  [Account & security](/team/account-security/).
- **The Danger zone buttons are disabled.** Only the Owner can transfer or delete. Admins hold every other permission.
- **<UiPath path="shell.switcherCreate" /> is missing at medium widths.** The narrow icon rail hides the switcher; open
  the account menu or widen the window.
- **The first-run instance admin can't create a second workspace or load the demo.** Finish the setup steps at
  `/setup` first; the demo workspace then loads next to your first workspace.
