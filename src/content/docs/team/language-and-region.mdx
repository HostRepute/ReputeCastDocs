Change ReputeCast language and region format for your own account: the language of the app's screens and of the
emails ReputeCast sends you, and how dates, times and numbers look. These settings are personal. Emails and pages for
your contacts use their own language (see [send each reader their language](/campaigns/languages/)).

## Before you begin

- No permission is needed for your own settings. Instance defaults need an
  [instance admin](/reference/glossary/#instance-admin).
- The app is available in English and Vietnamese (Tiếng Việt). Release builds offer only fully translated languages,
  and both are complete; builds run from source in development mode offer every language. To add another language,
  see [translate ReputeCast](/developers/translating/).

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="shell.userMenuAccount > language.title" />. The account menu item that shows your current
   language opens the same card.

   <Screenshot
     id="language-region"
     alt="The Language & region card: App language English, Region format ReputeCast standard following the app language, 24-hour time and weeks starting on Monday, with a preview of a date, time, number, percentage and amount."
   />

2. Pick the <UiPath path="language.appLanguage" />. The first option follows the instance default.

3. Pick a <UiPath path="language.regionFormat" />: ReputeCast standard, English (United States), Tiếng Việt (Việt Nam),
   Deutsch (Deutschland), English (United Kingdom), Español (España), Français (France) or Português (Brasil). Region
   formats are independent of the language. Until you pick one, it follows the app language.

4. Optional: override the <UiPath path="language.time" /> (24-hour or 12-hour) and
   <UiPath path="language.weekStartsOn" /> (Monday or Sunday). Until you change them, they come from the region format.

5. Check the preview line, then choose <UiPath path="language.save" />. The app switches at once, without a reload. The
   confirmation offers Undo for 10 seconds.

</Steps>

## What changes

| Setting        | Changes                                                                                                                 | Stays the same                                                                 |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| App language   | Translated screens, messages from the server (notifications, check results, audit log lines), and emails to you (invitations, password resets, notifications) | Audit action names, CSV exports, webhook payloads, permission keys, API error codes, provider names and providers' own messages, demo data, anything people typed |
| Region format  | Dates, times, numbers, percentages and amounts in the app and in emails to you                                           | The workspace timezone used for schedules and reports                          |

Text that ReputeCast stored before your instance had a language, such as older audit log lines and notifications,
keeps its English.

Times in a workspace always use the workspace timezone from <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.general" />. Your
own timezone, in <UiPath path="account.profile" />, sets your notification quiet hours.

## Instance defaults

Instance admins set the <UiPath path="language.defaultLanguage" /> and <UiPath path="language.defaultRegion" /> in
<UiPath path="shell.userMenuInstanceAdmin > admin.instance > admin.instanceCard" />. They apply to the sign-in page,
to members who haven't chosen, and to invitations. When you invite people, the
<UiPath path="language.invitationLanguage" /> field sets the language of the invitation email and the page it opens
(it appears when the instance offers more than one language; release builds offer English and Vietnamese). Once they
sign in, members pick their own.

On the sign-in, invitation and password-reset pages, visitors can switch language from the menu at the bottom of the
card. If your browser prefers a language ReputeCast offers and you still follow the instance default, the app asks
you once whether to switch.

<Aside type="note">
  ReputeCast does not support right-to-left languages. The app and its emails are left to right.
</Aside>

## Check that it worked

- Menus and buttons show the language you picked, and the account menu item names it.
- Dates in reports follow the preview line you saw before saving.

## Troubleshooting

- **Your language isn't in the list.** ReputeCast offers English and Vietnamese. Release builds offer only fully
  translated languages; see [translate ReputeCast](/developers/translating/) to add one.
- **Some text stays in English.** Demo data, what people typed, providers' own messages, audit action names and text
  stored before your instance had the language show as they were written.
- **Times look an hour off.** Check the workspace timezone in <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.general" /> and your
  own timezone in <UiPath path="account.profile" />.
