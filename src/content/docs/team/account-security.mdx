Set up two-factor authentication (passkeys or an authenticator app), keep recovery codes, and control your password,
sign-in methods and sessions from <UiPath path="shell.userMenuAccount" /> in the account menu. The rules come from
the instance's authentication policy, which an [instance admin](/self-hosting/instance-admin/) sets.

## Before you begin

- Everything on this page is about your own account; no workspace permission is needed.
- If you hold a [sensitive permission](/reference/glossary/#sensitive-permission), two-factor authentication is
  required by default after a 7-day grace period. The Authenticator app card says whether it is required for you.
- Passkeys need the instance's public URL to use a domain name, not an IP address.

<Steps>

1. **Change your password.** In <UiPath path="account.password" />, enter your current password and the new one twice,
   then choose <UiPath path="account.changePassword" />. The new password must meet the minimum length, can't be your
   current one or one of your recent passwords, and is checked against known breaches when the instance turns that on.
   Changing it signs out your other sessions. If you sign in only through single sign-on, this card is hidden.

2. **Add a passkey.** In <UiPath path="account.passkeys" />, choose <UiPath path="account.addPasskey" /> and follow your
   browser's prompt (Touch ID, Face ID, Windows Hello, a password manager or a security key). A passkey signs you in on
   its own with <UiPath path="auth.signIn.passkeySignIn" />, and after a password it counts as your second factor.
   Rename it so you recognize it later. You can add as many as you like.

3. **Set up an authenticator app.** In <UiPath path="account.authenticatorApp" />, choose
   <UiPath path="account.setUp" />. Scan the QR code with any TOTP app (1Password, Google Authenticator, Authy) or
   type the key, then enter the 6-digit code. Codes change every 30 seconds.

4. **Save your recovery codes.** After the code is confirmed, ReputeCast shows 10
   [recovery codes](/reference/glossary/#recovery-code) once. Download, print or copy them, tick that you saved them,
   and finish. Each code works once at sign-in through <UiPath path="auth.signIn.useRecovery" />.
   <UiPath path="account.regenerateCodes" /> replaces every old code with 10 new ones.

5. **Link other sign-ins.** In <UiPath path="account.connectedSignIns" />, choose <UiPath path="account.link" /> next
   to Google, Microsoft or GitHub when the instance offers them. <UiPath path="account.unlink" /> is disabled for your
   last remaining sign-in method.

6. **Review your sessions.** <UiPath path="account.activeSessions" /> lists each signed-in device and browser with its
   IP address and last activity. Use <UiPath path="account.signOut" /> on a row, or
   <UiPath path="account.signOutAllOthers" />. To end the session you are using, choose
   <UiPath path="shell.userMenuSignOut" /> in the account menu.

7. **Change your email.** In <UiPath path="account.profile" />, choose <UiPath path="account.changeEmail" />, enter the
   new address and your current password, then <UiPath path="account.sendLink" />. Open the link from the new mailbox
   within 24 hours while signed in to the same account. Your old address gets a note after the change.

</Steps>

<Aside type="danger">
  You can't turn off an authenticator app yourself, only <UiPath path="account.replaceApp" />; replacing it also
  replaces your recovery codes. If you lose your phone and your codes, an instance admin can reset two-factor for you
  in <UiPath path="shell.userMenuInstanceAdmin > admin.users" />, or with the CLI as described in
  [recover access from the command line](/self-hosting/account-recovery/).
</Aside>

## Sessions, timeouts and lockout

- A session ends after the session lifetime, or after the idle timeout without activity. Two minutes before it ends,
  a banner lets you extend the idle time; the lifetime never moves.
- When an instance admin shortens the idle timeout, existing sessions follow within about 30 seconds. A new lifetime
  applies to sessions started after the change.
- Too many wrong passwords for one email lock password sign-in for that email. A passkey sign-in still works, and
  <UiPath path="auth.forgotPassword" /> sends a reset link that works once for 15 minutes and clears the lock. Using
  it signs out all your sessions.

## Check that it worked

- The Authenticator app card shows **On** with the setup date, and the recovery codes row shows 10 remaining.
- Your passkey is listed with "used now" after you sign in with it.
- On the [Team page](/team/members/), your 2FA column shows "On · TOTP" or "On · passkey".

## Troubleshooting

- **The 6-digit code is refused.** Set your phone's clock to update automatically and try the next code.
- **"This passkey is your only way to sign in."** Add another passkey or a password before removing it.
- **"Two-factor is required for your account" when removing a passkey.** Set up an authenticator app or add another
  passkey first.
- **Every page sends you to setup.** Your grace period is over: add a passkey or an authenticator app to continue.
- **"Sign-in is locked".** Wait for the lock to end, reset your password, or sign in with a passkey. The failed attempts
  are in the [audit log](/team/audit-log/) with their IP addresses.
