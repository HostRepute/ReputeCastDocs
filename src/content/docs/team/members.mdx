Invite team members to a workspace by email or with a link, give each one a [role](/reference/glossary/#role) and an
optional [scope](/reference/glossary/#scope), and change, deactivate or remove [members](/reference/glossary/#member)
later from the Team page.

## Before you begin

- Inviting needs `team:invite`. Changing roles, deactivating and removing members needs `team:manage` (sensitive).
  Owners and Admins hold both; every built-in role can view the team.
- You can only give a role whose permissions you all hold yourself, and only a scope within your own. Nobody can
  invite someone as Owner: [transfer ownership](/team/workspaces/) instead.
- Invitation emails go out on the System stream. Without a sending provider on that stream, ReputeCast creates the
  invitations and gives you the links to share (see [connect a sending provider](/providers/connect/)).

## Invite members

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.team" /> and choose <UiPath path="team.inviteMember" />.

   <Screenshot
     id="settings-team"
     alt="The Team page for an Owner: filters, the bulk bar, six members with role, sign-in method, two-factor status and last activity, and one pending invitation with Resend, Copy link and Revoke."
   />

2. In <UiPath path="team.invite.emails" />, paste or type up to 50 addresses. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or type a comma
   after each one.

3. Pick a <UiPath path="team.invite.role" />. Editor is preselected when you can grant it; roles you can't grant are
   listed but disabled. The hint under the field says when the role makes two-factor authentication required.

4. Set when the <UiPath path="team.invite.expires" />: in 24 hours, 7 days (default) or 30 days.

5. Optional: under <UiPath path="team.invite.scope" />, choose <UiPath path="team.invite.addScope" /> to limit the
   members to some lists or sender domains (up to 100 lists and 20 sender domains). Add a personal message of up to
   1,000 characters. When the instance offers more than one language, pick the
   <UiPath path="language.invitationLanguage" /> for the email and the page it opens.

6. Send the invitations, or create them and copy the links when no System provider is connected.

</Steps>

The invitee opens the link, sets a name and password (or continues with single sign-on, Google, Microsoft or GitHub
when those are turned on) and joins with the role and scope you chose. If the role holds a sensitive permission,
their two-factor grace period starts. You get an "Invite accepted" notification.

Pending invitations are listed under the members. Use <UiPath path="team.resend" /> to send one again and restart its
expiry, <UiPath path="team.copyLink" /> to share the link yourself (the copy is logged), or
<UiPath path="team.revoke" /> to make the link stop working.

## Manage members

Choose <UiPath path="team.manage" /> on a row to open a member's page. It lists their
<UiPath path="member.effectivePermissions" /> with where each comes from (the role, an SSO group mapping or the
scope), their recent audit entries, the API keys they created, their approvals and their sessions.

- A change made with <UiPath path="member.changeRoleOrScope" /> applies on the member's next request, and the old and
  new values go to the audit log. A role set by an SSO group mapping is applied again at the next sign-in.
- <UiPath path="team.deactivate" /> signs the member out everywhere and blocks this workspace only. You choose whether
  their API keys stay or are revoked; kept keys hold no permissions while the member is deactivated. Integrations they
  connected can be taken over. To bring them back with the same role, choose <UiPath path="team.reactivate" />.
- <UiPath path="member.remove" /> deletes the membership and revokes the API keys and MCP agents they created.
  Campaigns, templates and approvals they made stay. The audit log still names them.
- <UiPath path="member.forceSignOut" /> ends all their sessions without changing access.

To act on several members, tick their rows and use the bulk bar: <UiPath path="team.changeRole" />,
<UiPath path="team.require2FA" /> (ends the grace period for members without two-factor) or
<UiPath path="team.deactivate" />. Up to 200 members at a time; your own row is never selected.

<Aside type="note">
  A workspace always keeps one Owner. The last Owner can't be demoted, deactivated or removed and can't leave; transfer
  ownership first. You also can't change, deactivate or remove someone whose role holds a permission you don't.
</Aside>

## What a Viewer sees

<Screenshot

Members without `team:invite` see the team read-only, with a note naming up to two people who can change roles. On a
page their role can't open, they can choose <UiPath path="shell.stateRequestAccess" /> and
<UiPath path="shell.requestSend" />: everyone with `team:manage` gets a notification that links to the requester's
member page. Granting it means changing their role; nobody is told automatically. One request per role every 24 hours.

## Check that it worked

- The invitee appears under pending invitations with "email via" your provider, or "link only".
- After they join, they move to the members table with the role and scope you chose, and the audit log shows
  `invitation.accept`.

## Troubleshooting

- **"… is already a member".** The address belongs to a member, possibly deactivated: reactivate them instead.
- **"… already has a pending invitation".** Use <UiPath path="team.resend" /> on the existing invitation.
- **A role is disabled in the list.** It holds a permission you don't hold. Ask an Owner to invite them.
- **The invitee sees "This invitation has expired".** They can ask you for a new one from that page; resend it.
- **Their account is deactivated on the whole instance.** Only an instance admin can reactivate it, in
  [Instance admin](/self-hosting/instance-admin/).
