Turn on the ReputeCast dark theme for your account, keep the light one, or let the app follow your device's light or
dark setting. The choice is yours alone: it changes how the app looks to you, never what your readers see. The app
has had a dark theme since 1.6.0.

## Before you begin

- No permission is needed. The theme is set per person, on your account, and applies in every workspace.
- Every member starts on <UiPath path="appearance.system" />, so the app is already dark on a device set to dark.

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="shell.userMenuAccount > appearance.title" />. The card follows
   [Language & region](/team/language-and-region/).

   <Screenshot
     id="appearance"
     alt="The Appearance card with three choices, each with a small picture of the app: Same as device, selected, which says it is light now, Light, and Dark."
   />

2. Pick <UiPath path="appearance.system" />, <UiPath path="appearance.light" /> or
   <UiPath path="appearance.dark" />. Each choice has a small picture of the app in that theme, and
   <UiPath path="appearance.system" /> shows half of each. The app switches at once, every color in the same frame, and
   the choice is saved on your account. There is no Save button.

</Steps>

Two shortcuts set the same choice from any page:

- **The account menu.** Its <UiPath path="appearance.menuLabel" /> row has three icons: a screen for
  <UiPath path="appearance.system" />, a sun for <UiPath path="appearance.light" /> and a moon for
  <UiPath path="appearance.dark" />. The menu stays open while you try them.

- **The command palette.** Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows and Linux) and

  now: <UiPath path="appearance.useDark" />, <UiPath path="appearance.useLight" /> or
  <UiPath path="appearance.useSystem" />. A message confirms the switch. See
  [keyboard shortcuts](/reference/keyboard-shortcuts/).

## Where your choice applies

- **Every browser you sign in on.** The theme is saved on your account. Another tab in the same browser switches at
  once; another browser switches the next time it reads your account.
- **From the first paint.** Each browser also keeps a copy of your choice and applies it before the page is drawn, so
  a dark page never flashes white while it loads. Scrollbars, form controls and the browser's toolbar color follow the
  theme.
- **Live with your device.** On <UiPath path="appearance.system" />, the app follows your device as it switches
  between light and dark, without a reload. The card says which one it is now.
- **In the instance audit log.** A change of theme is recorded as `account.appearance_update`, with the old and new
  choice (see [audit log](/team/audit-log/)).

## What stays light or dark

The theme changes the app's own screens. Some parts keep their colors on purpose:

- **What readers see stays light**: email bodies and thumbnails, previews of public pages, forms and the website,
  phone previews of texts, push notifications and WhatsApp messages, and QR codes. To see how an email looks in a mail
  app's dark mode, use the [composer's preview](/campaigns/composer/).
- **Code and terminal panes stay dark** in both themes. In the dark theme, the code editors on the app's own cards
  (the segment SQL editor, the composer's compiled HTML pane and the transactional template editor) use a dark caret
  and selection too.
- **Printing is always light.**
- **Public pages don't change**: signup forms, the preference center and the website look the same to everyone.

## Check that it worked

- The card you picked is selected in <UiPath path="appearance.title" />, and the account menu highlights the same
  icon.
- Sign in from another browser: the app opens in the theme you chose.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Your theme wasn’t saved, so it’s back to how it was."** ReputeCast couldn't save the last choice, so the app
  went back to your account's theme. Choose <UiPath path="common.retry" /> in the message, or pick the theme again.
  When you pick several themes in a row, they are saved in order and the last one counts.
- **The app doesn't follow your device.** You picked <UiPath path="appearance.light" /> or
  <UiPath path="appearance.dark" />, which stay the same whatever the device says. Pick
  <UiPath path="appearance.system" />.
- **The first moment of a page follows the device instead of your choice.** The browser can't keep its copy, as in
  some private windows or when site storage is blocked. The page switches once your account loads.
- **An email preview is light in the dark theme.** Previews show what readers get, so they stay light.
