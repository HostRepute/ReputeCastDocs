Newsletter recommendations show other newsletters to your readers right after they confirm their subscription, and
in your emails. A [recommendation](/reference/glossary/#recommendation) is either a plain link to any newsletter's
subscribe page, or a partner link to a newsletter on another ReputeCast instance, where readers subscribe with one
click and confirm through the partner's own double opt-in.

## Before you begin

- Adding link recommendations, ordering them and choosing where they show needs the `grow:edit` permission. Owners,
  Admins and Editors have it.
- Partner links need `grow:connect`, a sensitive permission that only Owners and Admins have. Only people can use
  either permission; API keys and agents can't change recommendations.
- For partner links, both newsletters run ReputeCast, and both instances have a public URL set by an instance admin.

## Choose link or partner

- **Link:** any newsletter's subscribe page. Readers subscribe on the other site, so you see clicks only.
- **Partner on ReputeCast:** readers subscribe from your pages with one click, and the partner emails them to confirm.
  You see requests and confirmations; neither side sees the other's readers.

## Add a link

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.grow > grow.tabRecommendations" /> and select
   <UiPath path="grow.recommendations.addRecommendation" />.
2. Select <UiPath path="grow.recommendations.addLink" />.
3. Enter the newsletter's subscribe page (an `https://` address; private and loopback addresses are refused) and
   select <UiPath path="grow.recommendations.prefill" /> to read its name, description and logo.
4. Check the name and the description for readers (up to 120 characters), and choose where it shows: the
   confirmation page, the Recommendations block in emails, or both.
5. Select <UiPath path="grow.recommendations.addRecommendation" />. It shows to readers from now on.

</Steps>

## Connect a partner newsletter

The newsletter you recommend creates a partner code, and you paste it.

<Steps>

1. **The partner** opens <UiPath path="nav.grow > grow.tabRecommendations" />, selects
   <UiPath path="grow.recommendations.letRecommend" />, enters who the code is for, the list it offers and a daily
   limit (50 by default), and selects <UiPath path="grow.recommendations.codeCreate" />. The code is shown once, works
   once and expires after 7 days. The partner sends it to you.
2. **You** select <UiPath path="grow.recommendations.addRecommendation" />, then
   <UiPath path="grow.recommendations.addPartner" />, and paste the code.
3. ReputeCast asks the partner's instance for its profile over HTTPS and shows its name, description, list and daily
   limit. Select <UiPath path="grow.recommendations.connect" />.

</Steps>

During the connection, the two instances agree on a signing secret over HTTPS. It is stored encrypted on both sides
and never shown.

### How a partner signup works

1. A reader presses Subscribe on your confirmation page or in the hosted page an email links to.
2. Your instance sends the partner a signed subscribe request that carries only the reader's email address and your
   newsletter's name as the source.
3. The partner adds the address as pending and sends its own confirmation email on its System stream. The reader is
   subscribed only after confirming. An address already on the partner's list gets no email, and the answer is the
   same either way, so you never learn who already reads it.
4. The partner reports each confirmation back as a signed notice that names the request, never the reader. Your
   instance keeps counts only.

Requests are signed with HMAC-SHA256 over a timestamp, the method, the path and the body; a timestamp more than 5
minutes off is refused. An unanswered request is retried for 24 hours. When the partner's daily limit is reached, the
recommendation is hidden until 00:00 UTC. The partner request log keeps 90 days of requests and never shows
addresses.

## Where recommendations show

- **The confirmation page:** under "While you're here: newsletters … recommends", up to 3 recommendations in your
  order. Turn it off with <UiPath path="grow.recommendations.whereConfirmSwitch" />. Partner cards have a Subscribe
  button and say the reader's email address goes to that newsletter; link cards open the other site.
- **Emails:** add the <UiPath path="grow.composer.blockRecommendations" /> block from the composer's
  <UiPath path="grow.composer.group" /> blocks. Its buttons open a hosted page where the reader confirms, so link
  scanners can't subscribe anyone. That personal page works for 30 days. The block hides when nothing can show.

Shown counts confirmation pages and delivered emails, not opens, because machine opens would inflate it. See
[read campaign reports](/campaigns/reports/) for how ReputeCast tells human and machine opens apart.

## Manage partner links

From a partner recommendation's menu, you can <UiPath path="grow.recommendations.menuPausePartner" />, resume it, or
<UiPath path="grow.recommendations.menuEnd" />. Ending can't be undone: a new partnership needs a new partner code.
The partner can <UiPath path="grow.recommendations.menuChangeLimit" /> for the link it gave you. Removing a partner
recommendation also ends its link.

A partner link shows Connected while it works. Other statuses are Paused, Paused by partner, Daily limit reached,
Unreachable, Link invalid and Ended. People with `grow:connect` get a notification after a partner has been
unreachable for 30 minutes.

## Check that it worked

1. Subscribe to your own newsletter with an address you own and confirm. The confirmation page lists your
   recommendations.
2. Press Subscribe on a partner card. The partner's confirmation email arrives, naming your newsletter as the source.
3. In <UiPath path="grow.tabRecommendations" />, the recommendation counts one request, then one confirmation.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Code expired" or "Code already used".** Ask the partner for a new partner code.
- **"Private address refused".** The partner code points at a private or loopback address. Ask the partner to set
  their instance's public URL to an address reachable over the internet.
- **Link invalid.** The partner's instance rejects your signature, for example after it moved servers or ended the
  partnership. Paste a new partner code from them, or remove the recommendation.
- **Daily limit reached.** The partner accepts no more requests from you until 00:00 UTC. They can raise the limit.
