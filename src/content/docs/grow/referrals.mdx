A newsletter referral program gives every confirmed subscriber of a list a personal link to share. A referral counts
only after the friend confirms their address and a hold period passes, and referrers earn rewards at the
[milestones](/reference/glossary/#milestone) you set. Each list can have one
[referral program](/reference/glossary/#referral-program).

## Before you begin

- Setting up and changing the program needs the `grow:edit` permission; deciding on held referrals and handling
  rewards needs `grow:review`. Owners, Admins and Editors have both. Only people can use them, not API keys or agents.
- The list needs a verified sender for its milestone emails. See [add sending domains and senders](/providers/senders-and-domains/).
- Stripe coupon rewards need Stripe and the `earn:edit` permission. See [connect Stripe](/earn/connect-stripe/).

## Set up the program

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.grow > grow.tabReferrals" /> and select <UiPath path="grow.referrals.noneAction" />. A
   guide with five steps opens; <UiPath path="grow.setup.turnOn" /> is its last button, and you can save and finish
   later.
2. **List:** pick the list. People who sign up through a referral link join it. The list can't change once the
   program has been on.
3. **Milestones and rewards:** add a milestone for each number of counted referrals (for example 1, 3 and 10) with
   its reward. See [rewards](#rewards).
4. **Counting rules:** choose the hold period and the rules that hold referrals for review. See
   [how referrals count](#how-referrals-count).
5. **Emails and pages:** write each milestone's email, or choose to send none and use an automation instead.
6. **Turn on:** the guide checks that a milestone exists, that every milestone has an email or "No email", that
   download files are uploaded, that Stripe is connected for coupon rewards and that the sender is verified. Then
   select <UiPath path="grow.setup.turnOn" />.

</Steps>

When the program turns on, every confirmed subscriber of the list gets a link. People who confirm later get theirs
when they confirm, and contacts added by import or the API get theirs within a minute.

## Share links

Each subscriber's link looks like `https://news.acme.io/join/K7Q2MX`: six characters without look-alikes (no 0, O,
1, I or L). It opens an invite page with the referrer's first name, if the referrer allows it, and a signup form for
the list. Forms and the hosted subscribe page also accept `?ref=K7Q2MX`, and `?via=` records the channel (copy,
email, x, linkedin, whatsapp or other).

Signups through a referral link always confirm by email, even when the form doesn't use double opt-in. The first link
a person used wins. The link works only while the program is on, the link isn't paused and the referrer is still
subscribed; otherwise the visitor sees the normal subscribe page and nothing counts.

## How referrals count

A referral waits for the friend to confirm, then for the hold period (7 days by default; 0, 3, 7, 14 or 30). It
counts when the hold ends and the friend is still on the list. A counted referral never goes back, even if the friend
leaves later.

These referrals never count:

- **Self-referral:** the referrer's own address, including plus-tags and Gmail dots.
- **Already on the list:** the address is, or ever was, on the list.
- **Never confirmed:** no confirmation within 7 days.

You can turn these off in <UiPath path="grow.subSettings" />, but they are on by default:

- **Same IP address as the referrer**, used by the referrer in the last 30 days.
- **Disposable domain.**
- **Left during the hold:** unsubscribed, hard-bounced or complained before the hold ended.

These hold referrals for review instead of counting them:

- **Burst:** more than 5 confirmed referrals from one referrer within an hour.
- **Shared IP address:** 3 or more referrals from one IP address within 24 hours.
- **Numbered addresses:** addresses that differ only by a number, such as `ben.t01@` and `ben.t02@`.

## Review held referrals

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.grow > grow.tabReferrals > grow.subReview" />. Held referrals are grouped per referrer,
   with the signal that held them and whether anyone opened or clicked since confirming.
2. Select referrals and choose <UiPath path="grow.review.count" />, <UiPath path="grow.review.dont" /> or
   <UiPath path="grow.review.dontPause" />. The review suggests one, with its reasons. An optional note goes to the
   audit log.
3. You have 10 seconds to undo, before any reward goes out. After Count, whatever is left of the hold still applies.

</Steps>

People with `grow:review` get a notification when the queue goes from empty to waiting, then at most once a day.
Addresses and IP addresses show only to people who also have `subscribers:view`. To stop a referrer's link without
deciding on each referral, use <UiPath path="grow.referrers.pauseLink" /> in
<UiPath path="grow.subReferrers" />: visitors then see the normal subscribe page, without being told why.

## Rewards

A referrer gets each milestone's reward once, in order; someone who passes two milestones at once gets both.

<TableScroll label="Reward types">

| Reward             | How it reaches the referrer                                                                                          |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Download link      | A PDF, ZIP or image up to 100 MB, or an `https://` link. Download links expire after 7 days; the hub makes new ones.  |
| Code from a list   | One code per reward from a list you upload (up to 100,000 at a time). You get an alert before the codes run out.     |
| Manual fulfillment | Anything you send by hand. It waits under To fulfill until someone marks it fulfilled; it can ask for a shipping address. |
| Stripe coupon      | Free paid access through a single-use Stripe promotion code, from an offer of the kind Referral reward.              |
| Webhook            | ReputeCast posts a signed `referral.milestone_reached` event, and your system creates the reward.                    |

</TableScroll>

A webhook reward needs an `https://` endpoint that answers `2xx`. It can answer with `{"code": "…", "url": "https://…"}`,
which the milestone email and the hub then show. Failed calls are retried 8 times over about 6 hours, then the
reward shows Failed with a Retry button. See [receive webhooks](/developers/webhooks/) for the signature and
[webhook events](/reference/webhook-events/) for the `referral.*` events.

## Milestone emails and automations

Each milestone can send its own email when a referrer reaches it. Milestone emails go out on the Automations stream,
so the usual suppression rules apply. They come from the program's sender (the Automations default sender until you
change it in <UiPath path="grow.subSettings" />) and open in the composer with the
<UiPath path="grow.composer.blockReward" /> and <UiPath path="grow.composer.blockReferral" /> blocks.

To do more, use the automation trigger <UiPath path="grow.trigger.kind" />. It starts once per contact per milestone,
whether or not the milestone sends its own email. See [triggers and steps](/automations/triggers-and-steps/).

## What subscribers see

- **The Referral block:** add it from the composer's <UiPath path="grow.composer.group" /> blocks to show each
  reader their link and progress, in one of three layouts: Single line, Card with progress or Card with next rewards.
  It hides for readers who aren't on the program's list, while the program is off, and in the archive.
- **Liquid tokens:** for your own text, such as `{{ referral.link }}`, `{{ referral.count }}` or
  `{{ referral.to_next }}`. They print nothing for readers outside the program, so add a fallback with
  `| default:`. See [personalize emails with Liquid](/campaigns/personalization/).
- **The referral hub:** a private page ("Your referrals") with the reader's link, progress, rewards and referrals
  (numbers and dates only). Its link works for 60 days; an expired link offers "Email me my link". The hub is never
  cached or listed by search engines.
- **The leaderboard:** off by default. When on, `/leaderboard` shows the top 10 referrers (or 5 or 25) this month or
  all time, updated hourly. Names show as "Sam R." only for readers who opt in on their hub; everyone else is "A
  reader".
- **The preference center** shows an "Invite friends" section with the reader's progress.

<Aside type="note">
  The invite page, the hub and the leaderboard are in English only for now, whatever the reader's language.
</Aside>

## Check that it worked

1. In <UiPath path="nav.grow > grow.tabOverview" />, the Referral program tile shows the program as on.
2. Open your own hub from a test contact's milestone email or Referral block, copy the link and sign up with another
   address you own from a different network.
3. Confirm the signup. In <UiPath path="grow.subResults" />, the referral shows as waiting for the hold to end.

## Troubleshooting

- **A referral didn't count.** <UiPath path="grow.subResults" /> gives the reason for every referral that didn't
  count, such as "self-referral" or "never confirmed". Referrers see a plainer version on their hub.
- **Referrals wait for review.** The burst, shared IP or numbered-address rule matched. Decide in
  <UiPath path="grow.subReview" />, or raise the burst or shared IP threshold in <UiPath path="grow.subSettings" />.
- **A Code from a list reward shows Waiting for codes.** The list ran out. Upload more codes: waiting rewards go out
  as soon as codes are added.
- **The Referral block doesn't show in a test.** The test contact isn't on the program's list, or the program is off.
