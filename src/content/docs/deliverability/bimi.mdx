[BIMI](/reference/glossary/#bimi) (Brand Indicators for Message Identification) lets mail apps that support it show
your logo next to your messages. This BIMI setup guide covers what mailbox providers require, how to publish the
record, and what ReputeCast checks: it confirms that a valid BIMI record exists for the sending domain and shows it
under <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.senders" />. BIMI is optional: a missing record never changes the
[Send Readiness](/reference/glossary/#send-readiness) verdict, and an invalid one only warns.

## What BIMI needs

<TableScroll label="BIMI requirements by source">

| Requirement   | BIMI Group                                                   | Gmail                                                                     | Yahoo                                         |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| DMARC policy  | `p=quarantine` or `p=reject`, also for subdomains (`sp=`); `pct` 100 | `p=quarantine` or `p=reject`, `pct=100`                          | `p=quarantine` or `p=reject`                  |
| Logo          | SVG Tiny PS, the profile the BIMI standard defines           | SVG Tiny PS (`baseProfile="tiny-ps"`, `version="1.2"`), at least 96 px, size in absolute pixels | A valid SVG logo                             |
| Certificate   | VMC or CMC highly recommended; logos without one have limited support | Required: a Verified Mark Certificate (VMC) or a Common Mark Certificate (CMC) | Not listed                       |
| Other         | SPF, DKIM and DMARC aligned for all mail                     | Files served over HTTPS                                                   | Bulk mail with sufficient reputation and engagement |

</TableScroll>

Sources, checked October 2026: [BIMI Group, "Implementation Guide"](https://bimigroup.org/implementation-guide/),
[Google Workspace Admin Help, "Set up BIMI"](https://support.google.com/a/answer/10911320) and
[Yahoo Sender Hub, FAQ](https://senders.yahooinc.com/faqs/). A VMC needs a registered trademark for the logo; Google
notes that trademark registration can take 6 to 12 months. A CMC covers logos without a trademark. Both are issued by
the [Mark Verifying Authorities](https://bimigroup.org/vmc-issuers/) listed by the BIMI Group.

## Before you begin

- The sending domain has working SPF, DKIM and DMARC ([Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC](/deliverability/spf-dkim-dmarc/)),
  and the DMARC reports show that your legitimate mail passes ([Read DMARC aggregate reports](/deliverability/dmarc-reports/)).
  Moving to an enforced policy before that sends real mail to spam.
- You can publish DNS records for the domain and files on an HTTPS web server.
- Seeing the BIMI status in ReputeCast needs `providers:view`, which the Owner, Admin, Editor and Viewer roles have.

## Publish your BIMI record

<Steps>

1. Move DMARC to enforcement: `p=quarantine` or `p=reject`, with `pct` left out (it defaults to 100) or set to 100. If
   the record sits at the organizational domain, set `sp=` for subdomains too.

   ```txt title="_dmarc.acme.io TXT"
   v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; sp=quarantine; rua=mailto:dmarc-reports@acme.io
   ```

2. Prepare the logo as an SVG Tiny PS file. For Gmail, center it in a square on a solid background and keep it at
   32 KB or less ([Google, "Set up BIMI"](https://support.google.com/a/answer/10911320), checked October 2026).

3. Request a VMC or a CMC for the logo from a Mark Verifying Authority. You receive a PEM file with the certificate
   and the logo.

4. Upload the SVG and the PEM file to a web server on your domain that serves them over HTTPS.

5. Publish a TXT record at `default._bimi.<sending domain>`, for example `default._bimi.news.acme.io`:

   ```txt title="default._bimi.news.acme.io TXT"
   v=BIMI1; l=https://acme.io/brand/bimi-logo.svg; a=https://acme.io/brand/bimi-certificate.pem
   ```

   Fill in `l=` with the address of the SVG even when you also publish a certificate in `a=`: ReputeCast reports a
   record without an `https://` logo address as invalid. Without a certificate, leave out `a=`.

6. Wait for mailbox providers to pick it up. Google says it can take up to 48 hours before the logo shows.

</Steps>

## What ReputeCast checks

ReputeCast reads the TXT record at `default._bimi.<sending domain>` each time it checks the domain's records: every 30
minutes, and when you open the domain's records page.

- It looks only at the sending domain itself. A record published only at `default._bimi.acme.io` doesn't count for
  `news.acme.io`.
- The record must start with `v=BIMI1` and have an `l=` address that starts with `https://`.
- ReputeCast doesn't download the logo or the certificate, and doesn't check the DMARC policy for BIMI. Mailbox
  providers check those themselves.

The result shows in three places:

- **Domains table** under <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.senders" />, column BIMI: `Set`, `Invalid`, `Not set`,
  or `Not checked` before the first check.
- **The domain's records page** (select <UiPath path="senders.records" />): the <UiPath path="connect.domain.optionalTitle" />
  card says whether the record is published.
- **Send Readiness**, Authentication group: "BIMI record found" when it exists, otherwise "BIMI not required"; both
  pass. An invalid record warns ("The BIMI record of news.acme.io is invalid"), because mailbox providers show no logo
  until it's fixed. A DNS lookup that fails is <UiPath path="review.statusUnknown" />. BIMI never blocks a send.

## Check that it worked

- The BIMI column for the domain shows `Set`.
- After a send, your logo appears next to your messages in Gmail and Yahoo Mail on the web. It appears only where the
  mailbox provider's own conditions are met, so it can take a while and may not show for every message.

## Troubleshooting

- **BIMI shows Invalid.** The record has no `l=` tag, an empty one, or a logo address that isn't `https://`. Fix the
  record; ReputeCast checks again within 30 minutes.
- **BIMI shows Not set, but the record exists.** It is published at the organizational domain or under another
  selector. Publish it at `default._bimi.<sending domain>`.
- **No logo in Gmail.** Gmail needs a VMC or CMC, DMARC at `quarantine` or `reject` with `pct=100`, and an SVG Tiny PS
  logo. See Google's [Troubleshoot BIMI issues](https://support.google.com/admin/security/troubleshoot-bimi-issues)
  (checked October 2026).
- **No logo in Yahoo Mail.** Yahoo shows logos only for bulk mail from senders with enough reputation and engagement.
  Check in Yahoo Mail on the web with a cleared cache.
