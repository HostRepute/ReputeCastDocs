ReputeCast sends web push notifications to readers' browsers on your own sites without an outside push account: it
generates the VAPID keys for your workspace, serves the service worker, shows a prompt you design and sends campaigns
or automation steps with a daily cap and quiet hours. Readers who turn notifications on become push subscribers, linked
to their contact when they're signed in or known.

## Before you begin

- Turning push on, adding sites and changing the cap or quiet hours needs `providers:manage`. Designing and publishing
  the prompt needs `forms:edit`.
- Sending or scheduling a push campaign needs `campaigns:send` and `push:send`, and publishing an automation with a
  push step needs `push:send`. Both send keys are [sensitive permissions](/reference/glossary/#sensitive-permission).
  Without them, the composer offers **Review & request approval** instead. Read
  [Require approval for risky sends](/campaigns/approvals/).
- Sites must use HTTPS (plain HTTP works only for `localhost`).

## Turn on push

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.providers > channels.push" />. If push is the first channel besides email, choose
   <UiPath path="channels.addChannel" /> and pick push.

2. Choose <UiPath path="push.turnOn" />. ReputeCast creates the workspace's VAPID key pair: the public key is shown,
   the private key is sealed (encrypted) and never returned. The contact address the push services can use if
   something goes wrong starts as your email; change it with **Edit** (a `mailto:` or `https:` address). Turning push
   on asks nobody anything until you publish a prompt. If the workspace has a
   [newsletter website](/forms/website/), it's added as the first site.

3. Add your other sites with <UiPath path="push.addSite" />, for example `help.acme.io`. Browsers accept a service
   worker only from the site's own origin, so each site hosts one file:

   - Add the snippet to every page:

     ```html title="Every page of the site"
     <script async src="https://reputecast.acme.io/push/v1/prompt.js"
       data-workspace="acme-newsletter"
       data-site="help.acme.io"></script>
     ```

   - Download `rc-push-sw.js` from the Setup page and upload it to the site's root, so it's served at
     `https://help.acme.io/rc-push-sw.js`. If the site already has a service worker, add the import line the Setup page
     gives you (`importScripts("https://reputecast.acme.io/push/v1/sw.js");`) to it instead.

   Then choose **Check**. The publication website needs nothing: ReputeCast serves its worker itself.

</Steps>

ReputeCast checks each site's worker every day. When a worker goes missing, pushes to that site are held, members with
`providers:view` get a notification, and the held pushes go out when the worker is back (unless they expired). Push
subscriptions from sites that aren't on the list are refused.

<Aside type="caution" title="Rotating the keys ends every subscription">
**Rotate keys** creates a new key pair and ends all subscriptions on every site: readers have to allow notifications
again. Queued pushes are cancelled. Rotate only if the private key may have leaked; you type the subscription count to
confirm.
</Aside>

## Design the prompt

Choose <UiPath path="push.newPushPrompt" /> on the Setup page. ReputeCast first shows its own soft prompt, and the
browser's permission prompt appears only after the reader chooses **Allow**, so "Not now" never uses up the browser's
one chance to ask.

- **Style**: Slide-in bar (bottom of the page), Dialog (a centred card) or Bell button (opens on click; bottom right or
  bottom left).
- **Text**: the title (up to 40 characters), the message (up to 120) and the two button labels.
- **When**: after a number of seconds on a page, after scrolling a share of a post, after reading a number of posts, or
  when the reader clicks your own button (any element with `data-rc-push`). At least one must be on.
- **Where**: every page except some, or only some pages (up to 20 paths).
- **Frequency**: after "Not now", ask again in 1–365 days.
- **iPhone and iPad**: Safari supports web push only for sites added to the Home Screen. Show the steps to add the
  site, or stay hidden.

**Save & publish** puts the prompt live. The prompt is never shown in a browser that has blocked notifications, and each
subscription keeps a consent record.

## Send a push campaign

<Steps>

1. In <UiPath path="nav.campaigns" />, start a new campaign and choose **Push notification**. The card appears once
   push is set up.

2. Write the notification: **Title** (up to 50 characters recommended; Chrome on Windows shows about 40),
   **Message** (up to 120 recommended), **Link** (clicks get `utm_source=push`, `utm_medium=push` and
   `utm_campaign=<campaign>` when the link has none), the icon (the site's icon, else the workspace logo), an optional
   image (2:1 works best), up to two buttons (shown by Chrome and Edge), **Expires after** and an optional **Tag** to
   replace an earlier notification with the same tag.

3. Check the previews. Each one marks where that platform cuts the text: Chrome on Windows, Chrome on macOS, Safari on
   macOS, Chrome on Android and Safari on iPhone. **Send to my browser** sends a test to you.

   <Screenshot
     id="push-composer"
     alt="The push composer: title 22 of 50 characters, message 94 of 120, the link, icon, image and two buttons on the left, and previews for Chrome on Windows, Chrome on macOS, Chrome on Android and Safari on iPhone on the right."
   />

4. Choose who gets it: one or more sites, optionally only subscribers linked to contacts on chosen lists or segments
   (anonymous subscribers drop out).

5. Choose **Review & send**. The checks are Keys, Service worker, Audience, Frequency cap, Quiet hours, Link, Lengths,
   Image and Approval. A missing key, no working service worker on any chosen site, an empty audience, link or title
   block the send; the rest warn. Send now or schedule, and choose what happens to subscribers outside quiet hours:
   **Hold until the window opens** (the default) or **Skip them**.

</Steps>

The report shows sent, shown and clicked notifications, people who unsubscribed within 24 hours, subscriptions removed
because the browser dropped them, and held and skipped notifications, by browser and by site. "Shown" is reported by
the browser and can be late or missing, most often in Firefox.

In [automations](/automations/triggers-and-steps/), the **Send push** step reaches subscriptions linked to the
contact only, and waits for the window or skips the notification outside quiet hours.

## Frequency cap and quiet hours

- **Frequency cap**: at most 2 notifications a day per subscription (browser), counted across campaigns and automation
  steps; set 1–24. Anyone over the cap is skipped and counted in the report.
- **Quiet hours**: on by default, in each subscriber's time zone from the browser. Push uses the same window as SMS and
  WhatsApp (09:00–20:00 by default, never outside 08:00–21:00); change it in
  <UiPath path="nav.providers > channels.sms > sms.tabRules" />. Read
  [Send SMS campaigns](/channels/sms/#quiet-hours).

## Check that it worked

- On <UiPath path="nav.providers > channels.push > push.tabSetup" />, every site shows a found service worker and a
  live prompt.
- <UiPath path="nav.providers > channels.push > push.tabEvents" /> lists each notification as sent, shown and clicked.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Service worker missing"**: the file isn't at the site's root or answers with an error. Upload `rc-push-sw.js` to
  the root and choose **Check**.
- **"Not a service worker"**: the address redirects or isn't served as JavaScript. Serve the file directly with a
  JavaScript content type.
- **"Site unreachable"**: the site answered 5xx or 429. Try **Check again** later.
- **The prompt never appears**: the prompt isn't published, the page is excluded, the reader chose "Not now"
  recently, or the browser blocked notifications for the site.
- **No prompt on iPhone**: the site isn't added to the Home Screen.
