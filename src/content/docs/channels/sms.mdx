ReputeCast lets you run SMS marketing self-hosted, through your own account at Twilio, Vonage, Plivo, Telnyx or AWS End
User Messaging. Texts are routed by country to a registered sender, go only to people who agreed to
texts on that list, wait for quiet hours to end and stop on their own when failures, carrier filtering, opt-outs or
your budget pass a limit.

## Before you begin

- Connecting providers, adding senders and changing routes and rules needs `providers:manage` (Owner and Admin).
  Everyone with `providers:view` can see them and use the route tester.
- Sending or scheduling an SMS campaign needs `campaigns:send` and `sms:send`. Publishing an automation with an SMS
  step needs `automations:publish` and `sms:send`. Both send keys are
  [sensitive permissions](/reference/glossary/#sensitive-permission); without them the composer offers **Review &
  request approval**.
- Have an account at the SMS provider and at least one sender that is registered for the countries you text (see
  [Senders and registration](#senders-and-registration)).
- Contacts need a phone number and SMS consent on the list you send to. Consent for texts is separate from email.

## Connect an SMS provider

<ProviderMatrix family="sms" />

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.providers" />. Until a second channel is set up, choose
   <UiPath path="channels.addChannel" /> and pick SMS; after that, switch to
   <UiPath path="nav.providers > channels.sms > sms.tabProviders" /> and choose
   <UiPath path="smsProviders.addProvider" />.

2. **Connection.** Enter the provider's credentials:

   - **Twilio**: Account SID, an API key SID and its secret (never the auth token), and live or test.
   - **Vonage**: API key, API secret and the signature secret (Settings › API settings › Signed webhooks, with HMAC
     SHA-256).
   - **Plivo**: auth ID and auth token.
   - **Telnyx**: API key, the account's public key and, optionally, a messaging profile ID (empty creates one).
   - **AWS End User Messaging**: the region, a role ARN with external ID or access keys, and the opt-out list.

   ReputeCast registers its webhook at the provider. If that fails, it shows a webhook address and a signing secret
   once, to paste in the provider's console.

3. **Senders.** Import the provider's numbers and add sender IDs. Twilio numbers are sorted into toll-free, 10DLC and
   local for you.

4. **Test text.** Send "This is a test from ReputeCast." to your own number, with the brand prefix and opt-out text.

5. **Countries.** Choose which countries this provider texts, then **Finish**.

</Steps>

### Provider health

A provider is **Degraded** when texts went out but no delivery receipt came back for 2 hours, and **Down** after 3
failed calls in a row; rejected credentials show as **Credentials rejected**. Registrations are read every hour, prices
every day and provider opt-out lists every 15 minutes.

## Senders and registration

Carriers only deliver from registered senders. ReputeCast tracks each sender's registration per country and never
texts a country where it isn't Verified, Approved or Allowed without registration: those texts are held.

| Sender | Where it works | Registration |
|---|---|---|
| Toll-free number | The countries listed on the sender (US and Canada) | Toll-free verification |
| 10DLC number | US only | An approved brand and campaign |
| Local or mobile number | Its own country | Allowed without registration |
| Sender ID (up to 11 letters and digits) | Country by country; not in the US or Canada | Allowed in many European countries, registered elsewhere |

Registration statuses are Not registered, Pending, Verified, Approved, Rejected, Allowed without registration and Not
supported. Vonage reports no registration status through its API, so you set those by hand (with the date, and a reason
when rejected). Approving a sender releases the texts held for it.

## Route by country

On <UiPath path="nav.providers > channels.sms > sms.tabRules" />, each country belongs to one route with a sender and an
optional fallback. The country comes from the phone number, not the contact's address.

- <UiPath path="smsRules.otherCountries" /> is <UiPath path="smsRules.dontSend" /> by default, so a number in a country
  you didn't plan for is skipped. You can send them through a chosen route instead.
- A fallback takes over when the primary provider is down or its credentials are rejected. It must be registered for
  the same countries; it doesn't cover a sender that isn't registered.
- The **Route tester** shows, for a phone number, the provider, sender, registration, price per segment, local time and
  whether quiet hours would hold the text.

## Consent and keywords

SMS consent is kept per list, apart from email: STOP never unsubscribes someone from email, and an email unsubscribe
never stops texts. Each change keeps its evidence: the phone number, where it was given (a form, the preference center,
an import, the API or your team), the exact consent text and version, the keyword, IP address and time. Read
[Lists, consent and double opt-in](/audience/lists-and-consent/).

- **Recording consent by hand** (<UiPath path="nav.audience" /> › a contact › **Record SMS consent**, with
  `subscribers:edit`) needs where they gave it, the text they agreed to and when.
- **Imports** bring SMS consent only with date, source and consent-text columns; otherwise the number is imported
  without consent.
- **Opt-in confirmation** is on: the first text on a list confirms the signup. **Ask them to reply YES first** turns it
  into a double opt-in.
- **Text message terms**: ReputeCast publishes a terms page carriers ask for; edit it under
  <UiPath path="nav.forms" /> › <UiPath path="channels.smsTermsTab" />.

Keywords work when they are the whole reply, in any case:

| Keyword | Words | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Opt-out | STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, OPTOUT, REVOKE | Consent is withdrawn on every list for that number, the number is suppressed, and one reply is sent |
| Opt-in | START, UNSTOP | The suppression is lifted and exactly the consents STOP removed come back |
| Help | HELP, INFO | The help reply is sent |

You can add up to 30 words per list and edit the replies (up to 640 characters each); STOP and HELP can't be removed.
Keyword replies go at once, even in quiet hours. Any other reply appears on the contact's timeline and in the campaign
report; there is no two-way inbox.

A number that opted out by text stays suppressed until the person texts START; your team can't remove it. Numbers are
also suppressed automatically when a text fails as a landline, invalid or not-in-service number, when the provider's
opt-out list has them, or after 3 carrier-filtered texts in 30 days. Read [The suppression list](/audience/suppressions/).

## Quiet hours

Texts go only between 09:00 and 20:00 in each recipient's local time. You can narrow the window under
<UiPath path="smsRules.quietTitle" />, but never beyond 08:00–21:00. The time zone is the contact's time zone field, or
it comes from the phone number (the area code, else the country's main zone).

Texts outside the window wait for it to open; they aren't dropped. Push notifications and WhatsApp messages use the
same window.

## Write and send a campaign

<Steps>

1. In <UiPath path="nav.campaigns" />, start a new campaign and choose <UiPath path="sms.newText" />. The card appears
   once an SMS provider is connected.

2. Write the text. The composer counts characters and segments, including the brand prefix and the opt-out text
   ("Reply STOP to opt out" by default), which are added to every campaign text. **Personalize** inserts tokens such as
   `{{ first_name | default: "there" }}`; read [Personalize emails with Liquid](/campaigns/personalization/).

   <Screenshot
     id="sms-composer"
     alt="The SMS composer: the text with the brand prefix and opt-out text locked in, the character and segment count, a per-country cost estimate, and a phone preview."
   />

3. Mind the segments. A text in plain GSM-7 characters fits 160 characters in one segment (153 per part when longer);
   a single character outside GSM-7, such as a curly quote or an emoji, switches the whole text to Unicode: 70
   characters, 67 per part. **Replace them all** swaps curly quotes, dashes and ellipses for plain ones. Review warns
   above 3 segments.

4. **Links** are shortened on your own short-link domain, set under <UiPath path="smsRules.messageRulesTitle" />
   (a CNAME to your instance, for example `go.acme.io`). Each recipient gets their own 6-character code, and clicks get
   `utm_source=sms`, `utm_medium=sms` and `utm_campaign` tags. Public shorteners such as bit.ly or tinyurl.com block
   the send, and a link without a verified short-link domain blocks it too.

5. **Send test** texts your test numbers. Then choose **Review & send**. Review checks the audience, senders,
   registration and routes, consent, suppressions, quiet hours, time zones, the opt-out text, the brand prefix, links,
   length and budget.

6. Pick the schedule: **Now**, **At one time for everyone** or **At a time in each recipient's time zone**. The cost
   estimate uses each provider's pricing per country and each person's own text length. For more than 1,000
   recipients or an estimated cost above $100, you type the recipient count to confirm.

</Steps>

## Budget and auto-pause

Under <UiPath path="smsRules.budgetTitle" /> you can set a monthly budget in US dollars. Members with
`providers:manage` are alerted at the alert level (80% by default) and at 100%. At 100%, Review blocks new sends, a
running campaign pauses and automation texts are held until you raise the budget.

A running campaign also pauses itself when, after its first 50 texts, more than 5% fail, more than 2% are filtered by
carriers or more than 3% of recipients opt out. You can change the thresholds for the workspace or per campaign in
Review & send. Nothing already sent is recalled; resuming needs `campaigns:send` and `sms:send`.

## Read the report

The SMS report shows texts sent, delivered and failed (with reasons), clicks (link previews counted as machines, never
people), opt-outs and the cost, by sender and by country, plus delivery over time and replies. **Export CSV** gives the
same per country and sender.

Text content is kept for 30 days by default (none, 7, 30, 60 or 90 days under
<UiPath path="smsRules.retentionTitle" />); delivery events and clicks are kept for 90 days.

## Troubleshooting

- **Texts to a country are held**: the sender isn't registered there (status Not registered, Pending, Rejected or Not
  supported). Register it, or route the country through a sender that may text it.
- **Numbers in a country are skipped**: no route covers it and every other country is set to
  <UiPath path="smsRules.dontSend" />.
- **Review blocks on links**: set and verify the short-link domain, or replace a public shortener with the full URL.
- **The campaign paused itself**: open the report to see which threshold it passed, fix the cause, then resume or
  resume with changes.
- **A provider shows Degraded**: receipts aren't arriving. Check the webhook on the provider's side and the
  <UiPath path="nav.providers > channels.sms > sms.tabEvents" /> tab.
