Your branding (publisher name, logo, colors and font) styles every public page ReputeCast serves for a workspace:
the subscribe and confirmation pages, the preference center, the unsubscribe page, the archive, the website and
landing pages. Embedded signup forms use its accent color and your site's own fonts. Custom domain newsletter pages
answer at the root of an address you own, such as `https://news.acme.io/subscribe`, instead of under
`/p/<workspace>/` on your ReputeCast server.

## Before you begin

- You need the `forms:edit` permission. Owners, Admins and Editors have it.
- For a custom domain, you need access to the domain's DNS records and an operator who can give the reverse proxy a
  TLS certificate for the domain. ReputeCast doesn't issue certificates itself.

## Set your branding

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.forms > forms.tabs.branding" />.

2. Enter the <UiPath path="forms.branding.publisherName" />, up to 80 characters. It heads every public page and
   starts as the workspace's name.

3. Upload a <UiPath path="forms.branding.logo" />: SVG, PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, up to 256 KB. An SVG or a 200 px PNG
   is enough. An SVG with scripts, event attributes or embedded content (`<foreignObject>`, `<iframe>`, `<object>`,
   `<embed>`) is refused, not cleaned: export it again as a plain SVG or a PNG. The logo is also the website's
   favicon. Without a logo, pages show the publisher name's first letter on a dark tile.

4. Set the <UiPath path="forms.branding.accent" /> and the <UiPath path="forms.branding.background" /> as hex colors
   such as `#4338CA` and `#F5F3FF` (the defaults). See [how colors stay readable](#how-colors-stay-readable).

5. Choose the <UiPath path="forms.branding.font" />: IBM Plex Sans (the default), System default, or Custom web font.
   A custom font needs the family name as in its CSS, such as `Inter`, and an `https://` address ending in `.woff2` or
   `.woff`.

6. Optionally, enter your <UiPath path="forms.branding.privacy" /> address, such as `https://acme.io/privacy`. Pages
   show it as a Privacy link in the footer, and the words "privacy policy" in a form's consent text link to it.

7. Turn <UiPath path="forms.branding.poweredBy" /> on or off. It is on by default.

8. Check the preview and select <UiPath path="forms.branding.save" />. Every public page uses the new branding from
   its next visit.

</Steps>

### How colors stay readable

ReputeCast keeps text on public pages at the WCAG 2.2 AA contrast of 4.5:1, whatever colors you pick:

- If white text on your accent color is below 4.5:1, buttons use dark text instead.
- If the accent color is too light for links, links use a darker shade of it that keeps 4.5:1 on white and on your
  background.
- The only color ReputeCast refuses is a background so dark that the gray footer text falls below 4.5:1. Pick a
  lighter background.

The message after saving says which colors were adjusted and why.

## Use your own domain

<Steps>

1. In <UiPath path="forms.branding.customDomain" />, enter the domain without `https://` or a path, for example
   `news.acme.io`, and select <UiPath path="forms.branding.save" />. A domain can serve only one workspace, and it
   can't be your ReputeCast server's own address.

2. At your DNS host, add one of these records. The app shows the exact values for your domain.

   - **For a subdomain** such as `news.acme.io` (the usual case): a `CNAME` record from `news.acme.io` to your
     server's public URL host, such as `reputecast.acme.io`.
   - **For an apex domain** such as `acme.io`, which can't have a CNAME: `A` and `AAAA` records with the same
     addresses as your server's public URL host.
   - **To prove ownership another way:** a `TXT` record named `_reputecast.news.acme.io` whose value is
     `reputecast-verify=` followed by 24 characters.

   The TXT record only proves the domain is yours. Visitors still have to reach your server, so the domain's own
   records must lead to it or to your reverse proxy.

3. Select <UiPath path="forms.branding.checkDns" />. While the record is missing, the domain shows "Waiting for DNS";
   a wrong record shows "DNS check failed" with the reason, for example a CNAME that points elsewhere. DNS changes can
   take up to an hour to show. ReputeCast checks again every 10 minutes by itself.

4. Ask your operator to issue a certificate for the domain on the reverse proxy and to pass the original `Host`
   header through. See [custom domains in the reverse proxy guide](/self-hosting/reverse-proxy/#custom-domains).

</Steps>

### What changes when the domain is verified

Only a verified domain serves pages. Once it is:

- Public pages answer at the domain's root: `/subscribe`, `/preferences`, `/unsubscribe`, `/archive`, the website,
  landing pages, and the Grow and Earn pages. The same pages keep working under `/p/<workspace>/` on your server.
- Canonical links, the sitemap and the page addresses shown in the app use the domain.
- [Signup form](/forms/signup-forms/) scripts load from `/p/<workspace>/forms/` on the domain. Copy the embed code
  again so your site uses the new address; the old one keeps working.
- Links in emails (unsubscribe, preferences, confirmation and view online) keep using your server's public URL.
- Without the [website](/forms/website/), the domain's root redirects to `/subscribe`.

A verified domain is checked again once a day. If one check fails, it keeps serving pages and shows "Last check
failed"; a second failure stops it until DNS is fixed. While the website is on, people with the `website:manage`
permission get a notification when the domain stops checking out or its certificate fails.

### For operators: issue certificates for verified domains only

ReputeCast answers `GET /p/_/tls-check?domain=<host>` with `200` when the host is a verified custom domain and `404`
otherwise. Point an on-demand TLS check at it, such as Caddy's `ask http://localhost:8080/p/_/tls-check`, so your proxy
requests certificates only for domains a workspace has verified. ReputeCast routes requests by the `Host` header, so
the proxy must keep it. The full configuration is in [reverse proxy and TLS](/self-hosting/reverse-proxy/).

## Check that it worked

1. Open `https://<your domain>/subscribe`. The page shows your logo, colors and font, and the browser shows a valid
   certificate.
2. In the app, the subscribe and archive addresses under <UiPath path="nav.forms > forms.tabs.pages" /> start with
   your domain.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Use a domain name like news.acme.io, without https:// or a path."** Remove the scheme, path and port.
- **"… already serves another workspace's pages."** Remove the domain from that workspace first.
- **"No CNAME from … to … yet."** The record isn't visible yet. Wait up to an hour and select
  <UiPath path="forms.branding.checkDns" />.
- **The domain is verified, but the browser shows a certificate error.** The proxy has no certificate for the domain.
  Ask your operator to set up on-demand TLS or a certificate for it.
- **"This SVG contains scripts or embedded content."** Export the logo again as a plain SVG or a PNG.
- **"The logo is … KB; the limit is 256 KB."** Use a smaller file. An SVG or a 200 px PNG is enough.
