A multi-step signup form asks for its fields in two to four steps, one step at a time: for example the email first,
then a name and a question about what the reader wants. It sends the signup once, from the last step. Choice questions on any form add
a tag to the contact for each answer picked, so a [tag trigger](/automations/triggers-and-steps/) can start the right
automation. Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

## Before you begin

- You need the `forms:edit` permission. Owners, Admins and Editors have it.
- Start from a form that works in one step. See [add a signup form to your site](/forms/signup-forms/).
- Steps show one at a time where the embed script renders the form: embedded forms and popups. Every other place shows
  all the steps at once; see [where all the steps show at once](#where-all-the-steps-show-at-once).

## Split the form into steps

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.forms > forms.tabs.signupForms" /> and pick the form.

2. Under <UiPath path="forms.builder.steps" />, select <UiPath path="forms.builder.severalSteps" />. The email field
   moves to step 1 and every other field to step 2.

3. Select <UiPath path="forms.builder.addStep" /> for a third or fourth step. A form has at most 4 steps.

4. Give each step a <UiPath path="forms.builder.stepHeading" />, a <UiPath path="forms.builder.stepDescription" /> and a
   <UiPath path="forms.builder.stepButton" />, or leave them empty. An empty button reads
   <UiPath path="pagelabels.form.step.next" /> on the earlier steps and takes the form's own button text on the last
   one.

5. Move each field to its step with the up and down arrows next to it. A field at the edge of its step moves on into the
   step before or after it. You can also drag a field onto another step's card. Every field, the email included, sits in
   exactly one step.

6. To check a step, select its card: the preview shows that step. With three or four steps, each card has
   <UiPath path="forms.builder.remove" />; the removed step's fields join the step before it (the first step's join the
   next one).

7. Select <UiPath path="forms.builder.savePublish" />.

</Steps>

The last step always carries the lists, the consent box and the submit button. To show everything at once again, select
<UiPath path="forms.builder.oneStep" />. A field you add later joins the last step, and a field you take off the form
leaves its step. A step left without fields must get one back, or be removed, before the form saves.

## Ask a choice question

A choice question asks visitors to pick one or several answers. Each answer has a tag, and every answer picked adds its
tag to the contact. The answer itself isn't stored in a contact field.

<Steps>

1. Under <UiPath path="forms.builder.fields" />, select
   <UiPath path="forms.builder.addCustomField" /> › <UiPath path="forms.builder.addChoice" />.

2. Write the <UiPath path="forms.builder.choiceQuestion" />.

3. Keep <UiPath path="forms.builder.choiceMultiple" /> on (the default) to show a checkbox per answer, or turn it off
   for one answer only. Tick <UiPath path="common.required" /> when every signup must answer.

4. Give each answer a <UiPath path="forms.builder.optionLabel" /> and a <UiPath path="forms.builder.optionTag" />. The
   tag follows the label ("Product news" becomes `product-news`) until you write your own. A question has 2 to 12
   answers; use <UiPath path="forms.builder.addOption" /> for more. Tags are saved in lower case, up to 60 characters,
   and two answers can't add the same tag.

5. Select <UiPath path="forms.builder.apply" />, then <UiPath path="forms.builder.savePublish" />.

</Steps>

  id="form-choice-dialog"

>

1. Checkboxes for several answers, or one answer only.
2. Each answer and the tag it adds.

The question appears in the builder's field list with its number of answers and an
<UiPath path="common.edit" /> button. Each question gets its own key (`q1`, `q2` and so on), never a contact field's.

The tags are added when the signup arrives, before a [double opt-in](/reference/glossary/#double-opt-in)
confirmation, and each new tag fires the <UiPath path="automations.graph.kindTagAdded" /> trigger; see
[triggers and steps](/automations/triggers-and-steps/). The `form.submitted` webhook event carries the labels of the
answers picked under the question's key, separated by commas.

## How visitors move through the steps

In an embedded form or a popup:

- One step shows at a time, under the form's heading, with a row of dots and a line such as "Step 1 of 3".
- <UiPath path="pagelabels.form.step.next" /> (or the step's own button) checks the step's required fields and the
  email's format in the browser before it moves on. <kbd>Enter</kbd> on an earlier step does the same.
- On the next step, focus moves to the step's heading, or to the "Step 2 of 3" line when the step has none, and screen
  readers hear "Step 2 of 3" from a polite live region.
- <UiPath path="pagelabels.form.step.back" /> returns to the step before and keeps the answers.
- Nothing is sent before the last step. The form posts everything once, from the last step. When the server finds a
  problem with a field on an earlier step, the form opens that step and shows the message there.

"Back", "Next" and "Step 2 of 3" are built-in labels in English, German, Spanish, French and Vietnamese, and follow the
form's language.

### Where all the steps show at once

The hosted page, the [plain HTML form](/forms/signup-forms/#use-the-plain-html-form), a landing page's signup block
and Grow's join page don't run the embed script. They show every step at once, each as a fieldset whose legend is the
step's heading, or "Step 2 of 3" when it has none, with the step's description under it. Copy the plain HTML form again
after you change the steps.

## See how far visitors get

Under the builder's last 30 days, a form with several steps shows <UiPath path="forms.builder.steps" />: a bar for each
step's views and one for the signups, each measured against step 1. <UiPath path="forms.builder.showTable" /> lists the
same numbers with each step's share of step 1.

- Step 1's views are the form's views.
- The embed script counts a view of each later step the first time a visitor reaches it on a page. It sends the count
  only from the form's allowed sites, with the form's own token, and at most 60 a minute from one address. Crawlers and
  link previews don't count.
- The hosted page and the other places that show every step at once count no later step.
- While an [A/B test](/forms/form-ab-tests/) runs, the funnel counts variant A only.

## Translate the steps

When the workspace has two or more languages, <UiPath path="pageLanguages.forms.copyByLanguage" /> lists each step's
heading, description and button and each answer's label with the form's other texts; see
[send each reader their language](/campaigns/languages/). A step text you don't write in a language stays empty there,
so the step shows the built-in "Next" and "Step 2 of 3" in that language, not your English text. Tags aren't
translated: every language adds the same tags.

<Aside type="note">
  An A/B test's variants B and C can have steps of their own, or none. See [A/B test a signup form](/forms/form-ab-tests/).
</Aside>

## Check that it worked

1. Open a page of your site with the form: only step 1 shows, with "Step 1 of" and the number of steps.
2. Select <UiPath path="pagelabels.form.step.next" /> with the email empty: the form asks for the email and stays on
   step 1.
3. Fill in the steps, select <UiPath path="pagelabels.form.step.back" /> once and check that your answers are still
   there, then sign up with an address you can read.
4. In the audience, the contact has the tags of the answers you picked. See [manage contacts](/audience/contacts/).
5. In the builder, <UiPath path="forms.builder.steps" /> under the last 30 days counts your visit on each step.

## Troubleshooting

- **Saving says "Give every step at least one field, or remove the step."** A step has no fields left. Move a field
  into it, or remove it.
- **Saving says "Keep the email field in a step."** or **"Put every field in one step."** Reload the builder: the
  steps no longer match the form's fields.
- **The choice question won't apply: "Two answers add the tag “…”: give each its own."** Change one of the tags.
- **The hosted page shows every step at once.** That is how it works: only embedded forms and popups show one step at a
  time.
- **The funnel shows no views after step 1.** Visitors saw the hosted page, or the form is embedded on a site that isn't
  under <UiPath path="forms.builder.allowedDomains" />.
- **A tag trigger didn't start.** Its tag must match the answer's tag exactly, in lower case.
