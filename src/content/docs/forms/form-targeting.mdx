Signup form targeting decides where and when a popup or embedded form shows: on some pages of your site, to visitors
from a campaign or a kind of site, on some devices, to new or returning visitors, in some countries, to the contacts of
a [segment](/reference/glossary/#segment) or everyone else, and between two dates. A form that doesn't show counts no
view. Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

## Before you begin

- You need the `forms:edit` permission. Owners, Admins and Editors have it.
- Targeting works on embedded forms and popups. A hosted page takes only the dates; see
  [open a hosted page between two dates](#open-a-hosted-page-between-two-dates).
- Country rules need the server's GeoIP database (`geoip.database`). Without it the builder turns
  <UiPath path="forms.builder.countriesGroup" /> off and says why. See [add a GeoIP database](/self-hosting/geoip/).
- Segment rules need [website tracking](/integrations/website-tracking/) on the same site.

## Set where and when

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.forms > forms.tabs.signupForms" /> and pick the form.

2. Under <UiPath path="forms.builder.whereWhen" />, select <UiPath path="common.edit" />.

3. Under <UiPath path="forms.builder.pagesGroup" />, choose <UiPath path="forms.builder.pagesAll" />,
   <UiPath path="forms.builder.pagesOnly" /> or <UiPath path="forms.builder.pagesExcept" />. For each rule, pick
   <UiPath path="forms.builder.opContains" />, <UiPath path="forms.builder.opEquals" /> or
   <UiPath path="forms.builder.opStartsWith" /> and enter the address after the domain, like `/pricing` or `/blog/`.
   Use <UiPath path="forms.builder.addRule" /> for more, up to 20. A page matches when any rule matches.

4. Under <UiPath path="forms.builder.sourcesGroup" />, enter the `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` values
   that should see the form, separated by commas, and pick where visitors <UiPath path="forms.builder.cameFrom" />:
   <UiPath path="forms.builder.refAny" />, <UiPath path="forms.builder.refSearch" />,
   <UiPath path="forms.builder.refSocial" />, <UiPath path="forms.builder.refOther" /> or
   <UiPath path="forms.builder.refDirect" />.

5. Under <UiPath path="forms.builder.devicesGroup" />, tick <UiPath path="forms.builder.desktop" />,
   <UiPath path="forms.builder.tablet" /> or <UiPath path="forms.builder.mobile" />. With none ticked, the form shows on
   every device.

6. Under <UiPath path="forms.builder.countriesGroup" />, choose <UiPath path="forms.builder.countriesOnly" /> or
   <UiPath path="forms.builder.countriesExcept" /> and enter two-letter country codes, like `US, CA`. Up to 50.

7. Under <UiPath path="forms.builder.visitorsGroup" />, pick <UiPath path="forms.builder.visitorsAll" />,
   <UiPath path="forms.builder.visitorsNew" />, <UiPath path="forms.builder.visitorsReturning" />, or a segment rule
   (see [show a form by segment](#show-a-form-by-segment)).

8. Under <UiPath path="forms.builder.scheduleGroup" />, set <UiPath path="forms.builder.starts" /> and
   <UiPath path="forms.builder.ends" /> in the workspace's time zone. Either can stay empty.

9. Select <UiPath path="forms.builder.apply" />, then <UiPath path="forms.builder.savePublish" />.

</Steps>

The section then sums the rules up in one line, joined with dots, such as "Shown on 3 pages · mobile · from US and CA ·
until 30 Nov". Without rules it reads <UiPath path="forms.builder.shownEverywhere" />. Every rule must pass for the form
to show.

## How each rule matches

| Rule             | Checked by | Matches                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------- | ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pages            | The script | The page's path and query after the domain. <UiPath path="forms.builder.opContains" /> and <UiPath path="forms.builder.opStartsWith" /> compare text, letter case included; <UiPath path="forms.builder.opEquals" /> takes the path with or without a trailing slash, or the path with its query. Rules are plain comparisons, never regular expressions. |
| UTM values       | The script | The `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` of the visit's first page in the tab, in any letter case. A list matches any of its values; an empty list matches everyone.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Came from        | The script | The site that sent the visit's first page in the tab: search engines (Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo and others), social networks (Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Reddit, YouTube, Bluesky and others), any other site, or none.                                                                                                                                        |
| Devices          | The script | <UiPath path="forms.builder.mobile" /> up to 767 px wide; <UiPath path="forms.builder.tablet" /> up to 1,099 px wide, or wider with a touch screen; <UiPath path="forms.builder.desktop" /> the rest.                                                                                                                                                     |
| New or returning | The script | Returning: this browser saw one of the workspace's forms before today. New: it didn't.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Countries        | The server | The visitor's address, looked up in the server's GeoIP database and never stored. An unknown country hides a form set to <UiPath path="forms.builder.countriesOnly" /> and shows one set to <UiPath path="forms.builder.countriesExcept" />.                                                                                                              |
| Segments         | The server | Whether the contact that website tracking knows this browser as is in the segment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Schedule         | The server | The server's clock: from the start until the end.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |

The visit's first page is kept for as long as the tab is open, so a reader who came from a search engine or a
`utm_campaign` link still matches on the next pages they open in that tab.

The embed script checks the pages, the sources, the device and new or returning visitors before it asks the server
anything. When the form has dates, countries or a segment rule, or runs an [A/B test](/forms/form-ab-tests/), the script
asks the form's session first, and the server answers `"show": false` when the form shouldn't show. Either way, a form
that doesn't show counts no view, so its rates stay true. The session takes at most 60 requests a minute from one
address; past that, it answers `"show": false` too.

## Show a form by segment

Under <UiPath path="forms.builder.visitorsGroup" />, <UiPath path="forms.segment.visitorsInSegment" /> shows the form
only to visitors ReputeCast knows who are in a segment, and <UiPath path="forms.segment.visitorsNotInSegment" /> hides it
from them while everyone else sees it. Pick the segment in <UiPath path="forms.segment.pickSegment" />. For example,
<UiPath path="forms.segment.visitorsNotInSegment" /> with a segment of your active readers keeps a popup away from
people who already read every issue.

<Screenshot
  id="form-targeting-segment"
  alt="Where and when, Visitors: Not in a segment picked, the segment Active in last 30 days, and a note that the form needs website tracking on the same site and hides from visitors ReputeCast knows who are in the segment."
/>

The rule needs [website tracking](/integrations/website-tracking/) on the same site. The embed script sends the
tracker's id for this browser with its session request; the server finds the contact that id belongs to and checks
the segment's members. A visitor the tracker doesn't know, because the site has no tracker, they didn't consent or they
were never identified, is in no segment: <UiPath path="forms.segment.visitorsInSegment" /> never shows to them, and
<UiPath path="forms.segment.visitorsNotInSegment" /> always does. A dynamic segment a live form uses is kept up to date
like one a flow uses. See [build segments](/audience/segments/).

## Open a hosted page between two dates

A hosted page's section is called <UiPath path="forms.builder.when" /> and has the dates only. Its line reads
<UiPath path="forms.builder.openNow" /> until you set one, then "Opens 1 Dec", "Closes 30 Nov" or "Open 1 Dec – 24 Dec".
Outside the dates, the page says "This form is closed", also to a visitor who sends the form from a page they opened
before the end. When an embedded form or a popup becomes a hosted page, saving keeps its dates and drops its other
rules.

Embedded forms and popups outside their dates don't show at all. A form still open on a page when its end passes
answers "This form isn't taking signups right now." and saves nothing.

## Check that it worked

1. In a private window, open a page that matches every rule: the form shows.
2. Open a page that doesn't match: no form shows, and the builder's views for the last 30 days don't grow.
3. For a UTM rule, open a page with the tag, such as `?utm_campaign=launch`, then move to another page in the same
   tab: the form still shows there.
4. For a hosted page with dates, open it before the start: it says the form is closed.

## Troubleshooting

- **The form never shows.** Read the section's summary line: every rule must pass. A
  <UiPath path="forms.builder.visitorsNew" /> rule hides the form from your own browser from the second day on, and a
  narrow browser window counts as a tablet.
- **Saving says "Add a page rule, or show the form on every page."** <UiPath path="forms.builder.pagesOnly" /> and
  <UiPath path="forms.builder.pagesExcept" /> need at least one rule with an address. Add one, or pick
  <UiPath path="forms.builder.pagesAll" />.
- **Saving says "Use letters, numbers, spaces and . \_ ~ + - only, up to 100 characters."** A UTM value has other
  characters. UTM values are matched without letter case, so `Launch` and `launch` are the same.
- **Saving says "“…” isn't a country code. Use two letters, like US or DE."** Enter ISO 3166 two-letter codes.
- **Saving says "Make the end come after the start."** The end date is before the start date.
- **<UiPath path="forms.builder.countriesGroup" /> is greyed out.** The server has no GeoIP database. Ask its operator
  to [add a GeoIP database](/self-hosting/geoip/).
- **<UiPath path="forms.segment.visitorsInSegment" /> never shows the form.** The visitor isn't known to website
  tracking on that site. Check that the tracker runs on the same pages and that visitors give consent.
- **Saving says "That segment no longer exists. Pick another one."** The segment was deleted. Pick another one.
