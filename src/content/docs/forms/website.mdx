A newsletter website turns your [archive](/reference/glossary/#archive) into a site with a home page, a post for every
issue, tag pages with feeds, member sign-in and, when you want them, members-only and paid posts. It runs on your own
domain or under `/p/<workspace>/` on your ReputeCast server, uses your branding, and counts views on the server
without cookies.

## Before you begin

- Turning the website on or off, and changing its home page, navigation, tags and access, needs the
  `website:manage` permission. Putting posts on the site or taking them down needs `website:publish`. Writing drafts
  needs `website:edit`. Owners, Admins and Editors have all three; Viewers can look but not change anything.
- Optionally, verify a [custom domain](/forms/branding-and-domain/) first. Without one, the site runs at your server's
  address, and links move to the domain once it checks out.
- Paid posts need Stripe and at least one paid tier. See [connect Stripe](/earn/connect-stripe/) and
  [sell paid subscriptions](/earn/paid-subscriptions/).
- Members get their sign-in links on the System stream, so route it to a sending provider.

## Turn on the website

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.forms > forms.tabs.website" /> and select <UiPath path="website.turnOn" />.
2. Read the four checks:
   - **Address:** where the site runs, and whether the custom domain and its certificate are ready.
   - **What changes:** how many archived campaigns become posts and which lists become tags.
   - **Home layout:** Featured and latest, Latest as a list, or Grid. You can change it later.
   - **Members:** whether readers can sign in, and which lists they are offered when they sign up on the site.
3. Select <UiPath path="website.turnOn" />. It takes about a minute.

</Steps>

When the website turns on:

- The root of the address becomes the home page. `/subscribe` stays where it is.
- Every archived campaign becomes a public post, published at its send date. Its old `/archive/<issue>` address
  redirects (301) to the post. Nothing goes behind a paywall until you set a post's visibility.
- Each list with archived campaigns becomes a tag with its own page and feed. If the header had no links, it gets the
  new tags, up to 6.
- Campaigns sent later to those lists become posts by themselves.
- The archive stays at `/archive` as the full, searchable list of posts.

Turning on is blocked only when a verified custom domain's certificate is failing. An unverified domain doesn't block
it.

## Publish a campaign on the website

While the website is on, the campaign composer has a **Web** chip in its header and a
<UiPath path="website.composer.tabWeb" /> tab in the right panel:

- **Publish to** your site: on by default when the campaign's lists are in the archive.
- **Address:** lowercase letters, digits and hyphens. If you change it after publishing, the old address redirects.
- **Title and excerpt:** up to 200 characters each; they start as the subject and preheader.
- **Cover** with alt text, **tags** (up to 5) and a **byline** (a member, or a name you type).
- **Visibility:** Public, Members or Paid (every paid tier or chosen tiers). It follows the audience by default: a
  paid-only campaign becomes a Paid post.
- **When:** with the send (the default), at a time, or now for a campaign already sent.
- <UiPath path="website.composer.featureOnHome" />, up to 3 featured posts.
- <UiPath path="website.composer.comments" />: <UiPath path="website.composer.commentsSite" /> or
  <UiPath path="website.composer.commentsOff" />. See [let members comment on posts](/forms/post-comments/).
- **SEO and social:** SEO title, description, social image, canonical URL, and an option to hide the post from search
  engines.

Each block also has <UiPath path="website.composer.showOn" /> in its style settings: Email and web, Email only or Web
only. Footer, unsubscribe, view-online, referral and poll blocks are Email only by default; a poll's answers are each
reader's own links, so it is answered from the email. Posts published before 1.3.0 that showed a poll on the web are
drawn again without it. On the web, conditional blocks are hidden, except blocks for a paid tier, which show to
readers who hold that tier. On a post's page, the title is the main heading and the body's headings are numbered
below it for screen readers; they keep the size you gave them.

## Write a web-only post

<Steps>

1. In <UiPath path="nav.forms > forms.tabs.website > website.viewPosts" />, select <UiPath path="website.newPost" />.
   The composer opens in post mode: the canvas is the site's 680 px column, and email-only blocks aren't offered.
2. Write the post and fill in the <UiPath path="website.composer.tabWeb" /> tab as for a campaign.
3. For a Members or Paid post, drag the <UiPath path="website.composer.blockFreePreview" /> block where the free part
   should end. Without a marker, readers without access see the title, cover and excerpt, then the paywall card. Turn
   on <UiPath path="website.composer.neverFree" /> to leave the post out of the meter.
4. Use <UiPath path="website.composer.previewOnWeb" /> to see the post as a visitor, a free member or a paid member.
5. Select <UiPath path="website.composer.publish" /> or <UiPath path="website.composer.schedule" />. For 10 seconds
   after publishing, Undo takes the post back down.

</Steps>

## Manage posts

<UiPath path="website.viewPosts" /> lists every post with its source (a campaign or web only), visibility, status,
views, signups, upgrades and published comments, with how many wait for review. From a post's menu:

- Selecting <UiPath path="website.posts.unpublish" /> takes the post off the home page, tags, the archive, feeds and the
  sitemap. Its address answers 410 ("This post is no longer available"), or redirects (301) to a post or page you
  choose. The post is kept, and so are the campaign's emails. Change the target later with
  <UiPath path="website.posts.redirect" />.
- Selecting <UiPath path="website.posts.sendAsCampaign" /> turns a web-only post into a campaign draft once: the title becomes
  the subject and the excerpt the preheader. It needs the `campaigns:edit` permission. The draft starts from the
  workspace's default sender when your [scope](/reference/glossary/#scope) allows its domain, otherwise from one of
  your own senders, as a new campaign does.
- Deleting a post deletes its versions and numbers, and its address answers 410. Deleting a published post needs
  `website:publish`.

<UiPath path="website.import.menuImport" /> at the top of <UiPath path="website.viewPosts" /> offers two ways to fill
the site:

- <UiPath path="website.import.menuPast" /> lists sent campaigns that aren't posts yet. Each becomes a post at its send
  date, with the tags of its lists, and a visibility that follows its audience unless you choose Public or Members
  for all of them.
- <UiPath path="website.import.menuFrom" /> imports the posts of a Substack, Ghost or WordPress export. See
  [import posts from Substack, Ghost and WordPress](#import-posts-from-another-platform).

## Import posts from another platform

Upload a Substack `.zip`, a Ghost `.json` or `.zip`, or a WordPress `.xml` export of up to 100 MB, review every post,
and ReputeCast imports them with their original dates and images, without emailing anyone. On your custom domain,
the old site's addresses lead to the imported posts. Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

The whole procedure, with what each platform's export brings, is in
[import posts from Substack, Ghost and WordPress](/migrate/posts/).

## Set up the home page and navigation

In <UiPath path="website.viewHomeNav" />, every change shows in the live preview and goes live when you select
<UiPath path="common.saveChanges" />:

- <UiPath path="website.home.layoutTitle" />: Featured and latest, Latest as a list, or Grid.
- Featured posts: up to 3, in the order you set.
- <UiPath path="website.home.sectionsTitle" />: Subscribe band, Featured posts, Latest posts (6 by default), a row
  per tag, About text and a Paid tiers band from [Earn](/earn/overview/).
- <UiPath path="website.home.navTitle" />: up to 6 header links to a tag, a page, the home page, the archive or an
  outside address. Subscribe and Sign in are always in the header; on phones the links fold into a menu.
- <UiPath path="website.home.footerTitle" />: Archive, RSS, Email preferences, "Sent with ReputeCast" and the privacy
  policy.
- <UiPath path="website.home.shareButtons" />: on by default. See [share buttons on posts](#share-buttons-on-posts).
- <UiPath path="website.home.seoTitle" /> › <UiPath path="website.home.indexSite" />: on by default. The sitemap
  lists the home page, the archive, the tags and every post not hidden from search engines. In search results, the
  archive shows the line under its heading, and a tag page its own description or, without one, the site's. The card
  also lists the addresses of the sitemap, [`llms.txt`](#the-llmstxt-file-for-language-models) and the feeds.
- <UiPath path="website.home.identityTitle" />: site title, tagline, description and a 1200 × 630 social image. The
  favicon comes from the logo in your branding.

## Share buttons on posts

Every published post ends with "Share this post": the post's address with a Copy link button (without JavaScript,
the address stays there to select), then Email, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Bluesky and WhatsApp. They are plain links,
with no third-party script and no tracker. The
network buttons open in a new tab; Email opens the reader's mail app with the post's title as the subject and the
title and address as the message. On phones the buttons form a grid of two columns.

The row shows under posts of every visibility: a paid post's link leads readers to its free preview. Share buttons
are on for every site, existing ones included since 1.6.0. To turn them off, open
<UiPath path="website.viewHomeNav" />, turn off <UiPath path="website.home.shareButtons" /> at the end of
<UiPath path="website.home.navTitle" />, and select <UiPath path="common.saveChanges" />.

<Screenshot
  id="public-post-share"
  alt="Share this post under a published post: its address with Copy link, then Email, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Bluesky and WhatsApp."
/>

The share links are built in one place, which [referral links](/grow/referrals/#share-links) use too. An Email link
escapes the subject and the message as RFC 6068 asks, so a title such as `Q&A?` stays in the subject and can't add a
header in the reader's mail app ([RFC 6068, section 2](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6068#section-2), checked
November 2026).

## The llms.txt file for language models

While <UiPath path="website.home.indexSite" /> is on, the site serves `/llms.txt`: a Markdown file, in the format
[llmstxt.org](https://llmstxt.org/) describes (checked November 2026), that tells language models what the site
holds. It has:

- the site's title as its heading, and its description;
- a Posts section: the posts the sitemap lists, newest first, at most 500, each linked with its excerpt (a gated
  post's public excerpt, never more than a visitor can read);
- an Optional section linking the RSS feed and the sitemap.

Every value from the site is one escaped line of text, so a post's title can't add a line or a link of its own.
While the website is off or search engines may not index it, `/llms.txt` answers 404. Its address is in
<UiPath path="website.viewHomeNav" /> › <UiPath path="website.home.seoTitle" />.

## Members, access and the paywall

In <UiPath path="website.viewAccess" />:

- <UiPath path="website.access.signinOn" />: readers sign in with a link and a 6-digit code sent by email, with no
  password. The link and code work once. Members stay signed in for 90 days by default. Turning sign-in off makes
  members-only and paid posts unreadable on the web.
- <UiPath path="website.access.signupOn" />: choose the lists offered on the site's sign-up and their consent text.
  One email confirms the subscription and signs the reader in. Membership alone never adds anyone to a list.
- <UiPath path="website.access.visibilityTitle" /> for web-only posts and pages, and for posts from campaigns
  ("Follow the audience" by default).
- <UiPath path="website.access.meterTitle" />: both rules are off by default. Visitors who aren't signed in can read a
  number of members-only posts a month, and free members a number of paid posts a month, from 1 to 20. Months are
  calendar months.
- <UiPath path="website.access.paywallTitle" />: the heading, buttons and tiers that replace the rest of a gated post
  after its free preview.

Gating happens on the server: text a reader may not see is never sent to the browser, and feeds and the sitemap carry
only what a visitor sees. Views are counted on the server from a hash with a daily salt, with no cookie. Sign-in and
the meter do use first-party cookies.

Members manage their newsletters, plan, gifts, purchases, referrals, comment settings and sessions on their account
page at `/account`. Its newsletters are your public lists and the lists the member is on, and since 1.5.0 saving the
page can join only those. The plan's billing and cancel links work for an hour. Erasing a contact deletes their
membership, sessions and sign-in links.

## Comments on posts

In <UiPath path="website.viewAccess" />, the <UiPath path="website.access.comments.title" /> card lets signed-in
members, or paid members only, comment under the posts they can read, and chooses which comments wait for review.
Your team publishes, hides, pins and answers them in <UiPath path="website.viewComments" />. Available from the next
release after 1.6.0.

See [let members comment on posts](/forms/post-comments/) for the settings, what readers see, spam protection,
moderation and reply emails.

## Require approval to publish

In <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.approvals" />, turn on <UiPath path="website.elsewhere.policyPost" /> (off by
default). An approval covers the post's content, address, visibility and publish time, and any later change needs a new
one. Approvers are the people with `campaigns:approve`, never the author. With the rule
<UiPath path="approvals.policy.lacksSendTitle" /> on (the default), an author without `website:publish` gets
<UiPath path="website.composer.requestApproval" /> instead of Publish. A campaign post that publishes with the send is
covered by the campaign's own approval. See
[require approval for risky sends](/campaigns/approvals/).

## Turn the website off

At the bottom of <UiPath path="website.viewHomeNav" />, select <UiPath path="website.turnOffButton" /> and type the
site's address to confirm. The home page, tag pages, web-only posts, sign-in, the account page, the paywall and pages
with Members or Paid visibility go offline. Public pages, the shop and posts from campaigns stay online, without
comments, and the archive comes back at `/archive`. Every post, page and setting is kept for when you turn the
website on again.

## Website addresses

<TableScroll label="Website addresses">

| Address                       | What it is                                         |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| `/`                           | The home page                                      |
| `/posts/<slug>`               | A post                                             |
| `/tag/<slug>`, `/tag/<slug>/feed` | A tag's page and its feed                      |
| `/feed`                       | The site feed, with the 30 latest posts            |
| `/sitemap.xml`                | The sitemap                                        |
| `/llms.txt`                   | The site for language models, while it may be indexed |
| `/signin`, `/account`         | Member sign-in and the member's account page       |
| `/archive`                    | Every post, with search                            |

</TableScroll>

On your server, each address starts with `/p/<workspace>`. While the website is on, `/archive.rss` redirects to
`/feed`. See [public URLs and endpoints](/reference/public-urls/).

### Addresses the site keeps for itself

A [landing page](/forms/landing-pages/) can't take `/comments`, `/track`, `/sponsor`, `/team` or `/resume`: the site
uses them for the comments' own pages (such as "Stop these emails"), the
[site tracker's](/integrations/website-tracking/) script, the [sponsor media kit](/earn/sponsors/), and the links
of team subscriptions and paused members. Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

They join the site's other own addresses, such as the ones above and the public pages'. A landing page that asks for
one gets "/… belongs to the site itself. Pick another address."

On your custom domain, an old address of an [imported site](/migrate/posts/#old-addresses-keep-working) leads to its
post, and a path nothing answers gets the site's own 404 page instead of the app.

## Check that it worked

1. Select <UiPath path="website.viewSite" />: the home page opens with your layout and featured posts.
2. Open a post from an old archive link: it redirects to the post.
3. Sign in as a member from a private window with an address on your list, and open a Members post.

## Troubleshooting

- **Turn on is unavailable until the certificate is issued.** The custom domain is verified, but the proxy has no
  valid certificate for it. Ask your operator; see [reverse proxy and TLS](/self-hosting/reverse-proxy/#custom-domains).
- **"Three posts are featured already."** Remove one in <UiPath path="website.viewHomeNav" /> first.
- **A seventh header link is refused.** The header holds 6 links. Remove one first.
- **Paid isn't offered as a visibility.** Connect Stripe and create a paid tier in Earn.
- **A member gets no sign-in email.** The address bounced, complained or is suppressed (the attempt shows on the
  contact's timeline), or nothing can send System mail.
- **No share row under a post.** <UiPath path="website.home.shareButtons" /> is off, or the post isn't published.
- **`/llms.txt` answers 404.** <UiPath path="website.home.indexSite" /> is off, or the website is off.
- **"/… belongs to the site itself."** A landing page asked for one of the site's own addresses. Pick another.
