Every workspace has built-in public pages: a subscribe page, a confirmation page, an email preference center, an
unsubscribe page and an archive of past issues. They work as soon as the workspace exists, use your
[branding](/forms/branding-and-domain/), load no third-party code and work without JavaScript. This page shows what
each one does and how to change it.

## Before you begin

- You need the `forms:edit` permission to change the pages. Owners, Admins and Editors have it; anyone with
  `subscribers:view` can open the editors and previews.
- The pages answer under `/p/<workspace>/` on your ReputeCast server's public URL, or at the root of your custom
  domain once its DNS check passes. See [brand your pages and use your own domain](/forms/branding-and-domain/).

## The built-in pages

<TableScroll label="Built-in public pages">

| Page              | Address                                                  | What readers do there                                         |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Subscribe         | `/subscribe`                                             | Sign up with the form you choose for it                       |
| Confirmation      | `/confirm/<token>`, from the confirmation email          | Confirm a double opt-in signup                                |
| Preference center | `/preferences/<token>`, a personal link in every email   | Change lists, frequency, a pause, language and first name     |
| Ask for a link    | `/preferences`                                           | Get an email with a link to their preference center           |
| Unsubscribe       | `/unsubscribe/<token>`, a personal link in every email   | Take fewer emails or leave, and say why                       |
| View online       | `/view/<token>`                                          | Read an email in the browser                                  |
| Archive           | `/archive`, `/archive.rss`, `/sitemap.xml`               | Read, search and follow past issues                           |

</TableScroll>

Pages reached from a personal link (confirmation, preference center, unsubscribe and view online) are sent with
`Cache-Control: no-store`, `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` and `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`, so they are never
cached, listed by search engines or leaked to other sites. Their links don't expire. Emails always link to your
server's public URL, even when the workspace has a custom domain.

## Edit a built-in page

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.forms > forms.tabs.pages" />. The built-in pages are listed below your own pages.
2. Select <UiPath path="website.edit" /> next to <UiPath path="website.builtinSubscribe" />,
   <UiPath path="website.builtinPreferences" />, <UiPath path="website.builtinUnsubscribe" /> or
   <UiPath path="website.builtinArchive" />. The subscribe and confirmation pages share one editor.
3. Change the options on the left. The preview on the right is the real page, rendered by the server, and has a
   desktop and a mobile view.
4. Select <UiPath path="common.saveChanges" />. Subscribers see the change on their next visit.

</Steps>

When the workspace has two or more languages, a language switcher in the editor lets you write each page's texts
per language. See [send each reader their language](/campaigns/languages/).

## Subscribe and confirmation pages

The subscribe editor sets:

- <UiPath path="forms.pages.formOnPage" />: the built-in form (email, first name and every public list, with double
  opt-in) or one of your [signup forms](/forms/signup-forms/). The page uses that form's fields, lists and consent
  text, and its own heading.
- The heading, description and the line under the button, plus one line under each list. In a list line,
  `{subscribers}` shows the list's size.
- For the confirmation page: what each list brings after the confirmation click, and an option to ask new subscribers
  to add your sender to their contacts.
- <UiPath path="forms.pages.indexSubscribe" />: on by default.

## Preference center

Every email links to each subscriber's own preference center. Subscribers can:

- Join or leave lists. They see the lists you offer plus every list they are already on.
- Choose how often: Every issue, Weekly roundup or Monthly best-of. ReputeCast stores the choice on the contact.
- Take a break for 30 or 90 days. Paused contacts get nothing from these lists until the date passes, then resume by
  themselves.
- Pick the email language, when the workspace has more than one.
- Change their first name.
- Turn texts on or off per list, when the workspace sends SMS and the contact has a phone number, and choose which
  optional [transactional email](/transactional/overview/) categories they want.

In the editor, <UiPath path="forms.pages.listsOffered" /> sets the lists everyone sees, and
<UiPath path="forms.pages.choices" /> turns the frequency, pause and first-name options on or off (all on by default).

Someone who lost their link can open `/preferences`, enter their address and select "Send me a link". The answer is
the same whether or not the address is on your lists, and a contact gets at most one such email every 10 minutes.

## Unsubscribe page

The unsubscribe page asks before it acts. In the editor:

- <UiPath path="forms.pages.fewerInstead" /> › <UiPath path="forms.pages.offerBestOf" /> shows an offer with your
  heading, text and button. A reader who takes it stays on their lists, and their frequency becomes Monthly best-of.
- <UiPath path="forms.pages.askWhy" /> adds an optional question with five answers: "Too many emails", "Content isn't
  relevant to me", "I didn't sign up for this", "I'm switching email addresses" and "Other". The answers show in each
  campaign's report; see [read campaign reports](/campaigns/reports/).
- The page shows how many people left a list, used one-click unsubscribe, chose fewer emails or said why in the last
  30 days.

When a reader confirms, ReputeCast removes the contact from every list and suppresses Broadcasts and Automations to
the address. Account emails, such as password resets, still arrive because they go through the System stream. The
page then offers to resubscribe, which restores the lists and lifts the suppression. See
[the suppression list](/audience/suppressions/).

## One-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058)

Every campaign and automation email carries the two headers that let mail apps unsubscribe a reader with one click,
without opening the page:

```text title="Headers of a bulk email"
List-Unsubscribe: <https://reputecast.acme.io/p/acme-newsletter/unsubscribe/TOKEN>, <mailto:digest@news.acme.io?subject=unsubscribe%20TOKEN>
List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click
```

The mail app sends `POST` with `List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` to the same address as the unsubscribe page. ReputeCast
unsubscribes the contact at once, as described above, and answers `200` with `Unsubscribed.`; the signed token in the
address is the only credential. A `GET` or `HEAD` request never unsubscribes anyone, so link scanners can't do it.
[RFC 8058](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8058) requires the one-click address to be HTTPS (checked October 2026),
and Send Readiness warns when your server's public URL is not `https://`. For mailbox providers' requirements and how
to test it with `curl`, see [one-click unsubscribe](/deliverability/one-click-unsubscribe/).

## Archive

The archive lists your sent email campaigns at `/archive`, with search, a filter per list, an RSS feed at
`/archive.rss` (the 50 newest issues) and a sitemap at `/sitemap.xml`. In the editor:

- Turn the archive on or off with <UiPath path="forms.pages.publishAt" />.
- Under <UiPath path="forms.pages.listsPublished" />, choose the lists whose campaigns appear. With none ticked, every
  public list is published. A campaign appears once it finishes sending; paid-only campaigns never do.
- <UiPath path="forms.pages.indexArchive" /> lets search engines list the archive and its issues. Search results are
  always `noindex`.
- Each issue can be hidden, or given its own title, excerpt, label and list. Hiding removes it from the archive, the
  feed and the sitemap; the email itself doesn't change.

In archived issues, the unsubscribe and preference links point to the "ask for a link" page, so a public copy never
carries someone's personal link. When you [publish a newsletter website](/forms/website/), the archive becomes the
site's list of posts and old issue addresses redirect to their posts.

Each visit to an archived issue counts as a web view in that campaign's [report](/campaigns/reports/): once per visitor
and day, without cookies, counted on the server from a daily salted hash of the address and browser. Crawlers and
prefetches don't count.

## Which language a page shows

- Pages reached from an email (confirmation, preference center and unsubscribe) use the contact's language.
- Other pages use the language picked in the page footer's menu, then the browser's languages, then the workspace's
  default language.
- A language shows only when its page copy is live; otherwise visitors get the fallback language's pages.

The pages' own labels ship in English and Vietnamese. For any other language, you write them in the language
settings, starting from a copy of the English ones.

## Check that it worked

1. Open the page from the editor's address, or send yourself a test campaign and follow its links.
2. On the preference center, change a list and select "Save preferences". The contact's profile shows the change.
3. To test one-click unsubscribe, use an address you own, then check that the contact is no longer on any list.

## Troubleshooting

- **Send Readiness warns about one-click unsubscribe.** Your server's public URL is not `https://`. An operator sets
  it; see [reverse proxy and TLS](/self-hosting/reverse-proxy/) and
  [Gmail, Yahoo and Microsoft bulk sender requirements](/deliverability/bulk-sender-requirements/).
- **A reader says the preference link opens the wrong person.** The link was forwarded. Each link belongs to one
  contact; the page shows "not you?" next to the address, which leads to the subscribe page.
- **The archive is empty.** No campaign to a published list has finished sending yet, or
  <UiPath path="forms.pages.publishAt" /> is off.
- **Search engines list the subscribe page and you don't want that.** Turn off
  <UiPath path="forms.pages.indexSubscribe" />.
