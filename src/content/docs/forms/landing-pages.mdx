ReputeCast's landing page builder makes pages on your theme from blocks: an About page, a lead magnet, a launch with
a countdown, a webinar registration or a sales page for a digital product. A page lives at `/<address>` on your custom
domain, or at `/p/<workspace>/<address>` on your ReputeCast server, and works with or without the
[website](/forms/website/).

## Before you begin

- Creating and changing drafts needs the `website:edit` permission. Publishing, scheduling and unpublishing need
  `website:publish`, and changing a page's visibility, tiers or navigation needs `website:manage`. Owners, Admins and
  Editors have all three.
- Buy buttons need Stripe and a published product. See [connect Stripe](/earn/connect-stripe/) and
  [sell digital products](/earn/digital-products/).
- Signup blocks show one of your [signup forms](/forms/signup-forms/), so publish the form first.

## Create a page

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.forms > forms.tabs.pages" /> and select <UiPath path="pages.newPage" />.
2. Pick a template (see the table below), enter the page name and its address, and select
   <UiPath path="pages.npCreate" />. Addresses use lowercase letters, numbers and single hyphens, up to 80
   characters. Addresses the site uses itself, such as `subscribe`, `archive` or `account`, are refused.
3. The page opens in the builder as a draft that only your team can see.

</Steps>

<TableScroll label="Page templates">

| Template             | What it starts with                                                                                 |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| About                | Who's behind the newsletter: hero, text, author bios and a subscribe band                           |
| Lead magnet          | A free file for confirmed subscribers: benefits, a signup form, testimonials and FAQ                |
| Launch               | A countdown and a waitlist that turns into a Buy button when the countdown ends                     |
| Webinar registration | Event details in each reader's time zone, speakers, agenda and a registration form                  |
| Sales page           | One product: hero with the Buy button, what's inside, author, testimonials and FAQ                  |
| Blank                | An empty page on your theme                                                                          |

</TableScroll>

## Build the page

The builder has the blocks on the left, the page in the middle and three tabs on the right:
<UiPath path="pageBuilder.tabBlock" />, <UiPath path="pageBuilder.tabPage" /> and
<UiPath path="pageBuilder.tabChecks" />.

- **Layout:** Section, 2 columns, 3 columns, Divider and Spacer.
- **Content:** Hero, Text, Image, Button, Video, Features list, Testimonials and FAQ. Videos come from YouTube or
  Vimeo and load only when a reader clicks. FAQ answers open and close without scripts.
- **Conversion:** Signup form, Countdown, Event details, Product, Product grid, Store product and Pricing tiers.
- **From your site:** Recent posts (from a tag) and Author bio.

Styles are limited to your theme's colors, fonts and spacing, so every page stays on brand and keeps its contrast.
Images can be PNG, JPEG or GIF up to 5 MB. The saved page never contains scripts, styles, iframes or raw forms.

The builder saves every 30 seconds and whenever you add, move or delete a block. Saving never changes the live page:
<UiPath path="pageBuilder.publishChanges" /> does.

<Screenshot
  id="page-builder"
  alt="The page builder with the Deliverability Field Guide sales page: blocks on the left, the hero with a Buy for $29 button in the middle, and the Product block's settings on the right."
/>

In the <UiPath path="pageBuilder.tabPage" /> tab:

- <UiPath path="pageBuilder.pageTitle" /> (up to 120 characters) and <UiPath path="pageBuilder.address" />. If you
  change the address of a published page, the old one redirects (301) once the new version is live.
- <UiPath path="pageBuilder.visibility" />: Public, Members (anyone signed in) or Paid (every tier or chosen tiers).
  Members and Paid need the website, and such pages are hidden from search engines.
- Turn on <UiPath path="pageBuilder.hideHeader" /> for focused landing pages; only the logo stays.
- Turn on <UiPath path="pageBuilder.showInNav" /> to add the page to the header with its own label. The header holds
  6 links.
- <UiPath path="pageBuilder.seoTitle" />, <UiPath path="pageBuilder.seoDescription" /> and a social image (otherwise
  links show the page's first image).
- <UiPath path="pageBuilder.publishTime" />: when you publish, or at a time.

## Pass the publish checks

<UiPath path="pageBuilder.tabChecks" /> runs on every change. Blocking checks stop publishing; warnings don't.

<TableScroll label="Publish checks">

| Check          | Result   | Fails when                                                                       |
| -------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Address        | Blocks   | Another page or the site itself uses the address                                 |
| Visibility     | Blocks   | The page is Members or Paid while the website is off                             |
| Product        | Blocks   | A product block has no product, or its product is a draft, archived or deleted   |
| Stripe         | Blocks   | The page has a Buy button and Stripe isn't connected                             |
| Signup form    | Blocks   | A signup block has no form, or its form is paused, a draft or deleted            |
| Store blocks   | Warns    | A store product block has no connected store                                     |
| Dates          | Warns    | An event is in the past, or a countdown ended before the page was ever published |
| Images         | Warns    | An image has no alt text                                                         |
| Heading        | Warns    | The page doesn't open with a heading                                             |
| Links          | Warns    | A link to another site answers with an error or doesn't answer                   |

</TableScroll>

You can keep building without Stripe: drafts and previews work, only publishing a Buy button waits.

## Publish, schedule and unpublish

<Steps>

1. Select <UiPath path="pageBuilder.preview" /> to see the page on desktop, tablet and phone. Links and forms don't
   work in the preview.
2. Select <UiPath path="pageBuilder.publishChanges" />. The dialog shows what changed since the live version and the
   check results.
3. Choose Now and select <UiPath path="pageBuilder.publish" />, or choose a date, time and time zone and select
   <UiPath path="pageBuilder.schedule" />. A scheduled page goes live within a minute of its time; to cancel, select
   <UiPath path="pageBuilder.unschedule" />.
4. For 10 seconds after publishing, Undo takes the page back down.

</Steps>

To take a page down, select <UiPath path="pageBuilder.unpublish" />, give a reason for the history and, optionally, a
path on your site to send readers to. Without one, the address answers 410 ("gone"); with one, it redirects there.
The page, its versions and its numbers stay, and you can publish it again. Unpublishing happens at once; it can't be
scheduled.

Every save, settings change and publish makes a version. <UiPath path="pageBuilder.versions" /> lists them; restoring
one makes it the draft again, as a new version, and the live page doesn't change until you publish.

### Require approval

When <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.approvals" /> has <UiPath path="website.elsewhere.policyPost" /> turned
on, the header shows <UiPath path="pageBuilder.requestApproval" /> instead of publishing. While the rule
<UiPath path="approvals.policy.lacksSendTitle" /> is on (the default), people without `website:publish` see it too.
The approval covers that exact version (content, address, visibility and publish time); once
approved, the page goes live by itself, now or at its scheduled time. See
[require approval for risky sends](/campaigns/approvals/).

## Send webinar reminders

<Steps>

1. Create a custom contact field of type date for the event. See [manage contacts](/audience/contacts/).
2. In the Event details block, set the date, start time and time zone, and choose the field as the
   <UiPath path="pageBuilder.remField" />. Each registration writes the event's date into that field.
3. When the builder asks whether to create the reminder automation, select <UiPath path="pageBuilder.remCreate" />.
   You need the `automations:edit` permission.
4. Open the new automation, named after the page with "· reminders", check its two emails ("Tomorrow: …", sent the
   day before at the start time, and "Starting in an hour: …") and publish it.

</Steps>

The automation is created as a draft, so nothing is sent until you publish it. See
[publish automation changes safely](/automations/publish-and-versions/).

## Check that it worked

1. In the list, the page shows Published, and <UiPath path="pages.viewLive" /> opens it at its address.
2. Submit its signup or registration form with an address you own and confirm it: the contact appears in your
   audience. See [manage contacts](/audience/contacts/).
3. The page's views and its result (signups, registrations or sales) show in the list.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Buy buttons need Stripe."** Connect Stripe in Earn, or remove the Buy button.
- **"The address … is taken."** Pick another address; the builder suggests a free one.
- **"Members and paid pages need the website."** Turn the website on, or set the visibility to Public.
- **The navigation is full.** Remove a header link in
  <UiPath path="nav.forms > forms.tabs.website > website.viewHomeNav" /> first.
- **"Set the event's date, start time and time zone first."** Fill in the Event details block before you create the
  reminder automation.
