Comments on newsletter posts let the members of your [website](/forms/website/) talk under the posts they can read,
while your team decides what shows: you choose who may comment and which comments wait for review, then publish,
hide, pin and answer them in <UiPath path="website.viewComments" />. Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

## Before you begin

- The [website](/forms/website/) is on, and <UiPath path="website.access.signinOn" /> is on in
  <UiPath path="website.viewAccess" />: only signed-in members comment.
- Changing who may comment needs the `website:manage` permission. Publishing, hiding, deleting, pinning, replying
  and blocking need `comments:moderate`. Owners, Admins and Editors have both; Viewers can read every comment but
  decide nothing. Only people can moderate, not API keys or agents.
- For <UiPath path="website.access.comments.paid" />, connect Stripe and create a paid tier. See
  [sell paid subscriptions](/earn/paid-subscriptions/).
- Reply emails go out on the System [stream](/reference/glossary/#stream), so route it to a sending provider.

## Turn on comments

Comments are off for every site until you turn them on.

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.forms > forms.tabs.website > website.viewAccess" /> and go to the
   <UiPath path="website.access.comments.title" /> card.
2. Under <UiPath path="website.access.comments.who" />, choose:
   - <UiPath path="website.access.comments.off" /> (the default): no comments section under posts. Comments already
     written are kept for when you turn them on again.
   - <UiPath path="website.access.comments.members" />: any member who can read the post.
   - <UiPath path="website.access.comments.paid" />: free members read the comments and see an upgrade link.
3. Under <UiPath path="website.access.comments.review" />, choose which comments wait for someone to publish them:
   - <UiPath path="website.access.comments.reviewAll" />: nothing shows until it's published.
   - <UiPath path="website.access.comments.reviewFirst" /> (the default): once one of a member's comments is
     published, the next ones show at once.
   - <UiPath path="website.access.comments.reviewNone" />: comments show at once.

   Whatever you choose, a comment with 2 or more links waits for review.
4. Select <UiPath path="common.saveChanges" />. The change is recorded in the audit log.

</Steps>

## What readers see

Comments come under a published post, after its share row, for readers who may read the whole post: a reader who
meets the paywall sees no comments.

- The heading counts the published comments, such as "Comments (13)". Pinned comments come first, then the oldest.
  The post shows 50 threads at a time; "Show more comments" adds the next 50, up to 20 pages.
- Threads are one level deep: a reply to a reply joins the same thread.
- A member's comment shows their display name, else their name on your list, else "A reader". Their email address
  never shows. Links in a comment are linked, marked `nofollow ugc`.
- Replies from your team show the site's title and logo with a "Team" badge.
- A hidden or deleted comment that has replies keeps its place, with "This comment was removed." in place of its
  text. Without replies, it's gone.
- A member's own comment that waits for review shows only to them, marked "Waiting for review".

Who gets the comment form:

- **Signed-in members who may comment.** Their first comment asks for a display name, up to 60 characters, filled
  in with their name on your list. A comment is 1 to 5,000 characters. They can edit their own comment for 15
  minutes and delete it at any time. An edited comment waits for review again when the site reviews every comment,
  or when it now has 2 or more links.
- **Visitors** see "Sign in to join the conversation", with a Sign in button and, when readers can sign up on the
  site, "Subscribe free". Where only paid members comment, the note says so.
- **Free members, where only paid members comment,** see "Comments are for paid members", a paid tier's price and
  "Upgrade to comment".
- **Blocked members** see "You can't comment on" followed by the site's title. They can still read every post they
  could before, and their newsletters don't change.

Members change their display name and turn reply emails off or on in the Comments section of their account page
(`/account`). It shows while the site takes comments, or once the member has a display name.

### Spam protection

Every comment a reader sends goes through these checks:

1. **Same site:** the browser must say the form came from the site's own page (`Sec-Fetch-Site`, or else
   `Origin`). A post from anywhere else gets "This comment came from another site, so it wasn't posted."
2. **Timing:** the form carries a sealed token of when the page was drawn. A comment sent less than 3 seconds or
   more than a day after that is shown again with the text kept, to send once more.
3. **Honeypot:** a hidden field only bots fill in. A filled one answers as if the comment went, and nothing is kept.
4. **Limits:** 10 comments in 10 minutes and 50 a day per member, and 30 an hour per network (an IPv4 address, or an
   IPv6 /64). They are counted in the database, so every app process shares them.

Comments keep the writer's IP address for 30 days.

## Moderate comments

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.forms > forms.tabs.website > website.viewComments" />. The tabs are
   <UiPath path="website.comments.tabPending" /> (the default), <UiPath path="website.comments.tabPublished" /> and
   <UiPath path="website.comments.tabHidden" />, each with its count. In <UiPath path="website.viewPosts" />, the
   <UiPath path="website.posts.colComments" /> column links here, filtered to that post.
2. Narrow the list with the search (comments, display names and email addresses) or the post filter. Each waiting
   comment says why it waits: <UiPath path="website.comments.heldFirst" />,
   <UiPath path="website.comments.heldAll" /> (the site reviews every comment),
   <UiPath path="website.comments.heldLinks" />, or <UiPath path="website.comments.heldBlocked" /> (the commenter was
   blocked after writing it).
3. Select comments, or every comment on the page, and choose <UiPath path="website.comments.publish" />,
   <UiPath path="website.comments.hide" /> or <UiPath path="website.comments.delete" />. Deleting asks first and
   offers <UiPath path="website.comments.hideInstead" />: hidden comments can be published again.
4. For 10 seconds, <UiPath path="website.comments.undo" /> takes the decision back. After that, a deleted comment
   can't be brought back, and its text is erased.

</Steps>

From a comment's menu:

- <UiPath path="website.comments.pin" /> puts a published comment at the top of its post;
  <UiPath path="website.comments.unpin" /> takes it down. Replies can't be pinned.
- The reply item, "Reply as" followed by your site's title, posts your reply at once under the comment's thread,
  with the site's title and the "Team" badge. <UiPath path="website.viewComments" /> shows who on your team wrote it.
  Only a published comment takes a reply, and the member who started the thread gets a
  [reply email](#reply-emails).
- <UiPath path="website.comments.block" /> stops a member from posting or editing comments on the site; the form
  tells them so. Their comments that wait stay in the queue, marked <UiPath path="website.comments.heldBlocked" />,
  and published ones stay until someone decides. Their membership, newsletters and sign-in don't change.
  <UiPath path="website.comments.unblock" /> is in the menu of any of their comments.
- <UiPath path="website.comments.openContact" /> opens the commenter's contact profile.

<Screenshot

Every moderation action is recorded in the [audit log](/team/audit-log/): publishing, hiding and deleting each
comment, undoing a decision, pinning, replying and blocking. Comments members write aren't audited.

People with `comments:moderate` get a [notification](/team/notifications/) when the queue goes from empty to
waiting, then at most once an hour, such as "3 comments wait for review".

## Reply emails

When someone else's reply is published in a thread a member started, the member gets an email, at most once an
hour: "Sam Rivera replied to your comment on …", with their comment, the reply, how many more replies wait and a
"Read the reply" button. It goes on the System stream, in the member's language and your branding's colors. An
address on the [suppression list](/audience/suppressions/) for any reason but an unsubscribe gets none:
unsubscribing from your newsletters doesn't stop the reply emails a member asked for.

Reply emails are on for every member until they turn them off: with "Stop these emails" in the email, which opens a
page with one button (opening the page changes nothing, so a link checker can't turn them off), or with "Email me
when someone replies to my comments" on their account page.

## Erasure and exports

[Erasing a contact](/audience/contacts/#erase-contacts) removes the text and IP address of every comment they wrote.
Comments with replies keep their place, so other members' replies stay, under "This comment was removed." Reply
emails waiting for them are deleted. A contact's data export lists the comments they wrote, with the post and the
date.

## Check that it worked

1. Open a post on your site in a private window: the comments section shows, with "Sign in to join the
   conversation".
2. Sign in as a member with an address on your list and post a comment. With
   <UiPath path="website.access.comments.reviewFirst" />, it shows as "Waiting for review".
3. In <UiPath path="website.comments.tabPending" />, publish it. It shows under the post.

## Troubleshooting

- **No comments section under a post.** Comments are off in <UiPath path="website.viewAccess" />, the post is set to
  <UiPath path="website.composer.commentsOff" />, the post isn't published, or the reader can't read the whole post.
- **"You've commented a lot in a short time."** The member or their network reached a limit. Wait a few minutes.
- **"That was quick. Wait a moment, then send your comment again."** The form was sent less than 3 seconds after the
  page opened.
- **"This page was open too long to send from."** The page is more than a day old. The comment is in the form: send
  it again.
- **"Publish the comment before you reply to it."** Replies and pins need a published comment.
- **"This contact hasn't signed in to the website, so there's nothing to block."** Only website members can be
  blocked.
- **A member gets no reply emails.** They turned them off, their address is suppressed (other than by an
  unsubscribe), or nothing can send System mail.

<Aside type="note">
  "Stop these emails" opens a page under `/comments/`, an address of the site itself: no landing page can take it.
  See [website addresses](/forms/website/#website-addresses).
</Aside>
