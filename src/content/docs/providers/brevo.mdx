A Brevo API newsletter from ReputeCast goes out through Brevo's transactional email API, one call per message. You
connect an API v3 key; ReputeCast registers a transactional webhook, authenticates your sending domain and pulls
Brevo's blocked contacts into the shared suppression list. Two Brevo behaviors matter before you send: Brevo adds its
own unsubscribe header, and it tracks opens and clicks in a way ReputeCast can't turn off.

## Before you begin

- **A Brevo API key** from Brevo › SMTP & API › API keys. It starts with `xkeysib-`. Brevo also issues SMTP keys for
  its SMTP relay; those don't work here
  ([Brevo: Create and manage your API keys](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/209467485-Create-and-manage-your-API-keys),
  checked October 2026).
- **An account that can send.** While Brevo is validating a new account, or when it has run out of credits, the key
  check fails.
- **Permissions.** Connecting needs `providers:manage`; adding a new sending domain needs `domains:manage`.
- **A public HTTPS address** for the webhook: `<public URL>/hooks/brevo/<token>`.

Three Brevo behaviors matter before you route mail to it:

- **Brevo's unsubscribe header.** Brevo adds its own `List-Unsubscribe` header and has no API to change it, so
  ReputeCast leaves its own header out of mail sent through Brevo. One-click unsubscribes from mailbox providers go to
  Brevo, which reports them through the webhook, and the address joins the shared
  [suppression list](/reference/glossary/#suppression-list). The unsubscribe link in your footer still goes to
  ReputeCast.
- **Brevo's open and click tracking.** Brevo tracks opens and clicks in transactional email by default and has no API
  or per-message switch to stop it; in its settings you can only anonymize that tracking
  ([Brevo: Can I anonymize the tracking of opens and clicks for my emails?](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/11643306229906-Can-I-anonymize-the-tracking-of-opens-and-clicks-for-my-emails),
  checked October 2026). Brevo adds its own open pixel and rewrites links on top of ReputeCast's tracking; its
  numbers never reach ReputeCast's reports. The <UiPath path="providers.events.trackingTitle" /> can't read Brevo's
  setting and [Send Readiness](/reference/glossary/#send-readiness) doesn't warn about it, so review Brevo's
  tracking settings yourself.
- **Pull-only suppressions.** ReputeCast imports Brevo's blocked contacts (hard bounces, spam complaints and
  unsubscribes) every 15 minutes but doesn't push its list to Brevo, whose API blocks contacts only one at a time per
  sender. ReputeCast checks the shared list before it hands any message to Brevo, so suppressed addresses never
  reach it.

## Connect Brevo

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.providers" />, choose <UiPath path="providers.overview.addProvider" /> and pick Brevo in
   the catalog. [Connect a sending provider](/providers/connect/) explains the steps every provider shares.

2. Paste the <UiPath path="providers.fields.apiKey" />. If your plan is a monthly allowance, enter your
   <UiPath path="providers.fields.monthlyLimit" /> under the extra Brevo settings for the quota bar.

3. Choose <UiPath path="connect.connection.connect" />. ReputeCast reads your account with the key and stores it
   encrypted. The card shows your company name, the plan and the emails or credits left.

4. In <UiPath path="connect.connection.webhooksTitle" />, ReputeCast tests its own public URL, then creates (or
   updates) a transactional webhook named "ReputeCast" for delivered, hard and soft bounces, deferred, blocked,
   invalid, spam and unsubscribed events. Brevo doesn't sign webhooks, so ReputeCast gives it a bearer token to send
   with each request. Webhooks for your Brevo marketing campaigns aren't touched.

5. Choose <UiPath path="connect.connection.continueDomain" /> and enter your sending domain. ReputeCast adds it in
   Brevo, asks Brevo to authenticate it, and lists Brevo's records: the `brevo-code` verification TXT record, DKIM,
   SPF when Brevo gives one, and DMARC.

6. In <UiPath path="connect.stepTest" />, send a test and watch the delivered event arrive.

7. In <UiPath path="connect.stepStreams" /> and <UiPath path="connect.stepContacts" />, pick the streams and keep the
   option to pull Brevo's existing blocked contacts ticked.

</Steps>

## Check that it worked

- The Brevo card on <UiPath path="nav.providers > providers.tabProviders" /> shows
  <UiPath path="providers.statusHealthy" /> and what is left on your plan.
- In Brevo, the transactional webhooks list a "ReputeCast" webhook that points at `/hooks/brevo/…`.
- Deliveries and bounces appear under <UiPath path="nav.providers > providers.tabEvents" />.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Brevo rejected this key."** Use an API v3 key (it starts with `xkeysib-`), not an SMTP key.
- **"Brevo didn't answer the check"** with a key you know is right. Brevo refuses while the account is under
  validation or out of credits. Finish Brevo's account validation or top up, then try again.
- **The domain step fails with "Brevo didn't add …".** Brevo refused the domain; its reason follows the message. Fix
  it in Brevo, then run the step again.
- **Unsubscribe counts in ReputeCast look low.** One-click unsubscribes from Gmail or Yahoo go to Brevo first and
  arrive through the webhook. Check that the webhook is still registered in Brevo.
- **The catalog shows a "Sandbox mode" badge.** ReputeCast never sends through a Brevo sandbox: every message it hands
  over is delivered.
- **You disconnected Brevo.** ReputeCast deletes the transactional webhooks that point at its URL. The domain stays
  in Brevo.
