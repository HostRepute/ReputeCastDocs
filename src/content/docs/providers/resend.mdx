A Resend newsletter goes out from ReputeCast through Resend's email and batch endpoints, up to 100 messages per call.
You connect a Full access API key; ReputeCast registers the webhooks, adds your sending domain in the region you
pick, reads the monthly quota and keeps suppressions in sync both ways.

<ProviderMatrix family="email" id="resend" />

## Before you begin

- **A Resend API key with Full access**
  ([Resend: API keys](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/api-keys/introduction), checked October 2026). A Sending
  access key connects, but it can only send: webhooks, domains and suppressions then fail. The card says "Sending
  access key · quota and domains need a Full access key".
- **A verified domain in Resend, or one ReputeCast adds for you.** Until the account has a verified domain, ReputeCast
  shows Resend as <UiPath path="providers.statusSandbox" />: Resend sends test mail to your own address only.
- **Permissions.** Connecting needs `providers:manage`; adding a new sending domain needs `domains:manage`.
- **A public HTTPS address** for webhooks: `<public URL>/hooks/resend/<token>`. Once the key is accepted, ReputeCast
  can poll Resend once a minute while the address isn't reachable yet.

## Connect Resend

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.providers" />, choose <UiPath path="providers.overview.addProvider" /> and pick Resend in
   the catalog. [Connect a sending provider](/providers/connect/) explains the steps every provider shares.

2. Paste the <UiPath path="providers.fields.apiKey" /> and pick the <UiPath path="providers.fields.region" /> where
   new sending domains should be hosted (`us-east-1`, `eu-west-1`, `sa-east-1` or `ap-northeast-1`). Under the extra
   Resend settings you can enter your <UiPath path="providers.fields.monthlyLimit" /> for the quota bar. Leave the
   <UiPath path="providers.fields.webhookSigningSecret" /> empty: ReputeCast reads the secret of the webhook it
   registers.

3. Choose <UiPath path="connect.connection.connect" />. ReputeCast lists your domains with the key and stores it
   encrypted.

4. In <UiPath path="connect.connection.webhooksTitle" />, ReputeCast tests its own public URL, then creates (or
   re-enables) a webhook for `email.delivered`, `email.delivery_delayed`, `email.bounced`, `email.complained`,
   `email.failed` and `email.suppressed`. Opens and clicks aren't subscribed: ReputeCast tracks them itself. If the
   endpoint isn't reachable yet, choose <UiPath path="connect.connection.usePolling" />.

5. Choose <UiPath path="connect.connection.continueDomain" /> and enter your sending domain. ReputeCast adds it in
   Resend (in the region from step 2) and lists the DKIM TXT record at `resend._domainkey`, the MX and SPF records on
   `send.<your domain>`, and SPF and DMARC records for the domain itself. Resend's tracking and receiving records are
   left out.

6. In <UiPath path="connect.stepTest" />, send a test. To try outcomes without touching real mailboxes, send to
   Resend's test addresses: `delivered@resend.dev`, `bounced@resend.dev`, `complained@resend.dev` or
   `suppressed@resend.dev` (labels such as `delivered+test1@resend.dev` work, except for the suppressed address).
   Test emails count against your Resend quota
   ([Resend: Send test emails](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/emails/send-test-emails), checked October 2026).

7. In <UiPath path="connect.stepStreams" /> and <UiPath path="connect.stepContacts" />, pick the streams and keep the
   option to pull Resend's existing suppressions ticked.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  A bounce from `bounced@resend.dev` is a real hard bounce for ReputeCast: the address goes on your shared
  [suppression list](/reference/glossary/#suppression-list) like any other.
</Aside>

## Check that it worked

- The Resend card on <UiPath path="nav.providers > providers.tabProviders" /> shows
  <UiPath path="providers.statusHealthy" /> and the number of verified domains.
- In Resend › Webhooks, a webhook points at `/hooks/resend/…` for the six events.
- Deliveries and bounces appear under <UiPath path="nav.providers > providers.tabEvents" />.

Webhooks are verified with the Standard Webhooks (Svix) signature and the webhook's `whsec_` signing secret, and
requests older than 5 minutes are refused. One message goes to `POST /emails`; two or more go to the batch endpoint,
up to 100 per call, each with an idempotency key so a retry never sends twice. If Resend refuses one message of a
batch, ReputeCast sends the rest one by one so only that message fails.

Resend tracks opens and clicks per domain once the domain has tracking turned on, and it can't be turned off per
message. The <UiPath path="providers.events.trackingTitle" /> on the Events & sync tab reports it and can turn it off
for your domains; until then [Send Readiness](/reference/glossary/#send-readiness) warns about double counting.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Resend rejected this key."** The key was deleted or mistyped. Create a Full access key in Resend and paste it.
- **Webhook registration fails with `restricted_api_key`.** The key has Sending access only. Replace it with a Full
  access key under <UiPath path="providers.overview.settings > providers.overview.credentials" />, then choose
  <UiPath path="connect.connection.registerAgain" />.
- **Resend shows as <UiPath path="providers.statusSandbox" />.** The account has no verified domain. Finish the domain
  records from step 5; the status clears at the next health check (every 5 minutes). Resend has no production-access
  request: verifying a domain is the fix.
- **The test fails with `validation_error`.** The From domain isn't verified in Resend. Use a sender on a verified
  domain.
- **Signatures stop verifying after you recreated the webhook in Resend.** If you once pasted a webhook signing
  secret, ReputeCast keeps using it. Replace it with the new webhook's secret, or reconnect Resend.
- **Transactional messages with Cc, Bcc or attachments fail.** The Resend adapter can't send copies or attachments;
  route those templates through another provider.
- **The catalog badge says "Bulk via Broadcasts API".** ReputeCast doesn't use Resend Broadcasts; it sends every
  message itself through the email and batch endpoints.
- **You changed the public URL or disconnected Resend.** A new public URL adds a second webhook; delete the old one in
  Resend. Disconnecting deletes the webhook at the current URL; domains and pushed suppressions stay.
