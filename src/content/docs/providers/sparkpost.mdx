To send a SparkPost newsletter from ReputeCast, you connect an API key and the region of your SparkPost account (US
or EU). ReputeCast registers a webhook protected by basic auth, sets up DKIM for your sending domain, turns
SparkPost's tracking off on every message and keeps both suppression lists in sync.

<ProviderMatrix family="email" id="sparkpost" />

## Before you begin

- **A SparkPost account** at `app.sparkpost.com` (US) or `app.eu.sparkpost.com` (EU). The adapter speaks SparkPost's
  API; Bird Email accounts use a different API and can't be connected with it.
- **An API key** with the Transmissions, Webhooks, Sending Domains, Suppression Lists, Account, Metrics and Message
  Events permissions, created under Configuration › API Keys in SparkPost
  ([SparkPost: Create API keys](https://support.sparkpost.com/docs/getting-started/create-api-keys), checked October
  2026). EU accounts create keys in the EU app.
- **Permissions.** Connecting needs `providers:manage`; adding a new sending domain needs `domains:manage`.
- **A public HTTPS address** for the webhook: `<public URL>/hooks/sparkpost/<token>`. SparkPost posts a test batch
  to the address before it saves a webhook, so the address must be reachable. Until it is, ReputeCast can poll
  SparkPost's events API every minute.

## Connect SparkPost

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.providers" />, choose <UiPath path="providers.overview.addProvider" /> and pick SparkPost in
   the catalog. [Connect a sending provider](/providers/connect/) explains the steps every provider shares.

2. Paste the <UiPath path="providers.fields.apiKey" /> and pick the <UiPath path="providers.fields.region" />: `us`
   for `api.sparkpost.com`, `eu` for `api.eu.sparkpost.com`. Under the extra SparkPost settings you can name an
   <UiPath path="providers.fields.ipPool" /> of dedicated IPs to send through.

3. Choose <UiPath path="connect.connection.connect" />. ReputeCast reads your account and its usage with the key,
   then stores it encrypted. The card shows the account ID, its status and your plan.

4. In <UiPath path="connect.connection.webhooksTitle" />, ReputeCast tests its own public URL, then creates (or
   updates) a webhook named "ReputeCast" with basic auth (user `reputecast`, a secret ReputeCast generates) for
   deliveries, bounces, out-of-band bounces, delays, spam complaints, policy rejections, generation failures and
   unsubscribes. Open and click events aren't subscribed. If the endpoint isn't reachable yet, choose
   <UiPath path="connect.connection.usePolling" />.

5. Choose <UiPath path="connect.connection.continueDomain" /> and enter your sending domain. ReputeCast adds it as a
   SparkPost sending domain with a 2048-bit DKIM key and lists the DKIM TXT record. For a subdomain such as
   `news.acme.io` it also lists the bounce CNAME (to `sparkpostmail.com`, or `eu.sparkpostmail.com` in the EU); an apex
   domain such as `acme.io` can't hold a CNAME, so it gets DKIM only. ReputeCast suggests DMARC if the domain has none.

6. In <UiPath path="connect.stepTest" />, send a test and watch the delivery event arrive.

7. In <UiPath path="connect.stepStreams" /> and <UiPath path="connect.stepContacts" />, pick the streams and keep the
   option to pull SparkPost's existing suppressions ticked.

</Steps>

## Check that it worked

- The SparkPost card on <UiPath path="nav.providers > providers.tabProviders" /> shows
  <UiPath path="providers.statusHealthy" /> and your monthly or daily sending limit with its usage.
- In SparkPost, the webhook list has a "ReputeCast" webhook that points at `/hooks/sparkpost/…` with basic auth.
- Deliveries and bounces appear under <UiPath path="nav.providers > providers.tabEvents" />.

SparkPost doesn't sign webhooks, so ReputeCast relies on the basic-auth credentials and the unguessable URL.
ReputeCast sends one transmission per message, with an idempotency key so a retried call never sends twice, and
with open and click tracking off. Every message goes out as transactional in SparkPost's terms, because SparkPost
adds its own `List-Unsubscribe` header to other mail and ReputeCast already adds one with one-click unsubscribe. For
the same reason, ReputeCast pushes each suppressed address to both of SparkPost's suppression lists, and pulls
SparkPost's bounce, spam complaint and unsubscribe entries back.

## Troubleshooting

- **"SparkPost rejected this key"** with `401 Unauthorized`. The key is wrong, lacks permissions, or belongs to the
  other region. Check the key's permissions and that the region matches the app you created it in.
- **Webhook registration fails.** SparkPost tests the URL before it saves a webhook, so registration fails while
  SparkPost can't reach ReputeCast. Fix the public URL (see [Reverse proxy and TLS](/self-hosting/reverse-proxy/)),
  then choose <UiPath path="connect.connection.registerAgain" />.
- **The domain step lists only a DKIM record.** You entered an apex domain. Send from a subdomain if you want
  SparkPost's bounce domain to align with yours.
- **Sending slows down with HTTP 420 errors.** SparkPost answers 420 when your daily or monthly sending limit is used
  up. ReputeCast treats it as throttling and retries later; raise the limit in SparkPost or route part of the
  traffic to another provider.
- **The catalog shows a "Sandbox domain" badge.** ReputeCast has no SparkPost sandbox mode: connect a real sending
  domain.
- **Account-level tracking is on.** The tracking check on the Events & sync tab says so even though ReputeCast turns
  tracking off on every message it sends. To change the account defaults through the API, the key needs write access
  to Account.
- **You disconnected SparkPost.** ReputeCast deletes the webhooks that point at its URL. The sending domain and the
  pushed suppressions stay in SparkPost.
