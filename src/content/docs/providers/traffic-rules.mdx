<UiPath path="nav.providers > providers.tabTraffic" /> holds the email traffic rules that
[guarded sending](/reference/glossary/#guarded-sending) applies to every send in the workspace: thresholds by bounce
type, rules for what mailbox providers reply, when a send starts with a [canary](/reference/glossary/#canary), and how
long complaints are watched after a send. Every workspace starts with working defaults; this guide shows how to read and
change them.

## Before you begin

- You need `providers:view` to see the rules and `providers:manage` to change them. Without it, the page shows the rules
  read-only.
- Read [Monitor a send and auto-pause](/campaigns/live-monitor/) for what a tripped rule does to a running send.
- Optional: connect [seed mailboxes](/deliverability/placement-tests/) so the canary can check inbox placement, and
  [HostRepute](/deliverability/hostrepute/) so the complaint watch can read Gmail's spam rate.

## Change the traffic rules

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.providers > providers.tabTraffic" />. Each card shows its current values; the line next to
   <UiPath path="traffic.save" /> says who saved them last, or that they are still the defaults. The side panel explains
   how the rules apply, what the live monitor shows and the <UiPath path="traffic.recentTitle" /> of the last 30 days.

   <Screenshot
     id="traffic-rules"
     alt="Sending providers, Traffic rules tab, saved 2 days ago by Linh Nguyen: the thresholds by bounce type, each with its limit and action (block/policy bounces 0.5%, unknown users 3% and 5%, soft bounces and deferrals 15% and 40% over 10 minutes, complaints 0.10%), and the side panel with how the rules apply, what the live monitor shows and the recent actions."
   />

2. Under <UiPath path="traffic.thresholdsTitle" />, set each limit and what it does when it trips. These are the
   workspace defaults for every send:
   - <UiPath path="traffic.blockTitle" />: rejections for reputation, policy or content, such as Outlook `S3150` or
     Gmail `550 5.7.1`, at one mailbox provider.
   - <UiPath path="traffic.unknownTitle" />: addresses that don't exist (`550 5.1.1`) over the whole send, at two
     levels. Many at once point to an old or bought list.
   - <UiPath path="traffic.softTitle" />: temporary 4xx replies at one mailbox provider over a window of 5, 10, 15 or 30
     minutes, at two levels.
   - <UiPath path="traffic.complaintTitle" />: complaints per mailbox provider, counted only where mailbox providers
     report them (Outlook, Yahoo and some in Other). It is the same value as the complaint limit per mailbox provider in
     [workspace sending defaults](/providers/workspace-defaults/).

   Each action is one of: pause that mailbox provider, slow it to half rate, pause the send, or warn. The first level
   of a two-level threshold must be lower than the second.

3. Under <UiPath path="traffic.repliesTitle" />, check the reply rules. Each rule matches a text in a mailbox
   provider's SMTP replies, plain and not case-sensitive, on every route, and acts as soon as its count is reached,
   before any rate threshold. The card shows what a known reply means and when the rule last matched. Use the switch to
   turn a rule off, or remove it.

   <Screenshot
     id="traffic-rules-replies"
     alt="The Mailbox provider responses card with three rules on: Yahoo replies containing TSS04 pause Yahoo for 2 hours after 10 replies in 10 minutes, Outlook replies containing S3150 pause Outlook on that route and alert on the first reply, and Gmail replies containing 421-4.7.28 slow Gmail to 50% for 30 minutes after 5 replies, each with what the reply means and when it last matched, above the Add rule form."
   />

4. To add a rule, choose <UiPath path="traffic.addRule" /> and fill in <UiPath path="traffic.fieldMailbox" />,
   <UiPath path="traffic.fieldContains" /> (for example `4.7.650`), <UiPath path="traffic.fieldWhen" /> (the first
   reply, or 5, 10 or 25 replies in 10 minutes), <UiPath path="traffic.fieldAction" /> and
   <UiPath path="traffic.fieldDuration" /> (30 minutes to 6 hours, or until someone resumes). The actions are: slow that
   mailbox provider to 50%, pause it on every route, pause it on the route that got the replies and alert, or alert
   only.

5. Under <UiPath path="traffic.canaryTitle" />, choose <UiPath path="traffic.runWhen" />:
   - <UiPath path="traffic.reasonNewDomain" />: the domain's first 30 days, or a dedicated IP listed on Sending
     providers that never sent for this workspace.
   - <UiPath path="traffic.reasonNeverMailed" />: for example contacts imported since the last send.
   - <UiPath path="traffic.reasonOverUsual" />: the usual size is the median of the last 8 sends.

   Then set the <UiPath path="traffic.size" /> (percent of the most engaged recipients), whether to add the seed
   mailboxes, the <UiPath path="traffic.watchTime" /> (10, 20, 30 or 60 minutes) and the
   <UiPath path="traffic.gates" /> the canary must stay under: bounces, block/policy bounces, deferrals, complaints per
   mailbox provider and <UiPath path="traffic.gateSeeds" />. With no reason checked, the card reads Off and no canary
   runs by itself; a person can still choose one in Review & send.

   <Screenshot
     id="traffic-rules-canary"
     alt="The Canary defaults card, On: a canary runs when the sending domain or IP is new, when some recipients never got mail from the domain or when the audience is over twice the usual size; it goes to the 5% most engaged plus the seed mailboxes, is watched for 20 minutes, and must stay under its gates for bounces, block/policy bounces, deferrals, complaints per mailbox provider and seeds in spam."
   />

6. Under <UiPath path="traffic.afterTitle" />, set <UiPath path="traffic.watchFor" /> (24, 48 or 72 hours, or 7
   days), <UiPath path="traffic.alertTitle" />, and <UiPath path="traffic.postmasterTitle" />.

7. Choose <UiPath path="traffic.save" />. New sends use the rules; a campaign's own limits from Review & send still come
   first for that campaign. The save is recorded in the [audit log](/team/audit-log/) with what changed.

</Steps>

## How the rules apply

- **Order.** Reply rules act first, as soon as their count is reached. The thresholds by bounce type come next, then the
  bounce and complaint thresholds. The live monitor lists each one on its thresholds card.
- **Evidence.** Every threshold waits for at least 5 events and 500 messages to that mailbox provider, then trips only
  when there is enough evidence that the rate is really over the limit. Complaints and block/policy bounces don't wait
  that long: from 3, a rate clearly over the limit trips however small the send. Block/policy bounces are also judged on
  the last 10 minutes alone, so a mailbox provider that starts blocking late in a large send trips too.
- **Scope.** An action applies to one mailbox provider unless it says "the send".
- **Duration.** Slowdowns end by themselves after their time. Pauses wait for someone who can resume sends, and the
  page names them. A reply rule acts once at a time; when its action ends, only new replies count.
- **Where you see it.** Every action shows on the [live monitor](/campaigns/live-monitor/), in
  <UiPath path="nav.providers > providers.tabEvents" /> and in <UiPath path="traffic.recentTitle" /> here.

### The default reply rules

Every workspace starts with these rules, and workspaces made before them got them too, unless they already had rules of
their own. Change, turn off or remove them like any other rule.

<LimitsTable area="traffic-rules" />

ReputeCast reads Yahoo's `TSS04` (and the older `TS04`) as deferrals for unexpected volume or complaints, Outlook's
`S3150` as the sending IP being on Microsoft's block list, so every further message would bounce, and Gmail's
`421-4.7.28` as a rate limit on an unusual rate of mail. A rule matches plain text, so `TS04` needs its own rule:
`TSS04` doesn't contain it. See [Yahoo 421 4.7.0 TSS04](/smtp-errors/yahoo-421-4-7-0-tss04/) and
[Gmail 421 4.7.28](/smtp-errors/gmail-421-4-7-28/).

### The canary

A canary runs by itself only on an audience of 1,000 or more, so a few percent of it can show something. The canary
starts at the scheduled time and everyone else waits for it, so a send with a canary finishes about 25 minutes later
with the default 20-minute watch. When the watch ends and every gate with enough evidence passes, the send continues by
itself; if a gate fails, it stays held and the people who can send campaigns are asked. Gates still collecting evidence
don't hold the send; they become its thresholds.

<LimitsTable area="canary" />

### The complaint watch

Complaints keep arriving for days after delivery. During the watch, a mailbox provider that passes its complaint limit
with enough evidence raises an alert, in the app and by email, to the people who can resume sends. Gmail reports one
spam rate per day for the whole domain, not single complaints: with the Postmaster Tools option on, the watch alerts
when that rate passes the complaint limit per mailbox provider. Nothing is paused, because the send is over. Late
complaints add the contact to the suppression list and count in the campaign report.

<Aside type="note">
The Postmaster Tools rate comes from the data [HostRepute](/deliverability/hostrepute/) syncs for your sending domain.
Without it, the option has nothing to read.
</Aside>

## Check that it worked

- <UiPath path="traffic.saved" /> appears, and the line next to <UiPath path="traffic.save" /> names you.
- The next send's thresholds card on the live monitor shows your limits, and <UiPath path="live.model.thresholdReplies" />
  counts your enabled reply rules.
- After a rule acts, it shows its last match on this page and in <UiPath path="traffic.recentTitle" />.

## Troubleshooting

- **"The first level must be lower than the second."** Lower the first level of the unknown-user or deferral threshold,
  or raise the second.
- **"Enter the reply text to match, for example TSS04."** A rule needs at least 2 characters of reply text.
- **The fields are read-only.** Your role can view sending providers but not manage them. Ask someone who can manage
  sending providers.
- **A rule never matches.** It matches the text of the SMTP reply your sending provider reports, anywhere in it. Check
  the exact text in <UiPath path="nav.providers > providers.tabEvents" />, and look for a shorter part, such as the code
  alone.
- **No canary ran.** The audience was under 1,000, no reason applied, or every reason is off. Choose
  <UiPath path="review.canaryOn" /> in Review & send to run one anyway.
- **The canary has no seed gate.** No seed mailboxes are connected, or the option is off. Add them under
  [placement tests](/deliverability/placement-tests/).
- **A running send ignores a change.** Sends use the rules they started with. Change a running send's thresholds with
  <UiPath path="live.editThresholdsLive" /> on the live monitor.
