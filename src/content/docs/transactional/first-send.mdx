In about 10 minutes you send transactional email through the API: the first-send guide walks you from a sender to
a delivered message, and its last step proves the whole loop with a real call from your app.

## Before you begin

- A [sending provider](/providers/connect/) connected to the workspace. The guide asks you to connect one if there
  is none.
- A sending domain you can add DNS records to. A subdomain of its own, such as `notify.example.com`, keeps product
  mail's reputation apart from your newsletters. See [add sending domains and senders](/providers/senders-and-domains/).
- Permissions: `transactional:publish` to publish the template and `apikeys:manage` to create the key. The Owner and
  Admin roles have both.
- `curl` or your app's HTTP client to make the first call.

## Send it

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.transactional" /> and choose <UiPath path="transactional.overview.startGuide" />. The
   guide has five steps: Sender, Route, Template, Key and Watch it arrive. You can leave with **Exit guide** at any
   time.

2. **Sender.** Enter a **From name** and a **From address** on the domain that sends transactional mail. If the
   domain is new, the guide lists the DNS records it needs (DKIM, a return path, SPF and a DMARC policy, depending
   on the sending provider) and checks them live. Most DNS hosts take a few minutes. When HostRepute is connected,
   you can add the domain to its blocklist monitoring here.

   <Screenshot
     id="transactional-guide"
     alt="Step 1 of 5 of the first-send guide: From name Acme Cloud and From address no-reply@notify.acme.io, with the five steps listed on the left."
   />

   Choose **Continue to route**.

3. **Route.** Pick the **Primary** sending provider and, if you have a second one, a **Fallback**. The fallback is
   used at once when the primary is down. Leave **First in line on shared providers** on so that transactional
   messages go before a campaign that uses the same sending provider. Choose **Continue to template**.

4. **Template.** Start from a starter: Password reset, Sign-in link, Email verification, Welcome, Receipt or
   invoice, Trial ending, Team invitation or Usage alert. Each comes with its variables declared and a footer for
   account email. Choose **Publish v1 and continue**, or **Edit before publishing** to change it first in the
   [template editor](/transactional/templates/). If your templates are at Postmark, SendGrid, Mailgun or Notifuse,
   you can [import them instead](/migrate/transactional-templates/).

5. **Key.** Name the key (for example `Acme Cloud · production`) and choose **Create send-only key**. A
   [send-only key](/reference/glossary/#send-only-key) holds `transactional:send` and nothing else. Copy it now: it
   is shown once. Then choose **I copied it · watch it arrive**.

6. **Watch it arrive.** The guide shows **Your first call**, filled with the template ID and its sample data. Run
   it from your laptop or your app with the key you copied. The request looks like this; take the exact body from
   the guide, because it carries your template's variables:

   ```sh title="Terminal"
   curl -X POST "https://reputecast.example.com/api/v1/workspaces/acme-newsletter/transactional/messages" \
     -H "Authorization: Bearer $REPUTECAST_KEY" \
     -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
     -H "Idempotency-Key: first-send-1" \
     -d @first-message.json
   ```

   The **Delivery timeline** follows the message: **Call received**, **Rendered**, **Handed to** the sending
   provider and **Delivered**, with the round trip from your call to the delivery event. If you'd rather not run
   the call, **Send it from here instead** sends it to your own address with the sample data.

7. Choose **Finish** once the first message is delivered.

</Steps>

## What you built

- A verified sender for transactional mail and a Transactional route with automatic failover.
- A published template your app calls by its ID. The ID is fixed after this first publish.
- A send-only key for your app, listed under <UiPath path="nav.transactional > transactional.common.tabSetup" />,
  where you can rotate or revoke it.
- A first message in the [message log](/transactional/message-log/), with its full timeline.

If the call was rejected, the timeline says why. A template with a draft but no published version can't send:
publish it, then call again with the same idempotency key.
