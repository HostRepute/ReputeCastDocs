Use the transactional email logs to answer "did my customer get the email?": find the message, read its delivery
timeline, and resend it or export the log when you need to.

## Before you begin

- `transactional:view` to read the log and timelines.
- `transactional:content` to open a message's rendered content and to export the log, because both hold personal
  data. Every content view and export is recorded in the [audit log](/team/audit-log/).
- `transactional:send` to resend a message.

## Find a message

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.transactional > transactional.common.tabMessages" />. The log shows the last 24 hours and
   updates live; choose **Pause** to stop new rows from moving the list while you read.

2. Search by recipient, message ID, idempotency key, tag or metadata, or filter by template, sending provider, key
   and time range (last hour, 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or custom dates).

3. Narrow by status with the chips above the table: Delivered, Deferred, Bounced, Complained, Rejected, Failed or
   Test, each with its count.

   <Screenshot
     id="transactional-messages"
     alt="The transactional message log: 1,992 messages in the last 24 hours with status chips, and rows showing recipient, template and subject, sending provider, status and engagement."
   />

4. Read the status detail in each row. For example, **Rejected · suppressed** means the address is on the
   [suppression list](/audience/suppressions/); **Rejected · opted out** means the recipient turned off an Optional
   category; **Test · not delivered** means a test-mode key sent to an address it isn't allowed to deliver to. Mail
   that arrived through the [SMTP relay](/transactional/smtp-relay/) shows **Raw (SMTP)** instead of a template.

5. Open a row to see the message's **Timeline**: the call (key, IP address and answer), the render, the hand-off to
   the sending provider (primary or fallback), and every delivery and engagement event with its original code.

</Steps>

## View the content

On a message's page, choose <UiPath path="transactional.log.showContent" />. Content is kept for the workspace's
retention period (30 days by default); after that only metadata and events remain, for 90 days. Sensitive variables
were never stored, so they show a placeholder. Change retention in [transactional settings](/transactional/settings/).

## Resend a message

Choose <UiPath path="transactional.log.resend" /> on the message's page. ReputeCast renders the live version of the
template again with the stored data. Resend isn't available when:

- a sensitive variable wasn't stored (your app sends it again instead),
- the message had attachments, which aren't kept,
- the mail arrived by SMTP,
- the template has no live version anymore,
- the message was never sent.

## Export the log

Choose <UiPath path="transactional.log.exportCsv" /> to download the filtered log, up to 50,000 rows. Exports need
`transactional:content` because they hold recipient addresses.

## Get alerted

ReputeCast checks every minute over the last hour and alerts you when:

- a template's bounce rate passes its limit (2% by default, after at least 20 sent),
- failed messages reach 10 or more,
- the median delivery time is slower than 30 seconds (after at least 20 delivered).

Alerts appear in the app and follow your [notification preferences](/team/notifications/). Transactional mail is
never paused by an alert. Set the limits under <UiPath path="nav.transactional > transactional.common.tabSettings > transactional.settings.alertsTitle" />.

<Aside type="tip">
Your app can get the same events without polling: subscribe a [webhook endpoint](/developers/webhooks/) to
`message.*` events, or read the log with the API (see
[send transactional email with the API](/developers/transactional-api/)).
</Aside>

## Check that it worked

The message you looked for shows **Delivered** with the receiving host in its timeline, or a status and reason you
can act on.

## Troubleshooting

- **A message is missing** → widen the time range; metadata and events are kept 90 days. Check the key filter if
  several apps send.
- **Rejected · suppressed** → the address bounced, complained or was suppressed earlier. Fix the address, or remove
  the entry from the suppression list if it was a mistake.
- **Bounced · soft** → the mailbox provider deferred or refused it for now (for example, mailbox full). After 3 soft
  bounces within 7 days the address is suppressed.
- **Show content says it isn't visible** → you need `transactional:content`; the page names who can help.
