Use the transactional email logs to answer "did my customer get the email?": find the message, read its delivery
timeline, cancel or move it before its send time, and resend it or export the log when you need to.

## Before you begin

- `transactional:view` to read the log and timelines.
- `transactional:content` to open a message's rendered content and to export the log, because both hold personal
  data. Every content view and export is recorded in the [audit log](/team/audit-log/).
- `transactional:send` to resend a message, and to cancel or move a scheduled one.
- `subscribers:edit` to take a rejected recipient off the [suppression list](/audience/suppressions/).

## Find a message

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.transactional > transactional.common.tabMessages" />. The log shows the last 24 hours and
   puts new messages on top every 10 seconds; the counts refresh every minute. Choose **Pause** to stop new rows from
   moving the list while you read.

2. Search by recipient, message ID, idempotency key, tag or metadata, or filter by template, sending provider, key
   and time range (last hour, 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or custom dates).

3. Narrow by status with the chips above the table: Delivered, Deferred, Bounced, Complained, Rejected, Failed or
   Test, each with its count. Queued, Scheduled and Cancelled get a chip while the range has such messages.

   <Screenshot
     id="transactional-messages"
     alt="The transactional message log for the last 24 hours: status chips with their counts, the Live indicator and Pause, and rows showing recipient, template and subject, sending provider, status and engagement."
   />

4. Read the status detail in each row. For example, **Rejected · suppressed** means the address is on the
   [suppression list](/audience/suppressions/); **Rejected · opted out** means the recipient turned off an Optional
   category; **Test · not delivered** means a test-mode key sent to an address it isn't allowed to deliver to. Mail
   that arrived through the [SMTP relay](/transactional/smtp-relay/) shows **Raw (SMTP)** instead of a template.

5. Open a row to see the message's **Timeline**: the call (key, IP address and answer), the render, the hand-off to
   the sending provider (primary or fallback), and every delivery and engagement event with its original code.

</Steps>

## Cancel or move a scheduled message

A message your app sent with `send_at` shows **Scheduled** until its send time. Its page says when it goes out, in
your time zone.

<Steps>

1. Open the message. To list every waiting message, choose the Scheduled chip.

2. To send it at another time, choose <UiPath path="transactional.schedule.reschedule" />, pick the day and time (up
   to 72 hours ahead) and confirm.

3. To stop it, choose <UiPath path="transactional.schedule.cancelSend" /> and confirm with
   <UiPath path="transactional.schedule.cancelBadge" />.

4. If your app sent the message in a batch with an `Idempotency-Key`, both dialogs ask
   <UiPath path="transactional.schedule.batchLegend" />: <UiPath path="transactional.schedule.batchOne" />, or every
   message of its batch that still waits. Messages of the batch already on their way or done stay as they are; the
   confirmation counts them.

</Steps>

A cancelled message stays in the log as **Cancelled**, with who cancelled it in its timeline. Its content is removed
at once; metadata and events stay for 90 days. [Webhook endpoints](/developers/webhooks/) that take
`message.cancelled` are told. Every cancel and move is recorded in the [audit log](/team/audit-log/). Your app can do
the same through the API: see
[cancel or move a scheduled message](/developers/transactional-api/#cancel-or-move-a-scheduled-message).

## Remove a recipient from the suppression list

A message rejected because its address is suppressed explains why on its page, with two actions:

- <UiPath path="transactional.schedule.removeSuppression" /> asks for a reason, kept in the audit log, and takes the
  address off the suppression list. If it was added for a bounce or a complaint, transactional email goes through
  again, but marketing email waits until the contact confirms their subscription again.
- <UiPath path="transactional.schedule.openSuppression" /> opens the entry in
  <UiPath path="nav.audience > audience.tabSuppressions" />.

The rejected message itself was never sent: send it again from your app.

## View the content

On a message's page, choose <UiPath path="transactional.log.showContent" />. Content is kept for the workspace's
retention period (30 days by default); after that only metadata and events remain, for 90 days. A cancelled message's
content is removed when it is cancelled. Sensitive variables were never stored, so they show a placeholder. Change
retention in [transactional settings](/transactional/settings/).

## Resend a message

Choose <UiPath path="transactional.log.resend" /> on the message's page. ReputeCast renders the live version of the
template again with the stored data. Resend isn't available when:

- a sensitive variable wasn't stored (your app sends it again instead),
- the message had attachments, which aren't kept,
- the mail arrived by SMTP,
- the template has no live version anymore,
- the message was never sent.

## Export the log

Choose <UiPath path="transactional.log.exportCsv" /> to download the filtered log, up to 50,000 rows. Exports need
`transactional:content` because they hold recipient addresses. A cell that starts with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, a tab or
a carriage return gets a single quote in front, so a spreadsheet never runs it as a formula (since 1.5.0).

## Get alerted

ReputeCast checks every minute over the last hour and alerts you when:

- a template's bounce rate passes its limit (2% by default, after at least 20 sent),
- failed messages reach 10 or more,
- the median delivery time is slower than 30 seconds (after at least 20 delivered).

Alerts appear in the app and follow your [notification preferences](/team/notifications/). Transactional mail is
never paused by an alert. Set the limits under <UiPath path="nav.transactional > transactional.common.tabSettings > transactional.settings.alertsTitle" />.

<Aside type="tip">
Your app can get the same events without polling: subscribe a [webhook endpoint](/developers/webhooks/) to
`message.*` events, or read the log with the API (see
[send transactional email with the API](/developers/transactional-api/)).
</Aside>

## Check that it worked

The message you looked for shows **Delivered** with the receiving host in its timeline, or a status and reason you
can act on.

## Troubleshooting

- **A message is missing** → widen the time range; metadata and events are kept 90 days. Check the key filter if
  several apps send.
- **Rejected · suppressed** → the address bounced, complained or was suppressed earlier. Fix the address, or
  [remove it from the suppression list](#remove-a-recipient-from-the-suppression-list) if it was a mistake.
- **The message page offers no way to cancel or move it** → the message is already on its way or done, or you don't
  hold `transactional:send`.
- **Bounced · soft** → the mailbox provider deferred or refused it for now (for example, mailbox full). After 3 soft
  bounces within 7 days the address is suppressed.
- **Show content says it isn't visible** → you need `transactional:content`; the page names who can help.
