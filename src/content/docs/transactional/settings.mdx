Adjust the transactional email settings of a workspace: which categories recipients can turn off, how long rendered
content is kept, when delivery alerts fire, and the defaults new templates start with.

## Before you begin

- `transactional:publish` to change settings and categories. With `transactional:view` only, the page is
  read-only and says your role can't change these settings.
- Know [how categories and the Transactional stream work](/transactional/overview/).

## Manage categories

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="nav.transactional > transactional.common.tabSettings" />. The
   <UiPath path="transactional.settings.categoriesLabel" /> table lists each category with its kind, what recipients
   see, its templates and how many recipients opted out.

2. Choose <UiPath path="transactional.settings.addCategory" />, or **Edit** on a row. Give it a **Name**, a
   **Kind** and **What recipients see**, the description shown under the category in the preference center.
   - **Essential**: always sent. Use it for sign-in, password, billing and account emails.
   - **Optional**: recipients can turn it off in the preference center or with the unsubscribe link.

3. Choose **Save category**.

</Steps>

A category's kind can't change while templates use it, and a category with templates can't be deleted: move its
templates first. The **What recipients see** panel previews the preference center, which lists each Optional
category with its description (see [subscribe, preference and unsubscribe pages](/forms/public-pages/)).

## Set retention

Under <UiPath path="transactional.settings.retentionTitle" />:

- **Keep rendered content for**: don't keep content, or keep it 7, 30 (default), 60 or 90 days. Message metadata
  and events stay 90 days either way, and sensitive variables are never stored.
- **Link recipients to contacts**: a message to an address that matches a contact shows in that contact's
  [activity timeline](/audience/contacts/). A recipient who isn't a contact is never added as one.

## Set delivery alerts

Under <UiPath path="transactional.settings.alertsTitle" />, set the three limits. ReputeCast checks them every minute
over the last hour:

| Alert                       | Default    | Range          |
| --------------------------- | ---------- | -------------- |
| Bounce rate per template    | 2%         | 0.1–100%       |
| Failed messages             | 10         | 1–100,000      |
| Median delivery slower than | 30 seconds | 1–3,600 s      |

Alerts reach people through their notification preferences; they never pause transactional mail. Read alerts in the
[message log](/transactional/message-log/).

## Set defaults for new templates

Under <UiPath path="transactional.settings.defaultsTitle" />, pick the **Default sender** (a verified Transactional
sender) and **Default reply-to**, and whether new templates **Track opens** and **Track clicks**. Both are off by
default: opens in account emails tell you little and Apple Mail inflates them, and rewritten links in a password
reset look suspicious to recipients and filters.

Choose **Save settings**. Each change is recorded in the [audit log](/team/audit-log/).

## Check that it worked

- The toast says **Transactional settings saved**.
- An Optional category appears in the workspace's preference center.

## Troubleshooting

- **The fields are read-only and say your role can't change these settings** → changing them needs
  `transactional:publish`; ask an Owner or Admin.
- **The kind can't be changed** → templates use the category. Move them to another category first.
- **No default sender to choose** → there is no verified Transactional sender yet. Add one in
  [sending domains and senders](/providers/senders-and-domains/).
