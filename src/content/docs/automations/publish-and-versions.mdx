Publish email automation changes without surprising the contacts already in the flow: read what changed since the
live version, decide where those contacts continue, and let the pre-publish checks stop a broken version before it
goes live.

## Before you begin

- You need `automations:publish`, a [sensitive permission](/reference/glossary/#sensitive-permission) that the
  Owner, Admin and Editor roles hold. Restoring an old version as a draft needs only `automations:edit`.
- A flow with a <UiPath path="automations.graph.typeSms" />, <UiPath path="automations.graph.typePush" /> or
  <UiPath path="automations.graph.typeWhatsapp" /> step also needs `sms:send`, `push:send` or `whatsapp:send`.
  Without it, publishing goes to [approval](/reference/glossary/#approval) by a member who holds it.
- When your access is limited to some lists or sender domains (your [scope](/reference/glossary/#scope)), you publish
  and resume only a flow that stays within it, since 1.5.0. The same holds for API keys and agents. A live flow sends
  with nobody behind it, so ReputeCast checks when it goes live or goes on again:
  - Lists: every trigger of the flow must be <UiPath path="automations.graph.kindJoinedList" /> on one of your lists.
    A segment, a tag, a form, a date or an event lets in contacts of any list.
  - Sender domains: the flow's emails go out from the workspace's default sender, so its domain must be one you may
    send from.

  Neither check goes to approval: ask a member whose access covers the flow to publish or resume it.
- Two workspace settings change what publishing does. A member with `workspace:manage` sets them:
  - In <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.approvals" />, the rule
    <UiPath path="approvals.policy.automationTitle" /> sends every publish for approval. While it is off,
    publishing is only logged. See [require approval for risky sends](/campaigns/approvals/).
  - In <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.general > accessibility.policyTitle" />, the policy is
    <UiPath path="accessibility.warn" /> or <UiPath path="accessibility.block" />. With
    <UiPath path="accessibility.block" />, serious accessibility issues in the flow's emails stop publishing. See
    [check email accessibility](/campaigns/accessibility/).

## Publish a new version

<Steps>

1. Open the flow from <UiPath path="nav.automations" />. The header shows the live version and its date, such as
   Live · v3 since 3 Nov, and how many changes the draft has that the live version doesn't.

2. Select <UiPath path="automations.flow.publish" /> in the header; the button carries the draft's version, such as
   v4. ReputeCast saves the draft first, then opens the publish dialog.

3. Read the diff. The dialog counts the changes since the live version was published, with the date and who
   published it, and lists them under the version diff, such as v3 → v4. Each row is
   <UiPath path="automations.flow.kindAdded" />, <UiPath path="automations.flow.kindChanged" /> or
   <UiPath path="automations.flow.kindRemoved" />, with what it means for contacts. In the demo workspace, the
   Welcome series draft reads:
   - Added: an A/B split after step 2, 50 / 50, with two new email steps that pass the content checks.
   - Changed: the delay before step 2 goes from 1 day to 2 days; contacts already waiting keep their timer unless you
     move them.
   - Removed: a webhook that had returned 404 since 31 Oct, with its failed calls still in the webhook log.

   The last row, <UiPath path="automations.flow.kindUnchanged" />, lists what stays the same. Moving steps on the
   canvas is never a change. A changed trigger says that new contacts enter on the new trigger from the moment the
   version is live, and how many contacts already in the flow are not affected. When only
   <UiPath path="automations.complete.exitOnReply" /> changed, the trigger row says so and that it applies to the
   contacts on the new version once it is live; contacts who stay on an earlier version keep that version's setting. A
   flow's first publish has no diff: the dialog says every step is validated before it goes live.

4. Decide what happens to the contacts in the flow. Under <UiPath path="automations.flow.migrationLabel" /> the
   dialog says how many contacts are in the flow right now and offers two choices:
   - **Let them finish on the live version** (the default, marked recommended). New contacts start on the new
     version; contacts already in the flow finish on the old one, so nobody gets a step twice or skips one. The old
     version retires when its last contact leaves, and the dialog estimates when, such as “about 5 days”.
   - **Move them to the new version at their current step.** Each contact continues from the same step in the new
     version. A contact on a removed step goes on to the next step that still exists; a contact in a changed delay
     gets the new delay, counted from when they reached it. The dialog spells out the effect, such as “612 contacts
     waiting before step 2 would get the 2-day delay”. ReputeCast moves the contacts of each step together, so even
     tens of thousands of waiting contacts move in seconds.

5. Read the <UiPath path="automations.flow.checks" />. Green passes, amber warns, red blocks:

   | Check                                                                                            | Blocks publishing                                                                       |
   | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
   | The Automations route: healthy, on its fallback, or no healthy provider (emails wait until one is) | No                                                                                      |
   | Every email step passes the [Send Readiness](/reference/glossary/#send-readiness) content checks   | Yes, when any email fails                                                               |
   | No step references a deleted list, tag, form, email or provider                                  | Yes                                                                                     |
   | Every branch and A/B split path is connected                                                     | Yes                                                                                     |
   | Any other problem from <UiPath path="automations.flow.beforePublish" />                          | Yes                                                                                     |
   | Text steps: quiet hours, and whether a sender can text each country                              | No                                                                                      |
   | Language versions that are outdated                                                              | No                                                                                      |
   | Serious accessibility issues, one badge per email step, such as “Step 3 B: 1 serious issue, button contrast” | Only with the <UiPath path="accessibility.block" /> policy; the live version keeps sending |
   | A countdown in the emails that ends within 30 days, after which the flow keeps sending it                     | No                                                                                      |
   | A discount code pool the emails use that is under its low mark or archived, or a pool name no pool has        | No                                                                                      |

   The countdown and discount code badges come from the emails' [countdowns](/campaigns/countdown-timers/) and
   [discount codes](/campaigns/discount-codes/). Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

   While a check blocks, the publish button is disabled and says why. It also says why when your access doesn't cover
   the flow (see [before you begin](#before-you-begin)).

6. Publish. Select the publish button with the new version number. The version goes live, a message confirms it,
   and you return to the list. <UiPath path="automations.flow.saveDraftOnly" /> keeps the draft without publishing,
   and <UiPath path="automations.flow.backToCanvas" /> closes the dialog.

   When <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.senders > senders.defaultsTitle > senders.utmLinks" /> is on, the
   version adds the workspace's UTM tags to its emails' links as the setting stands now, since 1.6.0; a later change
   to the setting reaches the flow at its next publish (see
   [codes, countdowns and UTM tags in emails](/automations/triggers-and-steps/#codes-countdowns-and-utm-tags-in-emails)).

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  Publishing goes to approval instead of live when the workspace rule
  <UiPath path="approvals.policy.automationTitle" /> is on, when an agency client's sign-off covers automations, or
  when a channel step needs a send permission you lack. The message then says the version is waiting for approval
  and names the approvers: members with `campaigns:approve` other than you, who also hold the missing send
  permission when that is the reason. Once approved, ReputeCast publishes that version with the choice you made in
  step 4. If the draft changed after the approval, it is not published: you get a notification, and publishing
  again asks for a new approval.
</Aside>

## Restore an earlier version

<Steps>

1. In the flow's header, select <UiPath path="automations.flow.versionHistory" />. Each published version shows its
   date, who published it and what it changed. The live version shows how many contacts are in it; an older one
   is retiring while contacts still finish on it, or retired with the date it emptied.

2. To see what the draft changes, select <UiPath path="automations.flow.compare" />: it shows
   <UiPath path="automations.flow.changesSinceLive" />.

3. Next to the version you want back, select <UiPath path="automations.flow.restoreAsDraft" />. When the draft has
   unpublished changes, ReputeCast asks whether to replace them: select <UiPath path="automations.flow.replace" />
   or <UiPath path="automations.flow.keep" />.

4. The old version is now the draft, under a new version number. Check it on the canvas and publish it like any
   other change.

</Steps>

## Pause and resume a flow

<Steps>

1. In a live flow's header, select <UiPath path="automations.flow.pause" />. The dialog says how many contacts are
   in the flow.

2. Optionally enter a <UiPath path="automations.flow.reason" /> of up to 200 characters, such as “beta is full”;
   the automations list shows it on the flow's row. Select <UiPath path="automations.flow.pauseAutomation" />, or
   <UiPath path="automations.flow.keepRunning" /> to cancel.

   While the flow is paused, no new contacts enter. Contacts in it stay where they are, delays keep counting, and
   nothing is sent.

3. To restart it, select <UiPath path="automations.flow.resume" />. Resuming checks your access as publishing does.
   New contacts enter again and waiting steps continue. When some contacts' waits ended during the pause, a dialog
   says how many and asks what happens to them:
   - <UiPath path="automations.complete.sendNow" /> (the default): they move on at once, within warm-up caps and quiet
     hours.
   - <UiPath path="automations.complete.skipOverdue" />: the email they missed is skipped, and they continue at the step
     after it.
   - Spread them over 1 to 72 hours (6 to start with): about the same number move on each hour, so the backlog
     doesn't land at once.

   Select <UiPath path="automations.flow.resume" /> in the dialog to restart the flow with that choice.

</Steps>

## Check that it worked

- The header shows the new version as live with today's date, and the list's row says it was published today.
- In <UiPath path="automations.flow.versionHistory" />, the new version shows as live and, if you let contacts
  finish, the old version shows as retiring with the number of contacts still in it.
- A paused flow shows <UiPath path="automations.statusPaused" /> on its row in the list, with who paused it and the
  reason.
- Every publish, approval request, pause and resume, with the choice for overdue contacts, is in the workspace's
  audit log.

## Troubleshooting

- **The publish button is disabled and says there is nothing to publish.** The draft is the same as the live
  version, or it only moves steps around on the canvas. Change a step's settings or connections to publish.
- **Publishing fails with “The draft is now v5. Review the changes again before publishing.”** Someone saved the
  draft after you opened the dialog. Close it and open it again to review the new diff.
- **A red check says emails fail content checks.** Open the email step, fix the email in the composer (the check
  names the first problem, such as a missing unsubscribe link) and publish again.
- **The publish button is disabled and says your access is limited to some lists.** The flow can start in a way that
  lets in contacts of other lists. Use a <UiPath path="automations.graph.kindJoinedList" /> trigger on one of your
  lists, or ask a member with access to all lists to publish or resume it.
- **The publish button is disabled and says your access is limited to some sender domains.** The flow's emails go out
  from the workspace's default sender, whose domain you may not send from. Ask a member who can send from that
  domain to publish or resume it.
- **A badge warns about a discount code pool.** Publishing still works. Add codes to a pool that runs low, and point
  an email at another pool when its pool is archived or doesn't exist. An email whose pool has no code for a contact
  waits up to a day, then is skipped; a pool name no pool has leaves the code out.
- **Publishing is blocked by the accessibility policy.** The blocking message names each issue and step. Fix them in
  the emails, or ask a member with `workspace:manage` to set the policy to <UiPath path="accessibility.warn" />. The
  live version keeps sending in the meantime.
- **Publishing fails although your workspace requires approval.** Approval needs a member other than you with
  `campaigns:approve` (and the missing send permission, when that is the reason). Give it to another member's role
  in <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.roles" />; authors can't approve their own request.
- **You got an “Approved, but not published” notification.** The draft changed after the approval, or a check now
  fails. Fix the flow and publish again to request a new approval.
- **Pause and resume are disabled.** They need `automations:publish`. Only a live flow can be paused, and only a
  paused one resumed.
