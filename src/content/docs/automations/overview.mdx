Build an email automation workflow on the flow canvas: a [trigger](/reference/glossary/#trigger) lets contacts in, steps
send, wait and decide, and a [goal](/reference/glossary/#goal) ends the flow when a contact does what you wanted.

Every [automation](/reference/glossary/#automation) has one of three statuses. It is a
<UiPath path="automations.statusDraft" /> until its first publish, <UiPath path="automations.statusLive" /> while
contacts enter it, and <UiPath path="automations.statusPaused" /> when someone stops it. You always edit a draft:
contacts only get a version after you publish it.

## Before you begin

- You need `automations:edit` to change drafts, and `automations:publish` to publish, pause and resume flows.
  `automations:publish` is a [sensitive permission](/reference/glossary/#sensitive-permission). The Owner, Admin and
  Editor roles hold both; a Viewer can open a flow and look around but cannot change it. See
  [permissions and roles](/reference/permissions/).
- Flows send on the Automations [route](/reference/glossary/#route). Connect at least one sending provider and check
  which providers the route uses in [route mail across providers](/providers/routing/).
- Have the list, form, tag or event that should start the flow ready.
  [Triggers and steps](/automations/triggers-and-steps/) lists what each trigger needs.

## Build a flow

<Steps>

1. Go to <UiPath path="nav.automations" />. The list shows every flow with its status, trigger, number of steps and
   its numbers for the last 30 days; the cards above it count contacts in flows now and the last 7 days. The tabs at
   the top filter the list by status.

   <Screenshot
     id="automations-list"
     alt="The Automations list of the Acme Newsletter workspace: 10 flows, of which 8 are live, 1 paused and 1 a draft, with each flow's status, trigger, steps and 30-day numbers."
   />

2. Select <UiPath path="automations.newAutomation" /> and pick a starter template or
   <UiPath path="automations.blankCanvas" />. ReputeCast creates a draft and opens it on the canvas. The
   <UiPath path="automations.starterTemplates" /> cards below the list do the same in one click;
   [set up a welcome series](/automations/welcome-series/) walks through one of them.

3. Add the trigger. In the left panel, under <UiPath path="automations.panels.triggers" />, drag a trigger onto the
   canvas, or press it to place it. A flow has one trigger. Picking another kind under
   <UiPath path="automations.panels.startsWhen" /> replaces it, and the trigger's other settings start fresh.

4. Add steps. Drag an item from <UiPath path="smsStep.messages" /> or <UiPath path="automations.panels.steps" /> onto
   the canvas, or onto a connection to insert it between two steps. Pressing an item adds it after the selected step.
   To connect two steps yourself, drag from the handle at the bottom of one step to the top of the next.
   - Only a <UiPath path="automations.graph.typeBranch" /> (paths <UiPath path="automations.graph.pathYes" /> and
     <UiPath path="automations.graph.pathNo" />) and an <UiPath path="automations.graph.typeSplit" /> (one path per
     variant) lead to more than one step.
   - End a path with <UiPath path="automations.panels.goalOrExit" />, or leave it open: a contact who reaches the
     last step of a path finishes the flow there.
   - The <UiPath path="automations.graph.typeSms" />, <UiPath path="automations.graph.typePush" /> and
     <UiPath path="automations.graph.typeWhatsapp" /> steps appear in the palette once the workspace has set up that
     channel.

5. Set up each step. Select it to open its settings in the right panel's <UiPath path="automations.flow.tabStep" />
   tab; a step with a problem lists it at the top of the panel. <UiPath path="automations.panels.duplicate" /> and
   <UiPath path="automations.panels.delete" /> are at the bottom of the panel. <kbd>Delete</kbd> or
   <kbd>Backspace</kbd> also deletes the selected step, and the steps before it then lead to the step after it.

   <Screenshot
     id="automation-flow"
     alt="The flow editor of the live Welcome series: the palette of triggers, messages and steps on the left, the flow with live 30-day numbers on each step in the middle, and the selected Send email step's settings on the right."
   />

6. Move around the canvas. Scroll to pan; hold <kbd>⌘</kbd> (macOS) or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> and turn the wheel to zoom
   between 30% and 200%. The canvas controls are <UiPath path="automations.flow.zoomIn" />,
   <UiPath path="automations.flow.zoomOut" />, <UiPath path="automations.flow.fitView" /> (the whole flow on screen)
   and <UiPath path="automations.flow.lock" />, which stops steps from moving while you read a large flow. The
   <UiPath path="automations.flow.minimap" /> shows where you are in the flow. The toolbar above the canvas switches
   <UiPath path="automations.flow.showStats" />, <UiPath path="automations.flow.snapToGrid" /> and
   <UiPath path="automations.flow.animate" /> on and off.

7. Let the draft save itself. ReputeCast saves the draft 0.7 seconds after you stop changing it; there is no save
   button. Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> on Windows and Linux) to save at once.
   Leaving the editor saves what is left. The first change to a published flow starts a new draft version: the live
   version keeps running, and the header counts the unpublished changes.

8. Check the flow. Select an empty part of the canvas: the right panel shows the flow's name and description, and
   under <UiPath path="automations.flow.beforePublish" /> anything that still blocks publishing, such as a step that
   is not connected to the trigger or a branch path that leads nowhere.

9. Publish. Select <UiPath path="automations.flow.publish" /> in the header (the button carries the draft's version,
   such as v1), read the dialog and publish. [Publish automation changes safely](/automations/publish-and-versions/)
   explains each part of the dialog.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  Steps keep their ids across versions, so moving steps around on the canvas is never a change: publishing needs a
  change to a step's settings or connections.
</Aside>

## Check that it worked

- The header shows the flow as live with its version and the publish date, and the list shows
  <UiPath path="automations.statusLive" /> on its row.
- Contacts who match the trigger enter within seconds. The toolbar shows the flow's live stats for the last 30 days
  with the number of contacts who entered, and with <UiPath path="automations.flow.showStats" /> on, each step shows
  its own numbers.
- The left panel's <UiPath path="automations.flow.tabStats" /> tab lists every step with its key number. Select a
  step and open the right panel's <UiPath path="automations.flow.tabStats" /> tab for its 30-day numbers: how many
  contacts entered it, are there now, moved on and finished there. Email steps add sent, skipped by a rule,
  delivered, [human opens](/reference/glossary/#human-opens), all opens, clicks and unsubscribes; webhook steps add
  failed calls and a delivery log.
- Stats start at the first publish and count across versions: a step keeps its numbers when you publish a new
  version, and a step that is new in the draft has none until it is published.

## Troubleshooting

- **The canvas is read-only and says your role can't edit automations.** Ask an Owner or Admin for a role with
  `automations:edit`. Publishing, pausing and resuming also need `automations:publish`.
- **The editor says <UiPath path="automations.flow.conflictTitle" />.** Someone, maybe you in another tab, saved a newer version
  of the draft, so your last change was not saved. Select <UiPath path="automations.flow.loadLatest" /> and make the
  change again.
- **The header shows <UiPath path="automations.flow.draftNotSaved" />.** The last save failed, for example because
  the connection dropped. Select <UiPath path="common.retry" /> next to it, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> or
  <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>. ReputeCast asks before you leave the page with unsaved changes.
- **A step says it isn't connected to the trigger.** No contact could reach it. Connect it to a step above it, or
  delete it.
- **<UiPath path="automations.graph.typeSms" /> or another channel step is missing from the palette.** The channel is
  not set up in this workspace yet: see [send SMS campaigns](/channels/sms/),
  [send web push notifications](/channels/web-push/) or [send WhatsApp messages](/channels/whatsapp/).
- **The list shows <UiPath path="automations.pausedAutomatically" /> above a flow.** No sending provider on the
  Automations route is healthy. The flow's emails are queued and go out as soon as a provider is healthy again; delays
  keep counting. Fix the provider the alert names, or add another provider to the route.
- **A flow's emails go out later than its delays say.** A sending provider on the Automations route is
  [warming up](/providers/warm-up/): automation emails count toward its daily cap. Once the cap is used up, or while
  its warm-up plan is paused, they stay queued, never dropped, until the next day's cap or the plan resumes. With
  <UiPath path="providers.warmup.overflowLabelNone" /> on, they go through the Automations route's fallback instead
  when that provider is warm and can take them.
- **The list shows <UiPath path="automations.stepFailing" />.** A webhook step keeps failing. Contacts continue past
  it, so the flow is not blocked; fix the URL or remove the step, then publish.
