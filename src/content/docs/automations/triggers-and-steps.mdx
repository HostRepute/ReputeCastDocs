This page lists the email automation triggers, steps and conditions of the flow editor, with every option and its
default. [Build automations](/automations/overview/) explains the editor itself.

## Triggers

A flow has one [trigger](/reference/glossary/#trigger) step; you pick its kind under
<UiPath path="automations.panels.startsWhen" />, and it can hold [more than one trigger](#more-than-one-trigger).
The palette groups the kinds: <UiPath path="automations.panels.triggers" /> for the general ones, and
<UiPath path="earn.automation.group" /> once the workspace has a live payment connection, since 1.6.0. The
<UiPath path="commerce.flows.storeGroup" /> group holds a connected store's orders, checkouts and
[store flows](#store-flows), and <UiPath path="leads.automation.group" /> the lead ad trigger once the workspace has a
lead form. Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

Every trigger except <UiPath path="scoring.trigger.kind" />, <UiPath path="grow.trigger.kind" /> and the
[store flows](#store-flows) also has <UiPath path="automations.panels.reentry" />:
<UiPath path="automations.panels.oncePerContact" /> [default] lets a contact through the flow one time,
<UiPath path="automations.panels.everyTime" /> lets them enter again, and
<UiPath path="automations.complete.reentryDays" /> lets them enter again once that many days have passed since they
last entered. Exceptions:

- A contact enters <UiPath path="scoring.trigger.kind" /> flows at most once in the set number of days, and
  enters <UiPath path="grow.trigger.kind" /> flows once per milestone.
- A store flow takes the same contact again only after 7 days.
- The [paid-member triggers](#paid-members) start with <UiPath path="automations.panels.everyTime" />, so a member who
  comes back goes through again. <UiPath path="earn.automation.kindStarted" /> and
  <UiPath path="earn.automation.kindEnded" /> still come once per subscription.
- A <UiPath path="automations.graph.kindDateField" /> flow with <UiPath path="automations.panels.everyYear" /> lets a
  contact through once a year: its re-entry choice reads <UiPath path="automations.panels.oncePerYear" />.
- <UiPath path="automations.graph.kindOrderPlaced" /> and <UiPath path="automations.graph.kindFirstOrder" /> flows let
  a contact in once unless re-entry is <UiPath path="automations.panels.everyTime" />.
- An <UiPath path="automations.graph.kindAbandonedCheckout" /> flow starts for every new abandoned checkout; the
  7-day rule above limits it.
- With <UiPath path="pollTrigger.onChange" />, a changed answer starts the flow again whatever the re-entry setting.

### Lead ads

<UiPath path="leads.automation.kind" />, in the palette's <UiPath path="leads.automation.group" /> group once the
workspace has a lead form, starts a flow when someone sends one of your Facebook or Instagram lead forms and
ReputeCast adds or updates them as a contact. Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

- <UiPath path="leads.automation.leadForm" /> [<UiPath path="leads.automation.anyForm" />]: one form, named with its
  Page, such as "Spring webinar signup · Acme Inc".
- With double opt-in on the form's mapping, the flow starts once the lead confirms; with single opt-in, at once.
- Meta's test leads, leads of a form that isn't mapped or is paused, suppressed addresses and single opt-in leads who
  didn't tick the form's consent box add nobody, so they start nothing.
- It can also be a flow's second or third trigger.
- When the form's Page is disconnected, the form is gone, and publishing asks you to pick another form or any lead
  form.

[Collect leads from Meta Lead Ads](/integrations/meta-lead-ads/) connects the forms and maps their questions.

### More than one trigger

<UiPath path="automations.complete.addTrigger" /> in the trigger's settings adds a second and a third trigger, and
any of them starts the flow. The added ones are <UiPath path="automations.graph.kindJoinedList" />,
<UiPath path="automations.graph.kindFormSubmitted" />, <UiPath path="automations.graph.kindTagAdded" />,
<UiPath path="automations.graph.kindApiEvent" />, <UiPath path="automations.graph.kindSegmentEntered" />,
<UiPath path="automations.graph.kindSegmentLeft" />, <UiPath path="automations.graph.kindLinkClicked" /> or
<UiPath path="leads.automation.kind" /> triggers; the other kinds keep their own entry rules and can only be the
first. Re-entry and leaving the flow with the list follow the first trigger. The trigger step on the canvas names them
all, such as "Weekly Digest, or vip".

Wherever ReputeCast looks for the flows that start on a list, a form, a product or a store, such as a list's welcome
series prompt or <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.integrations" />, it reads every trigger of a flow, not only the
first.

### Stop when the contact replies

Every trigger has <UiPath path="automations.complete.exitOnReply" />. With it ticked, a contact's run ends when they
reply to any email of the flow: they get none of its remaining steps, count as left early at their step, and the run
ends with the reason `replied`. Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

- Only a person's reply counts: auto-replies and spam don't.
- Replies reach ReputeCast only while the workspace receives mail and keeps replies in the ReputeCast inbox (see
  [set up receiving mail](/providers/receiving-mail/)). Until then, the box can't be ticked and says
  <UiPath path="automations.complete.exitOnReplyWhy" />.
- The setting belongs to the version. When it is the only change to the trigger, the publish diff shows it as one
  trigger line, and it applies to the contacts on the new version once it is live; contacts who stay on an earlier
  version keep that version's setting.

The <UiPath path="automations.panels.condReplied" /> [condition](#conditions) works on the same replies.

### Held-out contacts

When the workspace's [holdout group](/audience/holdout-group/) covers automations, a contact in it passes every
<UiPath path="automations.graph.typeEmail" />, <UiPath path="automations.graph.typeSms" />,
<UiPath path="automations.graph.typePush" /> and <UiPath path="automations.graph.typeWhatsapp" /> step without a
message and goes on to the next step, so delays, branches and goals treat them like everyone else. Available from the
next release after 1.6.0.

- Each step counts them, apart from the contacts it skipped: "67 held out" on the canvas, and a held-out row in the
  step's <UiPath path="automations.flow.tabStats" /> tab.
- A branch that waits for an open, a click or a reply to an email they didn't get sends them down
  <UiPath path="automations.graph.pathNo" /> once its window ends.

### Codes, countdowns and UTM tags in emails

A flow's emails can show a discount code and a countdown, filled in for each contact when the email goes out.
Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

- **Discount codes.** An email that shows a code from a [discount code pool](/campaigns/discount-codes/) gets one for
  each contact when it is sent. When the pool has no code left for the contact, the step waits and asks again every 15
  minutes for up to a day, then skips the email, and the contact goes on.
- **Countdowns.** A [countdown](/campaigns/countdown-timers/) shows the time left when the email is opened, and zeros
  or its message once it has ended. Publishing warns when one ends within 30 days, because the flow keeps sending it.
- **UTM tags.** With
  <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.senders > senders.defaultsTitle > senders.utmLinks" /> on, each version adds
  `utm_source` (the workspace's slug), `utm_medium=email` and `utm_campaign` (the flow's name as a slug, such as
  `welcome-series`) to the tracked links
  that don't have them yet, as the setting stands when you publish it (since 1.6.0). Turning the setting on or off
  reaches a flow at its next publish; a published version's links never change.

## Webhook step calls

Each call sends this JSON body with `Content-Type: application/json`, `User-Agent: ReputeCast-Webhooks/1.0` and
`X-ReputeCast-Event: automation.step`. The step's name is its <UiPath path="automations.panels.name" />, or the
URL's host when it has none.

```json title="Webhook step request body"
{
  "type": "automation.step",
  "id": "01a16a2e-5c7e-7b51-9f0e-3d2c1b0a9e8f",
  "occurredAt": "2026-10-22T09:14:03Z",
  "automation": { "id": "01a1637b-8042-7b3b-ae2f-a664344e716b", "name": "Onboarding", "version": 7 },
  "step": { "id": "n7", "name": "Sync to CRM" },
  "contact": {
    "id": "01a1637a-91c4-7d2e-8b0f-5a6e3c2d1b0a",
    "email": "ana@example.com",
    "firstName": "Ana",
    "lastName": "Silva",
    "tags": ["trial"],
    "fields": { "plan": "team" }
  }
}
```

| Rule        | Value                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Signature   | With a signing secret set, `X-ReputeCast-Signature: t=<Unix seconds>,v1=<signature>`, where the signature is the lowercase hex HMAC-SHA256 of `<t>.<raw body>`, keyed with the secret. Endpoints from <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.webhooks" /> sign differently: see [receive webhooks](/developers/webhooks/). |
| Success     | Any 2xx response.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Timeouts    | 5 seconds to connect, 10 seconds for the response headers, 15 seconds in total. At most 3 redirects.                                                                                                                                                  |
| Retries     | Network errors, 429 and 5xx responses are retried: 3 attempts in total, about 30 seconds and 2 minutes apart. Other responses are not retried.                                                                                                         |
| Failure     | The contact moves on anyway. The call is in the step's <UiPath path="automations.panels.deliveryLog" />, and the list shows <UiPath path="automations.stepFailing" /> until a call works again.                                                       |
| Addresses   | Public addresses only. ReputeCast checks the address it actually connects to, after DNS and on every redirect, so private, loopback, link-local and internal addresses are refused.                                                                   |
| Log         | Calls are kept for 90 days; the delivery log shows the latest 30.                                                                                                                                                                                       |

## Flow rules

These problems block publishing; the editor lists them under <UiPath path="automations.flow.beforePublish" />.

- A flow has one trigger step, and nothing leads into it. Added triggers are of the kinds in
  [more than one trigger](#more-than-one-trigger), and leaving the flow with the list needs a
  <UiPath path="automations.graph.kindJoinedList" /> trigger.
- Every step is connected to the trigger, and no connection leads back to an earlier step.
- Each path out of a branch or A/B split is connected exactly once. Other steps lead to at most one step; an exit
  leads to none.
- Every step is set up: a picked email with a subject, a list, store, tier or lead form that still exists, a valid
  tag, event name or URL, a delay above zero, a wait on every open, click or reply branch, split weights that add up to
  100%, and a store flow's hours, views, collections, days and price drop within their ranges.
- A flow has at most 200 steps and 400 connections.

A webinar landing page can create a draft reminder flow for you: a Date field trigger on the field its registration
form writes, an email the day before at the session's time, a 23-hour delay and a second email an hour before. See
[build landing and sales pages](/forms/landing-pages/).
