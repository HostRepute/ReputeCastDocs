In about 20 minutes you build a welcome email series from the Welcome series starter template and publish it, so
every new subscriber gets a first email 10 minutes after joining and a follow-up that depends on whether they opened
it.

## Before you begin

- You need `automations:edit` and `automations:publish`; the Owner, Admin and Editor roles hold both.
- Have the list that new subscribers join, ideally with a signup form in front of it (see
  [add a signup form to your site](/forms/signup-forms/)).
- Check that the Automations route has a healthy sending provider: the publish dialog warns when it hasn't, and
  emails then wait until one is healthy (see [route mail across providers](/providers/routing/)).
- Keep an email address you can read at hand to test the series.

## Build the series

<Steps>

1. **Create the draft.** Go to <UiPath path="nav.automations" />. Under
   <UiPath path="automations.starterTemplates" />, select **Welcome series**. In a workspace without flows, the same
   card is in the middle of the page; <UiPath path="automations.newAutomation" /> offers it too. ReputeCast creates
   a draft called Welcome series with 7 steps and its three emails, starts it on the workspace's first list, and
   opens it on the canvas.

2. **Pick the list.** Select the trigger at the top of the flow. Under
   <UiPath path="automations.panels.startsWhen" />, <UiPath path="automations.graph.kindJoinedList" /> is selected;
   pick the list your subscribers join.
   - Leave <UiPath path="automations.panels.confirmedOnly" /> on: on a list with
     [double opt-in](/reference/glossary/#double-opt-in), contacts get the welcome once they confirm.
   - Leave <UiPath path="automations.panels.includeImports" /> off, so importing old subscribers doesn't welcome them.
   - Leave <UiPath path="automations.panels.reentry" /> at <UiPath path="automations.panels.oncePerContact" />, so a
     contact who leaves and rejoins the list isn't welcomed twice.

3. **Read the flow from the top.** The starter has these steps:

   | Step                                                           | What it does                                                                                               |
   | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
   | <UiPath path="automations.graph.kindJoinedList" />             | Lets a contact in when they join the list.                                                                 |
   | <UiPath path="automations.graph.typeDelay" />, 10 minutes      | Waits 10 minutes.                                                                                          |
   | <UiPath path="automations.graph.typeEmail" />, step 1          | Sends “You're in. Here's what to expect”.                                                                  |
   | <UiPath path="automations.graph.typeBranch" />                 | Asks “Opened step 1 within 2 days?”, counting human opens only.                                            |
   | <UiPath path="automations.graph.typeEmail" />, step 2          | On the <UiPath path="automations.graph.pathYes" /> path: sends “Three things to try first”.               |
   | <UiPath path="automations.graph.typeEmail" />, step 3          | On the <UiPath path="automations.graph.pathNo" /> path: sends “Did you get our welcome?”.                  |
   | <UiPath path="automations.graph.typeGoal" />, First click      | Ends the flow when the contact clicks any link in the flow's emails, wherever they are in it.             |

   Each contact gets step 1 and then either step 2 or step 3, so two of the three emails within about two days.
   The [goal](/reference/glossary/#goal) also ends the flow early for a contact who clicks a link in step 1 before
   the branch has decided.

   <Screenshot
     id="automation-flow"
     alt="The demo workspace's Welcome series, built from the starter and extended: the Joined list trigger, the 10-minute delay, step 1, the branch on opening step 1 within 2 days with its Yes and No paths, and step 1's settings in the right panel."
   />

   The demo workspace's version in the screenshot has more steps, such as a delay on each path and an A/B split;
   your draft starts with the seven steps above.

4. **Edit the emails.** Select step 1. In the right panel, under <UiPath path="automations.panels.email" />, select
   <UiPath path="automations.panels.editInComposer" />. Change the <UiPath path="automations.flow.subject" />,
   <UiPath path="automations.flow.preheader" /> and <UiPath path="automations.flow.html" />. Liquid tags such as
   `{{ first_name | default: "there" }}` are filled in when the email goes out. Select
   <UiPath path="automations.flow.saveAsNew" />: the step switches to the new version of the email and sends it once
   you publish. <UiPath path="automations.panels.preview" /> shows the result.

   Then check the step's own <UiPath path="automations.panels.subject" /> field in the right panel. That is the
   subject that goes out, so update it if you changed the subject in the composer. Repeat for steps 2 and 3.

   <Aside type="caution">
     Keep the `{{ unsubscribe_url }}` link in each email. An email without an unsubscribe link fails the content
     checks, and the publish dialog then blocks publishing.
   </Aside>

5. **Set the delays.** Select the <UiPath path="automations.graph.typeDelay" /> step and set
   <UiPath path="automations.panels.wait" /> to how long new subscribers wait for step 1. Select the
   <UiPath path="automations.graph.typeBranch" /> and set <UiPath path="automations.panels.waitUpTo" />: how long the
   flow waits for an open of step 1 before it sends step 3 instead. To space out step 2, drag
   <UiPath path="automations.graph.typeDelay" /> from the palette onto the connection between the branch's
   <UiPath path="automations.graph.pathYes" /> path and step 2.

6. **Check the sending rules.** Select each email step and look at the rules below the subject:
   - The <UiPath path="automations.panels.sendingRoute" /> is the Automations [route](/reference/glossary/#route).
   - The <UiPath path="automations.panels.sendWindow" /> is on: an email due outside 07:00 to 21:00 in the subscriber's
     time zone waits until morning.
   - The <UiPath path="automations.panels.skipIf" /> rule skips the email when the contact got any email in the last 4 hours,
     for example a broadcast; the contact then moves on without it. Clear it on step 1 if every new subscriber must
     get the welcome.

7. **Publish the series.** Select an empty part of the canvas: under
   <UiPath path="automations.flow.beforePublish" />, the right panel should say every step is connected and set up.
   Select <UiPath path="automations.flow.publish" /> (the button reads v1 for a first publish). The dialog says this
   is the first version; read the <UiPath path="automations.flow.checks" /> and publish. Contacts who join the list
   from now on enter the series. [Publish automation changes safely](/automations/publish-and-versions/) explains the
   dialog in full.

8. **Test it.** Join the list with your own address through your signup form and confirm. Step 1 arrives about 10
   minutes later, or the next morning outside the send window.

9. **Watch the stats.** Go back to <UiPath path="nav.automations" />. The Welcome series row shows
   <UiPath path="automations.statusLive" />, how many contacts entered in the last 30 days, how many are in the flow,
   the share that completed it, the [human opens](/reference/glossary/#human-opens) rate and the goal rate.

   <Screenshot
     id="automations-list"
     alt="The Automations list with the demo's Welcome series live on v3: 1,284 contacts entered in 30 days, 612 in the flow, 62% completed, 55.3% human opens and the Clicked docs goal at 24%."
   />

   Open the flow to see each step's numbers on the canvas. The branch shows how many contacts took each path; select
   any step and open <UiPath path="automations.flow.tabStats" /> in the right panel for its numbers over the last 30
   days.

</Steps>

## What you built

A live welcome series on your list: new subscribers who confirm get a welcome email 10 minutes after joining, a
second email chosen by whether they opened the first, and the flow ends for them at their first click. Each email is
versioned, so you can improve it and publish again without disturbing contacts who are already in the series.
