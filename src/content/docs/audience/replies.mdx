<UiPath path="nav.audience > replies.tab" /> is the newsletter replies inbox: what readers write back to your campaigns
and automation emails lands there, sorted into views, linked to the [contact](/reference/glossary/#contact) and the
email it answers. Read a reply, answer it from your own mailbox, mark it done, and unsubscribe the people who ask.
Available from the next release after 1.6.0.

## Before you begin

- ReputeCast must receive mail. Set up <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.senders > senders.receiving.title" />
  through a sending provider that receives mail or a mailbox you own, then set
  <UiPath path="senders.defaultsTitle > senders.replyHandling" /> to
  <UiPath path="senders.receiving.keepInInbox" />. Campaigns, automation emails and placement copies then carry a
  Reply-To address of the receiving domain, one per message. See
  [receive replies and unsubscribe emails](/providers/receiving-mail/).
- Replies have permissions of their own. Both are for people only: API keys and MCP agents can't hold them.

<TableScroll label="Permissions for replies">

| Action | Permission | Built-in roles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See <UiPath path="replies.tab" /> and read replies | `replies:view` | Owner, Admin, Editor |
| Mark replies done or open | `replies:manage` | Owner, Admin, Editor |
| Unsubscribe the contact a reply came from | `replies:manage` and `subscribers:edit` | Owner, Admin, Editor |

</TableScroll>

A Viewer doesn't see the tab. Opened from a link, the page says "You can't view replies." and names the people who can
give the permission. See [roles and permissions](/team/roles-and-permissions/).

<Steps>

1. Go to <UiPath path="nav.audience > replies.tab" />. The <UiPath path="replies.viewOpen" /> view lists people's
   replies you haven't handled yet, newest first, 50 at a time; <UiPath path="replies.loadMore" /> loads the next 50.
   Each row shows the sender, the subject, the start of what they wrote (quoted text left out) and the campaign or
   automation it answers.

2. Select a reply. It opens beside the list with the subject, the sender, when it arrived and a link to the email it
   answers, "Reply to October Product Update · sent Tue 10 Nov", which opens the campaign's report or the automation.

3. To answer, select <UiPath path="replies.replyByEmail" />. Your mail app opens a new email to the sender with the

4. Select <UiPath path="replies.markDone" />, or press <kbd>E</kbd>. The reply moves to
   <UiPath path="replies.viewDone" />, and a message offers <UiPath path="replies.undo" />. On a done reply the button
   reads <UiPath path="replies.markOpen" />.

</Steps>

### The views

<TableScroll label="Views of the replies inbox">

| View | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| <UiPath path="replies.viewOpen" /> | People's replies to your emails that nobody marked done yet. |
| <UiPath path="replies.viewDone" /> | People's replies someone marked done. |
| <UiPath path="replies.viewAuto" /> | Out-of-office answers, vacation responders, list and bulk mailers, and bounces that came back as replies. They never stop an automation. |
| <UiPath path="replies.viewSpam" /> | Replies and other mail the receiving server, or the message's own spam headers, marked as spam. |
| <UiPath path="replies.viewOther" /> | Mail to the receiving domain that doesn't answer an email ReputeCast sent: an address it never handed out, or a reply to an email that no longer exists, such as one from a deleted campaign. The detail shows the address it was sent **To**. |
| <UiPath path="replies.viewUnsubscribes" /> | Emails to the `mailto:` address in an email's List-Unsubscribe header. ReputeCast already unsubscribed the contact, so these are done when they arrive. |

</TableScroll>

The `mailto:` address belongs to ReputeCast only while it receives mail and
<UiPath path="senders.listUnsubscribeMailto" /> is on; see
[one-click unsubscribe](/deliverability/one-click-unsubscribe/).

### Search a view

Type in <UiPath path="replies.search" />. The search looks at the sender's name and address, the subject and the text,
in the view you are on, up to 200 characters. The counts on the chips don't change while you search. With no match,
the page offers <UiPath path="replies.clearSearch" />.

### Handle many replies at once

Tick replies in the list, or the box above it for every reply loaded in the view. A bar shows how many are selected,
with <UiPath path="replies.markDone" />, <UiPath path="replies.markOpen" /> and
<UiPath path="replies.clearSelection" />. The replies leave the view at once; the message that confirms the change,
"6 replies marked done", has <UiPath path="replies.undo" />. Changing the view or the search clears the selection.

## Unsubscribe a contact who asks

A reply whose subject or first line of its own text asks to be removed, in any of the app's languages ("Unsubscribe",
"Please take me off", "Abmelden", "Darme de baja", "Se désabonner", "Hủy đăng ký" and similar), carries the
<UiPath path="replies.badgeAsks" /> badge. Short words such as "Stop" count only when they stand alone. The badge
changes nothing by itself.

<Steps>

1. Open the reply and select <UiPath path="replies.unsubscribeContact" />. It shows while the contact is subscribed.

2. Read the dialog, then select <UiPath path="replies.unsubConfirm" />.

   <Screenshot
     id="replies-unsubscribe"
     alt="The Unsubscribe Diego Alvarez? dialog over his reply to Weekly Digest #141: Diego stops getting marketing email from Acme Newsletter, transactional email still reaches him, and the change is recorded on the contact with this reply as the evidence and in the audit log, with Cancel and Unsubscribe."
   />

</Steps>

The contact leaves every list and gets a marketing-only entry on the [suppression list](/audience/suppressions/),
as the unsubscribe link of the email they answered would do: Broadcasts and Automations stop, System and
transactional mail still arrive. The consent history shows "Unsubscribed from the replies inbox after a reply from …"
with <UiPath path="audience.source.reply" /> as the source, and the campaign counts the unsubscribe. A contact who had
already unsubscribed stays as they are, and the message says so.

## How ReputeCast sorts what arrives

1. Every message ReputeCast sends while replies stay in its inbox has a Reply-To address of its own on the receiving
   domain, with a signed tag that names the message. A reply to that address is tied to the message, so to its contact
   and its campaign or automation. An address with a tag that doesn't verify is other mail.
2. The headers decide whether a reply is automatic: `Auto-Submitted` other than `no`, `X-Autoreply`, `X-Autorespond`,
   `X-Autoresponder`, `Precedence` set to `auto_reply`, `bulk`, `junk` or `list`, or an empty `Return-Path`.
3. Spam is the receiving provider's verdict when it gives one, else `X-Spam-Flag: YES` or an `X-Spam-Status` that
   starts with `Yes`.
4. A message that arrives twice with the same `Message-ID` is kept once, so a provider's retry never shows a reply
   twice.
5. ReputeCast keeps the text of a reply, up to 64 KB; an HTML-only reply is turned into text. Attachments aren't kept:
   the reply lists their names and sizes, and asks the reader to send the file another way if you need it.

DMARC aggregate reports and feedback-loop reports that arrive at the receiving domain never show here. See
[receive replies and unsubscribe emails](/providers/receiving-mail/).

## Where replies show up elsewhere

- **The contact's timeline.** A person's reply shows on their profile as "Replied to October Product Update" with the
  start of the text and a link to the reply, under **Engagement**. Auto-replies, spam and other mail stay off it. See
  [read a contact's profile](/audience/contacts/#read-a-contacts-profile).
- **Notifications.** People who can view replies get a "New replies" notification, such as "3 new replies", at most
  every 15 minutes per workspace. It counts people's replies only. See [notifications](/team/notifications/).
- **Automations.** A flow can stop when the contact replies to one of its emails, and an email branch can check
  whether they replied. Both need replies in the inbox. See [triggers and steps](/automations/triggers-and-steps/).
- **Webhooks.** `email.replied` fires for every reply, auto-replies included (with `auto_reply` set), and
  `email.received` for other mail; spam fires neither. An endpoint gets them only while the person it sends for has
  `replies:view`. See [webhook events](/reference/webhook-events/).

## What is kept, erased and exported

- Received mail is deleted after a year; the message as it arrived, kept to read it, after 7 days.
- Erasing a contact deletes every message from their address or linked to them, and what arrived for those messages.
  See [erase contacts](/audience/contacts/#erase-contacts).
- <UiPath path="audience.profile.exportData" /> has a `replies` section with each message's time, kind, sender, subject
  and text.
- Marking replies and unsubscribing from a reply are in the audit log.

## Keyboard shortcuts

<kbd>J</kbd> and <kbd>K</kbd> open the next and the previous reply in the list, and <kbd>E</kbd> marks the open reply
done. They don't work while you type in the search or while a dialog is open. All
[keyboard shortcuts](/reference/keyboard-shortcuts/) are on one page.

## Check that it worked

- Reply to a test campaign from your own mailbox. Once it arrives (a sending provider passes it on at once; a mailbox
  is read every minute), the reply shows in <UiPath path="replies.viewOpen" /> with the campaign's name, and the
  contact's timeline shows "Replied to …".
- After <UiPath path="replies.markDone" />, the reply is in <UiPath path="replies.viewDone" /> and the chips' counts
  moved by one.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Replies arrive here once ReputeCast receives mail."** → nothing is set up yet. Select
  <UiPath path="replies.setUp" /> and follow [receive replies and unsubscribe emails](/providers/receiving-mail/).
- **"No replies yet." though readers replied** → their replies went to your own reply-to address. Check that
  <UiPath path="senders.replyHandling" /> is <UiPath path="senders.receiving.keepInInbox" />; emails sent before the
  change keep their old Reply-To.
- **A reply is in Other mail** → it was sent to an address ReputeCast didn't hand out, or the email it answers was
  deleted. Read it there; it is never tied to a contact.
- **Mark done or Unsubscribe contact is disabled** → hover it for the reason: your role lacks `replies:manage`, or
  `subscribers:edit` for unsubscribing.
- **No Unsubscribe contact button** → the reply isn't from a contact, or the contact isn't subscribed any more.
