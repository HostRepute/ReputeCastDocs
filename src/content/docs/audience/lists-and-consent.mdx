A contact joins one or more [lists](/reference/glossary/#list), and each list keeps its own
[consent](/reference/glossary/#consent) with the evidence of how it was given. With
[double opt-in](/reference/glossary/#double-opt-in), a new subscriber gets mail only after they click the link in a
confirmation email, which proves the address is theirs and that they asked for it.

## How a subscription is confirmed

1. Someone signs up on a [signup form](/forms/signup-forms/) or the subscribe page. Their list membership is pending.
2. ReputeCast sends the confirmation email through the System [stream](/reference/glossary/#stream), so it doesn't
   depend on your marketing routes.
3. They click **Confirm my subscription**. Every list they are pending on is confirmed at once, and ReputeCast records
   the time, IP address, browser, and the consent text and version they agreed to.
4. If they haven't clicked after 24 hours, one reminder goes out (for signups under 7 days old).
5. From then on, they receive Broadcasts and Automations for those lists.

Link scanners that only check a link without opening it never confirm a subscription. The confirmation link doesn't
expire. A contact who never confirms stays pending and gets no marketing mail; ReputeCast doesn't remove pending
contacts on its own.

If no sending provider is connected to the System stream, confirmation emails wait and are retried every 5 minutes
for up to 3 days. See [routing](/providers/routing/) to connect one.

### What evidence is kept

For every consent change, ReputeCast stores the action, the source (form, page, import, API, team member), the time,
the IP address and browser, the exact consent text with its version and language, and the page, UTM parameters and
referrer of the signup. The profile shows the source, date, IP address and version;
<UiPath path="audience.profile.exportData" /> downloads all of it. See [Manage contacts](/audience/contacts/).

SMS and WhatsApp consent are kept apart from email consent, each with its own evidence. A STOP by text never touches
email, and an email unsubscribe never touches texts.

## Where double opt-in is set

- **Signup forms** have their own double opt-in switch, on for a new form.
- **Adding a contact** by hand: <UiPath path="audience.contacts.sendConfirmation" /> (double) or
  <UiPath path="audience.contacts.alreadyAgreed" /> (single).
- **Imports**: trust the source's opt-in, or re-permission everyone first, which imports them as pending and sends one
  confirmation email. See [Import contacts](/audience/import/).
- **Each list** has a double opt-in setting, on for a new list. Product checkouts and integration actions that add
  contacts follow it.
- **Referral signups** always confirm.

## Unsubscribes

- The unsubscribe link in an email, [one-click unsubscribe](/deliverability/one-click-unsubscribe/) and the
  unsubscribe page remove the contact from every list and add a marketing-only entry to the
  [suppression list](/audience/suppressions/). Broadcasts and Automations stop; System and transactional mail still
  arrive. The confirmation page offers **Resubscribe**, which brings their lists back.
- The [preference center](/forms/public-pages/) unsubscribes from one list at a time, without a suppression.
- A transactional email's unsubscribe link turns off only its own category.

## Suppressed contacts never come back by themselves

- A form signup from an address suppressed for a bounce, complaint, spam trap, invalid address or by hand changes
  nothing and sends nothing; the visitor sees the usual "Check your inbox" page.
- A form signup from someone who unsubscribed is always treated as double opt-in, even on a single opt-in form. Their
  suppression is lifted only when they click the confirmation link.
- **Add contact**, bulk **Add to list** and imports refuse or skip suppressed addresses.
- Removing a bounce or complaint from the suppression list puts the contact back to pending, so they need a new
  opt-in before they get mail again.

## Lists

Lists are created by an import (**New list**), by the setup of a sending provider or store, from an agency's
workspace template, or with the API ([Create a list](/api/operations/create-list/)). Creating, renaming and deleting
lists needs `lists:manage` (Owner, Admin and Editor). To delete or restore a list, use its menu on
[List health](/audience/list-health/#health-per-list).

## When to change them

- Keep double opt-in on for public forms. It keeps typos, fake signups and spam traps off your lists, and mailbox
  providers expect it.
- Use single opt-in only when you already have the person's consent on record, for example a customer who ticked a
  box at checkout.
- For an old or single opt-in list you import, re-permission everyone first, then follow up or remove non-responders
  with an [automation](/automations/overview/).
