<UiPath path="nav.audience > scoring.tabGrowth" /> shows how many contacts joined and left, through which signup form,
page, API key or integration, and how good each source's addresses turned out to be. Use the subscriber growth
sources and the UTM parameters captured at signup to find the campaigns and pages that bring readers who stay, and the
ones that bring typos and complaints.

## Before you begin

- Viewing Growth needs `subscribers:view`; exporting the tables needs `reports:view`. Owner, Admin, Editor and Viewer
  have both.
- UTM parameters and referring pages are captured by [signup forms](/forms/signup-forms/) (embedded and popup) and the
  hosted subscribe page. Contacts from imports, the API and integrations have no first-touch page.

## Read the Growth page

<Steps>

1. Go to <UiPath path="nav.audience > scoring.tabGrowth" />.

2. Pick the period (<UiPath path="growth.days30" />, <UiPath path="growth.days90" />,
   <UiPath path="growth.months12" /> or <UiPath path="growth.custom" />, up to 731 days) and the list. With
   <UiPath path="growth.allLists" />, the page counts contacts; with one list, it counts that list's members, each under
   how they joined that list.

3. Read the tiles. **Joined** counts contacts who joined a list for the first time; **Left** counts contacts who left
   every list, bounced, complained or were erased. Imports are counted apart, because moving a list isn't growth;
   turn on <UiPath path="growth.includeImports" /> to add them, shown as a marker on the import day.

   <Screenshot
     id="growth-sources"
     alt="Growth for 30 days: 1,756 joined, 472 left, net +1,284, and 9,509 imported contacts counted apart, above a chart of joins and departures per day for the four biggest sources and Other."
   />

4. Read **By source**. Each row is how contacts first joined: a form (embedded, popup or hosted), the subscribe page,
   an API key, an integration (Zapier, Make, n8n, WordPress), a person in ReputeCast, a referral, a partner or an
   import. It shows joins, departures, net growth, and bounce, complaint and human-open rates over the period's sends.
   Select a row's contact count to open those contacts.

5. Read **Departures by kind**, **Cohort retention** and **UTM and referring pages** further down (described below).

</Steps>

### Quality by source

Rates use Broadcast and Automation emails sent to each source's contacts. Bounces are counted over sent messages;
complaints and human opens over delivered ones. Machine opens and bot clicks never count.

- A source is flagged when its bounce or complaint rate is more than twice the workspace's rate: "Bounces 2.4× the
  workspace". A flagged API key gets a note with the likely cause and **Open the key**.
- With fewer than 50 contacts from a source, or fewer than 100 delivered messages in the period, the rates read **Too
  few to compare**.

<Aside type="tip">
  A flagged signup form or API key usually means typos or missing consent on the site. Turn on
  [real-time address checks](/audience/verification/#check-addresses-on-signup-forms) on the form, or check addresses
  on your site before calling the API.
</Aside>

### Cohort retention

Each row is the contacts who first joined in one calendar month (imports left out), with the share still subscribed
after 30 and 90 days. A cohort shows "in progress" until every member reaches the mark. Pick a source to compare one
source's cohorts with the rest.

### UTM and referring pages

The **UTM parameters** tab groups joins by `utm_source / utm_medium / utm_campaign`; the **Referring pages** tab by the
page the visitor came from, stored without its query string. Both list the top five, then "Other". "(none)" means the
signup page had no UTM parameters; "None (typed or bookmarked)" means there was no referrer; "Not captured" means the
contact joined before capture started. The values are saved with each signup's consent record, and the first one is
the contact's first touch.

`utm_content` and `utm_term` are stored with the consent record too, but this page and its export don't show them.

## Export the tables

Select <UiPath path="growth.exportCsv" />. The CSV holds totals only, no addresses, in sections: sources, departures,
UTM combinations, referring pages and cohorts. Each export is in the audit log.

## Check that it worked

- A test signup through a form, with `?utm_source=test` in the page address, appears under its form in **By source**
  and under `test` in **UTM parameters**. Joins are attributed within about a minute.
- The contact's profile shows "Joined _date_ · _source_".

## Troubleshooting

- **A source's rates read "Too few to compare"** → wait for more sends, or pick a longer period.
- **All joins show "Not captured"** → those contacts joined before UTM capture started, or came from an import, the
  API or an integration.
- **Today's sends are missing from the quality rates** → quality figures are rolled up hourly for days before today.
- **Net growth here differs from List health** → this page counts contacts once; List health counts list members, so
  a contact on two lists counts twice there.
