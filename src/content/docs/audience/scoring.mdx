Give every contact a [score](/reference/glossary/#score) from 0 to 100 that adds points for what interested people do,
such as clicking your pricing page or starting a trial, and takes points away for soft bounces and unsubscribes. With
email engagement scoring you can find your most interested readers, send them a different version of a block, or start
an automation when someone becomes Hot.

<Aside type="note">
  The score and [engagement tiers](/audience/list-health/) do different jobs. Tiers protect your sender reputation and
  decide who gets Broadcasts. The score measures interest. A score never stops or starts mail by itself.
</Aside>

## Before you begin

- Editing scoring needs `scoring:edit` (Owner, Admin and Editor). Anyone who can see contacts can see the rules, the
  spread and every contact's score.
- Until there is a rule, contacts have no score: they show "—", and segments and triggers that use the score match
  nobody.

## Set up scoring

<Steps>

1. Go to <UiPath path="nav.audience > scoring.tabScoring" />. The first time, select
   <UiPath path="scoring.useSuggested" /> to start from suggested rules you can change (opens, clicks, a pricing-page
   click, a beta form, a `trial_started` API event, soft bounces and unsubscribes), or <UiPath path="scoring.addOne" />.

   <Screenshot
     id="scoring-rules"
     alt="Scoring: 29,528 Cold, 14,207 Warm and 4,816 Hot contacts with bands starting at 20 and 50, the spread in 10-point buckets, bands by tier, and the first of 8 rules."
   />

2. Select <UiPath path="scoring.addRule" /> and choose **When**:

   <TableScroll label="What a rule can count">

   | When | What counts |
   | --- | --- |
   | Human open | A human open of any email, or of emails to one list. Machine opens never count. |
   | Click | A person's click in any email, or on a link whose address contains some text (for example `/pricing`). A click counts once it [settles](/reference/glossary/#settled-click) as a person's; bot clicks never count. |
   | Form submitted | A confirmed submission of one signup form. |
   | API event | An event your app sends through the API, such as `trial_started`. |
   | Soft bounce | A soft bounce that wasn't delivered after the retries (counted 72 hours later). |
   | Unsubscribed from a list | Any list, or one list. |
   | Field condition | A custom field that is one of some values, is not a value, or is set. Counts while true. |

   </TableScroll>

   Only Broadcast and Automation emails count; transactional and System mail never does. A click also counts as an
   open.

3. Set **Points** from −50 to +50 (not 0) and a **Cap**: **No cap**, **Once**, **Once a day**, **Once a week**,
   **Once a month**, or at most a number of points a day, week or month. Field conditions use **While true**. Days,
   weeks and months follow the workspace time zone; weeks start on Monday.

4. Read **If you save**: how many contacts would change band, with the new spread drawn over today's. Select
   **Apply**.

5. Adjust the bands with **Warm from** and **Hot from**. The band names are fixed, so segments and triggers read the
   same everywhere.

6. Under **Decay and limits**, keep **Decay** on so Hot means interested now: after a number of weeks without a human
   open or click, a percentage of the score comes off each week, never below 0. Decay applies to activity points, not
   field conditions.

7. Select **Save**. The dialog lists who moves between bands. If a live automation starts on a score crossing, it asks
   **Start automations for them?**; keep <UiPath path="scoring.dontStart" /> unless you want everyone who crosses
   only because of your change to enter it now.

</Steps>

After a rule change, every score is recalculated in the background from the last 180 days. Until it finishes,
contacts keep their previous score, and segments and triggers use it. You get a notification when it is done.
Changing only the band thresholds applies at once, without a recalculation.

## Defaults and limits

<LimitsTable area="scoring" />

You can have up to 50 rules; a rule that is switched off still counts toward the 50.

## Where the score is used

- **Contacts**: a sortable **Score** column, a score band filter and a score range under **More filters**. See
  [Manage contacts](/audience/contacts/).
- **Contact profile**: the score, its band, the last change and a breakdown by rule.
- **Segments**: score and score band conditions, and the `score` and `score_band` columns in SQL. See
  [Build segments](/audience/segments/).
- **Automations**: the "score crosses" trigger starts when a score rises to or falls below a value, at most once per
  contact in a number of days (90 by default). See [triggers and steps](/automations/triggers-and-steps/).
- **Conditional content**: show a block only to Hot contacts. See
  [Show blocks to some readers](/campaigns/conditional-content/).

The **Used by** card on the Scoring page lists every automation, segment and conditional block that reads the score.

## Check that it worked

- The spread chart shows contacts in all three bands.
- A contact's profile shows the score and which rules gave the points.

## Troubleshooting

- **Every contact shows "—"** → no rule is saved yet. Add a rule or start with the suggested rules, then save.
- **"Points go from −50 to +50, and not 0"** → change the points.
- **"A cap of _X_ a week is lower than the _Y_ points…"** → raise the cap to at least the rule's points, or lower the
  points.
- **"Scoring can have at most 50 rules"** → delete a rule; switching one off doesn't free a slot.
- **"Someone else saved scoring a moment ago"** → select **Reload**, then make your change again.
- **Recalculation stopped** → scores are unchanged. Select **Try again**; it continues where it stopped.
