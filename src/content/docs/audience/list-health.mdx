[List health](/reference/glossary/#list-health) sorts every contact into an
[engagement tier](/reference/glossary/#engagement-tier) by how long ago they last opened or clicked, and a sunset
policy decides what happens to the ones who stopped reading. This is ReputeCast's email list hygiene: mailing people
who never open hurts your [sender reputation](/reference/glossary/#sender-reputation) with every mailbox provider,
so quiet contacts get one chance to come back before Broadcasts stop.

## How tiers work

1. **Engagement date.** For each contact, ReputeCast takes the latest human open or click. Machine opens (Apple Mail
   Privacy Protection, image proxies that fetch mail as it arrives, security scanners) and clicks from link scanners
   don't count. Engagement history carried over by an import counts. A contact who never engaged is measured from
   the day they joined.
2. **Tier.** The number of days since that date puts the contact in Active, Cooling, At risk or Sunset, using the
   thresholds in the sunset policy.
3. **Nightly run.** Tiers are recalculated once a night, at 03:00 in the workspace time zone. The page header shows
   the time of the last run. A saved policy change applies from the next run.
4. **Sunset actions.** When a contact enters Sunset and the re-engagement email is on, ReputeCast adds the tag
   `sunset` to them (and removes it when they come back). After the set number of days without an open, Broadcasts
   stop for them; Automations and System mail continue. If you turned it on, the contact is erased after the set
   number of months in Sunset.

A contact who opens again moves back to Active on the next run, and the `sunset` tag and the Broadcast stop are
cleared.

## The sunset policy

The <UiPath path="audience.health.policyTitle" /> card on <UiPath path="nav.audience > audience.tabHealth" /> holds
the thresholds and the actions. Saving it needs `lists:manage` (Owner, Admin and Editor).

- <UiPath path="audience.health.activeUntil" />, <UiPath path="audience.health.atRiskFrom" />,
  <UiPath path="audience.health.sunsetFrom" />: the tier thresholds in days. At risk must start at least two days
  after Active ends, so Cooling has room, and Sunset must start after At risk.
- <UiPath path="audience.health.countMachine" />: keep <UiPath path="audience.health.humanOnly" />. **Yes** lets
  machine opens keep contacts Active, so people who never read your email stay in Broadcasts.
- <UiPath path="audience.health.reengage" />: tags new Sunset contacts `sunset`. Build or edit the automation that
  sends the email with <UiPath path="audience.health.editFlow" />; make it start on the tag `sunset` being added (see
  [triggers](/automations/triggers-and-steps/)). The "win back so far" rate is the share of contacts who left Sunset
  after entering it.
- **Stop sending Broadcasts _N_ days after that if there's still no open**: on by default, 30 days.
- **Delete the contact after _N_ months in Sunset**: off by default. When on, contacts are erased with their consent
  history; suppressions stay.

Select <UiPath path="audience.health.savePolicy" />. The banner under the card says what happens this week, for
example how many contacts enter Sunset and how many stop receiving Broadcasts.

<Aside type="note">
  The card can't change the number of days for the Broadcast stop or the months before deletion, or switch the
  whole policy off. Use the API for those: [Save the sunset policy](/api/operations/save-sunset-policy/)
  (`stopAfterDays`, `deleteAfterMonths`, `enabled`).
</Aside>

## Health per list

The <UiPath path="audience.health.perList" /> table shows each list's size, tier mix and net growth, and its
human-open, unsubscribe, complaint and bounce rates for Broadcasts over the last 30 days. Each list gets a verdict:

<TableScroll label="List health verdicts">

| Verdict | When |
| --- | --- |
| Needs attention | Complaints 0.1% or more, bounces 2% or more, or unsubscribes 0.5% or more |
| Excellent | Human opens 50% or more, complaints under 0.02% and bounces under 1% |
| Good | Anything else, including lists with no sends yet |

</TableScroll>

A list's menu offers <UiPath path="verification.verifyEllipsis" /> and <UiPath path="audience.health.deleteList" />.
Deleting asks you to type the list's name and shows what it affects: signup forms and automations that use the list
pause, and scheduled campaigns come off the schedule. Contacts, their other lists and suppressions stay. **Undo** is
offered for 10 seconds; after that, restore the list from its entry in <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.audit" />
for 30 days.

## When to change the defaults

- **Shorter thresholds** suit daily newsletters, where 30 days without an open is a long silence. Longer ones suit
  monthly or quarterly sends, where a reader can't open more often than you send.
- **Keep the Broadcast stop on.** Contacts who haven't opened for 180 days or more raise bounce and complaint rates
  and drag down inbox placement for everyone else.
- **Verify At risk and Sunset addresses** before a win-back campaign: the At risk and Sunset tier cards have a
  <UiPath path="verification.verifyTitle" /> button. See [Verify email addresses](/audience/verification/).
- **Turn on deletion** only if your privacy policy promises to remove inactive people. Erasing can't be undone.

The [score](/audience/scoring/) is a different measure: tiers protect deliverability and decide who gets Broadcasts;
the score measures interest and never stops or starts mail by itself.
