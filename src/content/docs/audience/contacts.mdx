Find, filter and change your [contacts](/reference/glossary/#contact) in
<UiPath path="nav.audience > audience.tabContacts" />, and open any profile to see its fields, consent per list and
every email it was sent. This page covers how to manage email subscribers one at a time and in bulk; to bring many
in at once, [import them](/audience/import/).

## Before you begin

Permission keys say `subscribers`; the app says contacts.

<TableScroll label="Permissions for contact actions">

| Action | Permission | Built-in roles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See contacts, profiles and activity | `subscribers:view` | Owner, Admin, Editor, Viewer |
| Add a contact | `subscribers:create` | Owner, Admin, Editor |
| Add to or remove from a list, add or remove a tag, resend opt-in, suppress, edit fields and the address, change saved views | `subscribers:edit` | Owner, Admin, Editor |
| Verify addresses | `subscribers:verify` (sensitive) | Owner, Admin |
| Export a CSV or a contact's data | `subscribers:export` (sensitive) | Owner, Admin |
| Erase contacts | `subscribers:delete` (sensitive) | Owner, Admin |

</TableScroll>

A role limited to some lists sees only the contacts on those lists. See
[roles and permissions](/team/roles-and-permissions/).

## Find contacts

<Screenshot
  id="contacts"
  alt="Contacts: saved views as chips, each with a ⋯ menu, the filter row ending in 10,000+ contacts and Columns · 10, then the table with paid status, phone, lists, tier, score, consent and mailbox provider."
/>

<Steps>

1. Go to <UiPath path="nav.audience > audience.tabContacts" />.

2. Type in **Search email, name or tag**. A whole address finds that contact; otherwise search matches part of an
   email address or a name, or a tag exactly, whatever case you type.

3. Narrow the table with the filters: **List**, **Tier** ([engagement tier](/audience/list-health/)), score band,
   **Mailbox provider**, **Consent**, language and address check. <UiPath path="audience.contacts.moreFilters" /> adds
   <UiPath path="scoring.contacts.growthSource" /> (the [growth sources](/audience/growth-sources/) your contacts
   joined through), <UiPath path="audience.contacts.tag" />, <UiPath path="audience.contacts.joinedBetween" /> (two
   days in the workspace's time zone), <UiPath path="audience.contacts.fromImport" /> (the contacts one finished
   [import](/audience/import/) created), <UiPath path="audience.contacts.segment" />, UTM values and a score range.
   Filters for paid status, purchases, phone and SMS consent, push and WhatsApp appear once those features are in use.

   The mailbox provider comes from the address's domain. For a company's own domain it comes from the domain's MX
   records: Google Workspace counts as Gmail, Microsoft 365 as Outlook, Yahoo-hosted domains as Yahoo and iCloud
   custom domains as iCloud. A company domain counts as Other until its MX records are looked up, which a background
   check does every 2 minutes; an import looks them up as it runs.

4. Pick the columns with <UiPath path="audience.contacts.columns" />: Lists, Tier, Score, Consent, Mailbox provider,
   Last open, Joined, Source and any of your <UiPath path="audience.contacts.customFields" />.
   <UiPath path="audience.contacts.resetColumns" /> goes back to the usual ones.

5. To keep the filters and columns, select <UiPath path="audience.contacts.saveCurrent" />, name the view and select
   **Save view**. Tick **Only for me** to keep it private; shared views show for everyone in the workspace.

</Steps>

The table is sorted by last activity. Select the <UiPath path="audience.contacts.colContact" />,
<UiPath path="audience.contacts.colScore" />, <UiPath path="audience.contacts.colLastOpen" /> or
<UiPath path="audience.contacts.colJoined" /> header to sort by it (highest or newest first, email A to Z); select
it again to go back to last activity. **Last open** shows "never", a date, or "_date_ · machine" when the last open
came from a mail client that loads images by itself, such as Apple Mail Privacy Protection. A red **Suppressed** badge
in **Consent** means the address is on the [suppression list](/audience/suppressions/).

Past 10,000 matches, the line under the table reads "1 to 50 of 10,000+"; select
<UiPath path="audience.contacts.countAll" /> for the exact number.

### Change a saved view

Each view's chip has a **⋯** menu: <UiPath path="audience.contacts.renameView" />,
<UiPath path="audience.contacts.saveIntoView" /> (the view takes the filters and columns on screen) and
<UiPath path="audience.contacts.deleteView" />. Deleting a shared view removes it for everyone; its contacts stay
where they are. A personal view can be changed only by the person who saved it. Each view also has its own address
(Contacts with `?view=` and the view's id), so the "Used by" lists on [Lists & fields](/audience/lists-and-fields/)
and the table on List health open it.

On a phone, the saved views and the filters stack above the table, and the app's sections move to a bar at the
bottom of the screen.

## Add a contact

<Steps>

1. Select <UiPath path="audience.contacts.addContact" />.

2. Enter the email, optionally first and last name and tags, and tick at least one list.

3. Under **Consent**, choose:

   - <UiPath path="audience.contacts.sendConfirmation" /> (default): double opt-in. The contact stays pending until
     they confirm in your forms' [confirmation email](/forms/signup-forms/#double-opt-in-confirmation), sent through
     the System stream.
   - <UiPath path="audience.contacts.alreadyAgreed" />: single opt-in. They are subscribed at once. Use this only when
     you have their consent on record.

4. Select **Add contact**. The contact's source shows as "Added by the team", with your name in the consent history.

</Steps>

An address on the suppression list, or one that is already a contact, is refused. See
[Lists, consent and double opt-in](/audience/lists-and-consent/) for how each consent status works.

## Change many contacts at once

Tick contacts on the current page, or the box in the header for the whole page, then pick an action in the bulk bar.
Once a whole page is ticked, a banner offers **Select all _N_ matching**: actions then reach every contact the
filters match, on every page, after the exact number is counted. <UiPath path="audience.contacts.selectPageOnly" />
goes back to the page. The selection clears when you change the page, a filter or the page size.

<Screenshot
  id="contacts-bulk"
  alt="Contacts with the page's rows ticked: the bulk bar with Add to list, Remove from list, Add tag, Remove tag, Resend opt-in, Verify addresses, Export CSV, Suppress and Delete, and the banner offering Select all 10,000+ matching."
/>

<TableScroll label="Bulk actions">

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| <UiPath path="audience.contacts.addToList" /> | Subscribes them to a list at once. Suppressed addresses are refused. |
| <UiPath path="audience.contacts.removeFromList" /> | Takes them off a list. They keep their other lists, and their history shows the team removed them, not an unsubscribe, so they can be added back. |
| <UiPath path="audience.contacts.addTag" /> | Adds a tag (lower case, up to 60 characters). |
| <UiPath path="audience.contacts.removeTag" /> | Removes the tag you type. |
| <UiPath path="audience.contacts.resendOptin" /> | Sends a confirmation email through the System stream as a background job in the jobs tray. It skips confirmed and suppressed contacts and anyone still within the [confirmation email limit](/audience/lists-and-consent/#defaults-and-limits), and says how many it sent and skipped. |
| <UiPath path="audience.contacts.verifyAddresses" /> | Opens the [verify dialog](/audience/verification/#verify-a-list-segment-or-selection) for the selection. |
| <UiPath path="audience.contacts.exportCsv" /> | Downloads the page's selected contacts as CSV. For all matching, the export runs in the background (see below). |
| <UiPath path="audience.contacts.suppress" /> | Adds the addresses to the suppression list for all mail. A reason is required. |
| <UiPath path="audience.contacts.delete" /> | Erases the contacts for good (see below). |

</TableScroll>

<Aside type="caution">
  <UiPath path="audience.contacts.addToList" /> subscribes everyone selected, including contacts who left that list in
  the preference center.
</Aside>

If some contacts can't be changed, a **Partly done** panel says how many succeeded and lists the others. Every bulk
change is in the audit log.

### Export contacts

<UiPath path="audience.contacts.exportView" />, next to <UiPath path="audience.contacts.columns" /> once a filter is
set, and <UiPath path="audience.contacts.exportCsv" /> on all matching run in the background, for any number of
contacts. The jobs tray follows the export and offers <UiPath path="audience.contacts.download" /> when it is done;
only the person who started it can download the file, until it expires (see
[Defaults and limits](#defaults-and-limits)). Every export is in the audit log.

The CSV has the columns `email`, `first_name`, `last_name`, `company`, `country`, `timezone`, `language`, `lists`,
`tier`, `consent`, `mailbox_provider`, `source`, `joined_at`, `last_open_at`, `last_click_at`, `tags`, then one column
per custom field. Times are in UTC.

## Erase contacts

Erasing removes the contact's personal data from everything ReputeCast keeps: the profile, tags, lists and consent
history, and also texts, push, paid membership, orders, scores, import reports and more (see
[how ReputeCast covers erasure requests](/deliverability/compliance/#how-reputecast-covers-each-one)). Delivery
statistics stay, without the address, so reports still add up.

<Steps>

1. Tick the contacts (or select all matching) and select <UiPath path="audience.contacts.delete" />.

2. Type the number of contacts to confirm, then select **Erase _N_ contacts**.

</Steps>

A contact who still pays through Stripe can't be erased: the dialog refuses the whole erase and links to Earn's paid
members. Cancel the subscription, then erase. Stripe keeps its own records of paid members; delete the customer in
Stripe too if you need to.

<Aside type="caution">
  Erasing can't be undone. It keeps the suppressions an address already has and adds none: an erased contact who
  unsubscribed, bounced or complained is still never emailed, even if an import brings them back. If the person must
  never be emailed again and isn't suppressed yet, [suppress](/audience/suppressions/) the address first, then erase
  the contact.
</Aside>

## Read a contact's profile

Select a contact's name to open the profile.

<Screenshot
  id="contact-profile"
  alt="Sam Rivera's profile: the address with Edit address, badges Active, Double opt-in, Outlook and Joined 9 Oct 2026 · Brevo import, email stats for 90 days, and a score card at 52, Hot, with a breakdown by rule."
/>

- **Header**: tier, consent, mailbox provider, when and how they joined, and a "Role address · no Broadcasts" badge
  for addresses such as `info@`. The figures cover emails sent in the last 90 days, human opens, clicks and the last
  open.
- **Address**: <UiPath path="audience.profile.editEmail" /> corrects a mistyped address. History and consent stay with
  the contact, the mailbox provider is looked up again, and the activity shows "Address corrected" with the old one.
  An address another contact has, or one on the suppression list, is refused.
- **Fields**: built-in and custom fields, tags and the address check result. **Edit** changes them; the audit log
  keeps the old and new values. Each tag has a **×** to remove it.
- **<UiPath path="audience.profile.consentTitle" />**: each list's status with how and when they subscribed or
  confirmed, from which IP address, and the consent text version.
  <UiPath path="audience.profile.removeFromList" /> takes them off one list, recorded as the team's change. With
  texting set up, email, SMS and WhatsApp consent show side by side.
- **Activity**: every delivery, open, click and consent change, filterable by **Deliveries**, **Engagement** and
  **Consent**. Each delivery names the [sending provider](/reference/glossary/#sending-provider) that carried it, marks
  failovers and retries, and shows the original SMTP code for bounces.
- Cards for the [score](/audience/scoring/), the [automations](/automations/overview/#see-who-is-at-a-step) they are in
  or went through, referrals, paid subscription and purchases appear when those features are in use.

The header actions:

- <UiPath path="audience.profile.resendOptin" /> sends a new confirmation email. If no System provider can send it,
  the page gives you the link to share instead.
- <UiPath path="audience.profile.exportData" /> downloads a JSON file with everything ReputeCast keeps about the
  contact, one section per source, with no cap (see
  [what the export holds](/deliverability/compliance/#how-reputecast-covers-each-one)). The export is in the audit log.
- <UiPath path="audience.profile.suppress" /> puts the address on the shared suppression list, with a reason.
- **Open preference center as _name_** (under <UiPath path="audience.profile.consentTitle" />) opens the contact's
  preference page in a new tab. Opening it is logged.

## Check that it worked

- A saved view appears in the **Saved views** row with its count.
- After a bulk action, a message says how many contacts changed; a resend or an export shows in the jobs tray.
- Exports, erasures and removals appear in <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.audit" />.

## Troubleshooting

- **A button is disabled** → hover it: it names the action your role can't take. Ask an Owner or Admin for a role
  that can.
- **"_email_ is on the suppression list"** when adding a contact → remove the address from the suppression list
  with a reason, but only if they gave you a new opt-in.
- **"No System provider could send the confirmation"** → connect a sending provider to the System stream in
  [routing](/providers/routing/), or share the link from the profile.
- **"A confirmation email went to _email_ a few minutes ago, or five already went today"** → the address reached the
  [confirmation email limit](/audience/lists-and-consent/#defaults-and-limits); try again after the time it names.
- **"This page of the list is out of date"** → contacts changed since the page loaded. Select
  <UiPath path="audience.contacts.firstPage" />.
- **A saved view has no ⋯ menu** → renaming, updating and deleting views needs `subscribers:edit`.
- **The count on the Contacts tab lags** → on large audiences a slow count is kept for a short while (see
  [Defaults and limits](#defaults-and-limits)); your team's changes refresh it at once.
