Connect an email verification service with your own account to find addresses that can't receive mail before they
bounce. ReputeCast sends each address to the service, then suppresses the invalid ones and tags the risky ones;
the service bills the checks to your balance there, and ReputeCast takes nothing.

Without a service, ReputeCast's own checks still run on every [import](/audience/import/), for free: syntax, MX
records, [role addresses](/reference/glossary/#role-address), disposable domains, typos and spam-trap patterns. A
service adds a mailbox-level check on top.

## Before you begin

- An account and an API key at one of the supported services:

  <ProviderMatrix family="verification" />

- To connect, change or remove a service you need `integrations:manage` (Owner and Admin; people only, never API
  keys or AI agents).
- To send addresses to a service you need `subscribers:verify`. Owners and Admins have it; Editors don't, and AI
  agents can never hold it. Downloading results also needs `subscribers:export`.
- The price per check from your plan at the service. ReputeCast uses it for every cost estimate.

<Aside type="note">
  Only the email addresses leave your instance, one per check. Names, fields, lists, tags and activity are never sent.
  Before every use, a notice names the service (and its data region) and the number of addresses.
</Aside>

## Connect a service

<Steps>

1. Go to <UiPath path="nav.settings > settings.integrations > verification.title" /> and select **Connect** on the
   service's card.

   <Screenshot
     id="verification-services"
     alt="Verification services with ZeroBounce connected as the default for bulk checks and imports and Kickbox for signup forms; each card shows its credits and price per check."
   />

2. Paste the API key. It is encrypted at rest and never shown again. For ZeroBounce, also pick the data region
   (United States or European Union).

3. Select <UiPath path="verification.testConnection" />. ReputeCast reads the credit balance and sends no address;
   nothing is saved until the test passes.

4. Enter <UiPath path="verification.priceLabel" /> (for example `0.008`).

5. Under <UiPath path="verification.defaultTitle" />, tick <UiPath path="verification.bulkAndImports" />,
   <UiPath path="verification.signupForms" />, or both. One service can be the default for both; ticking a default
   moves it from the other service.

</Steps>

## Verify a list or tier

<Steps>

1. Start from one of these places:

   - <UiPath path="nav.audience > audience.tabHealth" />: <UiPath path="verification.verifyTitle" /> on a tier card,
     or <UiPath path="verification.verifyEllipsis" /> in a list's menu under
     <UiPath path="audience.health.perList" />.
   - [Send Readiness](/campaigns/send-readiness/): **Verify _N_ addresses first** when At risk and Sunset recipients
     not checked in 90 days make up 10% or more of the audience.
   - A contact's profile: <UiPath path="verification.verifyNow" /> on the
     <UiPath path="verification.addressCheck" /> row (one credit).

2. In the dialog, pick the **Service** and keep <UiPath path="verification.skipRecent" /> on to avoid paying twice.
   Read the cost line (checks × price) and the balance after the job. If the balance is too low, the dialog says so
   and you add credits at the service first.

3. Select **Verify _N_ addresses**. The job runs in the background; you can close the tab. Its page shows the
   progress, the results so far and the time left. The service's rate limits set the pace.

4. Read the results when the job finishes:

   <Screenshot
     id="verification-job"
     alt="A finished job for the Sunset tier: 2,904 valid, 287 invalid and suppressed, 318 risky tagged as catch-all, disposable or role, and 106 unknown that can be checked again after 72 hours."
   />

   <TableScroll label="What each result does">

   | Result | What happens to the contact |
   | --- | --- |
   | Valid | Nothing changes but the result, its date and the service. Older `verify:*` tags are removed. |
   | Invalid | Added to the [suppression list](/audience/suppressions/) for all mail, reason "Invalid address" (or "Spam trap"), with the service as the source. |
   | Risky | Tagged with the reason: `verify:catch-all`, `verify:role`, `verify:disposable`, `verify:abuse` (ZeroBounce) or `verify:low-quality` (Kickbox, Bouncer, Emailable). Nothing else changes; segments and Send Readiness decide. |
   | Unknown | Tagged `verify:unknown`. It can be checked again 72 hours after the job ends. |

   </TableScroll>

   The service's own answer is kept beside the result and shown as "Invalid · _service_: _code_", the way bounce
   codes are.

5. Optional: select <UiPath path="verification.downloadCsv" /> for `email,result,reason,service_code,checked_at`.
   The download is in the audit log.

</Steps>

**Cancel** stops a running job and keeps the results so far; addresses not checked yet are never sent. When the
service stops answering, the job pauses and retries every 5 minutes for up to an hour (<UiPath path="verification.retryNow" />
tries at once), then stops with the results so far. A job stopped because the credits ran out offers **Resume** once
you add credits.

## Verify during an import

In the import [Preview](/audience/import/) step, the card **Verify the new addresses** checks only rows that would
become new contacts, with the cost shown. Invalid addresses are skipped; risky and unknown ones are imported and
tagged. The check is optional: <UiPath path="verification.importWithout" /> continues with ReputeCast's own checks.
This check always sends every new address, even ones checked in the last 90 days.

## Check addresses on signup forms

In the [form builder](/forms/signup-forms/), under <UiPath path="forms.builder.spam" />, turn on **Check each address with _service_
as people sign up**. It uses the default service for signup forms and needs `forms:edit` and `subscribers:verify`.

- Invalid: the form asks the person to fix the address.
- Disposable: rejected only when the form rejects disposable domains and role addresses; otherwise accepted and
  tagged.
- Risky and unknown: accepted and tagged.
- No answer within 3 seconds: the signup goes through and the address is checked later.

Real-time checks are counted per day and per form on the service's page, not one by one in the audit log. Turning
them on or off is audited.

## Defaults and limits

<LimitsTable area="verification" />

When the balance drops under 1,000 credits, or below what a stopped job needs, everyone with `integrations:manage`
gets a "Verification credits low" notification. The balance is read at each use and every hour.

## Check that it worked

- The contact's <UiPath path="verification.addressCheck" /> row shows the result, the service and the date.
- In <UiPath path="nav.audience > audience.tabContacts" />, the <UiPath path="verification.addressCheck" /> filter
  lists contacts by result or **Never checked**.
- <UiPath path="nav.audience > audience.tabSuppressions" /> shows the invalid addresses with the service as the source.

## Troubleshooting

- **The Verify buttons are disabled** → you don't have `subscribers:verify`. Ask an Owner or Admin.
- **"Not enough credits"** → add credits at the service, then select
  <UiPath path="verification.readBalanceAgain" /> on the service page.
- **"The key was rejected"** → select <UiPath path="verification.replaceKey" /> on the service page, then resume the
  job.
- **Check _N_ again finds nothing to check** → Unknown results count as checked. Untick
  <UiPath path="verification.skipRecent" /> in the dialog.
- **Unknown results stay unknown** → ReputeCast doesn't recheck them on its own. Verify them again after 72 hours.
